### §9.0 — The grain of a unit (PUT TO THE USER, not settled) — **ENRICHES**

Everything read is about **decisions**, not factors: annotation standards, agreement between

**The one exception is worth the user's attention at the moment he rules.** Row 3's unified chord
model [RELAYED] represents a chord as **a decision carrying an explicit attribute set beneath it** —
levels from score-surface pitches through pitch functions (root, bass, **chord-tone status**) to
chord properties, with the unabstracted level retained in the same object. **That is precisely the
"reconciliation" reading §9.0 names as its third candidate**, exhibited by a working representation

### DP-A — Divide the deciding by published field? **CHOSEN: no.** → **SUPPORTS**, and newly

The framework's recorded ground is the neural multi-task lineage. **Two independent supports were
added by this pass, from different groups and different architectures:**

- **Row 1 [RELAYED, confirmed at a targeted re-read].** The chord classifier outputs a distribution
  over **whole 1,540-symbol absolute chord labels** rather than per-field heads, and the paper states
  the reason: *"it is important that every feature of a chord is considered holistically as a unit,
  rather than potentially classifying the chord as a C minor 7th chord in 1st inversion"* — the
  worked case being a bass C genuinely ambiguous between a C major triad and an A minor seventh in
  first inversion. **A primary-source statement of DP-A's ground by authors who chose the holistic
  form on it.**
- **Row 12, BACHI (2026) [RELAYED].** It *does* factor the label — root × quality × bass — and then
  spends its whole architecture undoing the factoring: its ablation moves the **full-chord
  joint-consistency column 66.1 → 68.1 while the per-element columns barely move.** **The gain is
  coherence between separately-predicted elements, not per-element accuracy** — the
  "adds-machinery-to-undo-the-separation" pattern the framework already names, now dated 2026.

**Also on point, and about the label space rather than the deciding:** row 21's insight 2
[AT THE OBJECT for its own quotations via a relayed read — see §0] argues that flat one-of-K chord
classes misrepresent chord relationships, since *"C:maj7 and C:maj"* are not mutually exclusive
*"since the former contains the latter"*, and that one-of-K quantisation *"effectively mak[es] all
errors equivalent."* **ENRICHES DP-A's neighbourhood without bearing on its cut.**

**All five figures DP-A's defense cites are VERIFIED at their own primaries (V9).**

### DP-C — Is segmentation decided before, with, or after chord identity? **CHOSEN: with.** → **SUPPORTS, with one RIVAL-SHAPED item stated in full**

**Supports:**

- **V10 VERIFIED at the object**, every endpoint value: 68.8% frame accuracy with boundaries given
  against 23.3% finding them on the same piece; the segmental gains; the semi-CRF as the largest
  ablation contributor; the linear-versus-exponential formal result; the 26% tie-breaking ceiling.
  *One wording imprecision recorded: the framework's "on one corpus … on another" compresses four
  corpora into two. No value moves.*
- **★ ROW 1 SUPPLIES DIRECT PRIMARY EVIDENCE ABOUT BOUNDARY-FIRST DESIGNS, and it was the second
  pass's largest miss** — session 1 held it and the cross-check confirmed it near-verbatim. **A
  dedicated, well-trained standalone boundary model over-segmented in practice and needed a repair
  bolted after it:** *"we noticed that the CTM was over-segmenting the input, and the thresholds were
  difficult to tune."* The remedy is a merge rule permitting two consecutive windows to combine when
  the merged duration is under the maximum and both carry the same chord. **And the system does not
  commit that segmentation: the transition scores constrain which windows are legal, and the beam
  search then finds "the most probable complete and labeled path through the score"** over windows,
  chord labels and key labels together.
- **Row 17, Sapp [RELAYED].** The window-size trade-off in an author's own words: *"if too much music
  is analyzed at once, fewer important keys are suppressed; if too little music is analyzed at once,
  the chordal structure of the music is really being analyzed instead of the key structure."*
  **Qualitative evidence about the problem a decoded segmentation answers**, and equally an argument
  that no single window size is right.

**★ THE RIVAL-SHAPED ITEM: ROW 12, BACHI (2026) [RELAYED] — the one place a modern system answers
DP-C differently and performs well.**

BACHI predicts a binary boundary sequence with a separate supervised head and uses it to **condition
the encoder by feature-wise modulation** — so boundaries are neither committed before labelling nor
decoded with the labels; they are **a separately-learned signal injected as context**. On a
~1,500-piece classical corpus it reaches full-chord macro-accuracy **68.1** against Harmony
Transformer v2 62.1, ChordGNN 58.5, AugmentedNet 57.2 and a rule-based system 28.4.

**Why it is not a falsifier, stated precisely rather than waved away:**

1. **Its input contract is poorer than ours** — an unspelled piano roll, quantised, with no voice
   membership. Its design solves the enharmonic problem L0 does not have.
2. **Its output is less than L2 must publish** — absolute chord labels only; no tonality in the main
   system, no chord-tone assignment, no rivals.
3. **Nothing in it measures its cut against joint decoding on our axis.** It is measured on
   absolute-chord accuracy without tonality; DP-C's ground is measured on segment F-measure and frame
   accuracy with boundaries given versus found.
4. **Its own ablation points the other way on the question that matters here** — the machinery's gain
   concentrates in joint consistency, not in boundary accuracy.

**But it is a genuine third answer to DP-C that DP-C's candidate list does not contain** — the list
reads *before (published; every fixed grid); with (semi-Markov, measured); after (nobody)*, and
"separately supervised, injected as conditioning" is none of those three. **Recorded as an addition
to the candidate list, and as the comparable a measurement phase should benchmark against.**

### DP-D — Where does the chord-tone assignment live? **CHOSEN: part of L2's one decision.** → **THE RIVAL IS READ AND DEFUSED AT THE PRIMARY — with one addition to DP-D's own candidate list**

This is the disposition surface's one rival-shaped item, and it was the pass's most closely read row.

**What row 2 [RELAYED, both passes, cross-checked] actually is:**

- It runs **after** a chord label whose **root and quality are already fixed**, and it **holds them
  fixed** — the merging it does operates on its own internal windows within one given chord label and
  **does not move the input chord boundaries**.
- **★ Its stated ground is a VOCABULARY problem, not an argument against joint assignment:**
  *"Adding an additional feature to each chord label results in a multiplicative increase in
  vocabulary size … which reduces the possible training data per label and weakens predictive
  power."*
- **★ AND THE PAPER CONTAINS NO COMPARISON OF POST-HOC AGAINST JOINT.** Established by a question put
  directly at the text: no such discussion, no argument that post-hoc is preferable, **no measurement
  of what post-hoc costs.** *(Both sessions reached this independently — the strongest convergence in
  the whole cross-check exercise.)*
- **Its measured performance where it matters is weak.** Non-default accuracy — the alterations
  themselves, the thing the method exists to find — sits at **36.7% given ground-truth chord labels
  and 35.8% given a model's**, and never leaves the 28.8–39.2 band in any condition. The headline
  **89.2%** is a **per-window exact pitch-class-vector match on ground-truth labels**, over a corpus
  partly unreleased; the comparable figure on model labels is **73.2%**.

**★ THE ADDITION TO DP-D's CANDIDATE LIST, recorded because the list is incomplete rather than
wrong.** DP-D enumerates four candidates: an input from a detector running **first**; a layer of its
own **between** boundaries and chords; **part of L2's one decision** (chosen); **not represented at
all**. **Row 2 is none of them.** It is a fifth shape — *derived **after** the chord decision, as an
augmentation of L2's published output*, which in framework terms is an **L3 read-off**. DP-D's
recorded exclusion ground — *"a first-running detector's mistakes are unrecoverable downstream"* —
does not reach it, because it does not run first.

**DP-D's chosen position is nonetheless untouched**, and the reasons are on the record rather than
invented here: the ledger's C26 (duration weight cannot separate a long elaboration from a genuine
added tone, the distinction being functional and voice-leading); the analyst's own annotation quoted
in §4.2, in which the chord label *is* a function of which note is heard as elaboration; and the
measured benefit running in both directions (V1, VERIFIED). **The fifth candidate can only assign
tones consistent with a chord already chosen, so it cannot recover a case where the assignment is
what decides the chord** — which is exactly §4.2's worked case.

**Converging from a second direction — row 14, Hu & Arthur 2021 [RELAYED].** A surface-feature-only
non-chord-tone classifier beats the trivial all-chord-tone baseline **by only about 4–5 points out of
sample**, is style-dependent, and its own ground truth is **derived circularly from human
Roman-numeral analyses**, which its authors call *"far from an objective process."* **Same direction
as V11's F .72 with many "errors" that are plausible analytical choices** (VERIFIED at the object).

**Fenced, so it is not enlisted later:** row 3 models chord-tone status as a pitch function at the
same level as root and bass. **That is a representation choice and says nothing about WHEN the
assignment is decided.** A paper by four of the same authors as row 2 modelling it that way is
exactly the adjacency a later reader turns into an argument. **It is not one — that paper measures
nothing and infers nothing.**

- **Row 1 [RELAYED]** publishes absolute chord symbols and hands applied chords downstream **as key
