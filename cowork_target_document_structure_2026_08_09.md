## The seven surfaces, and who points at whom

**A — What the system SHOULD be.** ONE `ARCHITECTURE.md`, a section per layer, plus the
small delegated set of contract homes a section of A explicitly delegates a concern to
under the decisions-register rules (g)–(k) (`cowork_confidence_contract.md`,
`docs/scoring_model.md`, `docs/llm_integration.md`, the factorization contract, and — per
Ruling 39 — `cowork_score_census.md`'s rule-stating sections for corpus decisions). Every
recorded decision is written here, in the owning section's own voice, WITH its defense
beside it (criteria C1 and C2). The shape is sections-plus-delegations, not one file per
layer; changing that would be a new structural ruling.

**B — What was DECIDED, and its status.** `DECISIONS.md` (index) + `decisions/group_*.md`
(entries): the verbatim, the plain restatement, live / superseded / shelved, the home
pointer, the provenance. **B points at A** (rule (e): the index and pointer, never a
substitute home); A names a register identifier only in status-bearing spots (supersession
notes, tried-and-closed lines). The defense TEXT lives in A, not here. The load-bearing
property is C4: conformance is measurable from A alone, without consulting B.

**C — The known gaps.** `OPEN_ITEMS.md` (index, canonical status tokens, authoritative
statuses) + `open_items/OI-<n>.md` (narrative). **C points at A** (the specification the
gap is about) **and at B** (a non-conformance row points back at the decision it
violates). A stage may not open while a row gating it is open; establishment obligations
always gate.

**D — What IS, measured.** The gate blocks (A)–(D) in `CLAUDE.md`: the ratified baselines,
the grading and measurement conventions, the pinned regression stops and their reference
artifacts (`tools/robust_stop/`), with `STATUS.md` as the living state pointer. A states
SHOULD; D states MEASURED-IS; the distance between them is exactly what C rows.

**E — How work is DONE.** The guiding principles and Conventions (`CLAUDE.md`), the
dispatch protocol (`cowork_audit_protocol.md`), the writing standards and the kind-scoped
structure rule (`cowork_design_doc_template.md`). E governs sessions, never the system's
content.

**F — Provenance.** The archives, the ruling records, the session reports, the
ratification surfaces: append-only evidence that B's fields point into. Never a
session-start read, never a home — draining decisions OUT of F into A is much of what
phase 1 was.

**G — The derived layer.** The generated artifacts (`tools/audit/`: the finish line, the
guard state, the classifications, the establishment records) that keep A, B and C honest
mechanically — regenerated from the record at HEAD, never hand-maintained, each with its
own reproduce-check or STOP.

## The reference rules, in one place

B→A always; A→B only for status; C→A and C→B always; F is pointed INTO (from B's
provenance and C's narratives) and points nowhere; G derives FROM A, B and C and asserts
nothing of its own; D is read beside A and corrected only by measurement events under
their own gates; E binds everything and is edited only by the user or under the user's
scoped licence.

the remaining distance is derived at `tools/audit/phase1_finish_line.json` and no

the deferred terminology batches (OI-229's disposition).
