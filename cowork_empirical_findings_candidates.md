### C1 — An absent root does not mean a wrong reading, corpus-wide

- **The fact, approach-level:** A chord reading whose own root is not sounding is not thereby wrong.
  The published human analysis itself commits readings with an absent root, so a rule rejecting any
  chord whose root is absent removes correct answers as well as wrong ones.
- **Provenance:** `docs/scoring_model.md:1393–1399`, at line 1396 — *"the premise 'an absent root
  means a wrong reading' is false corpus-wide"*; the ground at `:1394–1396` — *"it fixed fewer cases
  than it broke, and two of the cases it broke are readings the published human analysis itself makes
  with an absent root"*.
- **Uncertainty, in the source's own terms:** *"The counts are in the record and are not restated here
  (D-431)."* The source gives no interval and none is invented here.
- **Establishment status, as the source declares it:** measured — the guard was *"built, measured and
  reverted entirely"*. Not upgraded.
- **Failure diagnosis:** stated — the premise the guard rested on is false of the repertoire; a second,
  structural cause rides with it (any guard changing a committed root changes the predecessor every
  later stretch reads, so its effect cascades into regions it never judged).
- **Survives? YES. Approach-level? YES. → PASSES.** This is plan §6.4's own named PASS example. The
  claim is about what the annotated music does, not about our scorer; throwing the implementation away
  leaves it standing. **The neighbouring sentence — *"do not retry the absent-root guard"* — is the
  FAIL half and is not proposed.**

### C2 — The information that disambiguates a third-above reading is NON-LOCAL

- **The fact, approach-level:** Where a sonority may be read as a chord or as the chord a third above
  it — C-E-G as C or as E minor inside something larger, A-C-E likewise — nothing available at the
  moment of the sonority separates the two readings: not the sounding pitch classes, not their
  weights, not the chord vocabulary, not the key. What separates them is the surrounding music: the
  following chord's root, the preceding chord's identity, and whether the bass falls on a strong beat.
  Cases where the third-above reading is CORRECT are indistinguishable on local evidence from cases
  where it is wrong.
- **Provenance:** `docs/scoring_model.md:1321–1335`, the bullet headed *"The information that
  disambiguates the third-above reading is NON-LOCAL, so no local discriminator can exist"*.
- **Uncertainty, in the source's own terms:** the evidence is two built discriminators, both of which
  regressed — *"a simple stack raised the wrong-root count on the primary corpus and a more elaborate
  one raised it further"*, with *"a sample of the regressions flipping one wrong answer into a
  different wrong answer"*. No count is restated (D-431).
- **Establishment status, as the source declares it:** SHELVED WITH EVIDENCE. *"No code change was
  committed."* The source states in terms: *"What is shelved is the LOCAL-GATE approach, not the
  problem."*
- **Failure diagnosis:** stated and is the finding itself — the discriminating information is not
  present in the local evidence, so no local rule can exist, however tuned.
- **Survives? YES. Approach-level? YES. → PASSES.** The ambiguity and the evidence that resolves it
  are properties of the music. This is the case `cowork_report_plan_evaluation_2026_08_21.md:133`
  itself names as the approach-level example.
- **The source's own bound, carried unaltered:** the section banner (`:1316–1319`) marks the subject
  ⚠ LEGACY and says *"Whether the joint estimator's modelled segmentation shows the same coupling is
  not stated here and is not asserted."* That bound attaches to C3, not to the non-locality claim.

- **Provenance:** `docs/scoring_model.md:1336–1346`.
- **Uncertainty, in the source's own terms:** measured at the shelved attempt above, *"where some of

- **Establishment status, as the source declares it:** measured on the legacy path; and the source
  names its own consequence — it is *"the reason the governing regression stop is an EXPLAINED PER-RUN

- **Failure diagnosis:** not a failure; a standing constraint derived from one.
- **Survives? UNDECIDABLE. Approach-level? UNDECIDABLE.** The source itself refuses the generalization:

### C4 — In arpeggiated harmony the wrong pitch can sound longer than the right one

- **The fact, approach-level:** Over a stretch of arpeggiated harmony, the duration-weighted pitch
  content of the pooled stretch does not identify the chord's root: the non-root tone can carry more
  duration than the root, so a reading taken from the pooled, duration-weighted aggregate prefers the
  wrong root. The evidence is not the problem — the correct root is preferred over the sub-stretch
  where that root actually sounds.
- **Provenance:** `docs/scoring_model.md:1386–1392` — *"pooling makes the answer worse — the aggregate
  is duration-weighted and the wrong pitch sounds longer than the right one, so the wrong root still
  wins the pooled reading"*; and *"The evidence was never the problem"*.
- **Uncertainty, in the source's own terms:** *"implemented, measured and reverted"*, and *"the run
  regressed both presets"*. No figure is restated.
- **Establishment status, as the source declares it:** measured, reverted. Recorded as a dead end.
- **Failure diagnosis:** stated — *"what is wrong is the predecessor signal"*, not the vertical
  evidence.
- **Survives? YES, on the music half. Approach-level? YES, on the music half. → PASSES (music half
  only).** The duration profile of an arpeggio is a property of the music. **The prohibition —
  *"do not retry any tone-aggregation approach"* — is the FAIL half and is NOT proposed**; it is
  precisely plan §6.4's *"prohibition on re-attempting a specific mechanism"*.

### C15 — The DCML MS3 and *When in Rome* chorale numberings are different schemes; pair by content, never by number

### C26 — Duration weight cannot separate a long non-chord tone from a genuine added tone

- **The fact, approach-level:** A pitch that does not belong to the harmony can sound for as long as
  one that does — a half-note suspension, a sustained anticipation. So a model that decides
  membership by how much time a pitch occupies cannot make the distinction at all, at any threshold:
  both sides of the distinction sit on the same side of any weight cut. The distinction is
  functional and voice-leading, not one of magnitude.
- **Provenance:** `cc_anchor_redesign_dossier.md:91–94` — *"Duration weighting **cannot** separate a
  long suspension (non-chord by *function*) from a genuine added tone (chord by function): both have
  high duration. That is a **functional / voice-leading** distinction, not a weight magnitude."*
- **Uncertainty, in the source's own terms:** the source states the claim without an interval; its
  supporting case set is the 25 cases enumerated in the same document, and its thresholds are quoted
  at `:78–82` and **not restated here (D-431)**.
- **Establishment status, as the source declares it:** read at the source code at a named commit,
  read-only, with no build — the document's own banner says *"READ-ONLY investigation … No
  code/behavior change."* The claim is established by reading the mechanism plus the enumerated
  cases; it is **not** a corpus-wide measurement and is not upgraded to one.
- **Failure diagnosis:** stated and is the finding itself — the quantity being thresholded does not
  carry the distinction being asked of it.
- **Survives? YES. Approach-level? YES. → PASSES.** The claim is about what the music does: non-chord
  tones in this repertoire are not systematically shorter than chord tones. Throw every
  implementation away and it stands.

### C27 — Embellishment discrimination is a segmentation question, not a within-sonority one
