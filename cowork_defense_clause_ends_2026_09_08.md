## 1. Why this pass exists

`tools/audit/defense_share.json` bounds a marked defense clause at *the end of the paragraph containing it*, using the paragraph-end function imported from the read-size tool — **and that function walks forward only until a BLANK LINE.** In `CLAUDE.md` the guiding principles are one numbered list with no blank lines between items, and the two register sections are single unbroken blocks. **So a marked clause runs from its marker to the next marker, swallowing every rule that stands between them**, and the published figure is not a bound on defense material in either direction.

**This pass replaces the mechanical end with an AUTHORED one, marker by marker, read at the file.** The arithmetic is deliberately NOT computed here: a hand-added total over 34 ranges is the transcribed measurement #17(f) forbids. **The ends are published so a generator can consume them**, STOPping on any end it cannot locate or that falls outside its span.

## 2. The criterion, and where it comes from

A clause's DEFENSE is what the convention of 2026-08-01 names and nothing else: **the published research or algorithm adopted, the measurement that decided it, or the constraint that forced it** — together with **the declined alternatives and the accepted costs**, which this record's own 2026-08-17 pruning act already treated as defense when it archived spans under the class name `defense-and-declined-alternatives` (its pointers stand at lines 27, 52, 59, 60, 477 and 479).

The defense ENDS at the last sentence giving a ground. It does **not** extend to:

- a **pointer** telling a session where a derived artifact lives, or that a set is generated and never hand-listed;
- a **STOP condition** or any other bar a session acts on;
- a statement of **what the rule does or does not do**, or what it does not reach;
- a **caveat bounding how a rule or a figure is READ** — which the satellite ruling's reading (ii) already names as operative text that stays;
- a **restatement bar** such as *no figure is restated here (#17f, D-431)*.

**Reading (ii) is STATED AND NOT RULED**, and this pass applies it as stated. **If it is ruled otherwise, several ends below move outward** and the pass is re-derivable from this file without re-reading `CLAUDE.md`.

## 3. One finding that simplifies the whole question

**All 34 marked runs do open genuine defense text.** Not one marker introduces something that is operative from its first word. **So the eligibility question reduces entirely to where each clause ENDS** — there is no marker to reject and no marker table change owed.

**Twenty-four OVER-RUN, and thirteen of those over-run badly**, meaning the measured range carries at least one whole rule that a session acts on today. **The over-running is worst exactly where the measured share was highest**: every one of the eleven decisions-register clauses over-runs, and the largest clause in the file — the bold founding instance in Conventions — carries three complete bullets of live convention.

**So the direction of the correction is certain and its size is not.** The corrected figure will be **substantially smaller** than the published one, and the reduction will fall hardest on the decisions register and on Conventions, which is where the published shares of 76.27 % and 65.80 % came from. **No corrected figure is offered here**, because computing it is a generator's act and not this side's.

**And one thing this pass does NOT change:** the marker table stands, all three rows match real markers, and every marker opens a genuine defense. **The defect was never in what was found; it was in where each find was taken to stop.**

**Two bounds this side declares rather than leaves unstated.** Every end above is an AUTHORED judgment, challengeable at its marker; what is established is that each is located at the file and that each falls inside its own span. And reading (ii) — that a caveat bounding how a rule or figure is read is operative and stays — is **stated and not ruled**; several ends move outward if it is ruled otherwise.

**What this act does NOT do.** It moves no text and prunes nothing. It changes no rule `CLAUDE.md` states. It creates, flips or discards no open-items row, writes no register entry and allocates no identifier. It does not lift the reading gate, resume the pruning pass, or move a verdict. And it does not say that any character it bounds MAY move — movability is the satellite ruling's question and this pass answers no part of it.
