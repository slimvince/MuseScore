  Bold markers inside a quoted sentence are dropped; the words are unchanged.

  twice, #6): a sub-item appended to the block, `- *Pointer (written at the ratification of face (x),
  <ratification record file>, correction N).* <text>`.

  wording kept): a sub-item appended to the block, headed with the answering record and the ratification
  record, its text in the ratified wording.

  `(was: "<former>"; <record that orders it>)`, the shape the successor already uses.

  ratification record and says the derived wording stands at the derivation, L2-S<n> — the successor's own
  form at L2-S8, L2-S42 and L2-S45.
