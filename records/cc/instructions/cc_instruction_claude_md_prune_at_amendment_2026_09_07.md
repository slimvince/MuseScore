## The licence, stated first because two previous sittings got it wrong

**A previous handoff entry twice relayed that an archiving cut to `CLAUDE.md` is blocked by
`D-645`, whose licence is scoped to homing acts. THAT IS FALSE, and it is corrected here at three
objects.** `D-645` (2026-08-07) widens what a HOMING dispatch may edit. Rule (D) below (2026-08-16,
later) is a standing ruling that an amendment to a governing document MOVES the newly superseded
text to its archive in the same act — an archiving authority over these files in its own right.
Ruling 2 of 2026-08-17 then ORDERED the archivability test applied to any span in the five that it
places. And the proof the record already reads it that way is in `CLAUDE.md` itself: **nine spans
were archived out of it on 2026-08-17, each leaving a dated pointer naming its class**, which no
homing act could have performed. **The user has in addition ordered this act directly, on
2026-09-07.** No new licence is needed and none is claimed.
