## Task 2 — the comparison — ONE commit PER OUTGOING DOCUMENT, in the population's order

**The unit, fixed before you begin (Ruling 8: the grain of a unit is the decision).** An OUTGOING
STATEMENT is one sentence or one bullet of the outgoing text that states what the analysis does,
must do, may assume, or must not do, about L0's subject (what the notated record supplies, what
may be assumed of it, what happens when it does not supply it) or L1's subject (the brief's seven
faces (a)–(g): note eligibility, ties, the change-point set and slices, metric strength, notated
boundary evidence, the local cadence cues, the form of what is published). A sentence that is
none of those — narrative, provenance, a test count, a commit hash, a pointer to another document —
is NOT an outgoing statement and is listed once per document under *not a statement*, so that
completeness can be checked by arithmetic at the document. **A paragraph is never a unit.** Where
one sentence carries two claims, it is two statements, each quoted with the sentence and marked
(i) and (ii).

**(a) Per outgoing document, in the population's order, establish the population at the document
itself:** count its outgoing statements; the count goes in that document's manifest header in the
reading file and nowhere else.

**(b) Per outgoing statement:** quote it verbatim with its location (document and heading; a line
number only as a locator beside the heading, never as the anchor); name the derived statement or
statements (by S-number) that speak to it, or state that none does; on the **CURRENT-TEXT AXIS**
say, for each named derived statement, exactly one of **AGREES / DIFFERS / THE DERIVATION IS
SILENT** — evidence about the present text, never a verdict on the derivation; then the
**PROPOSED DISPOSITION**, exactly one of:

- **ADOPTED — carried**: a derived statement carries its content in substance (name it).
- **ADOPTED — proposed**: no derived statement carries it, it is not implementation-describing,
  not another charter's, not historical, and the worth test does not discard it. **This is a
  PROPOSAL for the user: the outgoing content would be added to the derived specification.** Write
  the proposal in one sentence beside the row. Never write it into the derivation.
- **RELOCATED**: its content belongs to another charter. Name the charter (L2, L3, the
  voice-leading axis, the measurement design) by `FRAMEWORK.md` §5's own words, and append the
  statement to the TRANSFER LIST section of the reading file.
- **QUARANTINED**: it states how the implementation currently works, or a measured property of it.
  Write the audit question in one sentence beside the row. (The derivation's §6.2 lists such
  passages it met inside the pack; where an outgoing statement is one of those, say so.)
- **DISCARDED**: under principle #10's worth test, with the finding, the date (2026-09-02) and the
  reason in the row — all three, or the disposition does not reach the row (D-677's shape).
- **HISTORICAL**: an event, a build state, a superseded plan, a status.
