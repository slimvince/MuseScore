2. **One operational definition was found, in the same authors' earlier paper** — "Grouping Structure Generator
   Based on Music Theory GTTM", IPSJ Journal 48(1), 2007, pp. 284–299 (in Japanese), open at the JAIST repository.
   **[relayed through the fetch tool's text extraction — not read at the page images; some mathematical symbols were
   lost in extraction.]** As extracted, at p. 291: GPR6 is computed as a per-note value D6(i); candidate parallel
   stretches start and end on beat positions and last a whole number of beats, up to half the piece's beats;
   similarity counts the notes whose onsets coincide within the two stretches and, among those, the matching pitch
   differences between neighbouring notes; three parameters (Wm, rhythm against pitch; Wl, preference for longer
   stretches; Ws, weighting towards start or end) adjust it; the paper says the similarity measure may be replaced by
   another. **This sitting's reading, not stated by the paper:** comparing pitch differences makes the measure
   tolerant of transposition.
3. **What this bears on, read against IC-L1 S-50 and S-51 [checked, in the copy at
