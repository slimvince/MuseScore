### §3 — How a second extraction is actually done

*(This side searched for no existing written procedure, so no claim is made that none exists. What
follows is the order this sitting ran, recorded because this sitting is the one that ran it.)*

The rule is `cowork_reading_pass_commission_2026_08_30.md` §4, fourth bullet: *"CENTRAL sources … are
extracted in a SECOND independent pass (a fresh session, or a cleanly separated re-read that does not
consult the first extract) and the two extracts cross-checked; disagreements are resolved at the paper
or recorded as unresolved."*

**Two routes. The remedial commission adds that a fresh session is the WORSE one wherever the newest
handoff entry already summarises the first extract** — which is why this entry deliberately does not
summarise the contents of any paper.

**The order this sitting used. What it produced was a landed extract and a cross-check that found a
defect of fact; whether the order is the best one was not tested:**

1. Stage the paper. Establish the page count **at the tool**, by a deliberately out-of-range request.
2. Read it whole. **Check the page images are present, not that the call succeeded** — cadence 4.
3. Write the extract in the §4 form — claims labeled FACT / THEORY / CONJECTURE, coupling facts
   mandatory (what it assumes upstream, hands downstream, its stated scope), measured results as the
   paper states them — **with the closing section ABSENT**.
4. Read the finished text back against the paper. Sweep it for absolutes, every hit read at its line.
5. **Then** add the closing section, as its own edit, recording what those two acts found.
6. Land it: commit twice by file id, stage back, prove at content in **both** directions and at size.
7. **Only then open the first extract** and cross-check. Resolve every disagreement **at the paper**.
8. Correct whichever extract the paper goes against, at its site, former wording preserved (#12).

**Declare the independence bound.** A booting session has read this entry and will read the progress
table, whose verdict cells state each first extract's conclusion. That is contamination and it must be
