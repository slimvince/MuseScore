### 1.1 The test, read at the object by this sitting

Ruling 2(ii) admits *"the first reading pass's extracts whose paper bears on L2"*. L2's charter was
read at `FRAMEWORK.md` §5, the section headed *"L2 — The tonal reading. The one entangled
decision."* **[checked]**, and its question is, verbatim: *"over this music, what is the tonality at
each moment, where does each harmony give way to the next, which sounding notes belong to the
harmony and which elaborate it, and what chord is read over each span?"* — together with the
publication clause that each span carries *"the **rivals** with their mass — including rivals that
differ in where the boundaries fall and not only in the label."*

**Entry 221 §4 rendered that charter accurately**; this sitting establishes it at the file rather
than carrying the relay.

### 1.2 The ground for each, named rather than counted

All eight were **read whole at their own files by this sitting**, which is what entry 222 §7 item 1
ordered, entry 221's grading of four of the twenty having rested on a sixteen-line window that cut
the evidence section short.

- **`sapp-2005-visual-hierarchical-key-analysis`** — the first limb. Supplies a worked case in which
  the choice of key profile flips a whole-piece key (Krumhansl wrong, Aarden right, on BWV 1007), and
  an independent restatement of the window-size pathology. Decides nothing and measures nothing
  systematically; enters as evidence about the form of key evidence, never as a method.
- **`viaccoz-harasim-moss-rohrmeier-2023-wavescapes`** — the first limb. A worked description of
  tonality without a label, and collection-family strength as a continuous quantity a scoring term
  could read. Discards spelling at the front door, which is less than L0 publishes; no evaluation
  exists or is possible in its own terms.
- **`hamanaka-hirata-tojo-2013-computational-music-theory-gttm`** — **the narrowest ground of the
  eight, and it answers none of the limbs.** No chords, no keys and no harmonic labels leave that
  system, its one harmony-bearing subtheory being unimplemented. It enters for two facts about the
  shape of the problem: the one developed implementation puts its hierarchy DOWNSTREAM of a tonal and
  harmonic analysis it does not itself compute, and executing that theory took forty-six hand-added
  parameters to reach an F-measure near 0.49 automatically on monophonic eight-bar excerpts. **The
  case against was on the surface and is not softened here:** different object, different ground
  truth, monophonic.
- **`humphrey-bello-2015-four-timely-insights-ace`** — the rivals clause. Measured human-human
  disagreement between defensible readings, and a recommendation to target affinities over several
  human readings rather than one label. **Audio and popular music**, which principle #21's own D-474
  block already refuses as a ceiling for this repertoire; it enters with that scope on its face.
- **`eerola-schutz-2025-relative-mode-continuum`** — the first limb. The one measured case in this
  group for a graded mode axis at the evidence level (r ≈ .82–.86 against expert ratings, against
  .474 for a categorical comparator, experts agreeing at r = .90). It names no key and no mode class.
- **`navarrocaceres-etal-irish-mode-detection-2024-2025`** — **the weakest of the eight.** Its one
  transferable fact is a negative result: binary pitch-class presence beat duration weighting for
  mode separation. The setting removes by assumption the part L2 owns — the tonic is given — and is
  melodic, monophonic and one mode per tune; the 2024 half is carried at abstract grade only.
- **`hentschel-moss-mcleod-neuwirth-rohrmeier-2021-unified-chord-model`** — **the clearest of the
  eight**, touching three limbs in the charter's own vocabulary: a key as tonic plus mode plus an
  optional global/local/secondary hierarchy type; a chord as a structure over explicit properties
  with the label derived rather than primitive; and suspensions as per-note functions naming what is
  suspended. It measures nothing, so nothing in it is established evidence under **#19**; it enters
  as a representation.
- **`lazzari-2023-knowledge-based-chord-embeddings-modal-harmony-ontology`** — the rivals clause. It
  returns ten scale readings for a four-chord progression with no ranking, no weight and no
  confidence, under its author's own bound that the Roman-annotation query is a proof of concept with
  no evaluation anywhere in the thesis. It is the third system in this population to publish
  alternatives with nothing separating them, which is evidence about what the clause's words *with
  their mass* carry.
