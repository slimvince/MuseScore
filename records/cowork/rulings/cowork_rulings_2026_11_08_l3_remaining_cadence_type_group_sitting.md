## 1. Ruling 1 — the derivation RATIFIED: Rows 7.96 and 7.110 (ii) recorded as decided against

- **Row 7.96** (the authentic cadence's *"family gate"*, requiring *"the leading tone resolves to the tonic across the
  boundary (the resolution, present as an event — not merely the leading tone being sounded)"*) — **Recorded as decided
  against** by L3-S17 (as ruled; the cadence-type record) with L3-S42 (fit-and-rest Ruling 1: *"L3 reads no fact of the
  axis in this specification"*), **in so far as it makes the leading tone's resolution, as an event in a voice, a
  condition L3 computes.** The definition remains a candidate for a declared vocabulary (cadence-type Ruling 2), written
  from its source when that vocabulary is declared; a declared type that needs the event carries its named reason (the
  method ratified with the cadence-vocabulary point: *"a type that cannot be computed carries its reason"*). Twin: L2
  Row 5.102, RELOCATED to L3 by L2 nothing-from-L3 Ruling 5.
- **Row 7.110 (ii)** (*"an inverted or seventh dominant is admitted but at lower weight"*, in a half cadence) —
  **Recorded as decided against** by cadence-mass Ruling 1 (*"No cadence type carries a fixed, by-rule lowering of its
  confidence"*), **in so far as it lowers a cadence's weight by a fixed rule**, as Rows 7.112 (ii) and 7.114 (ii). The
  tonic vote that weight belongs to is Row 7.117's, QUARANTINED with L3-S38's ratification. Twin: L2 Row 5.118,
  RELOCATED to L3 by L2 nothing-from-L3 Ruling 5.

**Documentation:** the two row-parts filed as above. The re-marking is the application dispatch's act (route record
§2 step 2).
**Algorithm: no change.** The currently decided method, as put: L3 publishes a cadence only at a phrase end it has
decided, reading six features off lower layers in the tonality L2 decided; each declared vocabulary's type is computed
from those features, side by side, none principal (which vocabularies are declared is not yet decided); the
perfect/imperfect split is published in two views; a type that cannot be computed carries its reason; a cadence's mass
comes only from doubt already counted (L2's rival objects and ranked readings, L3's rival phrase ends), with no fixed,
by-rule lowering; L3 reads no voice-leading fact in this specification; L2 reads nothing L3 publishes.

**Not decided by this ruling:** whether L3 should ever be able to compute the leading-tone event (a seventh feature) —
under the user's ruling of 2026-10-07 that would be opened by a reason to suspect improvement and settled by
measurement; whether the voices as notated (L0) count as *"a fact of the axis"* under L3-S42; whether suspensions and
appoggiaturas (feature (4)) do; which vocabularies are declared and their definitions.
