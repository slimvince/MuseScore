## 1. Ruling 1 — the sort's member-test widenings are ACCEPTED AS EXECUTED, and their general form is recorded (Alternative A)

**THE RULING.** The three corrections the sort's member test took while it was being built **stand as
executed**. **No review pass is ordered**, and the narrow own-source-only test is **not** restored.
**The general form is recorded here, and this record is its home; no separate site is written and no
finding number is allocated for it.**
