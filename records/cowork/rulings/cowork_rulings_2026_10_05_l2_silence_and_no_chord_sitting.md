## 1. Ruling 1 — position one: a chord reading is always inferred over sounding music; the framework's question stays open; "no chord over sounding music" is excluded by name

**The ruling.** Over every stretch where notes sound, the chord-and-key layer infers and publishes its
best reading of the harmony with the rival readings and their weights. **Even a reading with a very low
weight is inferred and handed on.** Doubt is shown by the weights alone; there is no "uncertain" flag and
no "no chord" answer over sounding music in the first specification. **The framework's DP-Q stays open
exactly as ratified; no framework text is amended.** L2-S44's sounding half stands as derived.

**The named exclusion.** "No chord" over sounding music is not an admissible reading in the first
specification. This ruling is the named exclusion the derivation's L2-S2 requires of a reading type an
analyst writes, so that L2-S2 is met whether or not a graded collection turns out to write a no-chord
label (unsettled; surface §3). **Its cost, as stated:** such a label would never be matched; the cost is
not counted.

**Where showing it belongs (the user's view, given in the sitting and carried with the ruling as its
stated ground, not as a separate ruling).** Whether a very low-weight reading is shown, shown greyed, or
replaced by "N.C." is the display's decision — the product stance the ratified amendment D-498 says is
still owed — and may include a user minimum-confidence setting. **The caution carried with it:** the
weights are not calibrated, and a consumer "may compare masses within one working span's publication and
may not read them as frequencies of being right" (derivation, L2-S40), so a minimum-confidence setting
that means the same in every piece needs a fitted calibration first.

**The ground, as put (surface §4).** Towards the objective (#4): every sounding stretch gets the reading
the evidence most supports, with everything else the analysis weighed. Towards the principles: #1 and the
framework's risk row R-6 (no open question filled without evidence); #6 and the framework's "no layer
whose job is uncertainty" (doubt has one home, the weights); #12 and D-295 (nothing computed is withheld
from the display); #20 and #21 (no answer created that no annotation read so far contains); consistent
with D-114 (a key is always named). Meta level: the analysis's own specification only.

**The costs, carried.** D-498's owed decision about mostly-doubtful and non-tonal music is not answered
inside this layer and stays owed. Over non-tonal music the layer still names a tonal reading; whether its
weights spread out there is unmeasured. The exclusion's ceiling cost is uncounted.

**Declined: position two** — "no chord" as a rival answer over sounding music: no annotation read so far
writes it, so its weight would be hand-set (#20, #21); used for weak evidence it duplicates the weights
(#6); a "no chord" winner counts as a wrong root at the regression check (D-212); it moves into the
analysis a display choice that may differ per user; the framework amended. **Declined: position three** —
never, written into the framework now: the same output as position one, at the cost of filling an open
question with the most plausible answer (R-6) and amending a ratified document.

**What it does not decide.** Whether any graded collection writes a no-chord label; how an annotation
over a silence is graded; whether the weights spread out over non-tonal music; where and how D-498's
display decision is written. **The silent half of L2-S44 and the twenty rows' placements are the
sitting's second question and are not ruled here.**

## 1b. Ruling 2 — the silent half of L2-S44 confirmed as derived, with the twenty rows' placements

**How it was put.** After Ruling 1 was recorded and reported, the user replied "continue"; the second
item was then put alone, as a confirmation and not a choice (the record answers it: the derivation

sitting that a rest is a fact the inference weighs is what the derived statements already do). **The
user's word, verbatim: "confirmed".**

**The ruling.** L2-S44's silent half stands as derived. A rest in any voice — one voice or all — is
evidence the chord-and-key layer weighs; it never forces and never forbids a harmony change (L2-S13). The
note-cutting layer publishes which voice went silent and whether every voice is silent (the L0/L1
derivation's S-39 REST-BEGINS and ALL-SILENT flags, and S-31's silent slice not merged into a neighbour;
surface §2). A complete silence carries two rival readings with their weights: the harmony continues
through it (a rest inside a phrase — the chord before carries on, or the next has already begun), or a
silent stretch with no harmony, labelled as silent (a general pause).
