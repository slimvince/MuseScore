## 1. Ruling 1 — L3-S19 RATIFIED as derived

**L3-S19, verbatim from the derivation:** *"The principal cadence is read off L2's principal reading at L3's principal
phrase end. Rival cadences are read off L2's rivals at that phrase end, and off L3's rival phrase ends. Because a cadence
reads several spans together, its mass is computed from L2's rival objects and ranked whole readings, never by
multiplying per-span marginals. The mass L2 does not publish is carried as unaccounted."*

- **Algorithmic (decided against the older documents):** a cadence's mass comes only from the doubt already carried —
  L2's rival objects and ranked whole readings, and L3's rival phrase ends — never from a product of per-span
  marginals; mass L2 does not publish is carried as unaccounted. **No cadence type carries a fixed, by-rule lowering of
  its confidence, and the top note does not nudge the mass.**
- **Documentation:** L3-S19 filed as the L3 statement. Recorded as decided against **in so far as they lower confidence
  by a fixed rule**: Rows 7.112 (ii), 7.114 (ii), 7.115 (ii), 7.249, and Row 2.9's confidence half (*"The top voice may
  nudge the confidence in a chordal texture"*; carried here by Ruling 1 of the 2026-11-03 cadence-type record). Row
  7.249's forecast — that the half cadence's confidence will tend to come out lower — is not decided against; only its
  by-rule part is. The re-marking of each row is the application dispatch's act.

**Grounds as put** (the D-599 derivation; this side's reading where marked):
1. The doubt the outgoing rule guarded is already counted: the phrase-ends Ruling 1 (2026-11-02) publishes rival phrase
   ends with their mass, and L3-S19 reads rival cadences off them; a plagal close that may be a post-cadential tonic
   prolongation enters as a rival reading. A fixed lowering would count it twice (#6).
2. A hand-set amount has no source or measurement behind it (#1, #17); fitted values are fit once against ground truth,
   never tuned by hand (D-096).
3. The weight-meaning Ruling 1 (2026-10-05/06) declares the mass the model's own distribution, made a calibrated
   probability only through a fitted reliability map; a hand lowering would make it neither (#10).
4. Nothing is lost (#12): if measurement shows a type less reliable than its mass, the fitted reliability map learns
   it; under Ruling 6 of `cowork_rulings_2026_10_07_l2_chord_tone_membership_sitting.md` a reason to suspect improvement
   opens a test, not a hand rule.
5. Precedent: the weight-meaning Ruling 4 placed the old key layer's declared confidence HISTORICAL, overtaken by the
   full spread of weight; doubt is shown by the weights alone (the silence family's Ruling 1, as that record cites it).
6. Row 7.115 (ii) additionally: under Ruling 3 item c of the 2026-11-03 cadence-type record the evaded cadence is
   published as *type not computable* with its reason until OQ-L3-5; no evaded type exists to carry a confidence.
7. Row 2.9's confidence half: under Ruling 1 of the cadence-type record the top pitch decides view (ii)'s type; a
