## 1. Ruling 1 — a recognizer is established by reconciliation against an INDEPENDENTLY-KNOWN population, ruled as a class (Alternative A)

**THE TEST, AND IT IS MECHANICAL RATHER THAN A MATTER OF TASTE: DOES SOMETHING OTHER THAN THIS
RECOGNIZER ENUMERATE THE POPULATION IT CLAIMS TO DESCRIBE?**

**THE RULING.** A recognizer over a population is **established** only by both-ways reconciliation
against an independently-known population. **Where no such population exists, the recognizer's output
IS the population, no seed set can establish it, and its output stands as a LOWER BOUND with its reach
declared UNMEASURED — never as a census.**

**THE GROUND, MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED.** **F84** establishes that a recognizer written from its
known instances reproduces its known instances: the twelfth batch's own derivation would have named
three members instead of seven and read as complete, and every miss was found only by seeking
candidates the recognizer did **not** return. **A seed set therefore proves that a recognizer is not
broken and says nothing about what it covers**, which is precisely the *merely unfalsified* that
**#19** exists against. What this ruling adds is the test that says **which** recognizers F84 reaches.

**THE SORT IS ORDERED BY D-254 AND IS NOT PUT AS A CHOICE.** A read-only derivation over the
recognizers the record leans on — placing each by whether an external enumeration of its population
exists — runs regardless of this ruling, under *investigate by default; never ask the user whether to
investigate*. **What is ruled is what its result authorizes: NOTHING.** It is published as data at its
own artifact and is the standing input to a later ruled act, never a work list.

**★ AND THE CLAUSE BINDS FORWARD, NOT BACKWARD — WHICH IS STATED SO IT IS NOT READ AS A SWEEP.** The
clause binds a recognizer written or touched from here. **Existing recognizers' own artifacts are NOT
retro-fitted with the statement**: the sort's artifact carries the per-recognizer verdict, so the
information has ONE home (**#6**), and retro-fitting a population under the standing mechanism freeze
would be tool work that blocks nothing.

**★ WHAT THE WRITING SIDE ALREADY ESTABLISHED, RECORDED AS THE ESTABLISHMENT AND NOT AS THE VALUE THE
DERIVATION MUST REACH.** Read at its own artifact this session, `gen_nongating_apparatus_rows.py`
reconciles **both ways against the parsed INDEX** — every gating identity an open row of it, every
open row placed as gating or non-gating with none in both and none in neither, every gating row
carrying a ground, and **a HALT on any row the derivation cannot place**. The INDEX is enumerated by
something other than that recognizer. **So the derived gating answer a session reads at boot is on the
ESTABLISHED side and F84 does not reach it** — which is the single most load-bearing consequence of
this ruling, because that answer replaced the whole-index read for every session on both sides. **The
sort DERIVES this rather than inheriting it.**

**AND ONE MEMBER IS MIXED, WHICH IS WHY THE TEST IS WORTH STATING RATHER THAN APPLIED BY FEEL.**
`gen_evidence_pin_membership.py` takes its population from an external file-system enumeration — every
root-level `cowork_rulings_*.md` — so its **membership** is checkable; but **which route places a
member** is decided by recognizers over the records' own text, and nothing outside those recognizers
enumerates which records name a generated document. **It is established on its population and
unestablished on its classification, and the sort says so per member rather than per tool.**

**THE COST THE USER ACCEPTED, RECORDED BECAUSE AN ACCEPTED COST IS NOT A DISCHARGED ONE.** This ruling
**CLASSIFIES** exposure and does not **MEASURE** it. A recognizer sorted into the
no-external-population class is known to be a lower bound and is **not** known by how much. That
residual is real, and the UNMEASURED declaration is what keeps it visible rather than closing it.

**THE TWO ALTERNATIVES DECLINED.** A re-establishment pass measuring each recognizer's miss rate was
declined: for a recognizer with no external population there is nothing to generate candidates FROM
except judgment, which is the F42 result already on the record — recognizers over prose are not the
route, and refuting them cost a whole pass — and it is measurement work across a broad population that
blocks nothing. Treating F84 as specific to the epoch enumeration was declined because F84's own
general form says otherwise, and leaving a known class unsorted is **#19**'s silent-failure direction:
the next session reads a derived answer without knowing whether it is a census or a floor.

**THE FORWARD CLAUSE: A RECOGNIZER OVER A POPULATION STATES, AT ITS OWN ARTIFACT, WHETHER AN
INDEPENDENTLY-KNOWN POPULATION EXISTS TO RECONCILE AGAINST — AND WHERE NONE DOES, IT PUBLISHES ITS
OUTPUT AS A LOWER BOUND WITH ITS REACH DECLARED UNMEASURED.** Its home is
`cowork_audit_protocol.md`'s dispatch-protocol section, beside the clauses already standing there,
with **F84** recorded as its ground and **F87** as the test it rests on.
