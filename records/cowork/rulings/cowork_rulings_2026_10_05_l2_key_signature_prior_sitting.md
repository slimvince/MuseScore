## 1. Ruling 1 — the file's declared major/minor tag is not read by L2 at all

**The ruling.** The chord-and-key layer does not consume the record file's declared major/minor tag (a
MusicXML `<mode>` element, printed nowhere on the page) in any way — not as a prior, not as a hint, not
as a rung of the evidence ranking. L2-S17's last sentence stands as derived: "A tonality or mode tag the
record file declares is not read at all." The tag stays where Ruling 57 put it: carried at L0, labelled
as read, for a reader outside the analysis. **This resolves the flag Ruling 57 left to L2's surface.**

**What it sets aside in the existing design.** The declared-mode half of register entries D-528 (the tag
"a second conditioning input with its own fitted strength"), D-450 ("the key-signature and
declared-mode prior"), D-057 (the tag as the weakest rung of the evidence ranking) and D-056 (the tag
passed on as "a weak hint (`declaredMode`)"). The first three rungs of the ranking — the sounding notes,
the surrounding music, the key signature — stand. **The register status of those entries is not changed

is the route; no decisions-register identity is allocated.

**The ground, as put (surface §3.1).** Ruling 57 decided it for every layer and excluded the tag-as-prior
alternative at the threshold; `FRAMEWORK.md` §8.6 ("No user-written analytical content — chord symbols,
Roman numerals, function, cadence or tonality annotations — enters the analysis in any storage form")

the notes cannot test. Towards the principles: #1; one labelled home, nothing lost (#6, #12). Meta level:

## 1b. Ruling 2 — the signature's lean acts at the start and at each notated signature change, and nowhere else

**How it was put.** After Ruling 1 was recorded and reported, the user replied "continue"; the second
confirmation (surface §3.2) was then put alone, no widget, with the word asked for. **The user's word,
verbatim: "confirmed".**

**The ruling.** The written key signature is a weak lean on the tonality at the start and at each
notated signature change, and nowhere else — `FRAMEWORK.md` C-2's form, and D-450's. **L2-S17's first
sentence is to be amended to that form**, and the lean acting on every span (D-450's "persistent pull
toward the signature at every step") is **excluded by name**. L2-S17's "It never constrains them"
stands.

**The ground, as put (surface §3.2).** C-2 is a ratified framework clause; D-450, user-ratified
2026-07-19, settled the question by hand-traced test: the every-stretch form taxes every key away from
the signature without limit over a modulation, bites hardest in passages without accidentals, and
D-450 records that no published system carries a signature lean at all. The derivation gives no ground
for the every-span form beyond its wording, and names the modulating passage as its own weak point.
Towards the objective: the notes govern once the piece is under way. Towards the principles: #1, a
ratified clause and a ratified decision kept; the alternative would amend the framework. Meta level: the
analysis's own specification only.

**Left for the application, not ruled:** "at the start" of what — the piece, or the stretch the user
selected and the analysis loaded (the ratified C-2 says only "at the start"; the framework's first-stage
draft adds "of the piece"); whether a notated signature change falling between two note change points
