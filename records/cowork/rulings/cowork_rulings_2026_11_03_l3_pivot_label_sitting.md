## 2. Ruling 1 — OQ-L3-8 ★: B CONFIRMED, L3 publishes the pivot label as a view

**As ruled:** at each change of tonality in L2's principal reading, L3 publishes the decided chord of the span on each
side of the change, re-expressed as a scale degree in the other tonality, each carrying a flag stating whether that chord
is diatonic in both tonalities — the flag a fact, not a test of pivot-ness (exemplar 2's Neapolitan pivot at bar 41,
B♭ major over D, is chromatic in A minor and would carry "no"). No chord is named "the pivot". Each rival whole reading
L2 publishes gives its own set (this side's proposal, put with B and confirmed with it).

**Declined:** A — no pivot label anywhere in the analysis (L3-S7 as written); C — a label computed only by a display.

**Grounds as put:** A's and C's grounds are procedural (the charter's list; the brief's reservation of L3's *what*; for
C, no charter amendment), not harms. The harms looked for were met by B's definition: a reading of "two keys at once"
(met: a re-expression of L2's decided chord, L2's one tonality per span untouched); choosing the pivot chord (met: none
named); build cost (small). For B over C: the charter places the chord symbol, itself a view, at L3; D-100 — its
application to a view as a "derived fact", and D-295's to a re-expression as an "inferred object", are this side's
reading. Towards the objective: a pivot-aware output can be compared directly with annotations that write pivots
(exemplar 2); how such an annotation is graded was left unsettled by the L2 pivot ruling, later rulings not checked; L2's
inference is not changed. Meta level: the analysis itself.

**Algorithmic change, decided:** L3 gains an output, the pivot label as above.

**Documentation:** L3-S7 to be rewritten accordingly; OQ-L3-8 closed; the charter's L3 *Publishes* list to gain the
pivot label (an amendment of `FRAMEWORK.md` §5, L3, by the application act — not edited here); L3-S7's falsifier
rewritten so the re-expression is allowed while a second tonality decided for a span is barred — the sketch put: *"falsified
if any L3 output changes or adds to the tonality L2 decided for a span"*. The re-writing is the application dispatch's
act.

**The currently decided method before this ruling, as stated with the question:** L3 publishes no pivot label; L2
publishes one tonality per span and a pivot's other key as a rival with its mass; key areas close at every change of
tonality.
