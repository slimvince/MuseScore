## 3. Ruling 3 — the take rule is replaced by an endpoint-inclusive spread, and strata 5, 7 and 8 are redrawn (Alternative C)

**Put at `cowork_take_rule_surface_2026_08_27.md`, delivered with no question in its turn.** The
choice question was announced for a later turn and **he ruled before it was asked**, naming the
alternative by its letter. His word, verbatim: *"C"*.

**This is the fourth consecutive occasion he has answered ahead of the one-per-turn form**, and it is
recorded the same way as the previous three rather than smoothed over: his rule of 2026-08-21
constrains how this side **puts** questions, not how he answers. **The single-limb test:** "C" names
one of four lettered alternatives, which is also the surface's recommendation; there is no fork and
nothing required disambiguation.

### 3.1 What is ruled

**The systematic take `1, 1+k, …, 1+24k` is replaced.** For a stratum with `N > T`, the drawn
positions are, for `i = 0 … T−1`:

> `p_i = 1 + ( i×(N−1) + 12 ) // 24`   *(integer division; `12` is `(T−1)/2`, `24` is `T−1`)*

**`T` is unchanged at 25, and remains DECLARED, NOT DERIVED.** No measurement in this project
supports it and no successor may cite it as measured. The threshold rule is unchanged: `N ≤ 25` is a
census.

**Written in integer arithmetic deliberately**, and that is part of the ruling rather than a
presentational choice: a rule stated with the word *round* has an implementation-dependent answer at
exactly one half, and a selection rule with an implementation-dependent answer is not deterministic
— which is the property Ruling 1 of 2026-08-27 requires of it.

### 3.2 The defect this cures, and the one it does not create

**The defect.** The old rule's last reachable position is `1 + 24k`, so everything after it in the
ordering is impossible to draw. Because the ordering is path-then-line, **that region is always the
end of the last-sorted files — an exclusion correlated with content, in a fixed place.** At `N = 33`
it also degenerates: `k = 1`, and the take becomes the first twenty-five items contiguously.

**The cure.** `p_0 = 1` and `p_{24} = N` for every `N`, so **no region at either end is unreachable,
at any count.**

**The cost, ruled with it and not hidden: item 1 and item `N` are always drawn.** Two of the
twenty-five slots are spent deterministically on the first and last item of the ordering. Half of
that cost was already being paid — the old rule also drew position 1 every time. **A fixed inclusion
everyone can see is a smaller defect than a fixed exclusion nobody can size**, and that is the whole
of the ground.

### 3.3 Two findings recorded because they correct the record rather than extend it

**(i) The cure this side proposed in the seventieth handoff entry does not cure the defect.** That
entry proposed `floor(i·N/T) + 1`. Its last reachable position is `floor(24N/25) + 1`, so at
`N = 730` it reaches position 701 and **twenty-nine items at the end of the ordering remain
impossible to draw**. It removes four items from the hole and leaves the rest. **This side derived
that and is correcting its own predecessor's proposal**; the alternative was put to the user at full
strength and rejected on that ground.

**(ii) One correction to Claude Code's arithmetic.** Its report gives stratum 8's unreachable region
as positions 51–59. By the rule's own text `k = floor(59/25) = 2`, so the last drawn position is
`1 + 48 = 49` and **the region is 50–59 — ten items, not nine.** Strata 5 and 7 are correct as
reported. Nothing turns on it; it is one more item than the record said and it was derived here
rather than repeated.

### 3.4 The counts-visible hazard, declared as part of the ruling

**This rule was changed with the counts visible — 33, 59 and 730 — and that cannot be undone.**
Ruling 1 of 2026-08-27 protects the sample by fixing the selection before any count is known; for
this change that property is **spent**.

**What partly answers it:** the threshold and the take both stay at 25, so the change cannot be
shaping *how much* of any stratum is drawn; and the replacement is a general formula chosen on a
property — that it reaches both ends of every ordering — statable and checkable without reference to
any count.

**What does not answer it:** the counts were used to size the defect and to compare the four
candidates. **A successor must read this change as made sighted, because it was.**
