## 1. Ruling 1 — the empirical findings ledger is a SINGLE HAND-WRITTEN RECORD whose entries CITE rather than transcribe; no generator is built (Alternative C)

**The user's word: "C".**

**Ruled.** The ledger is one Markdown record. Each entry names its fact, its gate verdict, and **where
its five ruled fields already stand** — it does not restate them. No data file, no generator, no new
guard is built for it now.

**The ground, and it is the narrow one:** every admitted fact already exists, written out in the ruled
five-field shape, in `cowork_empirical_findings_candidates.md`, and this project has already ruled that
retyping such a fact is the transcription D-431 forbids and *"would let the two copies drift"*. The
ledger's job is to hand deriving sessions established facts; C delivers that input without making a
second copy and without building a second register.

**The alternatives declined, by their ratings.** **A** — generated on the decisions register's pattern
with a `--check` tool: the strongest enforcement, but it imports that family's known pathologies — one
row moving five artifacts, per-citation anchor re-aiming, and two checks presently unsatisfiable with
the register's own rule — to govern thirty-one entries; apparatus generating apparatus, against the
just-in-time rule and the standing sharpening on self-generated work. **B** — hand-written and
transcribed: makes the second copy the record already forbids. **D** — promote the candidates file
itself: it carries the refusals and both harvests under a banner saying nothing in it is admitted;
promoting it destroys the audit trail of what the gate refused, which is what makes a gate mean
anything.

**Not foreclosed.** A remains available if the ledger outgrows review or acquires a second writer.
Nothing in C prevents generating it later from what C writes.
