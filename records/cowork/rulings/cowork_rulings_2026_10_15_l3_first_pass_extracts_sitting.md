## 2. Ruling 1 — L3's first-pass extract member is ELEVEN of twenty

**What was decided.** These eleven first-pass extracts are members of L3's pack under Ruling 3 (ii) of the
boot-list record, each with its section headed *"Bearing…"* cut and the per-file cuts of that ruling's limb A:

*Derived IN (eight):*
1. `feisthauer-2021-lille-thesis-modulations-cadences-sonata-form` — cadence classification with the PAC/HC
   asymmetry; medial-caesura location (a section boundary), correct for half the corpus; the dual-tonality
   principle (modulation and tonicization readings at one moment).
2. `hamanaka-hirata-tojo-2013-computational-music-theory-gttm` — measured automatic grouping (F 0.46 default,
   0.77 hand-tuned, 0.48 fully automatic); the grouping rule with the most influence on the result consumes a
   tonal-pitch-space analysis supplied by hand. Monophonic — *this side's reading:* nearer the melodic phrase than
   the whole-texture phrase.
3. `lazzari-2023-knowledge-based-chord-embeddings-modal-harmony-ontology` — sectional form segmentation from the
   chord stream alone (pairwise F1 about 0.60, popular music); chord quality inferred from intervals.
4. `napoleslopez-feisthauer-leve-fujinaga-2020-local-keys-modulations-tonicizations` — two defensible key columns
   (modulation, tonicization); traditions differ widely in how much they tonicize; the three local-key models it
   evaluated track the tonicization column better. *Bearing on key-area grouping: this side's reading.*
5. `mcleod-rohrmeier-2021-modular-harmonic-analysis` — the chord label decided as one whole symbol because separate
   per-field outputs combine inconsistently; inversion accuracy by position.
6. `bachi-2026-boundary-aware-symbolic-chord-recognition` — root, quality and bass predicted separately; the gain
   of its boundary and decoding machinery appears in whole-chord consistency.
7. `hentschel-moss-mcleod-neuwirth-rohrmeier-2021-unified-chord-model` — the label derived from explicit
   properties; spelled → enharmonic conversion one-way; suspensions per note.
8. `mcleod-rohrmeier-2024-chord-tone-alterations-suspensions` — suspensions found after the chord decision, the
   position L3 occupies; a method DP-D places elsewhere, so the L3 specification must carry why the figured bass
   reads L2's published elaboration relations instead (*the candidacy criterion's consequence (i), applied by this
   side by analogy*).

*Borderline, admitted on the ruling (three):*
9. `sapp-2005-visual-hierarchical-key-analysis` — key at every window size; a two-key form a single window hides.
10. `dehaas-magalhaes-wiering-veltkamp-2013-harmtrace-functional-analysis` — consumes a decided key and decided
    chords; multi-key pieces pre-segmented by an outside step; phrase structure left to post-processing. DP-O stays
    open at the framework; admitting the extract lets L3 adopt no hierarchy.
11. `deberardinis-etal-2023-choco-chord-corpus` — converting Roman numerals to absolute labels loses the functional
    information. Which conversion its "one of several possible conversions" refers to is not established.

**OUT (nine), stated so silence claims nothing:** `mueller-konz-bogler-arifimueller-2011-saarland-music-data`;
`hu-arthur-2021-statistical-model-melody-reduction`; `eerola-schutz-2025-relative-mode-continuum`;
