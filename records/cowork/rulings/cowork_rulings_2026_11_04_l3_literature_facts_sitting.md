## 1. Ruling 1 — the four row-parts RATIFIED as derivations, Row 36.106 (i) in the narrower wording

- **Rows 20.31 (i), 56.2 (i) and 56.2 (ii) — ADOPTED.** No validated deterministic rule set places phrase boundaries in
  polyphonic music; almost all published work on locating phrase endings addresses a single melodic line; for several
  simultaneous voices nothing comparable has been established. Ground: **D-607** (`DECISIONS.md` line 514, LIVE,
  2026-08-04 · user): *"Polyphonic phrase-boundary detection has NO validated deterministic rule set in the literature —
  the extension is engineering on monophonic cues, validated against our own corpus, and is not settled science"*; the
  three row-parts lie in its home (the reading file's member-20 marks, line 27108, and position-56 marks, lines
  71077–71078).
- **Row 36.106 (i) — ADOPTED in the narrower wording.** *"That L3's specification record, as a fact of the published
  literature, that the phrase-boundary cues are validated on single melodic lines only."* The reading file's proposal
  clause *"polyphony being the weakest-supported area"* is not adopted: it ranks the project's own risks, is the old
  design document's judgment, is not measured, and D-607 does not carry it. The row lies in no home at member 36 (the
  member's marks, lines 43046–43055); that D-607 covers its content is this side's reading of the two texts. The row's
  own text is carried as written.

**Documentation:** the 4 row-parts recorded ADOPTED (Row 36.106 (i) in the narrower wording); one statement to be added
to the amended L3 specification, written with D-607's standing visible: *"As facts of the published literature,
recorded with D-607's standing: almost all published work on locating phrase endings addresses a single melodic line,
and the cues it uses are validated on single lines; for several simultaneous voices no validated deterministic rule set
has been established, and L3's phrase model rests on fitted terms instead."* The re-marking and the writing are the

until that fit exists every L3 decision is provisional (L3-S33) (L3-S14 and L3-S33 as the reading file quotes them).
