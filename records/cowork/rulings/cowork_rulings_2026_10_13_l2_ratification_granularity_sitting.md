## 1. Ruling — per face

**The L2 successor is ratified one face at a time**, as L0/L1 was under Ruling 65 of
`records/cowork/rulings/cowork_rulings_2026_08_31_decision_surface_sitting.md`. A face is one of the
deriving brief's eight aspects of the layer's job, and the file is organised around them:

- (a) the candidate readings and the rule that admits them, L2-S1 to S10
- (b) where one harmony gives way to the next, S11 to S15
- (c) the tonality at each moment, S16 to S22
- (d) which sounding notes belong to the harmony and which decorate it, S23 to S26
- (e) the chord read over each span, S27 to S30
- (f) how a candidate reading is scored and how the parts of that score combine, S31 to S39
- (g) the competing readings, how much weight each carries, and the rule against discarding them early, S40 to S43
- (h) where the layer's job begins and ends, S44 to S49

There are nine surfaces, taken in the file's order: the eight faces, then one for §0 (terms) together with
§4 (open questions). §1, the finding on the brief's faces, rides on the ninth; that placement was this
side's suggestion. Each face surface names:

- the statements a ruling already fixed, ratified by confirming the written text against the ruling's
  words, with any mismatch put as the decision;
- the statements no ruling names, put with their derived defense;
- that face's points the application dispatch notes for the user, each tested under D-599 first, so that
  what the record settles is recorded as a derivation and not asked.

Each surface is delivered in its own turn, checked in the next, and the question is put after that, one
decision per turn. Each question says whether it ONLY files documentation or ALSO changes the algorithm,
and quotes the passages it is about.

**The clause put with it, and ruled with it.** If a face surface proves too large to be self-contained
once its noted points have been read, it is split at that face without a new ruling on the granularity.
The question asked whether the user ruled *"for option 3 with that clause, or for one of the others"*.
**That his word takes option 3 together with the clause is this side's reading of it.** The word did not
repeat the clause, so it is marked here, and the user's correction governs if he gives one.
