## 2. Ruling 1 — Alternative A: L3-S8 as derived

**L3 decides where phrases end as a weighed choice. Every change point and both edges of the working span are
candidate phrase ends; the principal partition is published with its rivals and their mass; no item of evidence forces
or forbids a phrase end; an absent flag is never read as an absent phrase end.** The re-homed primitive's strength
profile and the notated markers stay, as evidence entering as fitted terms (L3-S9 (i)), not as rules.

**What this supersedes, stated exactly:**
- **D-478**, at its L3 home, as a rule deciding a phrase end — the rule Ruling 48 kept on purpose. The profile's peak
  survives only as evidence.
- **D-481**, at its L3 home: no marker is a phrase end unconditionally.
- **D-477** survives only in the narrower reading, as a rule on the cue evidence computed from the written page, and not
  on the L3 decision, which uses cadential evidence (L3-S9 (ii)) [this side's reading, put to the user on the surface].

**Declined:** B — keep the re-homed primitive as L3's method (peaks plus unconditional markers, page only); C — A, but
notated markers (fermata, final bar line) force a phrase end.

**Grounds as put** (each named at its principle): towards the ultimate objective (#4, the prime objective by the
2026-10-07 ruling, Ruling 6 of `cowork_rulings_2026_10_07_l2_chord_tone_membership_sitting.md`) — a wrong phrase end can
be outweighed rather than fixed by a rule; phrase ends with weak or no notated sign become findable (the derivation's
elided cadences, exemplar 1 bar 42 and exemplar 3 bar 12 beat 3 [relayed, not opened]); rivals carry the uncertainty
upward (DP-K). Towards the principles — A follows the charter's "decided here" and "decisive further evidence is
cadential" most closely, and IC-L1's "absent is not absent"; under the 2026-10-07 ruling a design rule holds only while
there is no reason to suspect that breaking it would improve the inference, and here there is such reason (a
peak-picked boundary cannot use the evidence the charter calls decisive; the fermata *"delineates the phrase and not
necessarily the exact arrival"*, de Clercq, relayed by the derivation). Against A: cost — a fitted model needs annotated
phrase boundaries (L3-S14, a later family), and D-480 asks the primitive be kept proportionate. Meta level of every
alternative: the analysis itself.

## 2a. Ruling 2 — OQ-L3-3 ★: yes, the cadential evidence may place a phrase end where the page marks nothing

Put in its own turn after Ruling 1, alone, plain text, no widget. **The user's word: "yes".**

**A phrase end may fall wherever the evidence as a whole puts it, including at a cadence with no notated cue** (the
derivation's cases: exemplar 1 bar 42 and exemplar 3 bar 12 beat 3, relayed). **The charter's sentence** *"the span's
boundaries come from the notated cues, and the cadence is aligned to them"* (`FRAMEWORK.md` §5, L3 block) **is to be
reworded** so that it states that the notated cues lead among the evidence and the cadence is aligned to the result,
not that boundaries may fall only at a notated cue. The circularity the charter names is avoided because the cadential
evidence at a candidate position reads only L2's decided spans and never a phrase end (L3-S9 (ii)). This follows from
Ruling 1.

**Declined:** no — phrase ends only where the page carries a cue; the charter sentence kept as written; Ruling 1's
candidate set narrowed to cued positions; elided cadences without a cue missed by construction.

**Documentation and algorithm.** Algorithmic: the candidate set includes positions the page does not mark (as Ruling 1
already decides). Documentation: the charter sentence's rewording — **the rewording is the application dispatch's act,
not done here**; its exact text is not ruled by this record and comes to the user with that act.
