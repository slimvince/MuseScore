## 1. Ruling 1 — the selection rule is authored by the writing side; CC enumerates, applies it mechanically, and commits the sealed sample (Alternative C)

**Ruled.** The placement sample is produced by one Claude Code dispatch that (a) enumerates the
eight strata, (b) applies a **deterministic selection rule written into the dispatch by the writing
side before any count is known**, (c) writes the drawn set to a file, and (d) commits it.

**What this ruling is protecting, stated so it cannot be softened later.** Ruling 1 of 2026-08-26
made Claude Code the side that **runs** the placement test. If that same side also chose which
statements entered the sample, **the side being tested would be picking its own examination
questions** — a defect of the same class the sealing property exists against, arriving through a
different door. The cure ruled here is not supervision; it is that **CC is left no choice to
make.** The discretion is exercised once, in writing, by a side that neither authors the frame nor
runs the test, at a moment when the counts it would be tempted to shape are not yet visible.

**The sealing becomes a commit.** The sealed sample is a committed file at a named tip, dated
before the frame's author starts. This is the strongest form of the sealing property available in
this project: it replaces a declaration in prose with an object whose date cannot be re-argued.

**Recorded with the ruling, and it binds this session.** Having authored the selection rule, **this
session is barred from authoring the frame.** Ruling 1 of 2026-08-26 requires a fresh Cowork
session in any case; this adds an independent ground, so a successor does not read the bar as
resting on freshness alone.

**Also recorded, because a mechanical rule cannot notice it:** the rule cannot tell that a
stratum's enumeration came back malformed or that a stratum has no determinable membership. The
dispatch carries STOP conditions for those cases (Ruling 3's §3 below), and CC reports rather than
decides.

## 2. Ruling 2 — census the small strata, sample the large ones, at a threshold and take-rate declared before the counts are known (Alternative B)

**Ruled.** Every stratum whose enumeration returns at or below a declared threshold goes into the
sample **whole**. Every stratum above the threshold contributes a declared share, taken by a
declared ordering.

**Its ground, restated.** Ruling 3 of 2026-08-26 requires a size per stratum and, in the same
breath, refuses the only measurement that could have supplied a number — the pilot's sizing record,
labelled NOT A BUDGET on three named defects. So a basis had to be ruled rather than inherited. A
stratum taken **whole** yields a finding that needs no uncertainty range at all, which is the
property Ruling 3 stratified to obtain: *the frame cannot hold dormancy declarations* is a
different finding from *the frame cannot hold deferred register entries*, and neither is legible in
a pooled number.

**★ THE NUMBERS ARE DECLARED, NOT DERIVED, AND THE RECORD SAYS SO AT THE POINT THEY APPEAR.** No
measurement in this project supports the threshold or the take. They are set by the writing side in
the dispatch, before any stratum is counted, and any successor citing them must cite them as
declared. **Treating them as measured would be the exact error Ruling 3 declined to make from the
sizing record.**

### 2.1 A correction of this side's own reasoning, placed beside the recommendation and not over it

The surface's recommendation closed: *"If the enumeration returns a stratum so large that the
declared take is not payable, that is a finding to put to you, not a licence for the drawing side
to quietly shrink it."*

**That case cannot arise under the rule as written into the dispatch, because the take is capped
per stratum.** A stratum of ten thousand items and a stratum of forty both contribute at most the
declared take, so the cost of the placement test is bounded by the number of strata regardless of
what the population turns out to hold. **The residual risk is the opposite one:** a very large
stratum sampled at the cap carries a wide uncertainty range, and that range — already required by
Ruling 3 of 2026-08-26 — is what must be reported rather than glossed.

The ruling is unaffected; the sentence anticipated a hazard that the instrument removes. It is
corrected here beside the text, per #12, and counted as this session's error at §5.

## 3. Ruling 3 — the overlap between the sample and what the frame's author must read is DECLARED and reported per stratum; the author's read list is written into the frame (Alternative A)

**The subject, restated so the ruling is readable on its own.** Ruling 3 of 2026-08-26 seals the
sample so the frame cannot be shaped around material its author had just been reading. **For two of
the eight strata the seal does not achieve that**, and cannot: the frame's author is a fresh Cowork
session that must read the governing records to know what it is permitted to do, and those records
are *ruling records* and *decision surfaces* — two strata the sample is drawn from.

**Ruled.**

1. **The frame's author records, in the frame, the list of documents it read.** The overlap is then
   measurable — that read list against the sealed sample's sources — rather than assumed in either
   direction.
2. **The placement report gives the unplaceable proportion per stratum and never as one pooled
   number**, and the two overlapping strata carry the overlap on their face.
3. **A placeable result from those two strata is weaker evidence than the same result from a
   non-overlapping stratum, and the report says so.** An *unplaceable* result from them is
   unaffected — a statement its own author had read and the frame still cannot hold is a finding of
   full strength.

**What was declined and why, recorded so it is not re-proposed as new.** Blinding the frame's
author to those records — drawing the two strata only from documents a closed read list withholds —
was put and not pressed. A deriving session that cannot read a ruling it turns out to be bound by
either breaches the list or derives against a constraint it never saw; and restating withheld
rulings into a pack instead is the carrier that has already turned one rule of this project into
three materially different wordings. **It is declined on that ground, not rejected on its merits,
and it may be re-put if the declared overlap ever proves to be doing real damage.**

**One consequence that follows and is stated so it is not missed:** the sealed sample file is
itself a document the frame's author **must not read**. It is on the author's do-not-read list
alongside the code, and the read list required by limb 1 is what makes that checkable.
