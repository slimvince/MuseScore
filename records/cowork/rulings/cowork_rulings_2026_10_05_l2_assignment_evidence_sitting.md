## 1. Ruling 1 — a note's status is weighed, not fixed by a rule on its motion (question 1)

**The user's word, verbatim: "one".**

**The ruling.** For L2's specification, whether a sounding note is a chord tone or an elaboration is
**weighed inside L2's one decision**: how the note is approached and left, its metric position and its
duration are terms of the candidate score, and **no fixed tier makes a note a chord tone or an
elaboration**. Once the reading has made a note an elaboration, its relation's name follows that
relation's definition (Ruling 1 of `cowork_rulings_2026_10_05_l2_elaboration_relations_sitting.md`); a
foreign note fitting none is the unclassified elaboration. **L2-S25 stands as derived.** **D-333's
second half** — the weighting set per idiom, never a structural branch — **is carried**, L2-S25's weights
being measured; **its first half — the three-tier structure fixed — does not carry into L2's
specification.** D-333's register status is settled at the ratification act, not here (the shape Ruling 2
of the second family used for D-385).

**The ground, as put and as ruled (surface §5).** Towards the objective (#4): the surface features a fixed
rule would read beat "every note is a chord tone" by only about four to five points out of sample (Hu &
Arthur 2021, relayed by the derivation, not read by this sitting), so they are weighed rather than allowed
to decide; the charter excludes a first-running detector because its mistakes "are unrecoverable
downstream" and says a reading the search cannot reach "cannot be found however good the scoring is" —
that a fixed rule on a note's motion is of that kind is this sitting's reading. Towards the principles:
#12 (no assignment excluded before scoring); #6 (D-333 and D-527 said different things; this keeps D-527,
the later, whose covariates include stepwise approach and departure and metric weakness — its verbatim read
at its group file); DP-D's ground, "the distinction is functional and voice-leading", met by reading the
voice leading as evidence. Meta level: the analysis's own specification.

**The costs, carried, as the surface stated them.** The weights must be fitted, and no annotated corpus
with per-note chord-tone labels is known to the derivation (OQ-L2-13), so until one is found or the user
rules otherwise they are fitted and graded only through the chord; a fixed rule needed no fit. D-333's
fixed structure is set aside.

**Declined: position two** — the three-tier rule fixed, as part of the admission rule: an added sixth
approached and left by step between chord tones could never be read as a chord member, and a foreign note
leapt to and from would be forced into the chord (L2-S8's premise; the path the second family's Ruling 1
closed); it leaves D-527's covariate design unused for these features (#6). **Declined: position three** —
only tier 2 fixed: the same forced chord tone for a note leapt to and from, contradicting the second
family's unclassified elaboration (#12).

## 1a. Ruling 2 — L2 reads its voice-leading evidence from L0 and L1 only (question 2; a confirmation; OQ-L2-9 answered)

## 1b. Ruling 3 — L2 publishes no relation for a grace note (question 3; a confirmation; OQ-L2-10 answered)

**The question as put, alone, in its own turn after Ruling 2:** "For L2's specification, should L2 publish

Do you confirm?" **The user's words, verbatim:** as quoted at §0.

**The ruling.** For L2's specification, a grace note carries no assignment and L2 publishes no
elaboration relation for it; it is evidence for its host's assignment and for the chord. **L2-S26 stands
as derived; its status "Open on publishing a relation (OQ-L2-10)" closes. OQ-L2-10 is answered no.**

sounding set and is published, with its spelled pitch and notated form, as an attachment of its host
(read); L3 is the layer of facts read off a settled reading (read). Meta level: the analysis's own
specification.

## 1c. The condition the user attached to Ruling 3 — checked at the objects

**His words:** that "not publishing" in this and the previous rulings does not break *"no information
loss / always publish everything known, inferred"*, and that it is up to the consuming layer to choose
what to use. **What this sitting checked, and what it found:**

- **The rules concerned, read.** #12 (`CLAUDE.md`): negative evidence is carried "unless the exclusion is
  recomputable from what is kept", and the clause beside it: a collapse "is a loss only when the several
  cannot be got back". The fact-publication corollary: every derived analytical fact is published "exactly
  once, on the producing layer's output surface; consumers read, never re-derive", and its 2026-07-12
  amendment: evidence-class facts are published "broadly even without a named consumer". D-297 (index
  line, `DECISIONS.md`): "never computing a possibility is not information loss; only discarding a
  computed one is". D-295 (index line): every inferred object must be displayable.
- **Ruling 3 (grace notes).** Nothing is discarded: the grace note's spelled pitch, notated form and host
  stay published by L1 (S-16, S-50), and the chord by L2. The grace relation is recomputable from those
  (#12's clause), and if a layer computes it, it publishes it once, as that layer's fact (the corollary).
  **The condition holds.**
- **Ruling 2 of this sitting (L0 and L1 only).** It decides what L2 *consumes*, not what anything
  publishes: L1 still publishes the per-voice relations and the chordal count, and the axis's facts stay
  the axis's. That is the consuming layer choosing what it uses, the user's own framing. **The condition
  holds.**
- **Ruling 1 of this sitting (weighed, not fixed).** It changes no publication: each note's assignment and
  relation, and the rivals with their mass, are published as the charter says. **The condition holds.**
- **The second family's rulings (2026-10-05, earlier).** Ruling 1 added an unclassified elaboration so a
  foreign note is carried rather than forced into the chord (#12); Ruling 2 keeps the pedal reading and its
  rivals published with their mass (L2-S40). **The condition holds**, as this sitting reads those records.
- **One bound, stated rather than left to be assumed.** The ratified charter's L2 → L3 contract lets
  nothing cross that L2 did not publish, "in particular no intermediate quantity of L2's own scoring"
  (read). So "always publish everything known"
  does not reach the values of L2's internal score terms. *This sitting's reading:* that is not a loss
  under #12, because those values are a deterministic function of published inputs and the specification's
  terms and weights, and the scored outcome is published as the rivals with their mass. Whether the user
  wants that ratified bound read any differently is his; it is recorded here, not decided.
