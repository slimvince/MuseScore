## 1. Ruling 1 — Position B RATIFIED (the user's word: "yes")

**L3 publishes, as a read-off fact, which entries of the catalog of named progressions fit which stretches of L2's
decided chords: per match, the entry's name and the stretch of decided chords it covers, overlapping matches all
published. How a match is shown to an end user, and whether a preset filters what is shown, is presentation, decided
later and separately.**

**Algorithm: a change.** The ratified framework is amended at three places (the writing is the application act's):
- the L3 charter's *Consumes* line, now "L2's published facts, plus L0's and L1's. It may not consume anything L2 did
  not publish, and it may not revise L2." — to name the catalog as fixed reference knowledge L3 reads, not input about
  the piece (so that §3.1's "And nothing else" is not read against it);
- the L3 charter's *Publishes* line, now "the cadence at each phrase end, with its type; the phrase and section
  grouping; the chord symbol — root pitch class, quality and bass note; the figured bass; the harmonic rhythm" — to add
  the matches;
- §3.2's last sentence, now "Over the whole: the phrase and section grouping, the chord symbols as a derived view, the
  figured bass, and the harmonic rhythm" — to add the matches.

**Grounds, as put:** the user's point 3 (surface §9: the fact that one or more progressions fit a stretch of chords is
established before any presentation decision); the fact-publication corollary (`CLAUDE.md`, *Guiding principles*: a
derived analytical fact is published once, "on the producing layer's output surface"); #7 (the fact is read off L2's
settled reading, which is the L3 charter's question). It does not improve the chord or key reading ("L3 → L2:
Nothing").

**What the amendment and L3's detail design must settle (surface §3 B items 1 to 7, as checked at §10), not decided
here:** the catalog named in L3's *Consumes* line; that matches do not tile the music (stated per match, or a
non-tiling span kind admitted); line-defined entries matched on chords only, with D-507's *chords-only* mark; which old
register decisions carry into L3's design (D-406, D-410, D-411, D-414, D-502, D-506, D-507); **that no preset chooses
the catalog L3 reads** (Row 38.79 (i)); the fact's consumers named, all outside the analysis (presentation; a future
chord-suggestion tool), as the corollary's dormancy clause asks; whether recognised substitutions are part of the fact
(left to the family's substitution rows). **Exact or tolerant matching** is the detail design's: exact matching
involves no choice; a tolerance threshold would need its own measurement.

## 2. Ruling 2 — the catalog term CONFIRMED as a named DP-O candidate (the user's word: "yes")

**The use of the catalog as evidence for choosing chords (use (a): where a chord is in doubt, a reading that makes
the passage a known progression is the likelier) is added to the DP-O note as a named candidate, beside the longer
chord-to-chord memory, the cadential-formula term, the sequence term and harmonic rhythm: when DP-O is measured, a term
that rates each competing reading by its match to catalog entries is one of the arms.** Ground: the user's ruling of
2026-10-07 (`CLAUDE.md` Conventions step 4: a design rule holds while there is no reason to suspect that breaking it
would improve the inference; when there is, the departure is investigated, tested and measured); his objection at
surface §8 is that reason; D-254.

**Documentation only. Algorithm: no change.** The method that stands, as put: L2 decides key and chord together; its
progression term is a table of chord-to-chord pairs counted once from annotated music, so a progression longer than
two chords is weighed link by link, not whole; DP-O stays open, its named candidates unmeasured and unadopted.
Adopting a catalog term after a measurement would be its own ruling.

**Travels with the candidate:** D-503 (the idiom mixture seeded by the preset weights a match as evidence) and its
