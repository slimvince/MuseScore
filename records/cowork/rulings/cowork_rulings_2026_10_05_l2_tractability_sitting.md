## 1. Ruling 1 — L2-S36 stands, read with D-297; the end it means is the end of the loaded music

**The ruling.** (i) **L2-S36 stands as derived**: the search weighs every reading it has begun to score
through to the end, or drops one only by a bound that provably cannot discard a reading that could win; no
beam that discards on partial candidate scores; where exactness is unaffordable, the charter's rule gives
way only by a ruling. **OQ-L2-8 stays open as derived.** (ii) **"The end" is the end of the music loaded for
the selection** — the working span with its context (L2-S43), the context extended by convergence (D-261)
under the safety cap (D-624) and C-8's scaling — not the end of the whole score, unless the whole score is
the selection or convergence does not settle before the cap. (iii) **D-297 and L2-S36 govern different
acts**: a reading the admission rule never generates is not discarded (D-297, `FRAMEWORK.md` §8.4; the
admission rule itself governed by L2-S2's closure test with exclusions ruled by name); L2-S36 bars dropping a
reading the search has begun to score. **Proposed addition to L2-S36:** "A reading the admission rule never
generates is not discarded (D-297; FRAMEWORK §8.4); what this statement bars is dropping a reading the search
has begun to score." (iv) An effort setting may not quietly reduce how many readings the search keeps; if one
ever does, that is the ruling OQ-L2-8 names.

**The ground, as put (surface §3, §4.1).** Towards the objective (#4): a reading that would have won is
never lost to an early cut (the charter's "a wrong reading can be a local optimum"). Towards the principles:
#12 (nothing computed is thrown away); #6 (D-297 and L2-S36 each govern one act); D-200 (precision traded for
speed last). Meta level: the analysis's own specification only.

**The placement, Ruling 1.** **ADOPTED — proposed**, placed from UNPLACED: Row 21.73, the proposal being
the sentence in (iii).

**What it does not decide.** The route for OQ-L2-8 (surface §4.2), L2-S23 (§4.3) and the other L2-S36
placements (§4.4) are this sitting's later questions. What "stops changing" means for convergence
(OQ-L2-6) is not decided.

**Application.** Nothing is applied by this ruling. The reading file's rows are re-marked, and the amended
derived L2 specification written as a new file beside the untouched derivation, by the application dispatch
the L2 route ruling names as its step 2 — after the family rulings, not now (D-250).
