## 2. Ruling 1 — K1 CONFIRMED: L3 publishes key areas

**As ruled:** L3 publishes key areas — runs of consecutive spans of L2's segmentation grouped by tonality — as an output
of its own; what exactly a key area is, is K2 (§4).

**Grounds as put:** towards the principles — D-295; D-100; D-605 (the key-area grouping a post-grouping of the resolved
key, so it sits above L2); adding an output to L3's charter is the user's (the brief's reservation of L3's *what*);
towards the ultimate objective — key areas are a candidate evidence for the form-function names of Ruling 3 (sections
record) and L3-S23 family (iii) already reads L2's tonality changes for sections. Cost: small where a key area reads off
L2's decision (K2 A). Put as a confirmation under D-599, the alternatives having collapsed as at Ruling 3 of the
sections record. Meta level: the analysis itself.

**Algorithmic change, decided:** L3 gains an output, key areas, its definition per K2.
**Documentation:** the charter's L3 *Publishes* list to gain key areas (an amendment of `FRAMEWORK.md` §5, L3, by the
application act — not edited here); the derivation to gain a statement for them. Rows 3.1(iii), 3.4, 8.12, 8.75,
20.47(ii), 57.278 and 57.369 travel with K2, since each also states a definition. The re-marking is the application
dispatch's act.

**The currently decided method before this ruling, as stated with the question:** L3 publishes no key areas; the
charter does not list them; the derivation is silent; L2 publishes a tonality per span with rivals and mass, and a
ranked list of whole readings; applied chord against new tonality decided at L2.

## 2b. Ruling 2 — K2: Alternative A, a key area closes at every change of tonality in L2's principal reading

Put in its own turn after Ruling 1, alone, plain text, no widget, stating that it changes the algorithm and also files
documentation, and stating the currently decided method. **The user's word: "A".**

**As ruled:** a key area is a maximal run of consecutive spans of L2's segmentation sharing one tonality in L2's
principal reading, closed and a new one opened at every change of tonality; it decides nothing beyond L2's reading. Each
rival whole reading L2 publishes gives its own sequence of key areas, carrying whatever L2 publishes for that reading
(this side's proposal, put with A and ruled with it).

**Declined:** B — D-472's smoothing (a span whose tonality differs without clearing a confidence test grouped into the
enclosing area).

**Grounds as put:** towards the principles — the charter's one-home reasoning (lines 489–492, *"a second chance to
disagree with it"*); L2-S18 as confirmed 2026-10-07 (applied chord against new tonality decided by the candidate score,
*"not by a rule"*); the phrase-ends Ruling 1 and sections Ruling 1 (evidence weighed, never forcing or forbidding),
applied to key areas as this side's reading. For B: D-472 is the user's live ruling and gives the coarser view analysts
use. Against A: a weak change of tonality L2 chose still starts a new key area. A nested, weighed key-area grouping is
named as the later route if the coarser view proves useful — a question opened by measurement, not decided. Meta level:
the analysis itself.

**Algorithmic change, decided:** key areas defined as above. **Documentation:** Rows 3.1(iii), 3.4, 8.12, 8.75,
20.47(ii), 57.278 and 57.369 ADOPTED (the definition as ruled); Rows 3.5(i) and 3.5(ii) decided against; D-472 decided
against by this ruling, its register status settled at the ratification act, not edited at its home. The re-marking is
the application dispatch's act.

**The currently decided method before this ruling, as stated with the question:** L3 publishes key areas (Ruling 1);
how they are formed not decided.
