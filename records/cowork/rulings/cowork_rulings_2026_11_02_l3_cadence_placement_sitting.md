## 2. Ruling 1 — Alternative A: L3-S15 as derived

**L3 publishes a cadence only where some phrase reading — the principal or a rival — ends a phrase, at most one per
phrase end, an elided one included. A cadence-like pattern at any other position is published as evidence named for
the pattern, never as a cadence, with its establishment status.**

**What this supersedes, stated exactly:** **D-455**, at L3, in that an off-phrase-end pattern is no longer published as
an "internal cadence". Its substance — never moved to a boundary, never discarded — survives: the pattern stays
published as evidence. **UNSETTLED:** whether D-455 was re-homed to L3 by Ruling 48 of the L0/L1 comparison (that record
not read); the supersession is for the application act and the register route to carry either way.

**Declined:** B — A plus D-455 kept (a cadence marked "internal" where the pattern is strong and no reading ends a
phrase); C — no publication of the pattern away from phrase ends.

**Grounds as put, after the check's corrections** (each named at its principle): towards the ultimate objective (#4)
— the missed-boundary case D-455 guarded is absorbed by the weighed phrase decision, cadential evidence entering at
every candidate position (L3-S9 (ii)), the weight it earns fitted (L3-S14) and not yet known; L3's cadences cannot
contradict its own phrase reading, by construction; cadence-like endings occur where analysts write no cadence
(Karystinaios & Widmer 2022, relayed through the derivation, not read). Towards the principles — A follows the
charter's "a claim about what the decided chords do at a decided phrase end" most closely; nothing is lost (#12); the
word "cadence" has one home (#6). Against A — an analyst's cadence written inside a phrase, with no rival phrase reading
ending there, may count as a miss in location grading (D-462); unmeasured; if measurement after L3-S14's fit gives
reason to suspect B does better, the 2026-10-07 ruling (Ruling 6 of
`cowork_rulings_2026_10_07_l2_chord_tone_membership_sitting.md`) opens it for testing. B — a cadence L3's own phrase
decision does not support, against the charter's wording; the naming bar of IC-L1 S-50 (relayed, by analogy, not read);
on this side's reading a threshold or a use of mass to fit. C — against #12 and the evidence-publication rule; D-455
itself rejected discarding. Meta level of every alternative: the analysis itself.

**Documentation and algorithm.**
- **Algorithmic change, decided:** a cadence-like pattern away from every decided phrase end is published as pattern
  evidence, not as an "internal cadence"; a cadence there is published only through a rival phrase reading.
- **Documentation:** L3-S15 filed as the L3 statement; Rows 8.47 (ii), 8.86, 8.116 (ii), 8.129 (ii) and 8.130 recorded
  as decided against. **The re-marking of each row is the application dispatch's act** (route record §2 step 2).
