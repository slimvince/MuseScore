`V(64)`), each read as it stands. The type can be the same under both, while the arrival position and the pre-dominant
feature differ. Those differences are published as rivals, and DP-N is not decided."*

- **Algorithmic (decided against the older documents only):** L3 reads the cadence under each of L2's two readings as
  published, and never converts one into the other or collapses the six-four and the dominant into one dominant
  approach; where the readings differ in arrival position or in the chord before the dominant, the cadences are
  published as rivals with their mass. Nothing currently ratified is changed: this follows from Ruling 1 of the L2
  cadential six-four record and from Ruling 1 of `cowork_rulings_2026_11_03_l3_cadence_mass_sitting.md` (L3-S19).

  7.94 (the collapse); Row 7.273 (the glossary entry) in so far as it picks one theory; Row 7.37 in so far as it fixes
  one label for the analysis's output — its stated purpose, comparison with the ground truth, belongs to *the
  measurement of the analysis*, as the L2 ruling placed Row 23.109 (i) and Row 5.42's six-four half. Row 42.36 (ii)
