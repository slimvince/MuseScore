## 2. The ground, recorded so a later session can test it rather than re-argue it

1. **The measured quantity is small against the boot.** The thirty-four explicitly-marked defense
   clauses hold **12,957 characters** — **13.25 %** of what a session reads of `CLAUDE.md` and
   **5.29 %** of the whole 244,912-character boot. Every value is read at
   `tools/audit/defense_share.json` and none is transcribed from a narrative (**D-431**).
2. **The saving would be smaller than that, by an unmeasured amount.** A moved defense leaves a
   POINTER at its rule, and every pointer costs boot characters. **The net is therefore below the
   gross and was never measured; no estimate of it was offered or ruled.**
3. **The cost is apparatus in the very file the exercise exists to shrink** — a new class of file, a
   conditional-read rule, and a pointer at each rule whose defense moves.
4. **The boot is not a binding constraint at present.** Two consecutive Cowork sittings spent about
   one part in fifty of their available budget on the whole boot. **This is the same test Decision
   B's own deferral rests on**, and it gives the same answer.
5. **The published value is a LOWER BOUND and that cuts both ways.** The marker table admits three
   explicit forms only, and the 2026-09-08 classification found defense clauses inside passages
   carrying no marker. **The true quantity is larger and is unknown.** But reaching it needs a second
   authored pass over the whole of the six spans — a larger job than the one just done, not a
   mechanical extension of it — which is what option (B) was and what this ruling declines for now.

**★ AND THE MEASUREMENT IS NOT BEING IGNORED, WHICH IS THE MISREADING THIS CLAUSE EXISTS AGAINST.**

building. **Using the figure to conclude that it does not yet pay is the figure doing its work.**
