## Ruling 1 — three changes to working practice for the rest of the site-table work

**The user's words:** *"I rule three changes to working practice for the rest of the site-table work, none of them
changing the algorithm and none skipping any check at the dispatch: (1) from the next boot on, a session reads the site
table only at its opening, Part L's form and index, and its last entries, and reads other parts only where an item
points to them; (2) a progress or status line that has been overtaken is replaced without keeping its former wording, as
a staleness fix under my rule of 2026-09-22, while former wordings of substantive text are still kept; (3) the
instructions in Part L's index that a ruling has already taken up are entered together in one pass as a short list, not
one full entry each. Record this as my ruling, apply (2) and (3) from now on, write (1) into your handoff entry's boot
instructions, and then continue with step 1."*

**As ruled.**

1. **The boot's read of the site table.** From the next boot on, a session reads the site table at its opening (the
   header and progress paragraphs), Part L's form, count and index, and Part L's last entries, and reads any other part
   only where the item it is working on points to it. Written into handoff entry 785 §4 step 3.
2. **Status lines.** A progress or status line that has been overtaken is replaced, and its former wording is not
   kept; this is a staleness fix under the user's rule of 2026-09-22. Former wordings of substantive text (an entry's
   finding, mark, quotation or dispatch check) are still kept, labelled with the sitting and landing that wrote them.
3. **Part L's ruled instructions.** The instructions Part L's index names as taken up by a ruling are entered in one
   pass, as a short list (start line, list item, ruling), not one full entry each. The dispatch checks they rest on are
   the ones the named ruling's entry in the table records.

**Applied from this sitting on:** (2) and (3), at this sitting's landing of the site table.
