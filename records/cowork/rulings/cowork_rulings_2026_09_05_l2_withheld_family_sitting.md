**Ruled.** All 110 are withheld from L2's boot pack. **Recorded beside the ruling, at the user's
acceptance of this session's stated caveats:**

(a) **Nine entries are withheld on a reading of the test wider than the reading file's own §2 and
§5 state.** The file's test is: IN if the entry discloses the ruled answer to one of the four limbs

that reading: withholding the recorded how costs the deriving session nothing it needs, since the
charter itself tells it to publish rivals with their mass, while admitting it would make the
session's derived answer on that point indistinguishable from recall. **The reading file's stated
test is to be amended to name this ground** (§6), so the record says what it means.

chord-by-chord path. The asymmetry is recorded as harmless: the framework's own L2 block already
calls the reading "the one entangled decision", so none of the three discloses more than the

(c) **Thirty-two of the 110 carry the LEGACY mark** in `DECISIONS.md` (this session's count at the
