## 2. The rulings

### Ruling 1 — the elaboration relations (question 1; OQ-L2-4 ★ answered)

**The user's words, verbatim: "I agree with your recommendation position two".**

**The ruling.** The elaboration relations L2 admits in the first L2 specification are: **passing, neighbour,
suspension, anticipation, appoggiatura, escape tone, pedal point, and an explicit unclassified elaboration.**
**Retardation is a suspension that resolves upward** — a kind of suspension, not a name of its own. The
appoggiatura and the escape tone are the two one-sided relations (leapt to and left by step; stepped to and left
by leap); pedal point is D-207's class (a tone held or restruck against changing harmony, in any voice); the
unclassified elaboration is a note the reading finds foreign to the chord that fits no named relation.

**OQ-L2-4 is answered by this ruling**: the charter's four are not exhaustive for L2's specification; the four
named additions are admitted; retardation is not a separate name. **L2-S8 stands amended**: its statement names
the eight relations above with retardation as suspension's upward-resolving kind; its status closes; its
falsifier becomes "CODE: the relation set equals the ruled list". **OQ-L2-18's condition** ("only if OQ-L2-4
admits 'pedal point'") **is met**; its case still has no example, and OQ-L2-18 otherwise stays as the
derivation leaves it.

**The ground, as put and as agreed (surface §A.6).** Towards the objective (#4): it closes the derivation's
named false-negative path — an appoggiatura or escape tone mislabelled or forced to be a chord tone, "which then
changes the chord" (L2-S8's premise) — without giving one relation two names. Towards the principles: #12 (the
residual class carries a note's foreignness rather than dropping it); #6 (the charter's list, D-527's label list
and D-207's class become one vocabulary); #1 (standard names, each defined by approach and departure, or by a
tone held against changing harmony). Meta level: the analysis's own specification.

**The costs, carried, as the surface stated them.** (a) **It amends ratified charter text** — the publication
list at `FRAMEWORK.md` lines 397–398 — under #14, by the amendment mechanism Ruling 2 of 2026-08-29 calls
working rather than a defect; the amendment is made at the ratification act, not by this ruling. (b) If the
relations are part of the search state, each added relation enlarges it (L2-S23's premise; OQ-L2-8 ★); if the
names are read off the decided reading afterwards, the list costs the search nothing — the surface's reading,
not a fact; which of the two is L2-S33's family's question (surface §A.5(a)). (c) How often the unclassified
elaboration fires is unknown; no annotated corpus with per-note elaboration labels is known to the derivation
(OQ-L2-13).

**Declined: position one** — the charter's four, complete: the false-negative path stands; #12 (a foreign note
forced into the chord); the relayed published rule set uses seven models. **Declined: position three** — Row
13.3's full list (adding chromatic neighbour, cambiata, échappée): no gain the surface could name; échappée is the
escape tone's French name, two names for one relation (#6); a chromatic neighbour is already distinguished by
D-527's outside-scale category; a cambiata spans several notes, which the per-event assignment (L2-S9) does not
carry — the last a reading.

### Ruling 2 — where the pedal reading is chosen (question 2; a confirmation)

**The question as put, alone, in its own turn after Ruling 1:** "Do you confirm that, for L2's specification,
the pedal reading is chosen by L2's one decision, so that D-385's after-the-decode reader does not carry into
it?" **The user's word, verbatim: "yes".**

**The ruling.** For L2's specification, the pedal reading — the chord read from the upper voices with the held
note as an elaboration — is one of the readings L2's one decision scores and chooses, like any other, its rivals
(the reading rooted on the held note among them) published with their mass (L2-S40). **D-385's reader over the
stored alternatives, choosing the pedal reading after the decode by a confidence threshold, does not carry into
L2's specification.** D-385's register status is settled at the ratification act, not here (as D-103 was recorded
superseded by D-207). **L2-S23 stands as derived**: the assignments are made inside the candidate score, with no
exception for the pedal.

**The ground (surface §A.7), a derivation confirmed, not an option picked (D-599; Conventions, *HOW A DECISION
SURFACE IS WRITTEN*, step 2).** Judging that a held note does not belong to the chord is a chord-tone assignment;
the ratified charter's DP-D (2026-08-29, after D-385 of 2026-07-07 and D-527 of 2026-07-19) puts every such
assignment inside L2's one decision, "published from it", and the charter's *May not* forbids discarding a rival
before the whole sequence of spans is scored. D-385's reader makes that judgment outside the decision, by a
threshold. The same reading answers Rows 44.64, 44.66(i) and 44.67 ("chords first, NCTs classified against
them"). Relayed from the surface; `FRAMEWORK.md` and D-385's group entry were not opened by this sitting.

**What it does not decide.** When the *label* "pedal point" is written — that travels with Group H to L2-S33's
family. What becomes of the measurement D-385's build is hard-gated on at OI-4 — not decided here.
