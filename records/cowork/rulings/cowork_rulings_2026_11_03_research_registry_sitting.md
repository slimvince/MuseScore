## 1. Ruling 1 — Alternative D

**What is ruled.**

1. **The main pass.** The user's registry is the main input to the research iteration ruled on 2026-10-07: after the last
   layer's detail specification is ratified, and before the measurement-design phase opens. The pass works from a
   **fixed snapshot** of the registry, so its population is defined; entries the registry gains after the snapshot are
   taken in a follow-up, not into the pass already running. The pass sets aside what is already held and read here,
   reads at the primary source what is new and categorised as bearing on this project, and checks each finding against
   the specification statements it bears on: **adopted, rejected with a stated reason, or sent to a measurement.**
2. **Targeted lookups before then.** Whenever a family question needs research during the detail-specification phase,
   the session may search the registry for that one topic, and reads at the source only what the search turns up. The
   registry is not read whole for this purpose. OQ-L3-5 (thematic parallelism) is the first such lookup.
3. **Two rules that come with it.**
   - **(a) Sessions may read the registry.** For the registry, this replaces the bar recorded on 2026-08-30 that the
     workbook at `external resarch summary/external research.xlsx` is *"never opened by sessions or batches, never
     amended"*. Sessions still do not amend the registry; it is the user's.
   - **(b) Nothing in the registry carries weight in a specification until it has been read at its primary source here**
     (#1; the theory-grounding corollary, D-473). The user's categorisation decides what is worth reading; it is not
     itself the reading.

**The target, stated so it can be checked.** Before the audit, every registry finding that bears on a specified statement
has been dealt with in one of the three ways in point 1. This is the checkable form of the user's *"ALL knowledge should
be in our specs before we do the audit"*: a list compiled from outside cannot be shown to cover everything, but whether
each of its findings has been dealt with can.

**Where the registry lives — settled by the user after the ruling, his words verbatim:** *"It is here C:\s\ms-antigravity
and made using Antigravity and myself. It is NOT completed!"* So it is a separate folder outside this repository, not
the 2026-08-28 workbook, and it is still being compiled; a targeted lookup records the registry's state as it found it.
*(Former wording, kept (#12): "UNSETTLED, for the user when a session first needs it: where the current registry lives,
and in what form (whether it is the 2026-08-28 workbook grown, or a separate file); and the form of the snapshot …")*
**Still UNSETTLED:** the form of the main pass's snapshot (a dated copy placed in the repository is this side's
suggestion, not ruled), and whether the 2026-08-30 bar on the 2026-08-28 workbook itself is touched — this ruling reaches
the registry, and the workbook's bar stands as recorded.

**The ground, as put.** Towards the objective (#4): every specification is checked against the widest research gathered,
at the point where it is complete enough to aim the search, and the families decided now see the registry's coverage on
their own topic. Towards the principles: #1 and #2 (fact-based, targeted); #5; D-473 by rule (b); and the order the
2026-10-07 record gives — measurement design fixes what is measured and fitted, and the audit uses the specifications as
its yardstick, so a specification changed after either forces part of it to be redone. Meta level: the order of work on
the analysis's own specifications. **Declined:** A (the main pass alone — the families decided before it would not see the
registry); B (layer by layer now — the registry is still growing, the ratified layers would need the late pass anyway,
and a parallel stream costs session context); C (just before the audit — measurement design would be partly redone
wherever an algorithm changes). Accepted cost: findings against the already-ratified layers (L0/L1, L2) arrive as
amendments, which the 2026-10-07 iteration already expected.
