## 2. Rulings — vocabulary replacements (user-directed, this sitting)

The user: *"some words you should not use: clean room, world, mirror, airlock - needs replacing
otherwise you will have to explain them every time."* And later: *"Some words again that needs
replacing: pinned, pipeline, output snapshots (all three are not understandable)."* The
replacements, proposed by the writing side and accepted in sequence — these govern all plan
prose from this date; the phase definitions are written in these terms:

- **implementation-derived material** (replaces *the mirror*): our code, plus anything whose
  content reflects what that code does — outputs, saved results, fitted tables, measured
  behaviour, documents describing the implementation. The user's check that forced the
  precision: *"why not use 'our implementation' instead of 'mirror' (or are they not
  interchangeable)?"* — they are not; the term must cover the derived material too.
- **independent sources** (replaces *the world*): sources whose content was not shaped by our
  implementation — published research, the user's ratified rulings, musical scores annotated by
  others. The user's own gloss, confirmed correct: *"non polluted facts and data."*
- **fact-gate** (replaces *the airlock*; the user's own word): the admission mechanism in front
  of the empirical findings ledger (which keeps its name). The user's ground, confirmed: a gate
  can REFUSE entry, which is what this mechanism does — its test remains *does the fact survive
  the implementation being thrown away?*
- **implementation-blind redesign** (replaces *the clean room*): the derivation sessions, barred
  from implementation-derived material, reading independent sources plus what the fact-gate has
  admitted.
- Plain-phrase replacements for conversation and plan prose: *kept as a fixed reference* (for
  "pinned"), *the full analysis sequence / a complete analysis run* (for "pipeline"), *saved
  earlier results* (for "output snapshots").
