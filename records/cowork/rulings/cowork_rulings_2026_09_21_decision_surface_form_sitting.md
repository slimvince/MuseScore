## 1. Ruling 1 — the standing form of a decision surface, in four clauses

**Ruled, verbatim, the user's own words and numbering:**

> 1. If there really are not any choices (as valued visavi ultimate objective, guiding principles,
>    meta-level)  - say so and do not present a decision surface.
> 2. If there is a decision to be made (choices available), present a decision surface.
> 3. Decision surfaces should present all alternatives. Each alternative weighed against (at least,
>    but not most) guiding principles, ultimate objective, meta-level of suggested action.
> 4. A decision surface should include a recommendation, explained in terms of ultimate objective,
>    guiding principles, meta-level of suggested action.

**And, given with them:**

> I cannot think of any example of decision surface that should exclude a recommendation.

### 1.1 Two readings STATED rather than assumed, either of which the user may correct

Neither is built on before he does.

- **Clause 3's *"(at least, but not most)"*** is read as **at least these three, but not only these**
  — the three named are the floor, and a surface may weigh an alternative on further grounds where
  they bear. The literal string *not most* does not parse against the clause it qualifies, and this
  side did not invent a third reading to make it parse.
- **Clause 3's *"weighed against"*** is read as **weighed in relation to**, not as opposition. This
  is stated because the user's own vocabulary ruling of 2026-08-17 reserves *against* for the case
  OPPOSING an option and orders *"towards the objective" / "towards the principles"* when an option
  is rated. **This record uses *towards* in its own prose and quotes his *against* verbatim**, which
  is #12 applied to a quotation rather than a silent correction of it.

### 1.2 What it SUPERSEDES

**D-658's no-recommendation clause is superseded for decision surfaces.** That entry, ruled
2026-08-09 (Ruling 27 of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_09_fourth_stop.md`, read whole at its register
entry this sitting), carries as its form: *"every claim CITED AT ITS SOURCE and read in place; the
records concerned READ WHOLE; anything the record does not settle marked UNSETTLED rather than filled
— and NO RECOMMENDATION AT ALL."* **Clause 4 replaces the last of those four and no other.** The
other three stand and are untouched: citation at source, the concerned records read whole, and the
marking of an unsettled item as unsettled rather than filling it from the most plausible reading.

**The ground D-658 gave for the clause now superseded is NOT refuted here, and what carries its load
instead is named.** Its recorded reason was that *"a fact-gathering pass that ends in a recommendation
has decided the question it was sent to inform, so the user then rules on the session's reading rather
than on the facts"* — which rests on the user's own instruction of 2026-08-09, quoted in that ruling:
*"follow the rule: fact based decisions or exploration to gather facts are allowed, not decided on
unsure/fabulated/misremembered facts."* **That hazard is real and is now held by clauses 3 and 4
together rather than by silence:** all alternatives are presented and each is weighed, and the
recommendation is explained on the three named grounds — so the user rules on a reasoning he can check
at its own grounds, rather than on a verdict. **A recommendation given without its three-ground
explanation would reopen the hazard in full**, which is why clause 4's second half is not decoration.

### 1.3 The OPEN QUESTION it closes

`records/cowork/rulings/cowork_rulings_2026_08_31_decision_surface_sitting.md` records, twice and in
terms, that it does not settle **whether D-249's recommendation clause or D-658's no-recommendation
rule governs a surface of that kind** — *"Which of the two governs a surface of this kind is not
settled by this ruling and is not settled here."* **Clause 4 settles it in D-249's favour.** D-249,
the whole-decision-surface rule homed in `CLAUDE.md` Conventions, already lists among a surface's
parts *"the recommendation with its reason"*; clause 4 makes that unconditional and adds what the
reason must be given in terms of.

### 1.4 What it SHARPENS rather than replaces

- **Ruling 1 of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_31_decision_surface_sitting.md`** fixed that every option is
  rated on **two** axes — the ultimate objective and the guiding principles. **Clause 3 makes the
  meta level of the suggested act a THIRD standing ground**, where until now it was asked for per
  sitting; the L2 gate sitting of 2026-09-20 records the user asking for it in that sitting, which is
  the practice this clause generalises.
- **D-424** (a surface names the principle behind every pro and con and rates every option on two
  axes) and **D-599** (apply the standing principles first; what remains for the user is ratifying the
  derivation) are untouched and still run first.
- **Clause 1 generalises the user's ruling of 2026-08-28** — *"There is no choice anymore, B or
  what?"* — and entry 224 §7's own *where the alternatives have collapsed to one, say so instead of
  presenting a menu*. **What clause 1 adds is the TEST**: the collapse is judged as valued towards the
  ultimate objective, the guiding principles and the meta level, and not on the bare count of options
  a session can imagine.
- **Unaffected and still binding:** the surface delivered as user-visible text in a turn of its own,
  every identifier re-explained from scratch (2026-08-15); the choice question in a separate later
  turn for a consequential decision (D-249; the user may pre-empt it by disposing directly upon the
  surface, the shape the extracts sitting of 2026-09-20 §0 records); one decision per turn
  (2026-08-21); no multiple-choice widget (2026-09-04); plain ordinary English in conversation and
  the record form only in the records (2026-08-28).

## 5. The defect this ruling corrects, recorded because it is this side's own

The user instructed this side to supply a recommendation *"as per rules"*. This side had, in the same
surface, already established the fact that decides which rule applies — that the derived set of 21
documents is arithmetic over the user's own ruling of 2026-09-05 and carries no judgment — and then
applied **D-658**, which governs only where the record does NOT settle the question. **The test's
answer was stated and the opposite branch was taken.** The correct branch is recorded three times in
the 2026-08-31 record: *"D-658 governs only where the record does not settle the question, and here
the standing material largely does, so D-599 runs — the standing principles decide, and what remained
for the user was ratifying the derivation."*

**A second, separate defect stands and is not the cause of the first.** Entry 224 §7 item 2 reads
*"No recommendation unless the record fails to settle the question (D-658)"*, which permits a
recommendation in exactly the case D-658 forbade one — an inversion of the test. It was written by
this side at the preceding sitting. **It is not amended at its own object here**: entry 224 is the
current entry point and a correction to it belongs in the next handoff entry, the shape entry 152 (x)
sets. **Clause 4 above supersedes the rule that line misstates**, so what the next entry carries is
the correction of the record, not a live rule.
