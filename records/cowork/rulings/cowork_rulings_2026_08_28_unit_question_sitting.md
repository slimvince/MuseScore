## 1. Ruling 1 — a unit is a DECISION the analysis makes about the music

**§9.0 is ruled at the decision reading:** a unit of the architecture is one question about the
music that the analysis settles, and could settle differently — never a factor of the model (one
additively combined term of the numerical score over candidate readings, which remains a means of
computing a decision), and not a two-tier reconciliation carrying factors as sub-units.

**The ground, as the surface stated it and the user agreed to it:** every piece of evidence the
record holds about what the pieces of a harmonic analysis are is about decisions — the ground
truth's own form (a record of decisions, containing no factor), the disagreements between
independent analysts (disagreements about decisions), the empirical findings ledger's admitted
facts (overwhelmingly statements that a decision is underdetermined by the evidence at a moment —
C2, C6, C34, C35, verified at the ledger by this session), and the multi-task literature's measured
pathology (separately predicted heads are decisions, and the failure is decisions disagreeing). The
factor structure loses nothing by waiting: factors remain the means inside the middle layer's
detail specification, where the Premise Gate (#17) and the Class-A prohibition (#18) already govern
them.

**What follows.** The framework document stands drafted at this reading; no re-derivation of its §5
follows from this ruling. The layer-ownership question (Δ2 of Appendix A.4 — whether a layer owns
the answer to one question or one evidence source's contribution to it) becomes GRADABLE, its
premise now fixed. **It is NOT decided by this ruling.**

**Revisitability, stated with the ruling.** If the user's external list of published research, when
