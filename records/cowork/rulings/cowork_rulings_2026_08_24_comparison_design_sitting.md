## 1. Ruling 1 — WHO COMPARES: a fresh Claude Code session, ordinary boot, under a Cowork-written dispatch (Alternative A)

The comparer must know the oracle and the current text; an ordinary boot provides both, and the
dispatch protocol, guard set and verify-at-objects discipline already bind CC batches. **Declined:
B** (a fresh Cowork session — no guard discipline, no commit trail, wrong strengths); **C** (this
session — it authored the withheld family and the caveats, so it has an interest in the pack

## 2. Ruling 2 — THE READING ORDER: the blind output FIRST and whole, then the oracle's two arms, then the untrusted sources (Alternative A)

The comparer forms its understanding of what the blind session claimed before the oracle's frame
exists to map it onto; generous mis-mapping is the known failure of oracle-first grading.
**Applied to the boot, of necessity and of record:** the ordinary session-start read includes
`CLAUDE.md`, whose Conventions span carries the oracle's founding-instance statement in words —
so the comparison session reads the blind output whole BEFORE taking its session-start read; the
read SET is unchanged, its ORDER is changed by this ruling, and the dispatch states the departure
in terms. **Declined: B** (oracle first — exactly the anchoring A avoids).

## 3. Ruling 3 — THE GRADING VOCABULARY: closed, two axes, words not numbers, fixed before grading starts (Alternative A)

Against the oracle, one verdict per statement: **MATCHES** (reproduces the ruled intent in
substance); **DEFENDED ALTERNATIVE** (differs, with a defense the record cannot dismiss — these
go to the user for ranking); **SILENT** (the oracle does not speak to it); **CONTRADICTS,
UNDEFENDED**. Against the untrusted sources, separately: **AGREES / DIFFERS / THE TEXT IS
SILENT** — recorded as evidence about the current text, never as a verdict on the statement.
Every row carries the statement's own words beside the oracle's, so the user can re-grade any row
at the texts. **Default nothing:** a verdict the comparer cannot defend in one sentence at the
texts is delivered **UNGRADED**, with what was read, to the user. The ten open questions are not
graded; they are listed with what would settle each. **Declined: B** (free prose — cannot be
ruled on; prose is where anchoring hides).
