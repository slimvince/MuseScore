## 1. Ruling — one tonality per span; a pivot is two rivals with their mass (OQ-L2-5 answered)

**The user's word, verbatim: "one".**

**The ruling.** For L2's specification, each harmonic span carries exactly one tonality in the principal
reading. A chord an analyst reads in two tonalities at once (a pivot) is published as one span with one
tonality, and the other tonality's reading of the same span as a rival with its mass. **L2-S19 stands as
derived; its status "Open (OQ-L2-5 ★)" closes. OQ-L2-5 is answered: the charter's "the tonality" per span
is singular**, and a pivot is expressed as rivals. No charter sentence is amended. **L2-S16 stands as
derived**, untouched by this ruling.

**Carried, UNSETTLED.** Where a consumer wants the label "this chord is in both tonalities", it is the
decided chord re-expressed in the neighbouring span's tonality (the surface's reading). Whether and where
that label is published — at L3, or as a display view — is **not decided by this ruling** and is carried
to L3's own specification. L3's charter list is not amended.

**For the application act (the surface's reading, not ruled separately).** L2-S19's falsifier, "RESIDUAL
until ruled", becomes the code falsifier L2-S16 already states: "falsified if two tonalities are published
for one span as its principal reading."

**The ground, as put and as ruled (surface §5).** Towards the objective (#4): the two published analyses of
exemplar 2's piece disagree on whether bar 41 is a pivot (relayed from the derivation), so the rival pair
with its mass carries what the music supports — the change may fall on either side of that chord — and
nothing about the chord itself is lost (the surface's reading). Towards the principles: #12, the second
label is recomputable from what L2 publishes (the surface's reading, under #12's recomputable clause); #6
and the charter's own chord-symbol reasoning, a derivable label is a view and not a second decision; no
charter amendment. Meta level: the analysis's own specification.

**The cost, carried, as the surface stated it.** A consumer that wants "this chord is a hinge" as one fact
must compute it, and the masses of the two rivals express "either", not "both" (L2-S19's own premise). Which
chord an analyst names the pivot is a judgment a fixed rule may not reproduce (the surface's reading).

**Declined: position two** — a span may carry two tonalities in the principal reading: three charter
sentences and L2-S16 amended, a third kind of reading to score, fit and grade, its weight learned from the
traditions that write pivots, with no public deposit of one pivot source recorded by the census (relayed,
Row 11.52) (#21); and a second home for a derivable label (#6). **Declined: position three** — position one
plus L3's charter list amended now: an amendment of L3's charter from an L2 surface, before L3's own
derivation exists, out of the route's order (the surface's reading), with the rule that would pick the
pivot chord derived nowhere on the record.
