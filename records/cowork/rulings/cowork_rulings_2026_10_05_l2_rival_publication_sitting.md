## 1. Ruling 1 — Position 3, with the cut-off graded continuously across effort settings

**The ruling.** At the top effort setting the chord-and-key layer withholds no span-rival from publication.
Below the top, withholding is allowed only as an effort setting, and **the depth of the cut-off is continuous,
not on/off: different effort settings cut off at different depths** (the user's refinement). Wherever a
setting withholds, L2-S42's safeguards hold: a withheld rival is withheld only because it is recomputable, and
the cut-off and the withheld weight per span are published with the rivals. The ranked list of whole
readings is always bounded, because it cannot in practice be complete beyond the shortest passages (D-297:
never computing is not loss), and its length is likewise an effort setting.

**L2-S42 is to be amended** to read, as this side's wording of the ruling for the application act: "At the
top effort setting L2 withholds no span-rival from publication. Below it, the effort setting sets how deep
the cut-off lies, continuously across settings: a span-rival below the cut-off may be withheld only because it
is recomputable, and the cut-off and the withheld total mass per span are published with the rivals. The
ranked list of whole readings is always bounded, and its length is an effort setting." **L2-S41's clause
"down to the threshold of L2-S42"** is to read "down to the bound the effort setting sets". The cut-off
values and the defaults of each setting are not ruled here: they are decided at the effort setting's build
(D-202 stays DEFERRED; the sizing's §C).

**The ground, as put (surface §4).** Towards the objective (#4): no position changes a reading; the top
setting gives the next layer and the display everything. Towards the principles: the top setting carries the
2026-07-26 amendment (Row 49.23(i)), D-006, D-425, D-295 and the user's word in the silence sitting that the
display decides what to show; a reduction is the user's setting, not a design constant (D-035; the user's
sizing word on effort entry points), with #12's safeguards; D-200 is kept, since no lower setting need be
built before size proves a problem. Meta level: the analysis's own specification only. **Declined:**
Position 1 (a fixed measured cut-off in the design: against the amendment's wording and its reason, against
D-200's order, D-099's narrower wording not plainly met); Position 2 (no reduction at any setting: no entry
point for the output's size, against D-035 on this side's reading).
