## 1. Ruling 1 — Position B RATIFIED, with the 13 row placements (the user's word: "yes")

**Where a named progression appears in the music with one of its chords replaced by a substitute, L3 recognises it as
that progression, matched through the catalog's substitution mappings under the same exact test as any match (members
in order and contiguously, nothing skipped or inserted), and the published match states, beside the entry's name and the
stretch of decided chords, which member was filled by a substitute and by which operation. L2's chord and Roman numeral
are unchanged.** This meets the point key Ruling 1
(`records/cowork/rulings/cowork_rulings_2026_11_06_l3_catalog_key_sitting.md`) left open: *"whether recognised
substitutions are part of the fact (left to the family's substitution rows)"*.

**Algorithm: a change.** The method that stood: L3 publishes, per match, the entry's name and the stretch of decided
chords it covers, entries matched as written, with whether substitutions are part of the fact left open. B adds matches
made through a substitution mapping and, in each, the substituted member and its operation. No change to L2: L2 decides
key and chord together, its progression term a table of chord-to-chord pairs; DP-O stays open with the catalog term an
unmeasured candidate (key Ruling 2). Where the application act writes it is that act's (surface §1.3, marked as this
side's reading: with key Ruling 1's amendment to the L3 *Publishes* line and §3.2).

**Grounds, as put (surface §1.3):** the L3 charter, *"it may not revise L2"* (`FRAMEWORK.md`, L3 block); L2-S3 to L2-S5
and the L2 chord-vocabulary Ruling 1 (the replaced and replacing chords are L2's to decide); key Ruling 1's own grounds
(the user's point 3; the fact-publication corollary); D-506's two halves read for L3 — the numeral never rewritten
follows from the charter, the substitution recorded in the annotation is the published match — derived, not carried
over as a register decision.

**Not decided, named as waiting (surface §1.3):** which substitution mappings the catalog holds (the catalog's final
content); exact or tolerant matching — an operation that inserts a chord rather than replaces one would be a tolerance
(this side's reading); the generative slots (Rows 57.392 (ii), 57.434 (ii), 57.402); line-defined entries (Row 57.490
(i)); the carrier form; the composition direction (the chord-suggestion-tool surface).
