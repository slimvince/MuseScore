## 3b. Ruling 3 — Decision 2 (OQ-L3-6 ★): L3 works out form-function names and publishes them, as a confirmation

Put by the sitting that booted on entry 498. **The user's word: "yes".**

**How it was put.** Decision 2 was re-presented self-contained from the sources, as A (no form-function names, L3-S24
as written, recommended) against B (names published). The user asked whether not exposing such inferred judgements
violates the rule that nothing is kept from other layers, and whether all inference should be exposed regardless of
need. This side answered from D-295 and D-100 (below) that publishing is not a separate choice once a name is worked
out, and that the real question is whether L3 works names out at all. The user then asked why this side suggested not
naming at all — *"What harm is there in exposing such names?"* — on his reading that the names are useful to the end
user now, perhaps not yet for the inference. Reconsidered: A's grounds were the charter's list and the brief's
reservation of L3's *what* to the user — a procedural bound on the blind derivation, not a harm; the other harms named
(theories of form differ; no ground truth held; a user trusting a wrong label; build cost) are met by rivals with
their mass, establishment status, and the verifiability contract, or are build costs. **The alternatives collapsed to
B and it was put as a confirmation**, after a check turn (seven corrections, below), alone, plain text, no widget,
stating that it changes the algorithm and also files documentation, and stating the currently decided method.

**As ruled:** L3 works out, for each section at every level of the nesting, a form-function name where the chosen
naming scheme has one (e.g. *exposition*, *second theme*, *development*, *recapitulation*), "no name" being a possible
reading; each name is published with its rival names and their mass, carrying its establishment status — UNESTABLISHED
until measured against a ground truth.

**Declined:** A — no form-function names (L3-S24 as written).

**Grounds as put:** towards the principles — **D-295** (`DECISIONS.md` line 509, LIVE, 2026-08-02 · user): *"Zero
information loss to the end user - every inferred object must be displayable"*; **D-100** (line 267, LIVE): *"Every
derived fact is published exactly once, on the producing layer's output surface"* — its reading *"even where no
consumer is named"* ruled for L2 (`cowork_rulings_2026_10_06_l2_publication_to_next_layer_sitting.md` Ruling 1), applied
to L3 as this side's reading; the verifiability contract (Row 8.113's quoted text: *"NOT disqualified for lack of an
oracle"*). End-user usefulness enters through D-295, since `CLAUDE.md` (line 267) chooses designs *"from the principles
and the ultimate objective … alone"*. Towards the ultimate objective (best possible inference, #4): a name may move a
boundary in an analyst's reading (exemplar 1's phrase at bar 13 against exemplar 2's *second theme* at bar 14, L3-S24's
defense) — a possibility, not load-bearing; under Ruling 6 of the 2026-10-07 record it carries load only once
measured. Meta level: the analysis itself.

**Algorithmic change, decided:** sections gain published form-function names with rivals and mass, as above.
**Documentation:** the charter's L3 *Publishes* list to gain form-function names (an amendment of `FRAMEWORK.md` §5, L3,
by the application act — not edited here); L3-S24 to be rewritten accordingly; OQ-L3-6 closed. **Rows 8.23, 8.35(i) and

(L3-S24)"*, no longer stands as proposed, and they are **held, travelling with the naming-scheme question**. The
