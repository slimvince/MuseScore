## 1. Ruling 1 — Decision 1 (OQ-L2-14): Position B, a third kind in the confidence contract

**The ruling.** A span-rival's weight (the derivation's *mass*, L2-S40) is declared to be neither a decision
margin (Class M) nor a calibrated probability (Class P), but a **third kind: the model's own distribution** —
each value a share in [0,1], the shares of one stretch's rivals summing to at most one, not calibrated,
compared only within one working span's publication, never read as a frequency of being right, and turned
into a calibrated probability only through a fitted reliability map.

**What it amends, for the application act (wording not ruled here):** D-267 at its home
(`cowork_confidence_contract.md` §2: "exactly one" of two classes becomes one of three); the confidence
sentence of `FRAMEWORK.md` §7 and behavioural statement B9; and L2-S40's defense, which leaned to Class M.
U1 to U5 of the contract are unchanged. D-267's register status, and D-425's, are settled at the ratification
act, not here.

**The ground, as put (surface §3).** Towards the objective (#4): no reading changes; a consumer is told in
the contract what the weight is. Towards the principles: #10 (the name is true); it carries the words of the
option D-425 adopted — "model probabilities … NOT calibrated" — and does what that option said the contract
owed ("the #19 guard … must be explicit in the contract"). Meta level: the specification and the
cross-cutting confidence contract. **The strongest argument against, as put:** it changes a cross-cutting
contract from inside one layer's family before the other layers that publish weights are derived (Position D
would make the change once with all in view). **Declined:** Position A (declare it Class M: untrue of the
thing, #10, and opens the over-reading D-425 warned of); Position C (keep D-267, add a whole-sequence margin:
two numbers on one decision, #6, unsized cost, and a new reading of FRAMEWORK §7/B9 still needed); Position D
(defer the naming: L2 ratified knowing its weights fit neither kind, #10).

**Consequence for confirmation 5.2 (surface §5.2):** with Position C declined, Rows 7.5(i), 52.11(i) and
7.112(i) stay proposed HISTORICAL as the surface states, to be confirmed in their own turn.

## 1b. Ruling 2 — Decision 2: Position B, a cut-off never hides a whole chord root

side's wording for the application act; not ruled here).
