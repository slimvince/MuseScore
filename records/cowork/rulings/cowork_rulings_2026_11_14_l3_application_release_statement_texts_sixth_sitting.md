## What Ruling 17 decides

**Documentation only. No algorithmic change is decided by Ruling 17.** It fixes the text the successor carries for a
statement whose substance the figured-bass record's Ruling 1 decided on 2026-11-03; that ruling stands unchanged.

**The currently decided method, as stated with the question:** L3 publishes the span figure of L3-S25 (one figure per
chord over the structural bass, plus the suspension and appoggiatura figures), the full figure published and short
forms views (L3-S27), accidentals against the key signature (L3-S26); and, by the figured-bass record's Ruling 1, L1
publishes at every slice the spelled intervals above its lowest sounding pitch, and L3 publishes a second figured bass
at the continuo grain, read off that publication, named apart from the span figure, giving under every bass note,
passing bass notes included, what actually sounds above it, by the same two rules; L3 does not compute that interval
content itself; the composer's figures are never input (L3-S29).

   The figured-bass record's Ruling 1 does not state it; the ratified Status says so; no grain is chosen.

   note *"carries no continuo-grain figure"*; neither the figured-bass record's Ruling 1 nor the ratified text says what
   the figure is when nothing sounds above the bass note. Noticed by this sitting at its boot read; it is not written at
   the Part 5 surface, which this sitting did not edit.
3. **The Defense's kept sentence *"S-50 is exhaustive"*,** against IC-L1 S-50 gaining an item under the same Ruling 1:
   not weighed, IC-L1 and its Ruling 60 not read by this sitting or, as the surface says, by the sitting that wrote it.
