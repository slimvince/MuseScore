## 1. Ruling 1 — L2-S29 as derived: both readings carried as rivals; DP-N stays open; the own-category reading excluded by name

**The ruling.** For L2's specification, the cadential six-four is admitted under the two readings the
derivation names — a tonic chord in second inversion (`I6/4`), and a dominant with the sixth and fourth
above its bass as suspension-class elaborations (`V(64)`) — and the two are carried as rivals, the
decision publishing the one the evidence favours with the other as its rival. **No theory is principal.
The charter's DP-N stays open exactly as ratified; no charter text is amended.** **L2-S29 stands as
derived.**

**The named exclusion, under L2-S2.** The third candidate DP-N lists — the six-four as its own category
(`Cad64`) — is **not a state** in the first specification. This ruling is the named exclusion L2-S2
requires of a reading type an analyst writes, so that L2-S2 is met whether or not a grading corpus turns
out to write `Cad64` (whether one does is unsettled; surface §3). **Its ceiling cost, as stated:** a
ground-truth `Cad64` is matched only if the grading reads it as one of the two rivals; the cost is not
counted.

**The ground, as put and as ruled (surface §5).** Towards the objective (#4): both readings that differ in
how the notes are assigned — chord tones under `I6/4`, suspensions that must resolve under `V(64)` — are
reachable and published with their weight. Towards the principles: #1 and the framework's R-6 (no theory
chosen on no evidence; the literature pass found nothing to choose by); #12 (neither of the two dropped);
#21 (the annotators' disagreement kept as a fact about the ground truth, not fitted away); L2-S2 (met by
the named exclusion); #6 (no second state that differs from `I6/4` only by its label). Meta level: the
analysis's own specification only.

**The costs, carried.** The committed label can differ from a given corpus's convention; whether that
counts against the analysis is a grading question for measurement design (the graded-distance mitigation
the reading pass relays, and D-352's bar for tonality that counts any recorded reading, are there to draw
on). L3's cadence reading must handle a committed `I6/4`. The ceiling cost of excluding `Cad64` is
uncounted.

**Declined: position two** — DP-N settled for the dominant reading: a theory chosen where the framework
says that would be "on no evidence" (#1, R-6); "principal" would mean either a hand-set preference or a
conversion of the ground truth that erases the annotators' disagreement (#12, #21); the charter amended.
**Declined: position three** — `Cad64` admitted as a third rival: a state differing only by label, so the
fit would learn the annotators' convention rather than the music (#21); two states for one chord (#6);
the state grows (OQ-L2-8 ★); the charter amended.

**What it does not decide.** How a `Cad64` annotation converts into L2's four fields; whether any grading
corpus writes `Cad64`; whether the grading accepts either rival; which reading each grading corpus uses,
piece by piece (the framework's §S9 settling route for DP-N). All measurement design.
