## 1. Ruling 1 — L3-S14, L3-S41 and L3-S42 RATIFIED, with their rows placed whole

**The statements, as ratified (the derivation's wording, unchanged; quoted here from the first cut's §1, which quotes
the derivation — the derivation itself not opened by this sitting):**
- **L3-S14:** *"Every weight of the phrase model is fitted from annotated phrase boundaries, held out by piece, with its
  capacity declared before the fit, on licence-clear music only. No value is given here."*
- **L3-S41:** *"L3 is deterministic. Its publication after any sequence of enlargements, or after an edit, equals a
  single fresh run over the final span and the edited record."*
- **L3-S42:** *"Two dependencies on out-of-scope subjects are stated as statements in their own right. (i) The
  voice-leading axis. L3's phrase is the harmonic spine's phrase, which tiles. The accepted melodic phrase, which
  overlaps across voices, is the axis's, and L3 reads no fact of the axis in this specification. (ii) The measurement
  of the analysis. How an annotation's phrase signs, cadence labels, gaps and elisions are mapped onto L3's tiling and
  its features for grading is the measurement-design stage's (OQ-L3-12)."*

## 2. Documentation and the algorithm, separately

- **Documentation only.** L3-S14, L3-S41 and L3-S42 are ratified. Row 37.18 is to be marked HISTORICAL in the reading
  file, and the relocated rows the reading file's §10 transfer list carries leave it row by row, both at the application
  dispatch, not now.
- **Algorithm: no change.** The currently decided method, confirmed: L3's phrase model has no hand-set numbers; every
  number is to be fitted from licence-clear music with marked phrase boundaries, held out by piece, with a capacity
  declared first, and until that fit exists the model is provisional. L3 is deterministic: widening the selection or
  editing the score gives the same result as one fresh run over the final music. L3's phrases cut across all the voices
  and follow one another without gaps or overlaps; L3 reads nothing from the voice-leading side; thematic restatement
  comes from L1; grading against annotations is the measurement stage's.
