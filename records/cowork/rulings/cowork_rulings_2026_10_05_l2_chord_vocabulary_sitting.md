## 1. Ruling 1 — every listed chord kind is its own state; rarity is handled in the estimates (question 1)

**The user's word, verbatim: "one".**

**The ruling.** For L2's specification, every chord kind the vocabulary lists is its own state, choosable
and publishable however rare — the German, French and Italian sixths each, an applied chain such as
V7/IV/III. Where a kind's count is below the capacity threshold, its **table estimates** borrow from its
declared parent class (D-271 item 2's first sentence, which already does this for every table cell), and
D-533 keeps a rare continuation from scoring as impossible. **D-271's last sentence of item 2 — "The degree
vocabulary's rare-class pooling (factorization §1) is the same rule applied to the state space itself" —
does not carry into L2's specification.** D-271's register status is settled at the ratification act, not
here (the shape the second family used for D-385 and the third for D-333). **L2-S2, L2-S4 and L2-S5 stand
as derived**; L2-S4's and L2-S5's "Open … (OQ-L2-1)" stay open, the measurement unchanged.

**The ground, as put and as ruled (surface §5).** Towards the objective (#4): no listed chord kind is lost
to rarity; the augmented sixths are told apart by their notes, so the decision can choose between them with
borrowed transition estimates (the surface's reading); the fullest-label rows proposed for carrying (5.41,
5.47) become reachable. Towards the principles: #12 (the state merge is a collapse that cannot be got back
from what is kept — the display's re-derivation of the sub-type is the evidence); the fact-publication
corollary and #6 (one home for the chord fact, no consumer re-deriving it); #20 and D-271's own ground (no
overfitting on a 326-piece corpus), kept in the estimates; D-329's principle (complete admission by default,
any prune ratified), met. Meta level: the analysis's own specification.

**The costs, carried, as the surface stated them.** One sentence of a rule the user ratified is set aside
for L2; the state is larger, which adds to the tractability question OQ-L2-8 ★ (its own surface); a rare
kind's precision is bounded by its parent's estimates until more annotated music exists, a bound stated
(#24).

**Declined: position two** — the state merge kept as a named exclusion with its ceiling cost: #12's loss and
the display's re-derivation stay (#6, the corollary); the ceiling cost is uncounted. **Declined: position
three** — the incumbent's counted vocabulary (Row 10.10), L2-S2 amended: a kind absent from the training
corpus is unreachable on new music; L2-S2's closure test is given up, and with it, on the surface's reading,
D-329's complete-by-default principle.

**What it does not decide.** The vocabulary's completeness and the applied chain's maximum depth (OQ-L2-1, a
measurement); where in the phases that measurement runs; the declared parent classes themselves (the fit's
detail); tractability (OQ-L2-8 ★).

## 1a. Ruling 2 — a sounding ninth, eleventh or thirteenth is an elaboration in the first specification; the extended chords enter by a named increment (question 2)

**The user's words, verbatim: "one (and we know this might change when we increment with jazz)".**

**The ruling.** For the first L2 specification, the chord vocabulary carries no added-note classes: a
sounding ninth, eleventh or thirteenth over a chord is an **elaboration assignment**, with its named relation
or the unclassified elaboration (the second family's Ruling 1). Whether an added-sixth class is needed is
decided by the closure measurement (OQ-L2-1). **The extended chords that jazz treats as chord members — the
ninth, eleventh and thirteenth chords with their altered forms (♭9, ♯9, ♯11, ♭13), and the power chord
D-334 keeps — are written into that specification as a named increment, in the shape of the 2026-10-04 mode
ruling:** theory-built tables whose source is read on our side (#1); a premise-ledger entry with the
near-neighbour hazard enumerated (a ninth chord against a seventh chord with a ninth suspension, #17e); a
desk simulation (#17c); a grading convention; held-out jazz ground truth for the evaluation (#20, D-500).
**Its trigger is the premise-ledger entry**, so it may land with a declared bound before jazz ground truth
exists; D-526's "jazz-specific classes enter only under the OI-7 jazz-ground-truth gate" is read as gating
the increment's measurement rather than its landing (the surface's reading, put with the position and not
separately ruled). **L2-S4 stands as derived.**

**The user's qualification, recorded as he gave it:** this ruling may change when the jazz increment is
taken up. *This side's reading of it:* the first specification's choice is not offered as the final
vocabulary; the increment's own act is where it is revisited, with whatever its premise ledger and desk
simulation show — no ruling now binds that act's outcome.

**The ground, as put and as ruled (surface §6).** Towards the objective (#4): on the graded repertoire the
chord matches the annotation's chord (`iv7`, not `iv9`) and the ninth keeps its relation; jazz extensions
get a named route. Towards the principles: #12 (no note dropped; each carries its assignment); #6 (one
vocabulary, the ruled relations reused); #17 and #8 (no extended state before its premise ledger); #14 (the
increment its own ratified change); the user's 2026-10-04 principle (sequenced, not excluded for lack of
ground truth). Meta level: the analysis's own specification.

**The costs, carried.** Until the increment lands, a jazz `Cmaj9` reads as `Cmaj7` with the ninth as an
elaboration, most likely the unclassified one (the surface's reading). Row 21.77's rule is narrowed for L2:
everything found is stated, as chord plus assignments rather than in the chord's name.

**Declined: position two** — added notes as chord members now: extended states before a premise ledger
(#17); a ninth readable two ways (#6); on the graded repertoire the chord's name departs from the
annotation's. **Declined: position three** — both readings as rivals now: #17 as in position two, and the
state grows (OQ-L2-8).
