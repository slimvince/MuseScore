## 2. Ruling 1 — OQ-L3-10 ★: A CONFIRMED, L3 may ask for more music, as L2 does

**As ruled:** L3 may ask the caller for more music where its in-selection phrase or section reading depends on music
beyond the edge; it stops when its own in-selection phrases and sections stop changing between successive
enlargements, under the same safety cap (D-624), and marks its publication truncated if the cap ends the loop (D-263).
Units cut by the edge carry their mark (settled already in L3-S40).

**Declined:** B — L3 never asks, reads only the span it was given and marks every unit the edge cuts; C — L3 tags the
edge and the pipeline driver decides (the outgoing design, Rows 8.64, 8.65, 54.14, 54.36).

**Grounds as put:** towards the principles — D-261 applied to L3 in its own words (*"the layer's in-selection output"*
read as L3's phrases and sections); the requester rule [relayed]; L2's ruled method as the precedent. B falls on this
side's reading of D-261 (L3's amount of context not discovered from L3's own output); C is either A with the loop held
elsewhere or a stop test not taken on L3's output. Cost: section readings can need much music (exemplar 2's
recapitulation at bar 42 recognised against an exposition 41 bars earlier, derivation line 1143, FACT at the file),
against D-030; bounded by D-624 and marked by D-263. **The point named for the user to judge:** under A, L3's request
makes L2 read more music, so L2's in-selection reading can change because L3 asked; no L3 fact passes to L2, and L2's
result depends only on the music loaded (L2-S48) — on this side's reading, not engaging the condition attached to "L3 →
L2: Nothing". Meta level: the analysis itself.

**(b) Carried as an open point with the ruling, not decided by it:** what exactly L3 reads of the further music, given
that L2's publication covers exactly its working span and L3 receives no L2 reading of the context (L3-S40's defense),
and how an enlargement requested by L3 relates the working span to the selection whose output D-260 fixes. Not spelled
out in anything this sitting read; to be settled by the application act or put to the user later.

**Algorithmic change, decided:** L3 gains an enlargement loop with convergence on its in-selection publication, the
safety cap and the truncation mark.

**Documentation:** L3-S40's status moves from Open to Settled; OQ-L3-10 closed. Recorded as decided against: the
outgoing texts' "the grouping layer only tags the edge and never acts on the tag" — Rows 8.64, 8.65, 54.14(ii), 54.36(i),
