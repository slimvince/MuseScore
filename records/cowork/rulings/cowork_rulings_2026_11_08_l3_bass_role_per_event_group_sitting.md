## 1. Ruling 1 — Row 48.15: B, L3 publishes the bass role per slice, named apart from the structural bass

- **Row 48.15** (the outgoing sentence, `cowork_notation_output_contract.md` §3.2: *"the bass chord-factor role per
  event (from the published per-event bass facts vs the members)"*; axis THE DERIVATION IS SILENT; proposed UNPLACED) —
  **ADOPTED — proposed**, with this sentence as the proposal: **L3 publishes, per slice, which member of L2's chord the
  lowest sounding note is, or that it is not a chord tone (with L2's elaboration relation for it where L2 has one), read
  off L1's lowest sounding pitch per slice and its witness note and L2's chord and chord-tone assignment. It is named
  apart from the chord symbol's bass note (L3-S4), which stays the structural bass.** Whether the lowest note is a chord
  tone is L2's chord-tone assignment, not a pitch-class match (surface §2, check item 5). Twin: L2 Row 49.17 (i),
  RELOCATED to *L3 — The read-off facts* by L2 chord-as-degree Ruling 1.
- **Declined:** A (no layer publishes it; filed outside every charter for a display's own specification). Not proposed
  and not taken: RELOCATED to L2; HISTORICAL; recorded as decided against by L3-S4; the variant of carrying the role as
  a field of figured-bass Ruling 1's continuo-grain output (surface §3 B).
- **The grounds as put (surface §3, as corrected):** one producer (D-100); decision-free (surface §2); the twin's ruled
  home at L3's read-off facts; the same shape as the user's figured-bass Ruling 1 "B"; under the user's harm test
  (*"What harm is there in exposing such names?"*, as the figured-bass record quotes entry 499), the only harm this side
  found, cost aside, is a reader mistaking the per-slice bass for the structural bass, which naming them apart reduces.
  A's grounds were cost and D-297.

**Documentation:** Row 48.15 recorded ADOPTED — proposed, as above. At the application act (route record §2 step 2),
the row is re-marked, and the L3 charter's *Publishes* list (`FRAMEWORK.md` §5) and the amended L3 specification gain
the item, a new L3 statement being written there; the former wordings kept (#12).
**Algorithm: changed, as specified by the analysis.** L3 gains one output. Nothing L2 decides changes: the output reads
L1's and L2's facts and writes none; no inference changes. The chord symbol's bass stays the structural bass (L3-S4).

**The method now decided, as it bears on this row:** L1 publishes, per slice, the lowest sounding pitch with its witness
note, and the spelled intervals above it (both ruled, not yet applied). L2 publishes per span the chord (degree,
quality, figure, applied target) in its tonality, and per sounding note the chord-tone assignment and, where the
reading has one, the elaboration relation. L3 publishes the chord symbol whose bass note is the structural bass L2's
figure names, not the lowest sounding pitch of any slice (L3-S4); the span figured bass over that structural bass; the
continuo-grain figured bass under every lowest note, passing notes included; **and, by this ruling, per slice, which
member of L2's chord the lowest sounding note is, or that it is not a chord tone, named apart from the structural
bass.** L3 never re-derives a fact a lower layer publishes (L3-S39).

**Not decided by this ruling:** the output's name and its exact form; whether the charter amendment is worded as its own
