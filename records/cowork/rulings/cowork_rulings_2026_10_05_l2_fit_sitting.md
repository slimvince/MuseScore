## 1. Ruling 1 — Position 2: the tables counted once and frozen; the short list of weights fitted to the graded measure

**The ruling.** The term tables of L2's candidate score are counted from annotated music, once, and frozen;
the short list of combination weights over them is fitted discriminatively, **to the graded measure**.
D-525's staging stands (each table established on its own; the combination-weight list within D-271's
budget); D-525's own recorded fallback — the direct-metric few-weight search, the minimum-error-rate
protocol — becomes the main route, and a likelihood fit may still serve as its starting point. **L2-S38's
second sentence is to be amended** to: "The tables are counted from annotated music, once, and frozen; the
short list of combination weights is fitted discriminatively, to the graded measure." Every other sentence
of L2-S38 stands. **DP-P is answered for L2's detail specification by this ruling.** Joint learning of every
number (Position 3, D-525's recorded excluded alternative) is not adopted; D-525's re-test condition for it
stands as D-525 records it.

**The ground, as put (surface §3).** Towards the objective (#4): the learned numbers aim at what is graded —
the one measured comparison on record (DP-P; a published study, not a measurement on this project's music)
found likelihood-fitted combination weights at 12.2 against 19.6 when fitted to the wanted measure — while
every table keeps its musical meaning (D-534's counted chord-factor costs). Towards the principles: #19
(each table established on its own), #20 (D-271's budget kept), #3 and #13 (a wrong result traceable to one
table), #1 (L2-S38 names minimum error-rate training; D-525's fallback is that protocol). Meta level: the
analysis's own specification only.

**Declined: Position 1** — D-525 unchanged, weights by likelihood with the graded-measure search as fallback
only: aims the weights at a quantity other than the one graded. **Declined: Position 3** — every number
learned jointly against the graded measure: loses traceability to one table (#3, #19), requires D-271's
budget to be rewritten (#22; the surface's reading), turns the counted tables into free numbers, and
reverses D-525's recorded exclusion.

## 1b. Ruling 2 — the old fixed evidence ranking (D-383) and the old fitter's exemption do not carry into L2

**How it was put.** After Ruling 1 was recorded and reported, the user replied "continue"; the surface's §4.1
and §4.2 were then put together as one confirmation, re-explained from scratch, no widget, with the word
asked for. **The user's word, verbatim: "confirmed".**

**The ruling.** (i) D-383's fixed ranking — bass, spelling and key-consistency "decide", a licensed
progression "only breaks ties" and "may never override a committed root" — **does not carry into L2's
specification**, neither as a rule nor as a constraint on the fitted weights. Its measured finding (the
progression-driven override moving a correct root to a wrong one on about 78 % of its fires) is kept as
evidence, already set aside for the audit at Row 8.74(i). D-383's register status is settled at the
ratification act, not here (the shape used for D-333 and D-385). (ii) The old fitter's exemption for "any
value whose Phase-0 row names a ratified structural rationale" (Row 9.160(iii)) **does not carry**: it
governed the legacy scorer's hand-set constants, and L2 has none.

**The ground, as put (surface §4.1, §4.2).** D-383's object — selection over a committed root by a later
layer — does not exist in the one decision the ratified charter defines; D-383 itself fixed only "the
ordering/direction" and left "the channel weights and the deciding margin" to be learned, and under Ruling 1
the weights are learned against the graded measure, so an unhelpful progression term takes a small weight by
measurement; the third family's Ruling 1 set aside D-333's fixed rule for weighed evidence on the same
ground. A ranking written in as a constraint would be a number set by hand (L2-S38), and sits uneasily with
the charter's "a reading-shaped evidence producer is a score and never a constraint" (its reach to a ranking
being the surface's reading). Meta level: the analysis's own specification only.

**The placements, Ruling 2.** **HISTORICAL**, placed from UNPLACED: Rows 8.74(ii), 8.77, 8.78(ii), 8.82,
9.332(i) (under L2-S38), and Row 8.81 (L2-S31 only, outside this family's 138 parts, travelling with 8.77);
