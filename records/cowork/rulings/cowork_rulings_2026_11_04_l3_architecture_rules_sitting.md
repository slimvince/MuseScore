  never in its structure. Ground: **D-024** (line 254, LIVE), Row 19.33 in its home (line 25430). **Not decided:** which

- **C (5 row-parts: 19.56 (iii), 19.76 (ii), 19.77, 19.78, 19.82) — ADOPTED.** L3 reads, within its scope, all the
  information the layers beneath it publish without loss and discards none of it; a reading L3 rules out need not be

  an L3 fact no consumer reads is either declared dormant with its future consumer named, or removed, evidence-class
  facts being published broadly. Grounds: **D-033**, **D-099**, **D-297** (LIVE; Rows 19.56, 19.76, 19.77, 19.78 in their
  homes, lines 25432–25433); for Row 19.82, which no register entry is homed at, consistency with **D-100**, **D-581** and
  D-297, a display being a reader (D-295, D-522).
- **D (1 row-part: 20.7) — ADOPTED.** The code computing L3's facts depends on no type of the score editor. Grounds:
  **D-229** (line 491, LIVE), Row 20.7 in its home (line 27106); **D-072** (line 488, LIVE).
- **E (2 row-parts: 38.47, 38.48) — ADOPTED in the narrower wording.** *"A reading missing from L3's own candidate
  readings is remedied inside L3, by widening its candidate set; a backward edge is not the remedy, except as the
