## Ruling 1 — Decision 1 (the surface's §A.4.1): the old recogniser's harmonic sequences fed back to the key decision

**Put:** how to file the 9 parts — Rows 4.20(ii), 5.344(ii), 57.6(iii), 53.25, 53.48, 53.50(ii), 53.52,
53.53(i), 53.53(ii) — in which a separate recogniser of named progressions, working over chords already
decided, hands each recognised harmonic sequence back to the key decision (D-494's sequence channel, D-504,
D-505). Alternative A: HISTORICAL, the idea carried as a named remedy for DP-O and measurement. Alternative B:
held UNPLACED, travelling with DP-O. Recommended: A.

**Corrected before the question was put, on the user's objection.** The question was first framed in this
sitting's conversation as "should a recognised harmonic sequence count as evidence that the key is
changing". The user: *"this sentence has hyperbole and absolutes. Of course a recognized harmonic sequence
COULD influence our mode inference."* The framing was wrong on two counts: the decision never concerned
whether the musical idea is admissible, only how the nine passages describing the closed feedback route are
filed; and a sequence is not by itself evidence of a change of key — a diatonic sequence stays in one key —
so it is evidence bearing on key and mode, in either direction. Alternative A's trigger was widened
accordingly before the question was put (below). The surface's §A.4.1 heading, its point 3 and Alternative
A's trigger carried the same framing and are corrected at their sites, former wordings kept (#12).

**The user's word:** "A".

**What is ruled:**
- The 9 parts: **HISTORICAL** — a superseded plan. What is superseded is the route: a recogniser over decided
  chords feeding its output back to revise the key, which the ratified contract table ("L3 → L2: Nothing")
  closes.
- **Carried, not dropped (#12):** the musical idea — a harmonic sequence found in the chords L2 itself
  proposes, read as evidence about key and mode. If the measurement-design stage finds L2's key or mode
  reading falling short on sequential passages (D-494 names its measurement bed, a resolution-denying
  repertoire), a term that rates transposed repetition in the proposed chords is the named remedy to try.
  Such a term reads beyond adjacent pairs, so it belongs to DP-O and is taken up there. Whoever takes up DP-O
  reads the surface's §A.4.1 and this ruling.
- Declined: Alternative B (held UNPLACED, travelling with DP-O); adding a sequence term to L2 now (not
  offered as an alternative: it would settle DP-O from this family's surface, and its premise, that L2 fails
  on such passages, is unchecked, #18, D-254, D-630).
- The register status of D-494 (its sequence half), D-504 and D-505 is settled at the ratification act, not
  here. L2-S49's wording does not change.

**Ground, as put (the surface's §A.4.1, as corrected):** the charter and its contract table close the route;
the cadence-gate problem D-494 was written against does not arise in L2 (L2-S34 Ruling 2 placed "no key
change without a cadence" HISTORICAL); D-494's other channel, sustained dominant emphasis, is carried under
L2-S20; L2-S31 scores a reading only by single stretches and adjacent pairs, and whether L2 needs longer
patterns is DP-O, open and not foreclosed; D-599; D-254 and #18; D-630; #12. The same shape as L2-S31 Ruling 1
(D-616) and L2-S34 Ruling 1 (D-341). Meta level: the analysis's own specification only.

## A note for whoever takes up DP-O — recorded on the user's instruction

**The user's words:** "record that note since we do not yet know (without measurement) if pairs are better or
worse than longer chord sequences."

**The note.** L2-S31 (placed 2026-10-07 with its wording unchanged) has L2 score a reading by single spans and
adjacent pairs only, and is "falsified if some term reads three or more spans' fields". Its premise, that
pairwise dependence is enough, is unmeasured. `FRAMEWORK.md` DP-O asks only whether to adopt a hierarchy,
and its falsifier compares a tree model against a matched-capacity sequence model. **A middle option is
named by neither:** a flat chain whose terms may read three or more adjacent spans — a longer chord-to-chord
memory, a cadential-formula term, or the sequence term carried as the remedy under Ruling 1. **When DP-O is
taken up, the measurement compares pairs, longer flat terms and a hierarchy, on this repertoire's ground
truth, the same corpus and the same axis.** Whether pairs are better or worse than longer chord sequences is
not known without that measurement (D-254). Cost is not by itself a ground to keep pairs (D-200).

**Added by Ruling 4:** harmonic rhythm — whether a reading's chord changes speed up towards a likely cadence,
rated per competing reading inside L2 — is a further candidate longer-span term for the same measurement.

This note decides nothing: L2-S31's wording and placement are unchanged, and DP-O stays open. *(The defense
L2-S31 cites — that a decoded segment length buys "the expressive power of a high-order model" — is, in this
side's reading, about how long a chord lasts and not about chord-to-chord syntax.)*
