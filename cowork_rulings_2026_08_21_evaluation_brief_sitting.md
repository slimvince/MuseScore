**Ruled, with a correction of record about the putting of it.** The user's words say the question
was never open. **That is right, and the surface was defective in putting it:** the thirty-fifth
handover block already records the programme as *"two independent evaluations under one shared
neutral brief"*, and the brief's own §2 states blindness as a requirement. The brief's §8.1 listed
it as open; this session carried it onto the surface without checking it against the programme the
record already held. **Recorded as this session's third tell** (the first two being the two

**The ruling, for the avoidance of doubt:** neither evaluator is given `cc_report_plan_challenge.md`,
its dispatch, the two newest handover blocks, or the sealed prediction. An evaluator that meets a
prior verdict records where and how much, and stops reading that file.

## 2. Ruling 2 — both evaluators boot from the curated boot list; the dispatch for (a) departs from the handover-block clause, on this ruling's authority (Alternative A)

**Ruled.** `cowork_evaluation_boot_list_2026_08_21.md` is ruled **for this one use** — the two
evaluations under the brief — and, as its own banner states, ruling it for this use rules nothing
about the derivation sessions or about the curated boot list's standing definition.

**The departure, stated so it does not become precedent by drift.** The standing clause *"Every
dispatch's read-first block names the CURRENT HANDOVER BLOCK of `cowork_handoff.md`"*
(`cowork_audit_protocol.md`, dispatch-protocol section) binds every dispatch. The dispatch that
executes evaluation (a) does NOT send the executing session to that block, because the block
states the verdict under evaluation. **The clause's recorded ground is finding F58 — a
finding-number collision between the two sides — and the brief's §7 allocates no finding number,
so the ground the clause exists for is not engaged by this dispatch.** The departure is written in
the dispatch's read-first block in terms, citing this ruling; it reaches this one dispatch and no
other; the clause itself is not amended.

**Also ruled with it:** the second evaluator is a fresh Cowork session, on a model different from
the one that wrote the plan, opened with an instruction to read the boot list first and nothing
else. The session that held this sitting is NOT eligible — it has read the handover block, the
prediction and the refute-only dispatch.
