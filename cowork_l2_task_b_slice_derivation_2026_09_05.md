## 3. The test, quoted rather than paraphrased

A row is in L2's slice when its recorded reason names a method that is a candidate for a decision
inside L2's charter — L2's one sentence: *"what is the tonality at each moment, where does each
harmony give way to the next, which sounding notes belong to the harmony and which elaborate it, and
what chord is read over each span?"* (`FRAMEWORK.md` §5, L2), including what the charter leaves to
the detail specification — *"how the score over candidate readings is formed, what its terms are,
what tables they read, and how any weight is fitted"* — and its two fixed boundary conditions (the
coupling as a cost, the candidate-admission rule).

A row is in L3's slice when its reason names a method for what L3 publishes: *"the cadence at each
phrase end, with its type; the phrase and section grouping; the chord symbol; the figured bass; the
harmonic rhythm"* (§5, L3).

**Where a design point decides the row, its layer is read at §9:** DP-A (divide the deciding by
field), DP-B (tonality first, then chord), DP-C (segmentation with the chord identity), DP-D (the
chord-tone assignment "part of L2's one decision"), DP-E (tonality decided with the chords) and DP-P
("how are the terms of L2's score combined and fitted") are L2's; DP-O says in terms *"what chord
follows what is inside L2's charter, and a grammar is one way to answer it"*, so a DP-O row is L2's.
