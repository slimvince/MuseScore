## 1. How an entry enters, and what an entry must carry

**The route, and it has one shape.** A retrospective, a sitting or a session **PROPOSES**; the user
**RATIFIES**; the entry is then written here, citing the ruling record that carries the user's words.
Nothing is applied silently, and nothing enters this document on a session's own authority.

**Every RATIFIED entry carries, and an entry missing any of these is incomplete:**

- **the phase** it amends, or *all phases* where it is general;
- **the clause** amended, quoted from the surface rather than paraphrased, or **NEW** where nothing is
  being amended;
- **the amendment itself**, in its own words;
- **the ruling record** it comes from, and the **user's words**, verbatim;
- **the date**, established at the user's machine rather than asserted from a session's environment;
- **the scope** — one phase, or standing.

**Every PENDING entry carries** its proposed content or a pointer to where that content stands, its
origin, and the fact that it is **not ruled**. A pending entry binds nothing.
