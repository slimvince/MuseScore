## 2. The test

An extract's paper *bears on L2* when the extract's own bearing section touches something inside L2's
charter: L2's decision (tonality per span as tonic and mode; the segmentation; the chord per span;
the chord-tone assignment; the rivals with their mass), its detail specification (score formation,
terms, tables, fitting, the label and mode vocabulary the charter's publications need), or a design
point that §9 places in L2 — DP-A, DP-B, DP-C, DP-D, DP-E, DP-O, DP-P, and DP-K (what L2 publishes
where it cannot decide). A paper whose bearing section names only measurement design, corpus intake,
data representation for L3's chord-symbol view (DP-L), or nothing, does not bear on L2.

Two grades inside "bears", because the pack-build dispatch and the leak check will want to know which
is which: **direct** — the bearing is on L2's decision or a design point about how it is made;
**through what L2 publishes** — the bearing is on the rivals-with-mass shape (DP-K), the mode
vocabulary of the tonality, or an evidence representation an L2 detail specification could weigh.

## 5. Two observations for the pack-build dispatch, stated and not acted on

**(a) Ruling 2's second cut string does not match every heading.** Ruling 2 cuts *"the section headed
'Bearing on the framework…'"*. At the files, the heading takes these forms: `## Bearing on the
framework (first pass)` (the common form); `… and the ruled conventions (first pass)` (row 2);
`… — flagged, not decided (verdicts belong to Task 4)` (row 7); `… (first pass) — including one
verification-table consequence` (row 10); `… (first-pass reading; verdicts belong to the findings
surface)` (row 13); `… (first pass; verdicts belong to the findings surface)` (row 14); `… — NO
BEARING` (row 16); `… and the ruled measurement conventions (first pass)` (row 17). **Row 12's heading
is `## Bearing — flagged for the user and for any later verdict, NOT decided here` — it does not begin
"Bearing on the framework".** A prefix match on "Bearing on the framework" would miss row 12, which is
in the L2-bearing set. The dispatch's cut string must be written and verified against these headings,
as Ruling 2 already requires ("verified both ways on every render").

**(b) This project's own material appears outside the bearing sections of some extracts.** Read at
the files: row 7's status block, "Bibliographic by-catch" and "What this does and does not close"
name the reading pass's own records, rows and STOPs; row 12's "Identity" section quotes the decision
surface's recommendation about rows 6 and 7; row 16's status block names the population file's §5
STOP; row 10's "Verification targets touched" names V12's candidates. Whether any of that is a leak
for a blind deriving session is the leak check's question (Ruling 3's cross-reference additions and
leak check, "the leak list to him"); it is recorded here so the check is run knowing where to look.
