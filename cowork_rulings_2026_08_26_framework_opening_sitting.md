## 1. Ruling 1 — a fresh Cowork session authors the frame; Claude Code runs the placement test (Alternative A)

**Ruled.** The two roles the plan requires be held by different sides are allocated for the phase,
before the frame is authored, not after.

- **The frame's author** is a **fresh** Cowork session — not the fiftieth, and not this one. It
  holds the blindness barrier for the whole of its work: no source file, no measurement output, no
  document derived from the code. It performs the fetching and reading of the published literature,
  which is source (ii) of the four ruled sources and the reason this side of the allocation is
  Cowork's: the open web is reachable from a Cowork session and its reach from the repository side
  is the open question the phase's first act exists to answer.
- **The placement test** is run by **Claude Code**, by dispatch. The population is large, scattered
  and history-dependent — every current heading, every heading ever deleted from the document set,
  the ruling records, the decision surfaces, the dossiers, the deferred entries of the decisions
  register, the evidence inventory and the declared dormancies — and enumerating the deleted half
  requires the repository's history.

**Recorded with the ruling, because it must not be rediscovered.** A Claude Code session boots with
the whole of `CLAUDE.md` in its context before its first token. **The placing side is therefore not
blind to this project's own principles.** This does not defeat the placement test — the test
requires *non-authorship*, not blindness, and nothing in its brief asks the placing side to be
ignorant. It is named because that same boot behaviour is the route that voided the derivation
method once already, and a successor reading only the allocation would not see it.

**What this ruling does not decide:** which side runs the literature-reachability act of Ruling 2,
and which side draws and seals the sample of Ruling 3. Both are named as owed at §5.

## 2. Ruling 2 — the literature-reachability act is a SEPARATE, PRIOR act with a ruled stop rule (Alternative B)

**Ruled.** The framework phase's ruled first act — *establishing which of the literature this

**Its ground, which is this project's own precedent:** step zero of the successor plan was ruled

is not measurable, and that is a STOP to the user, not a licence to proceed.*

**Declared at the putting and not withdrawn:** this is an act about the process rather than about
the analysis software, and was flagged as such under the user's standing bar before it was put. It
is ruled on the ground that it costs a survey and a page, and buys the phase a stop point at its
cheapest moment rather than its most expensive.

## 3. Ruling 3 — the placement sample is a declared stratified sample, SEALED before the frame is authored (Alternative B)

**Ruled.** One stratum per source kind — ruling records; decision surfaces; dossiers; the deferred
entries of the decisions register; the evidence inventory; the declared dormancies; current
headings; previously deleted headings — with a size declared per stratum.

**The sealing property is part of the ruling and not an implication of the word "declared":** the
drawn set is written down and **closed before the frame's author begins**. The reason is
structural, not suspicion: a sample drawn afterwards allows the frame to have been shaped around
material its author had just been reading, and no declaration made later can undo that.

**What the report must carry** is unchanged from Ruling 5 of 2026-08-21: the sample's size, the
observed proportion of unplaceable statements, and that proportion's uncertainty range. **There is
no bright line and none is introduced here.** Every unplaceable statement is a finding put to the
user, who rules per finding.

**Explicitly not adopted:** sizing the sample from the pilot's sizing record. That record is
labelled **NOT A BUDGET** by the session that produced it, on three named defects — its blinding
failed; it covers 18 % of its intended input, 44 of 241 design-intent entries; and its own session
declared that its clock measures a model's generation time, not an analyst's hours. Spending
against it would treat as established a measurement its own record says is not.

## 4. Ruling 4 — the no-code-sites instruction STANDS; the ARM and the SITE are filled in and tested by a non-deriving side (Alternative A)

**The subject, restated so the ruling is readable on its own.** Field six of a statement says what
would falsify it. For a behavioural statement, five sub-fields were proposed: the **ARM** (which of
three code arms the statement binds — the joint model, the live legacy path, or the dormant legacy
path), a named **SITE** (where in the code the check is made), the **OBSERVABLE** read there, the
**DECISION RULE** applied to it, and the named **near-miss** the statement is *not* falsified by.
All five were carried as unestablished.

**The measured position, read at the object**
(`cowork_rulings_2026_08_26_sizing_tests_sitting.md` §8.2): the ARM and the SITE are absent from
all thirty-six statements of the pilot's sizing output, **by instruction** — the brief left naming
code sites to a later session and the deriving session opened no code. So the pilot's format test
reached **three of five**, and *no output produced under a brief carrying that instruction can
reach the other two.*

**Ruled.** The instruction stands. A deriving pass — in this phase or any later one — writes
sub-fields 3, 4 and 5 and does not name arms or sites. **The ARM and the SITE are filled in after
the derivation by a side that is permitted to read code, and their testability is judged there.**

**Three things ruled with it:**

1. **The gap is declared on the face of the frame**, not left for a reader to discover: the frame's
   statements are checkable in principle and not yet checkable in fact until the fill-in runs.
2. **The two sub-fields are not dropped.** Discarding them would be a disposal, and this project's
   disposal discipline requires the worth test with a finding, a date and a reason. No such test has
   been run on them. The alternative that drops them may still be right; it is to be reached by
   running that test, never by omission.
3. **The blindness barrier is not traded for a test of a form.** Lifting the instruction would have
   put implementation-derived material into a deriving pass, which is the framework phase's own
   NOT ALLOWED clause.

**Owed and named at §5:** which side performs the fill-in, and when.

## 6A. Ruling 6 — the literature-reachability act runs at WIDENED scope, and this session runs it (Alternatives A then B, on two questions put separately)

**Two questions were put in sequence and both are ruled here, because the second changes what the

**(1) Which side runs the act — Alternative A, the user's word: "Agree on A".** **This session, the
fiftieth, runs it, now.** Grounds recorded: the act is **not a deriving act** — it establishes what
is gettable and writes no specification statement — so the blindness barrier does not constrain who

**(2) Its scope — Alternative B, the user's word: "B".** Widened, on a finding the **user** raised
and this side confirmed at the objects. Recorded below because the finding is the reason for the
ruling.

### The finding, and whose it is

**The user asked, unprompted:** *"somewhere I read (you) that we restrict ourselves to research
(etc) that we already know about and have downloaded. If that is the case - at what point do we
make sure there is no public ideas we have missed?"*

**Checked at the objects and confirmed.** The independent sources are defined as *"published
research **actually fetched and read**"* (successor plan §0) and *"published research fetched and
read"* (phase-definition surface §3.3); the ruled act's own words scope it to *"which of **the
literature this project rests on** is reachable at all"* — a set closed by what this project
already cites.

**The only outward-facing hook in all six phases is reactive.** A design point may end *"underived:
open, needs a ruling or new research"* (phase-definition surface §3.3 outputs). It fires when we
notice we cannot defend a point — not when the field holds something we never thought to look for.
The ground-truth-ceiling record has the same shape: *the quantity principle #21 demands and the
literature does not hold*, a gap noticed from the inside.

**Where the gap is most expensive.** A per-unit search at the detail phase can recover a missed
paper about a unit that exists. It cannot recover a **different decomposition** — a published model
that carves the problem into different pieces than ours — because by then the decomposition is
ratified and every search runs inside a frame that has already decided the answer. A model outside
our four sources cannot surface as *a unit present in one source and absent from the others*, since
it is in none of them. **The cheapest moment to close this is before the frame is authored**, which
is this act.

### What the widened act runs

**(i) Unchanged, with its STOP intact:** which of the literature this project rests on is reachable
and readable. If what is reachable does not cover the state and factor spaces of those models,
that is a STOP to the user.

**(ii) Added:** what the published record holds on harmonic analysis models and their state and
factor spaces **that this project does not currently reference** — returned as a **named list of
CANDIDATES ONLY**, each with a one-line note on what it decomposes differently.

**The bound on (ii), ruled with it and not negotiable inside the act:** no admission, no ranking,
no establishment status, no ruling attached to any candidate. They become candidate inputs to
source (ii) of the four ruled derivation sources and **nothing more**. The stated reason: a widened
sweep that also judged its own returns would become a second frame written by the side that is
barred from authoring the first.

**The named risk, recorded because it was put and accepted, not discovered:** (ii) may return a
large pile that then needs its own dispositioning. That was the case for the narrow alternative and
the user ruled against it.

**Ground for ruling it at all** — the user's own standing rule of 2026-08-25: a finding that fits
the plan and moves the work forward is acted on. This one is about the analysis software's design
inputs, not about the apparatus, so the standing bar on process work is not engaged.
