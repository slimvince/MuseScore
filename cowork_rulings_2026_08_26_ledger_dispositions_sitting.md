  REFUSED AT RE-CHECK record of the original wording, pointing at the entry that replaces it.

**The ground.** The harvest's own verdict on C9 was SPLIT, with *"the leading-tone half PASSES"* — and
the defective subject phrase was in the wording the harvest offered, not authored by the user.

**The alternatives declined.** **A** — accept the refusal, nothing replaces it: loses a music fact the
harvest judged to pass. **C** — hold C9 with the measurement named: that measurement settles the
