## 2. Lessons that propose a change, each with its evidence and its home

**P-1 — The ordinary session-start read binds even when the opening instruction names a single
file.** *Evidence:* the fifty-eighth session skipped it on that ground and counted five errors, all
one shape — acting or asserting without going to the governing record — and its own handover names
the skipped read as the single cheapest cause; the fifty-ninth session performed it and counted
none. *Home:* `CLAUDE.md`, Conventions — one line stating that a single-file opening instruction is
not an exemption. *(Until ratified, the rule lives in the eightieth entry's errors block.)*

**P-2 — A dispatch is pinned to a blob at Task 0 as the standing form, and the pin is ordered in
the user's opening line.** *Evidence:* the pin order is unperformable as "pin before reading" when
only the dispatch file is named (established twice: the landing batch's finding 4, and the second
landing batch executing the declared-departure fallback); the fallback — declare the pre-pin read,
prove the blob unmoved at staging — worked and is the wording to keep. *Home:*
`cowork_audit_protocol.md`, dispatch-protocol section (this subsumes backlog item 9).

**P-3 — A footprint assumption is stated as the list of the batch's own ordered acts, never as a
blanket; movement no order caused is graded and reported, never auto-stopped.** *Evidence:* the
fifty-eighth session's error (5) — a blanket its own tasks broke, the third instance of that shape
— against the first landing batch's §5.3, which graded a third-party file appearance correctly
under the act-list form. *Home:* `cowork_audit_protocol.md`, dispatch-protocol section (this
subsumes backlog item 10).

**P-4 — A disposition given in conversation does not exist until a ruling record lands on disk;
a sitting record is therefore written in the same session, and an in-progress sitting extends its
record per ruling.** *Evidence:* the August ratifications became owed precisely because
dispositions lived only in conversation (the framework-delta sitting's founding finding); the
ratification sitting wrote its record at the first ruling and extended it three times, so no
disposition was ever unrecorded. *Home:* `cowork_audit_protocol.md`, as a standing clause on
sitting records.

**P-5 — Before the writing side commits any file to the tree, it establishes whether a batch is
running: the expected report's absence and the ref's freshness are the cheap checks.** *Evidence:*
the §9.0 surface was committed minutes before this side established a batch was mid-flight; the
batch's enumeration flagged it (first landing report §5.3) and the timing had to be declared in
two records; every later write this session was held until the running batch's report landed and
verified. *Home:* `cowork_audit_protocol.md`, the writing side's standing rules.

**P-6 — An instruction whose mandatory precondition defeats one of its own requirements is checked
for at writing time.** *Evidence:* the brief's §8 bar could only be obeyed by a reader who had
already breached it (the framework document declares it as a live instance of the recorded defect
shape DT-20); the pin-order finding is the same shape in miniature. *Home:* the defect catalog
(`DEFECT_TYPES.md`) gains the two instances at its existing DT-20 entry — a CC act at a later
dispatch — and dispatch writers check new bars against it.
