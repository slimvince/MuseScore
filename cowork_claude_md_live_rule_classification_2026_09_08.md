## 2. The two classes, and the third the reading found

**LIVE RULE.** Text a working session acts on today: a rule, a bar, a STOP condition, a prohibition,
a live caveat that bounds how a figure or a rule is read, a pointer that tells a session where to
look, or a definition a rule needs in order to be applied. A rule's recorded defense is part of the
live rule, because this record requires every decision to carry its defense at its home.

**RECORD OF AMENDMENT.** Text whose subject is the history of the record itself: that a clause was
amended, narrowed, widened or superseded; what the former wording was; which alternatives were
declined; what costs were accepted; which register entry survived; and the dated provenance of who
ruled what when. A session that never opens the history acts no differently for having read it.

**MIXED — and this is the reading's central finding, not a hedge.** A large class of passages is
both, and inseparable as written: a single passage states the governing rule AND records the
amendment that produced it, frequently inside one sentence. **The record has already met this exact
shape and declined to cut it.** The prune-at-amendment act of 2026-09-07 read two candidate spans
whole and left both at site with the reason stated in its own words — the span "is a LIVE STATEMENT
THAT CARRIES THE OPENING OF A PRESERVED ONE", and what would be needed is "a cut that separates a
preserved wording from the live statement announcing it", which that act declined to invent. **This
pass reaches the same wall from the other side, over the whole session-start membership rather than
over two spans.** It is also what Ruling 3 of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_17_seventh_return.md` states of
the recogniser route: what remains is rule text and amendment records that no pattern separates from
the rules they amend.

**The answer to the question, in plain terms.** Neither twenty per cent nor seventy. The ceiling on

## 5. What is owed, and what this act does not do

**The character arithmetic is owed to a generator and is deliberately not asserted here.** Every
figure in this file is cited to `tools/audit/session_start_read_size.json` or read at a git object by
explicit hash; the per-class character and line totals over the classification above are NOT computed
here, because a hand-added total over 112 ranges is exactly the hand-transcribed measurement number
the Premise Gate's clause (f) forbids. The verdicts are authored and are published whole so that a
generator can consume them; computing the totals is a mechanical act for a later batch, and the
conclusion in §4 does not rest on them.

**Two bounds this side declares rather than leaves unstated.** The working copy read here is
size-equal to the committed blob at the tip and is not proven byte-identical to it. And the class of
every passage is an authored judgment, challengeable at the passage: what is established is that the
six span boundaries match the generated measurement's own, and that every line inside them carries
exactly one verdict.

**What this act does NOT do.** It moves no text and prunes nothing. It changes no rule `CLAUDE.md`
states. It creates, flips or discards no open-items row, writes no decisions-register entry and
allocates no identifier. It does not touch `CLAUDE_ARCHIVE.md`, the pinned span decompositions, or
either of the two recogniser passes. It proposes no restructure and rules out none — it reports what
a restructure of this file could reach, so that the question is decided on measured ground rather
than on an estimate. And it does not lift the reading gate, resume the pruning pass, or move a
verdict.
