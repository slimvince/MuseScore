## 0. What this is, in plain words

The **boot pack** is the bundle of documents a fresh, deliberately uninformed Cowork session reads
before it writes the Layer-2 detail specification. Its **extracts member** (member (8)) carries the
project's reading extracts of published papers — fifty-six files under `reading_pass/extracts/`:
the forty-one extracts of the forty L2-slice rows, and fifteen extracts from the first reading pass.
Each extract is quoted into the pack **with the sections that state this project's own positions
cut out**, so that the deriving session meets the papers and not this project's conclusions about
them.

The user's Ruling 1 of 2026-09-21 (`records/cowork/rulings/cowork_rulings_2026_09_21_l2_extracts_member_cutting_sitting.md`)
says how that cut is made: **per file**, the sections that state this project's own positions
**named by their own heading text**, the two ruled shared cut strings left unchanged, and the exact
sections and their anchors written by the pack-build dispatch and verified at each file. Entries 230
to 245 of the handoff record then read the fifty-six files one by one and named those sections per
file (limb A). Entry 246 measured, at `##` heading depth, how those per-file answers partition every
heading of every file.

**This file assembles those fifty-six per-file answers into one list**, with each named section
given by its **current** heading text as it stands in the file today — several headings were
re-worded by the stale-line correction pass of entries 240 to 245 after the judging entry named
them (the Centrality headings of positions 17 to 22, for instance, now read *"was OWED and has been
performed"* where entries 233 and 234 recorded *"is OWED"*), and a cut anchored to the older wording
would STOP the tool.
