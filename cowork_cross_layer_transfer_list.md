## 1. What this list is

Quoted once from its home, `ratification_surfaces/cowork_phase_definition_surface_2026_08_15.md`
§0, and not restated in other words anywhere in this file (#6):

> the parking list for content found in the wrong layer during derivation, so nothing is silently
> dropped and nothing is absorbed into the wrong charter.

**Its ruled form is NARROW** — the user's words at Ruling 5 were *"A for now, we can always go back
to it and rearrange"*, and what that ruled is stated at the ruling record: **one list, entries only
for content found in the wrong layer during a derivation pass. No second receiver. No governing file.**

## 2. The one admission test

**A candidate is admitted if, and only if, a derivation pass found content that sits in a layer other
than the one whose charter owns it.**

That is the whole test. It has one part on purpose: a list serving two admission tests is a list
whose entries can be audited by neither, which is the ground on which the wide form was declined.

## 3. What this list does NOT receive, stated because the near miss is real

**It does not receive content whose ruled destination cannot receive it.** That is a different
defect with a different shape — *this belongs somewhere that does not exist yet*, against this
list's *this belongs in a different layer*.

**The homeless routed material is therefore NOT admitted here and is NOT disposed of by this file.**
The record names it: the defect-catalogue rows, one half of one admitted finding and one further
candidate, all routed to the phase definitions' constraints and stop rules, a frozen surface with no
slot to receive them. **No identity or count is restated here** (#17f, **D-431**); the enumeration
stands at the framework-phase opening surface's Decision 5 and at the ruling record.

**★ THAT DEFECT IS STANDING, NOT SOLVED, AND THE RULING SAYS SO.** It had been observed and routed
around on five consecutive occasions when Ruling 5 was taken, and the ruling was taken with that on
the record rather than in ignorance of it. **The second-receiver shape is PARKED, NOT REFUSED** —
*"we can always go back to it and rearrange"* — so a successor must not read this section as a
decision that the wider forms were considered and rejected on their merits. **Re-putting it is not
the re-opening of a settled question.**

**THE LIST OPENS EMPTY, AND THAT IS A STATE RATHER THAN AN OMISSION.** The framework phase's
derivation decided the decomposition itself; it did not re-derive any layer's detail specification,
which is the pass that finds content sitting in the wrong layer at scale. **The expectation, recorded

**★ ONE CANDIDATE WAS EXAMINED AND EXCLUDED, and it is recorded so a later reader does not admit it
by mistake.** The framework document's **R-4** finds that the **type** of an elaboration — passing,
neighbour, suspension, anticipation — has no owner in the current arrangement, and assigns it to L2.
**That is homelessness, not wrong-layer placement:** the object sits in no layer rather than in the
wrong one, and the framework document gives it an owner outright rather than parking it. **Under §2's
test it is not admitted. Under §3 it is not this list's business either.** *(Recorded as an exclusion
because a ruled-out candidate is evidence about the test, and because this is the nearest thing the
framework phase produced to an entry.)*

## 5. How an entry is written

One row per item, and the columns are the test made concrete: **Content** — what was found, in plain
words; **Found in** — the layer or document it was sitting in; **Charter that owns it** — the layer
whose charter the derivation says owns it, or *undecided* where the derivation could not say;
**Found by** — the pass or the act; **Date**; **Disposition** — where it went, filled in when it
goes, and left open until then.

**An entry is not a decision.** Parking something here records that a derivation found it in the
wrong place. **Moving it is a separate act with its own authority**, and this file confers none.
