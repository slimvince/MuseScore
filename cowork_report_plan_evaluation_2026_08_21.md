## 5. The counterfactual — derived from maximum-precision inference, not from the plan

Precision is decided by five things: the inference model's structure; the evidence it is allowed to read and in what order; the values fitted to it; the ground truth it is graded against and that ground truth's own ceiling; and the failure classes that remain. A specification serves precision by making the first two checkable against theory and evidence before anything is built, and by making the code auditable against them afterwards. So the reconstruction should be organized around the inference model and the decisions it must make, and not around any document.

**The units.** The unit is a DECISION the analysis must make, and the list is DERIVED from three independent sources, reconciled: (i) the ground-truth annotation schema — what is graded (local and global key; Roman numeral as degree, quality and inversion; the boundaries); (ii) the state and factor spaces of the published models the project rests on, fetched and read; (iii) theory — what a harmonic analysis of this repertoire consists of, including what annotators do NOT write down (the limits at `CLAUDE.md:1003–1016`). A decision present in one source and absent from the others is a finding about the frame, not dropped. This is v1's principle (K13) with v2's refutation of the authored list honoured, and it coincides with A4's first two populations minus the code (R2). L5's point — annotators record conclusions, not decisions — is why (i) alone is insufficient and why (ii) and (iii) are required.

**The frame, in two levels.** Level 1, one document: the joint objective — what is maximized, over what state (tonic × mode × scale-degree chord, with segmentation modelled), which factors, the evidence ranking, what is committed and what is carried as alternatives, and what is abstained. Level 2: per-decision statement sets INSIDE level 1, in the plan's five-field form plus the epistemic label (R9) and the uncertainty (M3). This coincides with the ruled framework → detail order and with v2's frame-first; it differs from the plan in the frame's source (R1) and in making the joint objective the thing the per-decision statements must be coherent with (M2, M6).

**Order, by blast radius in the joint model:** first the note facts (what counts as evidence at all — sounding versus struck, the slice identity; the most upstream decision and the one with a live declared gap, `ARCHITECTURE.md:420–425`); then segmentation as a modelled variable; then the joint state and the emission; then transitions and key change; then function, spelling and rendering; last the measurement design, as ruled. v1's segmentation-first intuition survives as second, for its real reason (blast radius), and not for the legacy-scoped reasons v1 cites.

**What proves the method before full width — three tests, not one.** (a) The plan's own withdrawal rate at the record check (K1). (b) The format test A6 (K2). (c) The held-out test (M5): the deriving side derives one decision for which a user-ratified design-intent ruling exists and is withheld; if the blind derivation reproduces the ruled intent, or produces a defended alternative the user would rank beside it, the method is POSITIVELY established (#19); if it produces nothing, L7 is refuted on the refuting side and the ordering is waste. I would run (c) on the evidence-ranking decision (`ARCHITECTURE.md:394–402`, user-ruled 2026-08-11), because its ruling is recent, explicit and user-worded. On the pilot subject I would choose the segmentation decision of the production model over `docs/scoring_model.md`, for the reason at C1; I state that as my derivation and note that the ruled subject stands unless the user moves it.

**Cost.** Measured at the pilot per statement and per decision: statements per hour, share withdrawn, share needing a ruling, share whose fifth field could not be written. The budget per decision is set from those numbers, provisional until the first three decisions are done, with overrun a stop (K5, R6). No number is invented here.

**What would tell me the approach is failing.** A withdrawal rate near zero (the derivation adds nothing the record lacks — L7) or near one (the derivation cannot be trusted); more than a small fraction of statements with an unwritable fifth field (L2); the assembly check finding per-decision statements that conflict under the joint objective (L3); a frame-placement failure above the declared stop (K4); and a share needing a user ruling so high that the user's time, not the method, is the bottleneck (method-directions §2.11:94–95 parks it until sizing exists — this is what the sizing is for).

**Where this lands against the plan.** Substantially convergent on method: derive then check, atomic falsifiable statements, declared sources, measured budgets, an adversarial frame test, separated roles, a declared end state. The differences turn on four things: where the frame comes from (the model and the schema, not the old headings or the delegation-bar population); what the deriving side may read (no code, no failure lists — the audit's material); what gate our own findings pass through (the fact-gate); and whether the method is established positively (the held-out test) or only not falsified. On those four the independent derivation does not land where the plan does. On everything else it does, and that is evidence the plan's core is right.
