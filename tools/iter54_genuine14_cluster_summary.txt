Conclusion
----------
Only 2 of the 14 remaining genuine BIR=true cases are gate-fixable in the
post-ranking pipeline (alt with agreed root is already in results[]).
The other 12 require upstream candidate-generation changes.

Comparison with Iter 46 characterization (25 cases listed in
iter46_genuine21_characterization.txt; documented baseline was 21):
- 5 cases overlap with iter54: bwv184.5 m=13 b=4, bwv187.7, bwv227.11,
  bwv40.6, bwv43.11.
- 20 iter46 cases no longer in genuine-14 (resolved by Iter 54 greedy
  segmentation or moved to different position).
- 9 cases in iter54 are not in the iter46 list — positions created or
  modified by greedy-expand segmentation.

The Iter 46 Cluster A pattern (Min winner with HalfDim alt[1] at agreed
root PRESENT) was 7/25 cases; in iter54 only 1/14 retains that pattern
(bwv227.11). The 8 other Minor cases now show same-root Diminished alts
without the correct HalfDim alt — the upstream candidate generator no
longer produces the HalfDim reading for these regions. Investigating why
(likely a pitch-set difference from greedy segmentation cutting one of
the chord tones out) is the next step for further BIR=true reductions.
