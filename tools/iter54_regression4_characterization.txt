Conclusion
----------
The 4 new BIR=false cases are not direct migrations from former BIR=true
positions; they appear at different (measure, beat) coordinates created or
modified by greedy-expand segmentation. Without snapshotting the prior
BIR=false=128 list at Iter 46, the four specific regressed cases cannot be
identified deterministically from the current data.

Recommendation
--------------
If precise tracking of BIR=false regressions matters going forward,
introduce a saved baseline enumeration step in run_bach_preset.py output
(write tools/corpus/bir_false_baseline_<iter>.txt at each baseline update),
so the next iteration can do a deterministic diff.
