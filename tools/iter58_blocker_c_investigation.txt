The Iter 56 working hypothesis ("greedy boundary split fragmented the Xm6")
is only HALF the story. Two distinct root causes co-exist:

-- Hypothesis A (4 cases) --------------------------------------------
  The erroneous region has a 3-PC subset of the would-be HalfDim, and
  the missing chord tone sits in the IMMEDIATELY-preceding short region.
  Greedy segmentation placed a boundary between the bass entry and the
  added tone (or between the m7 release and the rest of the chord).

  Fix direction:
    R3-MERGE — after Round 2 boundaries are placed, run a "completion
    pass" that merges two adjacent regions when (a) both have duration
    < DIVISION/2 (i.e. they are short fragments, not full beats), (b) the
    UNION of their pitch-class sets matches a known seventh-chord PC mask
    (HalfDim, MinMaj7, Dom7, etc.), and (c) the bass PC remains unchanged
    across the boundary. The merge reconstructs the full PC set so the
    candidate generator sees the complete chord.

    Risk on BIR=false: LOW–MEDIUM. The duration guard limits merging to
    very short fragments; long Diminished/Minor chords are untouched.
    But the merge could occasionally swap a correct two-chord reading
    (e.g. passing-tone followed by chord) into a false single-chord call.
    Mitigation: require the union to be a strict match against a known
    seventh-chord pattern, not merely a superset of three notes.

-- Hypothesis C (4 cases) --------------------------------------------
  The erroneous region has the COMPLETE HalfDim PC set, yet the
  candidate generator does NOT produce a HalfDim candidate with the
  agreed root (the bass-minus-m3). It only produces same-root Minor and
  same-root Diminished alternatives.

  Concrete example — bwv244.44 m=5 b=1.0:
    PC set = {C#, E, G, B}, bass = E. C#m7b5 = {C#, E, G, B} exactly.
    Yet alternatives are only Em (alt[0]) and Edim (alt[1]). No C#m7b5.

  This indicates the candidate generator restricts root selection to a
  subset of the present PCs (likely bass-pc or strongly-weighted PCs).
  It does not enumerate "what if the M6-above-bass is the actual root,
  making this an X-half-diminished?" — even though that interpretation
  is enharmonically equivalent and structurally consistent.

  Fix direction:
    In the chord-candidate generator, when the bass pc forms a minor third
    above some other PC in the set, ADD a HalfDim candidate rooted at that
    other PC. (Equivalently: for any PC in the set, generate a HalfDim
    candidate rooted at PC if the set contains PC + {m3, b5, m7}.)

    Risk on BIR=false: MEDIUM. This is a broader candidate-pool change
    affecting all minor-triad-plus-major-6th voicings. Many of these
    are correctly read as Xm6 in the corpus. Mitigation:
      - Require minimum support score (e.g. all 4 chord tones present)
        before HalfDim is added, never as a fallback.
      - Let downstream gates (Gate K/L/M-style preferences) decide
        between Xm6 and (X+m3)m7b5 in tied-score situations using key
        context (the agreed root should be diatonic).

-- Combined recommendation -------------------------------------------
