## 1. Why this exists, in one paragraph

The pass established its own two bounds honestly and this commission acts on both. **First**, six of
the seven central rows rest on a relaying read tool the pass measured contradicting itself three
times — once answering backwards the single structural question DP-C turns on. The double
extraction bought session-and-prompt independence, **not read-tool independence**, so a systematic
error of that tool survives both passes and no cross-check can see it. The pass's own remedy —
reading at the object as page images — was applied to rows 19 and 7 and to all thirteen
verification targets, but not to those six. **Second**, the original commission's
candidacy-upgrade clause — *coverage follows load, not novelty*, the clause added on the user's
joint-evaluation argument — was never exercised: `population.md` §4 records **none**. That clause
is the one aimed squarely at chain-level evaluation, and it produced nothing.

Neither is a defect of care. Both are gaps between what the commission asked and what the pass
delivered, and both bear directly on the detail specifications that come next.

**The rows:** the six central rows whose reads were RELAYED — `population.md` and the findings

sentence's count on trust; if the derived set is not six, that is a finding — record it and
proceed with the derived set.**

**The method.** For each row, stage the held PDF through the bridge and read it **as page images
with the file tools** — the same grade every class-(c) verification read used. **No web-fetch read
of any kind on these rows.** The papers are already held under
`docs/research_papers/reading_pass_2026_08/`; nothing needs fetching.

**What is being checked — this is a TARGETED re-read, not a third extraction.** For each row,
enumerate from its existing extract and cross-check the claims that (i) are STRUCTURAL — how the
method decides, what it decides jointly or in stages, what it assumes and hands on — and (ii)
carry load somewhere: in a design point's verdict in the findings surface, in the §3.1 coupling
table, or in the §5 routed extracts. **Tabulated values are excluded** — no value disagreed
anywhere across eight pairs, and the pass characterises the read tool as reliable on those.
Verify each enumerated claim at the pages. Verdict per claim: **CONFIRMED** (with page location),
**CORRECTED** (both readings, the page, and what it changes), or **NOT ESTABLISHABLE** (the paper
does not settle it — the claim is downgraded to conjecture, as row 17's was).

**The one thing to watch for above all others:** a CORRECTED verdict on a claim the findings
surface used to reach a **SUPPORTS** or **rival-defused** verdict. **That is a falsifier candidate
and it is a STOP** — write it up on its own and put it to the user before continuing past it.

**Output:** one file per row under `reading_pass/object_reads/`, and one consolidated table.

## 3. Task B — derive and work the candidacy upgrades

**The criterion, stated so the derivation is mechanical rather than a matter of taste.** A held
paper is a **candidacy upgrade** when its METHOD — not merely a figure it supplies — is a live
candidate for a decision inside a ratified charter: that is, when a detail specification of L1, L2
or L3 could adopt, adapt, or have to argue against the way that paper actually does the thing the
charter owns. A paper that supplies only a measured figure, a corpus, an evaluation convention or
a representation is **not** an upgrade — it routes to measurement design or data design and is
already routed by the findings surface §5.

**Derive the set** from `docs/research_papers/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` against `FRAMEWORK.md` §5's charters
and §9's design points, applying that criterion per paper and recording the verdict and its reason
for **every** row, so the derivation is complete in both directions and auditable. **A paper whose
own text does not settle whether its method is a candidate is INCLUDED** — the default on doubt is
to read, which is this pass's whole ground.

*Starting hypothesis, offered so the work is concrete and NOT to be adopted without the derivation
above — the writing side's reading of the bibliography, not an established set:* the segmental
CRF chord-recognition model and the semi-Markov formalism behind it, and the neural semi-CRF
system, all three being the machinery of DP-C's chosen option; the probabilistic graphical model
and the unified probabilistic polyphonic model, as joint-decode designs; the concurrent
chord-and-key estimation paper, which is the primary for the soft-versus-hard coupling the L2
charter fixes; the chordal-analysis paper that supplies L1's partition-point construction and its
tie-breaking residual; the generalized parsing framework; the multi-task lineage read as candidate
scoring architectures for L2 rather than only as DP-A's pathology evidence; the figured-bass
annotation work, L3 publishing figured bass with **no producer anywhere in the read set**; and the
duration-and-language-model chord recogniser as a segment-duration design. *Each of these is a
hypothesis to be confirmed or refused by the derivation, per paper, in writing.*

**Then read whole, at the object**, every paper the derivation admits, in priority order by the
weight the charter puts on the decision it bears on — L2's entangled decision first. **Each is
