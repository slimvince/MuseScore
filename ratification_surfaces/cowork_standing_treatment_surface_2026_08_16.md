## 0. The referents, re-explained from scratch (read this section first)

- **The decisions register** — the record of WHAT WAS DECIDED about this system; a data file
  (the only edited surface), a rendered INDEX (`DECISIONS.md`) and rendered group files.
- **The soft-discard** — the ruled act: 165 entries with no deciding act found (194 minus the 29
  the sole-carrier guard withheld) retire into a retired block of the data file — preserved, not
  destroyed (#12), each revivable; the live record goes 677 → 512.
- **The reach ruling (§4, "Alternative A as recommended")** — four limbs: a derived split of
  every check the discard turns red into SUPERSEDED (serves only the old, superseded phase-1
  gate) and STANDING (serves a rule of the current record); the SUPERSEDED members reclassified
  HISTORICAL, artifacts frozen; the STANDING members regenerated under an enumerated-movement
  bound; the register's mechanism work landing with the act.
- **An authored judgment table** — a committed artifact whose verdicts were written by judgment
  and accepted, not derived: the `CLAUDE.md` rule triage, the home classification, the
  delegation bar's FORM judgments, the legacy-marking verification. Their checks re-verify the
  tables; they cannot re-author them.
- **A completed measurement that is a ruling's evidence** — an artifact produced once, read by
  the user, and ruled on: the filter classification (411 / 182 / 84 — the keep side RATIFIED)
  and the deciding-act recovery pass (72 ACT-FOUND / 194 NOTHING-FOUND — the ground of the
  discard ruling itself).
- **Pinned reading** — a check whose inputs are read from the git objects AT THE COMMIT ITS
  ARTIFACT RECORDS, not from the live tree — the pattern the sole-carrier tool and this batch's
  reader enumeration already use, established twice in this phase, precisely so that a completed
  measurement is not destroyed by the acts it authorized (the [[OI-301]] hazard).

## 3. The alternatives, each rated against the principles and the ultimate objective

**Alternative A (recommended) — each kind treated by its own nature; nothing regenerated that
records a ruling, nothing frozen that guards a live rule.**

- **Kind 1 — the two completed measurements that are rulings' evidence** (the filter, the
  recovery pass) **are PINNED**: their checks re-derive the artifacts from the git objects at
  the commit each artifact records, so the artifacts stand permanently as the rulings' evidence,
  still guarded against corruption, and permanently insensitive to the acts they authorized.
  *For:* it is the established pattern (#6 — the third batch built it for exactly this hazard);
  it keeps the guard value that reclassifying historical would lose; it honors #12 and #16 (a
  measurement's provenance includes the population it measured). *Against:* the checks stop
  watching the live tree — which is correct, because their subject is a completed act, not the
  live tree; stated so it is chosen, not slid into.
- **Kind 2 — the four authored tables with retired subjects gain a RETIRED-SUBJECTS section**,
  on the retired-block pattern: each authored judgment whose subject the discard retires moves
  verbatim, WITH its subject reference, into that section (#12 — nothing dropped, nothing
  re-authored); the check consults live subjects, and a judgment in neither section halts it, so
  the section cannot become a hole. Membership is DERIVED (S1), the move list published, the
  movement enumerated in the close. *For:* these four checks serve standing rules of the CURRENT
  record — the home classification underpins the register's home rules, the rule triage watches
  `CLAUDE.md`, the delegation bar is rule (i)'s check, the legacy verification guards the LEGACY
  markings — so they must keep running over live subjects (#19: freezing a live rule's guard is
  the silent-failure direction), and the refusal behaviour that stopped this batch is preserved
  for any FUTURE subject loss that is not ruled. *Against:* it is mechanism work on four tools in
  one act — the cost is bounded by S1's derived membership and the per-table STOPs.
- **Kind 3 — the two derived censuses regenerate under the bound WIDENED BY ONE NAMED
  CATEGORY:** movement whose cause is that a document's naming in the governing record sat
  inside a retired entry's own text (citation-carried standing), beside the two ruled categories
  (class standing; home standing). Every moved value enumerated and classed (S2); the
  caller-check run with the commit its own STOP demands. *For:* the census is the archiving
  wave's live tracking surface and must reflect the post-discard record; crossing to the uncited
  side makes a document a retirement CANDIDATE and nothing more — every ruled condition on
  candidacies (mined-first, members-seen-by-the-user, the caller-check at the objects) stands
  untouched, so the movement changes a derived census, never a fate. *Against:* the bound grows
  a third category, which is the direction that needs watching — mitigated by S2's
  enumerate-and-class STOP.
- **F22 is ACCEPTED AS A NAMED COST, recorded not slid:** the population that stops moving is
  the superseded doc-sync program's own; the live D-438 derivation keeps working over the frozen
  input; a future need re-opens it by revival, and the acceptance is recorded in the ruling so
  it is challengeable rather than discovered.

Rated against the ultimate objective: A completes the phase's blocked output (a) at the smallest
sound reach, preserves every guard that protects the current record, and destroys no evidence of
any ruling.

**Alternative B — freeze everything that complains: reclassify the four refusing tables (and
the two evidence artifacts' checks) historical alongside the six.** *For:* one uniform
treatment, least mechanism work. *Against:* it removes live guards from the current record's own
rules — the home classification and delegation bar are what the register's home rules are
checked by, and the rule triage is `CLAUDE.md`'s own watcher — which is #19's silent-failure
direction chosen deliberately, and the lapse-ruling's ground inverted: those checks are not
superseded apparatus, they are the current record's. Declined by rating.

**Alternative C — authorize the tables to regenerate by DROPPING the judgments whose subjects
retired.** *For:* every check goes green with no new mechanism. *Against:* it deletes authored,
accepted judgments — information loss #12 forbids flatly, and precisely the silent dropping the
tables' own refusal behaviour exists to prevent. Declined outright.

**Alternative D — hold the discard again.** *For:* nothing further can surprise. *Against:* the
measurement is complete — both STOPs have now mapped the entire reach at the objects, twice; a
third hold learns nothing, pays the noise cost every session, and leaves the phase's output (a)
blocked with no offsetting gain on any principle. Declined by rating.
