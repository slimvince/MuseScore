**The file under ratification** is `cowork_derived_specification_l2_2026_10_10.md`, the "successor". It is the

**Face (d)** is the fourth face: the four statements L2-S23 to L2-S26 (successor lines 911 to 1048). Together they
say: deciding which notes are chord tones and which are elaborations is part of L2's one decision, made inside the
candidate score for each candidate chord, never by a detector that runs first (L2-S23); the voice-leading evidence
for an elaboration (how a note is approached and left in its voice) is read from what L1 publishes per notated
voice, and L2 builds no note-to-note line inside a voice that sounds several pitches at once (L2-S24); a note's
duration and metric position are weighed as terms, and neither decides on its own (L2-S25); a grace note or an
ornament sign is evidence for its host note and for the chord, and gets no chord-tone-or-elaboration decision of
its own (L2-S26).

A "statement" in this file has six fields: the statement itself (in bold), its defense, its source class, its
status, its premise with the way the premise could fail unnoticed (the "false-negative path"), and its falsifier
(how to test the code against it). Rulings are written beneath a statement as short sub-items.

  register entries' standing is §6's. Correction 1 adds a pointer to a ruling already given; correction 2 adds two
  pointers.

**The currently decided method for face (d), in plain words.** For every candidate reading, each sounding note in a
stretch is either a chord tone of that stretch's chord or an elaboration with one of eight relations (passing,
neighbour, suspension — an upward-resolving one included —, anticipation, appoggiatura, escape tone, pedal point,
or unclassified). That choice is made inside the candidate score, together with the chord, the tonality and the
boundaries, never beforehand by a separate detector; a pedal reading (the chord read from the upper voices, the
held note an elaboration) competes like any other reading, with its rivals published. The evidence for each note
is how it is approached and left in its notated voice (read from what L1 publishes: the interval from the
preceding note, the time since that voice's last attack, the silence before it; the following note read at the
next onset in the same voice), its metric position and its duration — all weighed as terms with fitted weights,
none deciding by a fixed cut or tier. In a voice that sounds several pitches at once, no note-to-note line is
published, and L2 does not invent one; the elaboration evidence is absent there and the loss is declared. L2 reads
nothing from the voice-leading axis. Grace notes and ornament signs are evidence for their host note and for the
chord; they get no decision of their own and no relation is published for them. **Still open:** the weights'
values (learned through the chord labels until a note-by-note annotated collection exists, OQ-L2-13); whether an
elaboration's name is decided inside the score or afterwards (a measurement under OQ-L2-3); whether the joint
search can afford the assignment dimension at all (OQ-L2-8, the tractability route).
