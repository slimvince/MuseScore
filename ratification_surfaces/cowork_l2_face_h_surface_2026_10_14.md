## 0. What is being decided, from the beginning

**The file under ratification** is `cowork_derived_specification_l2_2026_10_10.md`, the "successor". It is the
specification of L2, the layer that decides for each stretch of music the tonality, the chord, where one chord
gives way to the next, and which sounding notes belong to the chord. A deriving session wrote it blind; it was then
amended by the forty-three L2 family rulings of 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-10. Its banner says NOT RATIFIED.

**How it is ratified.** On 2026-10-13 you ruled "option 3, one aspect at a time", so the file is ratified one face
at a time, nine surfaces in all (`records/cowork/rulings/cowork_rulings_2026_10_13_l2_ratification_granularity_sitting.md`).
Faces (a) to (f) — statements L2-S1 to L2-S39 — were ratified on 2026-10-13, and face (g) — L2-S40 to L2-S43 — this
morning. Their ruling records are the seven `cowork_rulings_2026_10_1*_l2_face_*_ratification_sitting.md` files in the
rulings folder.

**Face (h)** is the eighth and last face: the six statements L2-S44 to L2-S49 (successor §3h, lines 1698 to 1969;
§4 begins at line 1970). They say where L2's job begins and ends: what it does over silence, what it hands to the next
layer, in what order it reads repeated music, what it may take from the layer below, what music it covers, and what
it may take from the layer above. Three layer names first: **L1** is the layer below, which cuts the music into
"slices" (stretches over which the same notes sound) and publishes facts about them; **L2** is this file's subject;
**L3** is the layer above, which reads further facts (cadence types, phrase grouping, chord symbols) off L2's
settled reading. A **reading** is one complete candidate answer for the music loaded; a **rival** is an alternative
reading published beside the chosen one, and its **mass** is the weight it carries in the publication (L2-S40,
ratified with face (g)).

In plain words:

- **L2-S44 (silence):** where no note sounds at all (a slice L1 publishes as silent), L2 either carries the
  surrounding harmony through it, or publishes the stretch with a chord field that says "silent"; the two are rivals.
  Whether a stretch where notes *do* sound may be published with "no chord" is the framework's open question DP-Q.
- **L2-S45 (what goes up to L3):** L2 publishes the segmentation, the tonality and chord per stretch, the chord-tone
  decision per note, the rivals with their masses (with everything face (g) ruled about them), and the provenance;
  where it computes one, it may also publish a fact about the music with its standing; but no internal number of
  its own scoring crosses.
- **L2-S46 (repeats):** L2 reads the music in written order; at a repeat sign, the stretch on either side has one
  neighbour per pass, and the chord-to-chord terms are taken over each.
- **L2-S47 (nothing re-derived from below):** L2 never recomputes a fact L1 publishes; where it wants something L1
  does not publish, it writes an open question and does without.
- **L2-S48 (what music is covered):** L2's output covers exactly the selection, and the result after any series of
  enlargements of the loaded music equals one fresh run over the music finally loaded.
- **L2-S49 (nothing from above):** L2 reads nothing L3 publishes; the dependency is one-way.

A "statement" in this file has six fields: the statement itself (in bold), its defense, its source class, its
status, its premise with the way the premise could fail unnoticed (the "false-negative path"), and its falsifier
(how to test the code against it). Rulings are written beneath a statement as short sub-items.
