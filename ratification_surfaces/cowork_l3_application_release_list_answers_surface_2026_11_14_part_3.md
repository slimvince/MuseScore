## Item 1 — the list's (xxix): L-0850, §L.40 (3) — Row 48.17 (iii) is HISTORICAL whole

**The row.** Row 48.17 has three claims at the reading file's §6; claim (iii) is *"the derivation from the sounding
content is a read of L1 facts by the presentation layer, which can carry it"* (the augmented-sixth distinction),
proposed **UNPLACED**. Parts (i) and (ii) are not this record's and are not touched [relayed from the list, lines
831–833 and 844–845].

**The record and its surface.** The display-family record: *"Decided against (1): Row 48.17 (iii), in so far as it makes
the augmented-sixth sub-type a display's re-derivation from the notes; the sub-type is L2's chord kind (the
chord-vocabulary Ruling 1), which a display renders."* The surface, ratified *"as the surface states them"*, puts the
first half in its §2C table's placement column and the second half (*"A display renders the sub-type L2 publishes"*,
with the chord-vocabulary Ruling 1) in its ground column, and counts Row 48.17 (iii) as one row-part, decided against
(*"1 (2C)"*; §3: *"one decided against"*) [all relayed from the list, lines 833–841].

**What the dispatch says now.** Line 3462 (§L.40 table): *"**HISTORICAL**, form (b-H), for a part only"*, quoting the
first half, and then *"The rest of the record's sentence, "the sub-type is L2's chord kind …, which a display renders",
names no disposition word."* *Open* point (3), lines 3496–3497: *"Row 48.17 (iii): a HISTORICAL part and a rest with no
disposition word; the same question as §L.31's Open point (2)."* [both relayed from the site table's L-0850, which
checked them at the dispatch].

**The derived answer.** The clause after the semicolon is the ground of the decision, not a second part: the surface
says so by its columns and its count. And the *"in so far as"* clause covers the whole of claim (iii), since the claim
says only that a display re-derives the sub-type from the sounding notes. So **HISTORICAL**, form (b-H), for part (iii)
whole, the line quoting the record's sentence whole, its ground included. Nothing waits and no part is left, so the
question of §L.31's *Open* point (2) (a line now, or none until the rest is settled) does not arise, and point (3)'s
STOP-and-report does not fall on the row (the list, lines 841–845 [checked]).

**What ratifying it does.** The list's instruction L-0850: *"change §L.40's Open point (3) to point here, and correct the
§L.40 table's line for Row 48.17 (iii) (dispatch line 3462), which says "for a part only" and calls the ground "The rest
of the record's sentence", former wordings kept"* (list lines 850–853 [checked]). Line 3462 then reads HISTORICAL, form
(b-H), for part (iii) whole, quoting the record's sentence whole. §L.40's points (1) (Ruling 9 of the part 2 release
record), (2) (its Ruling 1) and (4) (its Ruling 37) are not touched.

**Seen: why this is put for ratification and not fixed as staleness.** The list labels the line's correction an apparent

**Method as decided** (the record's lines 47–51, as the list quotes them, lines 846–849 [relayed]): L2 decides key and
