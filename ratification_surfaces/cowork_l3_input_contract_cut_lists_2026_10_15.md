## 0. What Ruling 2 asks for, in its own words

Ruling 2 decided that the session deriving L3's detail specification receives
`cowork_derived_specification_l0_l1_2026_09_03.md` and `cowork_derived_specification_l2_2026_10_10.md` *"as its
**input contract**, given and not re-derived, each **cut verbatim** of every passage that states L3's ruled or current
design. **The cut list is put to the user before any session is booted.**"* It named two passages found by a name
search (*"So the cut includes those passages at the least"*) and said the rest is found by reading the lines matching
cadence, phrase, figured bass, chord symbol or harmonic rhythm.

**What this list treats as "stating L3's design".** A passage is proposed for cutting when it says **how L3 does its
job, or what L3 owns, beyond what the charter itself says**. The charter is `FRAMEWORK.md` §5 and §9, which the
deriving session reads whole under Ruling 1 of the same record, less three short authored removals that ruling
names. So a passage that only repeats the charter gives the
session nothing it does not already have. A passage that names L3 only as a later reader of L1's or L2's output says
nothing about how L3 works. Both kinds are proposed **kept**, and the close calls among them are listed in §3 so that
you can overrule any of them. That reading of the ruling is this side's, and it is stated here so that you can check
it. The charter's L3 block, read for this list: L3 publishes *"the cadence at each phrase end, with its type; the
phrase and section grouping; the chord symbol — root pitch class, quality and bass note; the figured bass; the
harmonic rhythm"*. The phrase reading is decided at L3 from L1's notated evidence. The chord symbol is a view: *"The
root is the tonic transposed by the degree's interval"*.

## 2. PROPOSED FOR CUTTING — seven passages

Line numbers are those of the files as staged on 2026-10-15. Each passage is quoted exactly as it stands. The line
breaks of the file are joined with spaces.

### From `cowork_derived_specification_l0_l1_2026_09_03.md`

**Cut 1 — §0, the naming clause on *slice*, lines 103–105.** From inside Ruling 56's quoted sentence:

> "the grouping layer's unit is the punctuation span, bounded by notated punctuation, as Row 13.1's own convention
> says;"

*Why.* Phrase and section grouping is L3's fact (the charter). This clause states the unit L3's grouping works in.
That is a ruled answer, and the charter does not state it.

**Cut 2 — §0, the naming clause on bare *phrase*, lines 108–110.** From inside Ruling 56's quoted sentence:

> "the grouping object is the punctuation span; the phrase-boundary primitive's code name is an inherited collision
> for OI-229, that primitive being L3's under Ruling 48."

*Why.* It repeats Cut 1's answer. It also states that the phrase-boundary primitive belongs to L3. Which layer owns
that primitive is the question D-476 answers, and D-476 is withheld from L3.

**Cut 3 — §3.5, beside S-39 (Ruling 48), lines 1128–1132.** The passage Ruling 2 names:

> "The graded profile, its gap-dominant weights, the marker spikes, the max-normalisation, the adaptive threshold,
> the peak-picking, the picked set and the *"ends a phrase"* flag are **RELOCATED to the consumer that decides the
> phrase boundary** — the phrase read-off at L3 under the L1 → L3 contract — *"with D-476 to D-485 re-homed there
> and D4 (D-478) remaining that consumer's ruled rule"*."

*Why.* It states the machinery L3's phrase reading is given, and names D-478 as L3's ruled rule. D-478 is withheld.

**Cut 4 — §3.5, the fermata displacement (Ruling 54), lines 1163–1164.** The last clause of the paragraph:

> "— and relating them is L2's cadence-factor covariate and L3's alignment window, to which Rows 11.12 and 13.14
> are relocated."

*Why.* It states that L3 has an alignment window that places the cadence relative to a displaced fermata, and that
two rows (11.12 and 13.14) were moved to it. That is a design answer for L3's cadence placement. The charter's L3
block says the span's boundaries come from the notated cues and *"the cadence is aligned to them"*, and does not say
how; the search of `FRAMEWORK.md` for *align* found no other statement of it. *(Corrected at the check, 2026-10-15.
Former wording: "two rows of findings were moved to it … The charter says only that the cadence *"is aligned to"* the
span." The rows' kind was not checked, and "only" was wider than the act that produced it.)* *(If this clause is cut, the paragraph's own sentence that the L1 facts
are "published already" still stands.)*

**Cut 5 — beside S-29 (Ruling 62, item 3), lines 887–888.** The last clause of the quotation:

> "the harmonic rhythm is over decided harmonic spans, the charter's L3."

*Why.* The charter lists harmonic rhythm as L3's fact without saying what it is measured over. This clause answers
that question. **It is the closest call among the seven**, because the answer follows almost directly from the
charter. It is proposed for cutting because a session should reach even an obvious answer itself. The clause before
it (*"L1 publishes no statistic over slice lengths; the series is the slice list, already published"*) is L1's and
stays.

## 3. Read and proposed KEPT — the close calls, so you can overrule any

Each of these mentions L3 or one of L3's facts. None of them, on this side's reading, gives L3 an answer that the
charter or the L2 → L3 contract does not already give.

- **The bass note, "which L3 publishes, read off the figure L2 decides".** L0/L1 file lines 77–80 (§0), 1256–1258
  (S-44), 1274–1277 (Ruling 42's amendment) and 1286–1292 (Ruling 2 of 2026-10-08). The charter lists the bass note
  in L3's chord symbol. L2-S27 in the L2 file says L2 decides the figure and publishes no bass note. L3 may not revise
  L2. So the bass note being read off L2's figure follows from the input contract itself. *Proposed kept.*
- **The words "reserves the word *bass* for L3"**, at S-45, S-46, S-47 and S-49 (L0/L1 lines 1320, 1351, 1389, 1443).
  These are naming notes. *Kept.*
- **S-8 and OQ-16: a composer's figured-bass figures are annotation, never analysis input** (L0/L1 lines 398–413 and
  1703–1709), with S-2's bar on consuming any annotation, a written chord symbol included (lines 199–213). These
  bear on L3's figured-bass and chord-symbol facts, because they tell L3 it may not read the composer's figures or a
  written chord symbol. But they are ruled rules of L0, the input contract itself, and not a design of L3.
  *Proposed kept.* Cutting them would hide a real limit on L3's input.
- **A derived spelling is published as derived, never in place of the notated one** (L0/L1 lines 298–303, Ruling 58
  clause (iii)): *"the layer that derives one publishes it as derived and never in place of the fact … the

  the chord symbol's root, so it is a rule L3 must obey. Like S-8, it is a ruled rule of the input contract and does
  not say how L3 works. *Proposed kept, and flagged.* *(Added at the check, 2026-10-15; it was formerly listed among
  the passages that only name L3 as a reader.)*
- **"The near-miss is L3's to weigh"** (L0/L1 line 1119), **"L2 and L3 weigh the flags"** (1174), **"L3's phrase
  reading"** (789), **"a phrase-grouping consumer at L3 might"** (693–694), **"L2 or L3 later admitting one of

- **"A phrase boundary with a restated chord is L3's to publish (DP-J) … L3's phrase grouping carries the division"**
  (L2 file lines 647–649), and the same point in L2-S49's defense (2109–2112). The charter gives both halves: DP-J
