## §A.1 What this family is

The derived statement, whole (*checked*, `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`, its lines 776–780):

> **L2-S31. The candidate score of a reading is a sum over its spans of span terms, plus a sum over
> adjacent span pairs of pair terms. A span term reads the span's position, its content (sounding set,
> assignments, attachments), the evidence at and inside it, and its four fields. A pair term reads two
> adjacent spans' fields and the evidence at their shared boundary. Span length is a variable of the
> reading, not a fixed grid.**

In plain words: L2 rates a whole reading of the music (where the harmonies change, the key, the chord, and
which notes belong to each chord) by adding up two kinds of number. One number per stretch of harmony,
rating that stretch on its own. One number per pair of neighbouring stretches, rating how they follow each
other. Nothing in the score looks at three or more stretches at once, and the stretches are not cut on a
fixed grid of beats or bars; how long each one is, is part of the reading being rated.

Its defense (*checked*, lines 781–790): the charter's DP-C chooses a segmentation decided together with the
chord, on the ground that a decoded stretch length "buys the expressive power of a high-order model at
linear rather than exponential inference cost"; the published systems of this kind use exactly this
decomposition (Masada & Bunescu; Yang, Cwitkowitz & Duan 2023, *relayed* by the derivation); and removing
the stretch-length decode was the largest loss in the one published ablation. Status: "Settled".

Its premise and its known gap (*checked*, lines 793–796): "Pairwise dependence between adjacent spans is
enough." The gap is longer syntax — a ii–V–I, or a dominant prolonged over several stretches — which the
pair terms see only link by link. "Whether a hierarchical model is needed is DP-O, which the charter leaves
open and 'not foreclosed'. This form does not foreclose it." Its falsifier: "falsified if some term reads
three or more spans' fields, or if span length is fixed in advance."

L2-S31: 17 AGREES, 22 DIFFERS (*checked*, its line 71199). Two further rows travel in from another family

  that case.

  slice. In my reading this half alone would be a difference of grain, not of design; what decides the
  placement is the reference.

  placements and the two rows).

**The placement:** HISTORICAL — a superseded plan, for both rows.

**The other way:** QUARANTINED. Not apt: these are design claims about where membership is judged, not

**The ground.** Towards the objective (#4): each note's role is weighed with the chord it would belong to,

- L2 has no recompute step: one decision, and the framework excludes "a pipeline with feedback — each stage
