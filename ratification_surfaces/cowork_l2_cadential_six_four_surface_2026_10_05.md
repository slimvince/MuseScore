## 5. The question — does L2's first specification choose a theory of the cadential six-four? (a real choice)

**What is being decided.** Whether the specification leaves DP-N open and carries the readings as rivals
(the derivation), or settles DP-N now. Settling it is a charter question as well as an L2 one, because
DP-N is written into the ratified framework as "none chosen".

One fact shapes every position: **the readings describe the same notes.** Under L2 they differ in what
the chord is and in what the sixth and fourth are assigned — chord tones under `I6/4`; suspensions, which
must resolve down to the fifth and third, under the dominant reading. So the rivals are told apart by how
the upper voices continue, which the suspension relation reads (*my reading*). The own-category reading
names no different notes and no different assignment; it differs from `I6/4` only in its label
(*my reading*).

**Position one — L2-S29 as derived: both readings carried as rivals; DP-N stays open in the charter; the
own-category reading is excluded by name (the recommendation).** At each cadential six-four the decision
weighs the two readings by the evidence and publishes the winner with its rival. The charter is
untouched. This ruling is the named exclusion of the third reading, so that L2-S2 is met whether or not
a grading corpus turns out to write it, with its cost stated: a ground-truth `Cad64` is matched only if
the grading reads it as one of the two rivals.
How a `Cad64` annotation is converted into L2's four fields, and which reading each grading corpus uses
piece by piece (the framework's own settling route), are measurement design.

- *Towards the objective (#4).* Both readings that differ in what the notes are assigned are reachable,
  and the output says which way the evidence leaned and what the rival was. Neither of the two is
  pre-empted where the annotators disagree; the own-category reading, which differs only in its label,
  is the one excluded.
- *Towards the principles.* #1: no theory chosen on no evidence, which is the framework's own ground and
  R-6's rule; the literature pass found nothing to choose by. #12: neither reading is dropped; the rival
  is published. #21: the annotators' disagreement is kept as a fact about the ground truth, not learned
  away. L2-S2: met, the third reading excluded by a named ruling with its cost. #6: one sonority, no
  second state that differs only in label.
- *The cost.* The committed label at a cadential six-four can differ from a given corpus's convention;
  whether that counts against the analysis is a grading question left to measurement design (the
  graded-distance mitigation, and D-352's bar for tonality that counts any recorded reading, are there
  to draw on). L3's cadence
  reading has to handle a committed `I6/4` (Row 5.101, relocated to L3, already says it never registers
  as a tonic arrival). The ceiling cost of excluding `Cad64` is not counted.
- *Meta level:* the analysis's own specification.

**Position two — settle DP-N now for the dominant reading (the outgoing texts' choice): the cadential
six-four is a dominant with the sixth and fourth as suspensions.**

- *Towards the objective.* The output matches a corpus that writes the dominant reading every time, and
  cadence detection reads five-to-one directly. Against a corpus that writes `I6/4` or `Cad64`, it
  matches less.
- *Towards the principles.* #1 and R-6: a theory chosen where the framework says it would be chosen "on no
  evidence". L2-S2: the tonic reading, which an analyst writes, must then either stay admitted (so
  "principal" means only a preference, which in a fitted decision has to be either a hand-set value —
  which the fit has no ruled place for; D-096's title says fitted values are fit against ground truth,
  never per-case tuned, and whether it reaches a hand-set preference was not read at its entry — or a conversion of the ground truth before fitting, which erases the
  annotators' disagreement, against #12 and #21) or be excluded by a named ruling with a ceiling cost
  (*my reading* of what "principal" can mean here). And the ratified charter is amended.
- *Meta level:* the analysis's own specification, and the ratified charter.

**Position three — admit the own-category reading too: three rivals (the incumbent's premise, Row
10.44(ii)).**

- *Towards the objective.* All three readings named in DP-N are states. But the third differs from
  `I6/4` only in its label, so the decision could only tell them apart by learning which annotators
  write which, from the tables (*my reading*).
- *Towards the principles.* L2-S2: met by admission rather than by exclusion. #21: the model would fit the
  annotation convention rather than the music. #6: two states for one chord and one set of assignments.
  OQ-L2-8 ★ (tractability): the state grows. DP-N is answered "all three", which changes the charter's
  "none chosen".
- *Meta level:* the analysis's own specification, and the ratified charter.

**Recommendation: position one.** Towards the objective: both musically distinct readings are reachable
and published with their weight. Towards the principles: no theory chosen without evidence (#1, R-6),
neither of the two readings dropped (#12), the annotators' disagreement kept as a fact about the ground truth (#21), L2-S2
satisfied by a named exclusion. Meta level: the specification only; the charter stays as you ratified it.
The cost is a grading question handed, as the framework already routes it, to measurement design, and an
uncounted ceiling cost for `Cad64`.
