## §A.5 The two forms, in plain words

- **Form 1 — no cue term (the derivation as written, my reading).** A cadence confirms a tonality only through the
  progression term: the reading proposes chords, and the term rates the pair "V then I in this key" as degrees. The
  cues are published by L1 and nothing in L2's fitted scoring reads them.
- **Form 2 — a cue term beside the progression term (the joint estimator's cadence factor in L2's shape).** At each
  moment a note starts, the cues L1 published there are read against *each candidate reading's own tonality* — for
  example "the leading-tone cue fired here, and this reading's tonic is the pitch it resolved to" — and the term adds a
  fitted amount to that reading. This is what Ruling 53 called "a consumer reading L1's cues and its own state". The
  fermata's written position and metric class can enter as covariates of where the arrival sits (Ruling 54).

**What differs between them, as far as this side can establish.**
- *Information.* The progression term sees chords, not voices — *my reading* of L2-S34's "a term on the pair of
  adjacent chords *read as degrees in their tonalities*" *(§B item 10: the mark added)*. The leading-tone cue carries one thing chord labels do
  not: that the resolution happened *in the same voice*. The fourth-and-seventh cue reads notes in the approach
  whether or not a reading counts them as chord tones. *My reading:* so Form 2 is not simply the same notes read twice
  — which is how the tonality-evidence record's Ruling 5 distinguished the dominant-shape vote it set aside — though
  it overlaps heavily with what the progression term already reads.
- *Measured value.* Bigo et al. show the local features separate authentic cadences well (F .80 and .69). Feisthauer
  et al. show a voice-leading V→I cue *inside* a key decision barely helped (α = 0.016), its false positives being on
  parallel keys and I→IV — the second of these is the limit Ruling 53 wrote onto S-46; the first is not *(§B item 11.
  Former wording, preserved (#12): "— the same limit Ruling 53 wrote onto S-46.")*. C42: the leading tone at a cadence does not
  separate a real change of tonality from a passing one. None of these measures Form 2 against Form 1 with a degree
  progression term present.
- *What is already allowed.* Under L2-S37 the leading-tone cue may carry load now; the other two only after OQ-L2-12.
  L2-S37's falsifier admits "an experimental, unfitted term reading [a provisional cue] in a measurement run that is
  itself establishing the window".
- *Cost.* One more term per onset change point and one more fitted weight per cue; not a ground for choosing now
  (D-200).
