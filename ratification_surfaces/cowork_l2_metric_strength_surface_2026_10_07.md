## §A.1 The statement, whole, and what it means

From `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`, its lines 348–373 (*checked*):

> **L2-S12. The metric strength class of a change point enters the candidate score as a term on
> reading a boundary there. No metric level forbids a boundary. The term reads the class and its period
> as L1 publishes them. Its weights are fitted per class, and none is set here.**
> - *Defense.* Harmonic change was counted at 71.5% of beats at the level above the tactus, 22.3% at the
>   tactus and 2.4% at the level below [FACT (relayed, 08) — Temperley 2009, Table 1, p. 6; the charter
>   carries the same figures, RULED]. The lowest figure is small but not zero, so a rule forbidding
>   sub-tactus boundaries would exclude real changes. […] Removing all metrical-accent features from a
>   segmental system lowered segment F-measure from 77.6 to 71.2 on one fold set [FACT (relayed, 08) —
>   Masada & Bunescu, Table 5, p. 8]. The class and its period are L1's published facts, and a per-slice
>   weight is "a consumer's fitted derivative, never published by L0 or L1" [RULED — IC beside S-35,
>   Ruling 47].
> - *Source class.* Derived for the shape. *Measured* for the weights […] **UNESTABLISHED; no value.**
> - *Status.* Settled for the shape. **Open** for the values.
> - *Premise.* The three-level gradient holds on this repertoire. […] **False-negative path:** a
>   repertoire whose gradient is flat (continuous Baroque textures). […]
> - *Falsifier.* CODE. […] falsified if a boundary at an off-grid or lowest-level change point is
>   inadmissible, or if the term reads anything other than L1's class and period. […]

(The cuts marked […] drop the Temperley "small loss of accuracy" sentence, the Raphael & Stoddard sentence,
and procedural detail; none bears on the rows below.) *(§B item 1; former wording, preserved (#12): "The two
cuts marked […]".)*

**In plain words.** A *change point* is a moment where some note starts or stops. L1 labels each change point
with how strong a beat it falls on — its *metric strength class*: on the bar line, on a main beat, on a
weaker subdivision, and so on, each with the length of that level (its *period*), and *off-grid* for a
position on no level, such as inside a tuplet (`cowork_derived_specification_l0_l1_2026_09_03.md` S-35, seen at
search hits on lines 1001, 1003 and 1004; S-35 not read whole) *(§B item 2; former wording, preserved (#12):
"lines 1001–1004, *checked*")*. L2-S12 says:

1. When a reading puts a chord change at a change point, the candidate score gets a reward or cost depending
   on how strong that beat is ("chords tend to change on strong beats").
2. It is never a wall: a chord may change on any beat or off the beat.
3. How large each reward is gets **fitted** from annotated music, one value per class. None is set by hand.

**What L2-S12 is not.** It is about **where a chord changes**. It is not about how much weight a note carries
as evidence inside a chord's stretch (a long note on a downbeat counting for more than a short passing note).
That second use of metre is carried by three other statements (§A.5).

## §A.7 The method that stands — unchanged by this confirmation

Quoted or summarised from the derivation, *checked* this sitting:
- **L2-S12:** the metric strength class of the change point where a boundary falls, with its period, is a term
  on the boundary; its weights fitted per class; no metric level forbids a boundary.
- **L2-S25, L2-S28, L2-S32:** duration and metric position weight a note's evidence — as covariates of the
  chord-tone/elaboration decision (S25), in the bass term (S28), and in the content term's duration-weighted and
  metric-weighted forms (S32); L2-S25 says neither decides an assignment ("no duration cut and no metric position
  makes a note a chord tone or an elaboration") *(§B item 9; former wording, preserved (#12): "none decides by a
  cut.")*.
- **L2-S7:** any change point may end a chord; regularities of harmonic rhythm are terms, never walls.
- **L2-S11:** boundaries are decided together with tonality, chord and assignments, in one decision.
- **On L1's side (S-35, Ruling 47):** L1 publishes the class per change point; every weight is L2's, fitted.
