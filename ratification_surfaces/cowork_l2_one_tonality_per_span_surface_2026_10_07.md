## §A.1 What this family is

The derived statement, whole (*checked*, `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`, its lines 440–450):

> **L2-S16. Each span has exactly one tonality, and the tonality can change only at a span boundary.**
> - *Defense.* DP-E: "a tonality change is located at a harmonic boundary"; its rival, an independent
>   tonality track changing anywhere, "the output the analysis must produce cannot express" [RULED —
>   charter, DP-E].
> - *Source class.* Given — charter.
> - *Status.* Settled.
> - *Premise.* The charter's. **False-negative path:** a pivot chord analysts read in two tonalities at
>   once. L2-S19 carries it as rivals.
> - *Falsifier.* CODE. **Observable:** tonality per span. **Decision rule:** falsified if two tonalities
>   are published for one span as its principal reading. **Not falsified by:** a rival with a different
>   tonality over the same span.

In plain words: L2 cuts the music into harmonic spans (stretches over which one chord holds). Each span gets
one tonality in the principal reading — say C major. The tonality can change only where one span ends and
the next begins; it can never change in the middle of a chord. The DP-E quotation is the derivation's, from
the framework charter (*relayed*; `FRAMEWORK.md` was not opened this sitting).

**What the record has already said about this statement.**
- **Pivot-chord Ruling 1 of 2026-10-05** (*checked*, `cowork_rulings_2026_10_05_l2_pivot_chord_sitting.md`,
  its lines 33–38; the user's word "one"): "each harmonic span carries exactly one tonality in the principal
  reading. A chord an analyst reads in two tonalities at once (a pivot) is published as one span with one
  tonality, and the other tonality's reading of the same span as a rival with its mass. … **L2-S16 stands as
  derived**, untouched by this ruling." Position two (a span carrying two tonalities, which would have
  amended L2-S16) was declined there. So L2-S16's one false-negative path is already ruled.
- The same sitting cut L2-S16's rows **out** of the pivot family and left them "for the confirmation sets"
  (its lines 16–20, *checked*), because the statement restates the charter.
- **The route ruling of 2026-10-04** (*checked*, `cowork_rulings_2026_10_04_l2_route_sitting.md`, its
  lines 84–88): the writing side "bundles the families whose derived statement restates a ratified charter
  … as confirmation sets". L2-S16's source class is "Given — charter". **So this family is a confirmation
  set; there is no decision in it (D-599).**

## §A.4 The one changed proposal — Row 5.68 claim (i)

**The two rows.** Row 5.68 claim (i): a key-span is "a maximal run of slices in one local key". Row 49.6: a
key run is "a maximal run of consecutive segments sharing one key". They define the same thing — a stretch
of consecutive harmonic spans that all carry the same tonality. The comparison carried the first under
L2-S16 and relocated the second to L3 "a grouping read off the tonality per span, which decides nothing"
(*checked*, both rows).

**Why I propose filing both as RELOCATED to L3.**
- L2 decides one tonality per span and publishes it. A run of spans with the same tonality is computed from
  that, decides nothing, and L2's derivation names no such object (a search of the derivation for "maximal
  run", "key run", "tonality run", "run of spans" and "key-span" found none, *checked*).
- What L3 reads off is already on record: the one-decision record of 2026-10-06, with its Ruling 3 (D-022),
  records that D-022's "coarser is derived" "stands for what L3 reads off (phrases, key areas, sections)"
  (*checked*, its lines 106–110 — recorded as put, beside the ruling).
- The comparison's own reason at Row 49.6 applies word for word to Row 5.68 claim (i). Filing two
  statements of one notion in two places would leave the specification with two homes for it (#6).

**What does not move.** Rows 21.49 claim (i) and 43.100 claim (i) ("chord-spans ⊂ key-spans") stay carried
under L2-S16: they state the rule itself — no change of tonality inside a chord span — not the grouping.

**Documentation:** Row 5.68 claim (i) gets RELOCATED to *L3 — The read-off facts*, travelling with Row 49.6,
instead of ADOPTED — carried. **Algorithm:** nothing changes.

**Towards the objective (#4):** no reading changes. **Towards the principles:** #6 (one home for one
notion); the charter's one-way dependency, "L3 reads L2" (L2-S49, *checked*). **Meta level:** filing only.
**If you prefer the comparison's proposal unchanged,** Row 5.68 claim (i) stays carried and Row 49.6
relocated; nothing else in this surface changes.
