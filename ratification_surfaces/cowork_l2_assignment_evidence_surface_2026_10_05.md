## 2. The three derived statements

**L2-S24 — where the voice-leading evidence comes from.** Quoted: "The voice-leading evidence an elaboration relation needs is read from L1's per-voice relations. These are, per onset change point and notated voice, the silence before the onset, the time since that voice's preceding attack, and the spelled melodic interval from its preceding note, with the chordal count. The *following* note of an event is read as the preceding-note relation published at the next onset in the same voice. Where a voice is chordal at either end, L2 has no published successor for a given note, and it does not construct one (OQ-L2-9…)."

- Its defence: these relations are defined by how a note is approached and left. L1 publishes the per-voice relations, which is Ruling 49 of the L0/L1 sitting. Consumers read and never re-derive (D-100). And pairing the notes of two chords into lines is voice separation, which the analysis never infers (D-389).
- Its status: settled for single-note voices; open for chordal voices (OQ-L2-9).
- Its falsifier: "falsified if the term reads any melodic interval not published by L1, or pairs notes across a chordal voice."

**L2-S25 — what duration and metre do.** Quoted: "Duration and metric position bear on an assignment as terms (covariates). Neither decides it: no duration cut and no metric position makes a note a chord tone or an elaboration."

- Its defence, all relayed by the derivation and not read by me:
  - ledger C26: duration weight cannot separate a long non-chord tone from a genuine added tone;
  - ledger C4: in an arpeggio the non-root tone can carry more duration than the root;
  - Hu & Arthur 2021: a classifier on surface features (duration, metric position, approach and departure intervals) beats "every note is a chord tone" by only about four to five points out of sample;
  - Raphael & Stoddard 2003: metrical position is a learned covariate, not a rule.
- Its status: settled in shape, with the weights to be measured.

**L2-S26 — grace notes and ornament signs.** Quoted: "Grace notes … and ornament signs … are evidence for the assignment of their host and for the chord. They carry no assignment of their own, because they are not sounding events. Whether L2 also publishes a relation for a grace attachment, such as 'appoggiatura to its host', is OQ-L2-10."

- Its defence: S-16 of the ratified L0/L1 specification, which I read: a grace note opens no change point and belongs to no sounding set, and it is published as an attachment of its host, with its notated form. The charter's assignment is "per sounding note". And D-295 (every inferred object must be displayable) is why OQ-L2-10 is asked rather than answered no.
- Its known weakness, stated in its own premise: a long appoggiatura that sounds for half its host's value, where L2 cannot place a harmony change inside the host.

## 5. Question 1 — is a note's chord-tone-or-elaboration status decided by a fixed rule on its voice leading, or scored? (a real choice)

**What is being decided.** Whether the specification fixes in advance which notes *can* be elaborations by their stepwise structure (the three-tier rule, D-333), or makes stepwise structure, metric position and duration *evidence* that L2's one decision weighs against everything else (L2-S25, and D-527's shape). Today's Ruling 1 already settles what an elaboration is called; this is about whether it is one.

**Position one — scored (L2-S25 as derived; the recommendation).** Approach, departure, metric position and duration are terms of the score. No fixed tier makes a note a chord tone or an elaboration. The relation's name still follows its definition once the reading has made the note an elaboration. A foreign note that fits no definition is the unclassified elaboration. D-333's second half, the weighting set per idiom, is carried, because L2-S25's weights are measured. Its first half, the fixed structure, does not carry into L2.

- *Towards the objective (#4).* Hu & Arthur (relayed) found that the same surface features beat "all chord tones" by only four to five points out of sample. A fixed rule over them decides wrongly wherever they mislead. The charter excludes a first-running detector because its mistakes "are unrecoverable downstream", and says a reading the search cannot reach "cannot be found however good the scoring is"; that a fixed rule on the note's motion is of the same kind is my reading. Scored, the chord fit and the neighbouring harmonies can outweigh them.
- *Towards the principles.* #12: no assignment is excluded before scoring. #6: D-333 and D-527 currently say different things, and this keeps the later one. The charter's DP-D ground, that the distinction "is functional and voice-leading" (read), is met by reading the voice leading as evidence.
- *The cost.* The weights must be fitted, and the derivation knows no annotated corpus with per-note chord-tone labels (OQ-L2-13). Until one is found or you rule otherwise, the weights are fitted and graded only through the chord. A fixed rule needs no fit. D-333's register status would be settled at the ratification act. The rows: 6.88, 6.92(ii), 6.198(i), 6.91 and 6.90 go HISTORICAL, as a design of the per-slice chord decoder decided before D-527 and DP-D (the shape Ruling 2 used for D-385's rows today).
- *Meta level:* the analysis's own specification.

**Position two — the three-tier rule, fixed (D-333 carried).** Tiers 1 and 2 bind; metric weight decides only the one-sided cases, with that weighting set per idiom. In the charter's terms this becomes part of L2's admission rule (my reading): readings that break a tier are never considered.

- *Towards the objective.* It is the textbook definitions applied directly (#1), and it shrinks the search, which bears on the tractability question OQ-L2-8 (★, a later surface). Against that, an admission rule bounds what the analysis can ever find (the charter, read). An example, my reading: an added sixth approached and left by step between chord tones could never be read as a chord member. A foreign note leapt to and from would be forced into the chord, "which then changes the chord" (L2-S8's premise). That is the false-negative path Ruling 1 was ruled to close.
- *Towards the principles.* It keeps D-333 and leaves D-527's covariate design unused for the stepwise and metric evidence (#6 the other way). Tier 2 conflicts with today's Ruling 1, which admitted an unclassified elaboration so that a foreign note is not forced into the chord (#12).
- *The cost.* L2-S25 is rewritten. The six rows become ADOPTED (6.90 adopted as the comparer proposed). D-527's covariate list is noted at the ratification act as not carried for these features.
- *Meta level:* the analysis.

**Position three — only tier 2 fixed, as an admission constraint (Row 6.90's proposal); tiers 1 and 3 scored.**

- *Towards the objective.* A smaller search than position one. It keeps tier 2's forced chord tone, with the same cost as position two for a foreign note leapt to and from.
- *Towards the principles.* It contradicts Ruling 1's unclassified elaboration for exactly those notes (#12).
- *Meta level:* the analysis.

**Recommendation: position one.** Towards the objective, the measured weakness of these surface features is a reason to weigh them, not let them decide. Towards the principles, of the three positions it is the one that sits with D-527, DP-D's ground and today's Ruling 1 together, at the cost of D-333's fixed structure and of weights that cannot yet be fitted against per-note labels.

*What the charter does not settle, said so it is not over-read:* its general sentence "a reading-shaped evidence producer is a score and never a constraint" is about producers of whole readings. In my reading it does not reach a rule on a single note's motion, so I do not rest question 1 on it.

## 7. Question 3 — does L2 publish a relation for a grace note (OQ-L2-10): a confirmation, not a choice

**What the record holds.** The charter has L2 publish the assignment and relation "per sounding note" (read). S-16 says a grace note belongs to no sounding set and is published, with its notated form, as an attachment of its host (read). L3 is the layer for facts read off a settled reading (read).

**My reading, tagged.** Whether a grace note clashes with the decided chord, and how it moves to its host, are fixed functions of L2's published chord and L1's published attachment. Nothing about them is decided. So they are read-off facts, L3's if a consumer wants them, and computing them later loses nothing (#12, the recomputable clause). D-295 is met because L2 infers no grace relation that goes undisplayed. Publishing one at L2 would extend L2's publication list, a charter amendment for no gain to the chord (the grace already enters the chord decision as evidence, under L2-S26).

**What you are asked to confirm.** That L2 publishes no relation for a grace note. OQ-L2-10 is answered no, and L2-S26 stands as derived. *If not confirmed:* the L2 charter's publication list gains a per-grace relation, and L2-S26 is amended. *Either way:* the rows go as proposed (43.125(ii) and 44.15(i) carried; 23.336 and 38.4 quarantined).
