## 1. The three statements, quoted, and what the record already decides about each

### L3-S14 — the fit

> *"Every weight of the phrase model is fitted from annotated phrase boundaries, held out by piece, with its capacity
> declared before the fit, on licence-clear music only. No value is given here."*

In plain words: no number in the phrase model is set by hand. Every number is learned from music where people have marked
the phrase boundaries. Pieces used for learning are never used for grading. How much the model may learn is fixed before
learning starts. And only music whose licence permits it may be used (public domain, CC0, CC-BY).

**What the record already decides.** The derivation grounds the statement on two ruled sources: pack principle #20 (no
value is graded on data that helped fit it), carried in L2 by L2-S38; and D-292 (a shipped value is fitted only on
public-domain, CC0 and CC-BY music), as relayed in L2-S38. L2-S38's own defense ties the held-out data and the capacity
budget to #20 (L2 derivation, lines 952–953: *"Held-out data and a capacity budget are declared before fitting [RULED —
principle #20, pack member 02]"*). The L2 fit sitting (`cowork_rulings_2026_10_05_l2_fit_sitting.md`) ruled on L2-S38:
its Ruling 1 amended L2-S38's **second sentence**, which reads in the derivation *"The fit is discriminative, on held-out
folds, with its capacity budget declared in advance"* (L2 derivation, line 949), to *"The tables are counted from
annotated music, once, and frozen; the short list of combination weights is fitted discriminatively, to the graded
measure"* (record lines 33–35). That amendment changes L2's text. It does not change principle #20 or D-292, which are
what L3-S14 rests on. *[Corrected by the check, §5 item 2. Former wording: "Each clause rests on a ruled source the
derivation cites: … L2-S38 was ratified by the L2 fit sitting (…). That sitting's Ruling 1 amended L2-S38's second
sentence (…). L3-S14 does not cite that sentence; the sentences it does cite stand unchanged ("Every other sentence of
L2-S38 stands", record line 36)."]*

**Its open question is not for the user.** L3-S14's status is *Open (OQ-L3-11)*. OQ-L3-11 (derivation lines 1247–1251)
reads: *"A measurement, not a ruling."* The numbers can only come from a fit, and the fit needs licence-clear annotated
music that has not yet been found. Investigation is already ruled (D-254, relayed from entry 291 §1; not opened by this
sitting), so nothing is put.

**Not decided here, and not raised as a question:** whether L3's weights are fitted to the graded measure, as L2's now are
by the L2 fit Ruling 1. L3-S14 does not say. The L2 ruling answered DP-P "for L2's detail specification" (record
line 36) and does not reach L3.

### L3-S41 — the same answer however the music was loaded

> *"L3 is deterministic. Its publication after any sequence of enlargements, or after an edit, equals a single fresh run
> over the final span and the edited record."*

In plain words: if the selection is widened step by step, or the score is edited, L3's answer must be exactly what a
single fresh run over the final music would give.

**What the record already decides.** D-264 states the rule for the whole system (quoted in the defense, as relayed by
L2-S48: *"The result after any sequence of extensions must equal a single fresh run over the final loaded span"*; D-264
not opened by this sitting). L2 meets it through L2-S48; the face-h ratification record lists *"working-span Rulings 1
to 3 (L2-S48)"* among its grounds (line 43). L3's inputs are L2's publication plus L0's and L1's, which meet it too
(IC-L1, Ruling 46 (ii), as the defense cites it; not opened). The tie order that removes L3's last source of
nondeterminism is L3-S36, ratified with L3-S32 to L3-S35 by mass-and-status Ruling 2 (relayed from entry 503 §1; that
record not opened). The derivation marks the statement *"Settled"* (line 1165). *[Corrected by the check, §5 item 4:
relays marked. Former wording ended "… ratified by mass-and-status Ruling 2. Nothing is open."]*

### L3-S42 — two dependencies on subjects L3 does not own

> *"Two dependencies on out-of-scope subjects are stated as statements in their own right. (i) **The voice-leading
> axis.** L3's phrase is the harmonic spine's phrase, which tiles. The accepted melodic phrase, which overlaps across
> voices, is the axis's, and L3 reads no fact of the axis in this specification. (ii) **The measurement of the
> analysis.** How an annotation's phrase signs, cadence labels, gaps and elisions are mapped onto L3's tiling and its
> features for grading is the measurement-design stage's (OQ-L3-12)."*

In plain words: (i) the phrase L3 works with cuts across all the voices at once and the phrases follow one another with
no gaps or overlaps. The melodic phrase of a single voice, which can overlap with other voices' phrases, belongs to the
voice-leading side of the design, and L3 reads nothing from that side. (ii) How human annotations are matched against
L3's output for grading is decided by the later measurement stage, not by L3.

**What the record already decides.**
- (i) rests on the charter, `FRAMEWORK.md` lines 500–513: *"every span kind on the harmonic spine cuts across the whole
  texture at once and its members tile the music, whereas a melodic phrase does not"*. The charter's L3 block names
  L3's unit as the phrase (line 479: *"the phrase and section grouping"*).
- The premise of (i) had one possible failure, thematic parallelism (OQ-L3-5), in case it had to come from the
  voice-leading side. That was answered on 2026-11-03 (`cowork_rulings_2026_11_03_l3_thematic_parallelism_sitting.md`,
  lines 86–88): *"L3-S42's premise (lines 1186–1188) is answered — the relation is published by L1, not the axis."* So
  "L3 reads no fact of the axis" still holds.
- (ii) rests on the charter, lines 521–522: the measurement of the analysis is *"a later stage's business"*. OQ-L3-12 is
  addressed to that stage, not to the user.

**One tension carried, not opened.** The derivation notes (lines 1180–1181) that *"neither L2's nor L3's consume line
names the axis"*, left by L2 for the whole-architecture coherence review. The L2 face-d ratification carried the same
tension beside L2-S24 (record line 57). Nothing new here.

## 2. The one differing row — Row 37.18

**The old text** (`cowork_voiceleading_axis_design.md`, lines 66–67, in its §0 list of one-sense declarations, quoted
at reading file line 43600):

> *"The harmonic-grouping unit is always the **punctuation-span** (Layer 6's object, `cowork_layer6_grouping_design.md`
> §0), never "phrase"."*

**The derived text** (L3-S42): *"L3's phrase is the harmonic spine's phrase, which tiles."*

**The reading file's proposal** (line 43612): UNPLACED — *"a rule on L3's own subject, the unit of the harmonic grouping,
that a derived statement differs from."*

**What the record already decides, as this side reads it.**
- The charter names L3's grouping unit *"phrase"* (line 479) and gives L3 *"the phrase reading"* (lines 494–498).
- The old rule banned the word "phrase" for the harmonic unit so that one word would not carry two senses (the harmonic
  span and the melodic phrase). The charter and L3-S42 keep the two senses apart by naming them differently: *"the
  harmonic spine's phrase"* on one side and *"the accepted melodic phrase"* on the voice-leading side (charter
  line 502).
- The old row's L2 twin, Row 46.12 (L2 reading file, lines 63997–64007), was ruled RELOCATED to L3 by nothing-from-L3
  Ruling 5. That moved the row to this layer; it did not decide the word.
- The phrase-units sitting's Ruling 1 adopted L3-S12 as derived (record line 45; its text at lines 47–48: *"The phrase
  reading tiles the working span with two kinds of unit — phrases, and the stretches between phrases …"*), using the
  word as the charter does. *[Corrected by the check, §5 item 3. Former wording: "The phrase-units sitting ratified
  L3-S12 (*"The phrase reading tiles …"*, record lines 30 and 45), using the word as the charter does."]*

**Proposed filing, this side's derivation: HISTORICAL** — the old naming rule is replaced by the charter's L3 block and
L3-S42, which keep the two senses apart by two names rather than by banning one. **Documentation only.**

**The strongest point against.** You want language that leaves as little room for misunderstanding as possible
(2026-08-17). "Phrase" alone, without "harmonic" or "melodic" in front, can still mean either. The old rule took the
word away from the harmonic unit entirely. Filing the row HISTORICAL accepts that "phrase" on the spine is always to be
read as the harmonic spine's phrase. If the alternative is wanted (keep the word "punctuation-span" for L3's unit), the
charter's L3 block and every ratified L3 statement that says "phrase" would need rewording. This side found no ground in
what it read that asks for that, so it does not put it as an alternative.

## 3. Documentation and algorithm, kept separate

**Documentation only, if the filing in §2 stands:** Row 37.18 marked HISTORICAL in the reading file, by the application
dispatch, not now.

**Algorithmic:** nothing is decided in this first cut. When the three statements are ratified (after the second cut),
they become part of L3's ratified specification. Measured against what L3 already does, that **changes nothing**.

**The currently decided method, on these three subjects.**
- L3's phrase model has no hand-set numbers. Every number is to be fitted from licence-clear music with marked phrase
  boundaries, held out by piece, with a capacity declared first. Until that fit exists the model is provisional, and
  nothing resting on it carries load (L3-S33, as L3-S14's premise cites it).
- L3 is deterministic. Widening the selection or editing the score gives the same result as one fresh run over the
  final music.
- L3's phrase cuts across all the voices and the phrases follow one another without gaps or overlaps. L3 reads nothing
  from the voice-leading side. Thematic restatement comes from L1. Grading against annotations is the measurement
  stage's.
