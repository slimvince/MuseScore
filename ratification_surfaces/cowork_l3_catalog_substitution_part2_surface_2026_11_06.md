## 2. The vocabulary, and terms used below

**Dispositions** as part 1 §2: **ADOPTED — proposed**, **RELOCATED**, **UNPLACED**, the re-marking left to the
application act. **The waiting points** are part 1's, by the same names (§1.1). Plain words, as part 1 §2:
- **a substitution entry** — a catalog record of an operation that replaces a chord with another filling the same role;
- **a substitution mapping** — the rule a substitution entry states. Part 1 §2 glossed it *"from the replaced member to
  its substitute"*; Row 57.432's glossary entry states it the other way, *"mapping a substituted surface chord to the
  function it replaces"* **[checked]**. Both name one relation read in opposite directions; the recogniser reads it from
  the surface chord, so Row 57.432's direction is the one a match uses **[my reading]**;
- **through a substitution** — a progression entry's member filled by a mapped substitute instead of the member as
  written, the rest of the entry matched as written.
