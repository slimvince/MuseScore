## 4. Documentation or algorithm

**This surface only files documentation. No algorithmic change is decided.** That a phrase end may fall inside a
harmonic span was already decided by phrase-ends Ruling 1 (its candidate set: every change point). What this surface
adds is the filing of two rows and one sentence in the amended L3 specification stating the relation, as span-kinds
Ruling 1 part C requires.

**The currently decided method for L3, as it bears on this group** (from the records in §1 **[checked at each]**): L2
divides the working span into harmonic spans, each with one chord and one tonality, with rivals and their mass, and two
adjacent spans never share both. L3 decides where phrases end as a weighed choice among every change point and both
edges of the working span, publishing the principal partition with rivals and their mass; no item of evidence forces
or forbids a phrase end; the evidence is L1's notated evidence, cadential evidence read off L2's decided spans, a
phrase-length term, and (if measured to repay) slurs and dynamics. The phrase reading tiles the working span with
phrases and the stretches between them. A change of tonality may fall inside a phrase unit, and a phrase may end
without one. L3 never revises L2. *(★ CORRECTED 2026-11-08 by the check (§7C item 8): two parts of this paragraph rest
