## 4. Documentation and algorithm, separately

- **Documentation only.** Part A carries two test-case questions as measurement matters. Part B records 39 row-parts as
  reached by phrase-ends Ruling 1, with the D-479 note. Part C ratifies the placements of 7 row-parts as their records
  state them. No row is re-marked now; the re-marking is the application dispatch's act (route record §2 step 2).
- **Algorithm: no change.** The currently decided method, as the rulings these items rest on state it:
  - **Phrase ends.** L3 decides where phrases end as a weighed choice over every candidate position, including positions
    the page does not mark. The principal partition is published with its rivals and their mass. The old strength
    profile and the notated markers enter only as evidence beside the cadential evidence; no marker forces a phrase end
    and no picked peak decides one (phrase-ends Rulings 1 and 2, 2026-11-02).
  - **Cadences.** The cadence is placed at the phrase ends L3 has decided, an elided one included
    (cadence-placement record, lines 37 and 55, read at those lines by search; the record not read whole).
  - **Figured bass.** The span figure is published in full with short forms as views, plus a second, continuo-grain
    figure read off L1's per-slice intervals (figured-bass Ruling 1).
  - **Measurement.** Values are fitted later, and test cases come from the measurement of the analysis.
