the three statements and the one differing row (Row 37.18) and left the 76 agreeing row-parts unplaced. This cut reads

agreeing claim.

- **L3-S41**, as the reading file quotes it (line 40476–40477 **[checked]**): *"Its publication after any sequence of
  enlargements, or after an edit, equals a single fresh run over the final span and the edited record."* In plain
  words: widening the selection step by step, or editing the score, gives exactly what one fresh run over the final
  music gives.
- **L3-S14**, as the reading file quotes it (lines 9898–9899 **[checked]**): *"Every weight of the phrase model is
  fitted from annotated phrase boundaries, held out by piece, with its capacity declared before the fit, on
  licence-clear music only. No value is given here."* In plain words: no number in L3's phrase model is set by hand;
  every number is learned from music where people have marked the phrases, pieces used for learning are never used for
  grading, how much the model may learn is fixed first, and only freely licensed music is used.
- **The row lists**, at the reading file's §7 (lines 78553 and 78691 **[checked]**): L3-S14 32 agreeing, none
  differing; L3-S41 3 agreeing, none differing.

## 4. Documentation and the algorithm, separately

- **Algorithm: no change.** The currently decided method on these two subjects (as the first cut §3 states it):
  L3's phrase model has no hand-set numbers; every number is to be fitted from licence-clear music with marked phrase
  boundaries, held out by piece, with a capacity declared first; until that fit exists the model is provisional. L3 is
  deterministic: widening the selection or editing the score gives the same result as one fresh run over the final
  music.
