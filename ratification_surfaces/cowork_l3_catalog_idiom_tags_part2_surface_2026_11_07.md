## 1. What this surface does, in plain words

### 1.1 The subject

`cowork_idiom_entry_mapping.md` (the L3 reading file's member 53) is a provisional working mapping that gives each entry
of the catalog of named progressions an **idiom tag**: one or more of the five idioms (diatonic-functional,
chromatic-functional, seventh-functional, triadic-modal, chromatic-coloristic; the member numbers them 1 to 5 **[the
numbering checked at the reading file's manifest, line 69121; that the numbers follow the order of the names is my
reading, from the glosses at Rows 53.3 ("seventh-functional") and 53.8 ("triadic-modal"); the member's banner is in its
cut and was not read]**). Its two tables tag the named progressions and schemas (its §5.2, Rows 53.1 to 53.13) and the
substitution operations (its §5.3, Rows 53.14 to 53.22). Each of the 21 row-parts states which tag one entry, or one
group of entries named on a single table row, carries. *(Former wording, kept (#12): "… (its §5.3, Rows 53.14 to
53.21). Each of the 21 row-parts states which tag one entry carries." — Row 53.22 is also in §5.3, and Rows 53.1, 53.2,
53.8 and 53.13 each name more than one entry.)* The reading file graded every one
THE DERIVATION IS SILENT and proposed every one UNPLACED, *"read as at Row 44.11 and Row 46.40: the entry's idiom tag is
an attribute of a catalog entry, and no charter of `FRAMEWORK.md` §5 names the catalog of named progressions"*
**[checked at each of the 21 entries, L3 reading file lines 69141 to 69476]**.
