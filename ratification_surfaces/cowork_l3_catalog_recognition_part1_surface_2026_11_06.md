the rest as the inventory §1D describes member 44 **[relayed]**). The consumer was dormant when those documents were
written (Row 8.94, *"the current dormant state"* **[checked]**; Row 24.1, *"Until the recognition consumer is built"*,
**[relayed from the inventory §1N]**). In the ratified framework L2 decides chords and keys and L3 reads off facts from
