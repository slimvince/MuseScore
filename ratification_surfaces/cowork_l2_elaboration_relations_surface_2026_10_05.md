### A.1 What this is

On 2026-10-04 the user ruled (Alternative A of the L2 route sitting) that the finished L2 comparison goes to
a ratified L2 specification the way the L0/L1 comparison did: disposition rulings by family, one surface per

This is the second.

**The objects, re-explained.** *The derivation* is `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`, at the
repository root: forty-nine statements about how L2 should work, written by a session that had read a brief,
a ten-member boot pack and the L0/L1 input contract, and nothing else of this project. Each statement has six
fields: the statement, its defence, its source class, its status, its premise with the premise's
false-negative path, and what would falsify it. *The reading file* is
`ratification_surfaces/cowork_comparison_l2_reading.md`: it sets every sentence of the project's existing
texts about L2 beside the derived statements, marks each as AGREES, DIFFERS or THE DERIVATION IS SILENT, and
*proposes* a fate for each — ADOPTED (carried by a derived statement, or proposed as an addition), RELOCATED
(another layer's business), QUARANTINED (describes the implementation; an audit question is written),
HISTORICAL (an event, a status, a superseded plan), DISCARDED, or UNPLACED (the comparer could not place it
without choosing between the texts; the user places it). Nothing in it is applied.

*L2* is the layer the ratified charter (`FRAMEWORK.md` §5) calls "The tonal reading. The one entangled
decision": given what the notation supplies, it decides together the tonality at each moment, where each
harmony gives way to the next, which sounding notes belong to the harmony and which decorate it, and what
chord is read over each span. *An elaboration* is a sounding note that does not belong to the chord it sounds
against — a passing note, a neighbour note, a suspension, and so on. *An elaboration relation* is the name of
the way such a note relates to the chord: how it is approached and left, or that it is held over from the
previous harmony, or held against changing harmony. *An assignment*, in the derivation's words (its §0), is
"the chord-tone or elaboration decision for one event within one span, with its relation where it is an
elaboration."

### A.6 Question 1 — which elaboration relations L2 admits (OQ-L2-4 ★)

**What is being decided.** The list of names an elaboration may carry in the first L2 specification. The
ratified charter (2026-08-29) names four and does not say the list is complete. Two earlier ratified
decisions, D-527 (2026-07-19) and D-207 (2026-07-26), carry a different label list and a pedal-point class.
The derivation says the four are a floor and asks the user about the rest. **No position fits every ratified
text: each position changes or reads past one of them.**

**Position one — the charter's four, complete.** Passing, neighbour, suspension, anticipation; any other
decoration is forced into one of those or read as a chord tone. *Towards the objective (#4):* the
derivation's false-negative path is the cost — an appoggiatura, leapt to and resolved by step, fits none of
the four; it is either mislabelled or becomes a chord tone, "which then changes the chord" (L2-S8's premise).
The chord is what the hard stop grades. *Towards the principles:* #12 — the fact that a note is foreign to the
chord is dropped when it is forced to be a chord tone; #1 — the published rule set the derivation relays uses
seven models, not four (relayed, not read by this sitting). *The ratified texts:* the charter's list stands
unchanged; D-527's label list and D-207's class do not carry into L2's specification, which needs saying for
both at the ratification act. *Meta level:* the analysis's own specification.

**Position two — the four, plus the record's own two, plus a residual class.** Passing, neighbour,
suspension, anticipation; appoggiatura and escape tone (the two one-sided relations — leapt to and left by
step, stepped to and left by leap — which member 6 already treats at Row 6.91; D-527 names the appoggiatura,
not the escape tone); pedal point (D-207's class: a tone held or restruck against changing harmony in any
voice); and an explicit **unclassified elaboration** — a note the reading finds foreign to the chord that fits
no named relation. **Retardation is written as a suspension that resolves upward, a kind of suspension rather
than a name of its own.** That is this sitting's proposal: the derivation lists retardation separately; the
relayed rule set pairs the two as "suspension/retardation"; example score 3 shows that retardations occur.
*Towards the objective:* no note is forced into a wrong relation or into the chord; the chord is read from the
notes that belong to it; a dominant pedal under moving harmony becomes expressible as what it is. *Towards the
principles:* #12 (the residual class carries rather than drops); #6 (the charter's list, D-527's list and
