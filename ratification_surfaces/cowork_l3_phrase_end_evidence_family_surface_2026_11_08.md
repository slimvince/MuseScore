## 4. Documentation or algorithm

**Documentation only. Algorithm: no change.**
- 3A records two rows as decided by a ruling already given.
- 3B files a sentence under evidence already admitted, and binds no constant.

The method that stands: L2 decides key and chord together and publishes the chord as a degree in its tonality, and the
tonality as tonic and mode. L3 publishes facts read off it: chord symbol, figured bass, harmonic rhythm, cadence with
type at each phrase end, phrase and section grouping, key areas, the pivot re-expression, the modal counts per key
area, and, once the application act amends `FRAMEWORK.md`, the catalog matches. L3 decides where phrases end as a
weighed choice over every candidate position, the notated flags and the re-homed strength profile entering as evidence
with fitted weights and no item forcing or forbidding a phrase end. No preset, style label or idiom mixture reaches L2.
No language model is part of L2's or L3's method. *(As stated in the family-6 and family-7 ruling records, §1, with
phrase-ends Ruling 1 added.)*
