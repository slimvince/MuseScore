## 4. Documentation or algorithm

**Documentation only. Algorithm: no change.** No part adds a published fact, an input, a field, a check or a setting to
L3. The method that stands: L2 decides key and chord together and publishes the chord as a degree in its tonality, and
the tonality as tonic and mode; L3 publishes facts read off it (chord symbol, figured bass, harmonic rhythm, cadence with

L2; no preset chooses the catalog L3 reads, and L3's method uses no idiom tag; a display turns published facts into text;
