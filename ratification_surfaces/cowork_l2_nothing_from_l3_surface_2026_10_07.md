## §A.1 What this family is

The derived statement, whole (*checked*, `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`, its lines 1175–1191):

> **L2-S49. L2 consumes nothing L3 publishes: no cadence type, phrase or section grouping, chord symbol,
> figured bass or harmonic rhythm. Where a statement above would have wanted one, it reads the L1
> evidence it is decided from instead. The dependency is one-way: L3 reads L2.**

In plain words: the chord-and-key layer (L2) decides the key, where the harmonies change, which notes are
chord notes and what the chord is. The layer above it (L3) then reads facts off that settled reading: the
cadence at each phrase end and its type, the grouping into phrases and sections, the chord symbol, the
figured bass and the harmonic rhythm. L2-S49 says L2 never reads any of those back. Where an older design
wanted L2 (or the old key or function layer) to use one of them, L2 reads instead the evidence from the
note layer (L1) that the fact would have been built from.

Its defense (*checked*): "L3 → L2: Nothing. A read-off fact may not revise the decision it was read off"
[RULED — charter, contract table]. The framework's contract table carries that line (*checked*,
`FRAMEWORK.md` line 484), and L3's charter says it "may not consume anything L2 did not publish, and it may
not revise L2" and lists what it publishes (*checked*, `FRAMEWORK.md` lines 426–433). You ratified the
charters and contracts on 2026-08-29 (*relayed*, Ruling 2 of that record, as the one-decision surface quotes
it). The derivation names three places where a want arose and how each is met inside L2: L2-S13 (phrase-
shaped notated evidence read as L1 evidence, not as L3's phrase decision), L2-S34 (cadential confirmation
read from the proposed chords, not from L3's cadence type) and L2-S14 (a restatement of the same chord after
a phrase end is L3's to divide). *(§B item 1; former wording: "and L2-S14.")* Source class: "Given — charter".
Status: "Settled".

Its premise and known gap (*checked*): "L1's evidence plus the progression term carry what L2 needs from
phrase and cadence." The false-negative path it names: an expert who places a modulation to respect a
2-bar + 2-bar phrase structure; L2 cannot use the phrase reading, and "its boundary rival carries the
expert's placement with its mass". *(§B item 2; former wording: "its boundary rival carries the expert's
placement with its weight".)* Its falsifier: "falsified if L2 reads any L3 output", and "not
falsified by L2 reading L1's notated phrase evidence".

**The rows.** Section 7 of `ratification_surfaces/cowork_comparison_l2_reading.md` lists 184 row-parts for

## §A.4 The 22 UNPLACED parts: one decision and three confirmations

Every one of the 22 is an older text in which something L3 now owns flows back into the choice of key or
chord. They fall into four groups by what flows back. In three of them the record decides the placement and
what remains is your confirmation. In one (§A.4.1) I found two placements the record does not choose
between, so it is put as a decision with its alternatives.

**What HISTORICAL means in this section, and what it does not** *(added at the check, §B item 4, after your
question whether cadences and progressions are being dropped as inputs)*. What is placed HISTORICAL below is
the **route** the old texts describe: a later layer detecting cadences, phrases or named progressions from
an already-decided result and feeding them back to revise the key or chord. The **musical evidence is not
dropped.** Inside L2, while the readings still compete:
- the tonality terms "read the chords the reading proposes (degree, function, cadential progressions)"
  (L2-S20, *checked*);
- the progression term rates each pair of neighbouring chords as degrees in their key, and cadential
  confirmation "enters through the progression term over the proposed cadence chords" (L2-S34 Ruling 2's
  ground, *checked*);
- L1's cadence cues are read as their establishment status allows: the leading-tone cue now, the
  falling-fifth and fourth-and-seventh cues once their window is established (L2-S37, OQ-L2-12, *checked*);
- the notated phrase evidence (fermatas, bar lines, rests, repeats) enters as L1's flags, as terms on where a
  harmony may change (L2-S13, *checked*).
The one limit is length: L2 rates single stretches and neighbouring pairs, so a pattern longer than a pair (a
whole sequence, a ii–V–I as one unit, phrase symmetry) is seen link by link. Whether L2 needs to see such
patterns whole is DP-O, open and not foreclosed by anything here.

### §A.4.1 Decision 1 — how to file the old route that fed a recognised harmonic sequence back to the key decision (D-494, D-504, D-505): 9 parts

**What the old texts say.** A recognised harmonic sequence (the same progression repeated at successive
transpositions, for example a chain of applied dominants moving down by step) implies that the key is moving.
The old design had a separate recogniser of named progressions, working over chords already decided, hand
each recognised sequence back to the function layer as evidence of a key change. Its rules: the recogniser
always hands it over; it corroborates or tempers a cadence's vote; and where no authentic cadence confirms
the new key, it stands in as the confirming evidence at a lower weight (*checked*, D-504's quotation at

not require a cadence", naming "sustained dominant emphasis" and "recognized transposition sequences, the
