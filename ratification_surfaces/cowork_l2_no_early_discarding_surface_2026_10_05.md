## §A. The surface

### 1. Where we are, and the cut

You are ruling, family by family, on how the new specification of the chord-and-key layer should read.
That layer decides, all at once, the key of each stretch, where one harmony gives way to the next, the
chord over each stretch, and which notes are chord notes and which are passing notes, suspensions and the
like. One complete answer of that kind is a **reading**. Eleven families are ruled. What remains of the
first list are the **confirmation sets**: statements of the derived specification that restate something
already ratified, whose rows need their placements confirmed (entry 279 §2 item 2, *checked*). Entry 292
names the first of them as three derived statements, L2-S35, L2-S42 and L2-S43.

**I propose to take L2-S43 alone in this set**, and to move the other two out of the confirmation sets:

- **L2-S43** (no reading thrown away before the end; exact ties) restates the framework's own rule and
  agrees with a ruling of yours; the record decides it (§2).
- **L2-S42** (the layer may leave out of its publication the rival readings below a declared weight, if they
  can be recomputed) **meets four standing texts that point the other way**: D-006, "two full candidate
  lists, with no truncation" (*checked* at the register's index line only); and, *checked* at the reading
  file's rows, a ratified amendment, "No truncation constant
  exists anywhere in the publication" (Row 49.23 claim (i)); D-331, the alternatives carried "never pruned"
  (Row 6.190); and the silence family's ground of today, "nothing computed is withheld from the display".
  The first three were written for the current engines, whose lists are per stretch; whether they bind a
  list of whole readings is part of the question (*my reading*). Against them stands a fact of size: a
  whole reading chooses one label per stretch out of about 60,000 to 12 million (the sizing, §3.3,
  *checked*), so two stretches alone already allow billions of whole readings, and listing every one is not
  practical (*my reading*). That is a real choice, not a confirmation. It belongs
  with what the layer publishes about rivals — L2-S35 (how the weights are normalised), L2-S40 and L2-S41
  (the rivals and their weights), L2-S45 (what crosses to the next layer). **L2-S35 goes with it**, because
  the whole-reading weight it defines is what L2-S42's threshold is measured in.

You may change this cut.

### 2. The statement, and what the record already decides

**L2-S43, whole** (*checked*, `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`): "No reading is discarded before
the whole working span, context included, has been scored. Where two readings have equal candidate scores to
the precision computed, both are published with equal mass. The principal is chosen between them by a
declared, deterministic rule that the publication names, and the tie is flagged." Its defense: the first
sentence is "the charter's [RULED — charter, L2 "May not"]"; on ties, a tie decided "silently by whichever
reading the arithmetic happens to reach first would make the published principal depend on evaluation order
rather than on the music, and would not be reproducible [RULED — principle #16]". Source class: given by
the charter for the first sentence, derived for ties. Status: settled. Premise: exact ties are rare, with a
bare fifth read as major or minor named as the case where they are not. Falsifier: one of two tied readings
absent, or the tie not flagged.

In musical terms: the analysis keeps every reading it has started weighing until it has heard the whole of
the music loaded for your selection; and when two readings come out exactly equal — a bare fifth, C and G
with no third, which could be C major or C minor — both are shown with equal weight, one is named first by a fixed,
published rule, and the tie is marked.

**What the record decides, so nothing here is asked (D-599):**

1. **The first sentence is the framework's own rule** — the L2 charter forbids discarding "a rival before
   the whole sequence of spans has been scored" (*relayed* from the tractability surface, which read it at
   `FRAMEWORK.md`). Your Ruling 1 of the tractability family today already fixed what "the end" means: the
   end of the music loaded for the selection, context found by convergence under the safety cap (*checked*).
2. **The tie rule follows from your ruling D-565** (LIVE, *checked* at `decisions/group_A.md`): exact ties
   "are real" and are resolved "by a declared TOTAL order, implemented identically in every decoder … No
   epsilon, no platform dependence". L2-S43 says the same thing and adds that both tied readings stay
   published, which is #12 (*my reading*). It is consistent with D-114 (the layer always names a key;
   *checked*) and with your silence ruling today (a reading is always given over sounding music; doubt is
   shown by the weights). D-114's own record names a tension with D-090 (calibrated abstention) and leaves it
   unresolved (*checked* at D-114's provenance; D-090 itself not read); for this layer your silence ruling has
   chosen. D-602 (a correct abstention counts as right when a layer is measured; index line only) governs
   measurement and is not touched here (*my reading*).
3. **Which fixed rule: D-565's own order** — fewer spans first; then the earlier sequence of boundaries; then
   a fixed order of the readings' contents. The reading file proposes adding exactly this (Row 10.48 claim
   (ii), ADOPTED — proposed, *checked*), and because D-565 is a standing ruling of yours that nothing
   supersedes, this follows from it (#6: one rule for one concern). *My reading, left for the application
   act:* D-565's third step orders the old engine's states; the new layer's readings also carry the note
   assignments, so the fixed order has to be stated over those too.
4. **The tie mark is not an "uncertain" mark** (*my reading*). Your silence ruling excludes an "uncertain"
   flag; the tie mark says only that two weights are exactly equal and the first-named was picked by the
   rule, which a reader could also see from the two equal weights. It decides nothing.

*One fact worth having (my reading):* D-565's evidence was eight pieces with "equal-score segmentations
differing by one boundary on repeated-chord runs". The new layer cannot produce that case in that form,
because L2-S14 forbids two adjacent stretches with the same key and the same chord ("Two adjacent spans
never carry the same tonality and the same chord", *checked* as the reading file quotes it at Row 21.48, not
at the derivation). So exact ties in the new layer may be
rarer than in the old one; the bare fifth is the case L2-S43 itself names.
