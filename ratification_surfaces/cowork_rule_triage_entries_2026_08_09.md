## 1. What is being reviewed, and what it is not

The triage classifies every rule the register homes in `CLAUDE.md` into one of three classes, and
treats *"could be a mechanism and is not"* as a defect. It is **a proposal about MECHANISATION**.
It is not a re-ratification of any rule, it does not touch any rule's text, and a KNOWLEDGE verdict
says only that no mechanism can enforce the rule — never that the rule is weaker.

**What the user is being asked:** whether each of the fifteen classes below, and the reason given
for it, is right. A verdict the user disagrees with is one edit to the tool's authored table.

## 2. Why fifteen were owed — the shape matters more than the count

The population is **derived**: every register entry whose home is `CLAUDE.md`. The class is
**authored**. So **every homing wave that writes a decision into `CLAUDE.md` adds to the population
and cannot supply its own triage**, and this check fails until a session writes one. That is the
same shape the tool's own comments already record for three earlier entries, and it is why the
check had been failing as a standing failure rather than for any defect in the rules.

Nine of the fifteen arrived by the licensed-homing waves of 2026-08-07 (the desk-simulation rules,
the three further measurement conventions, the two grading conventions, the correction of record);
five by the 2026-08-03/04 rulings that were homed into `CLAUDE.md` itself; and one — **D-315** — by
the away batch of 2026-08-08, which recorded the third local patch.
