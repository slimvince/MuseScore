**The method as decided.** For L3 generally, as the Part 11 ruling record states it (lines 77–80 [checked]): L3 reads
everything L2 publishes (its reading, its rivals with mass) plus L0's and L1's published facts, and nothing else; L3
never revises L2; L2 reads nothing L3 publishes; a backward edge is tested by measurement as soon as there is reason to
suspect it helps; key areas are read off L2's principal reading, a new one at every change of tonality. For the three
statements this record ratified (L3-S14, L3-S41, L3-S42), as the record's §2 states it (quoted at list lines 1784–1789
[checked at the list; the record relayed]): *"L3's phrase model has no hand-set numbers; every number is to be fitted
from licence-clear music with marked phrase boundaries, held out by piece, with a capacity declared first, and until that
fit exists the model is provisional. L3 is deterministic: widening the selection or editing the score gives the same
result as one fresh run over the final music. L3's phrases cut across all the voices and follow one another without gaps
or overlaps; L3 reads nothing from the voice-leading side; thematic restatement comes from L1; grading against
annotations is the measurement stage's."* Ratifying Item 1 leaves this unchanged.
