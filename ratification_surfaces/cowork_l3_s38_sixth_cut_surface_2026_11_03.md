## 4. Documentation and the algorithm, separately

  with mass) plus L0's and L1's published facts, and nothing else; never revises L2. L2 reads nothing L3 publishes. A
  key change is chosen by L2's candidate score over the whole sequence and can be chosen with no cadence (L2-S18,
  **[relayed]**); cadential confirmation enters L2 through the progression term over the chords L2 proposes. A cadence is
  published only where some phrase reading, the principal or a rival, ends a phrase (cadence-placement Ruling 1, as quoted
  at the fifth-cut surface). L3 publishes key areas as runs of L2's spans grouped by tonality (key-areas Ruling 1).
