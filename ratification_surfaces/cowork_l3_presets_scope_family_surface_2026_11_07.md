## 3. Documentation or algorithm

**Documentation only. Algorithm: no change.** No part adds a published fact, an input, a field, a check or a setting to
L3. The method that stands: L2 decides key and chord together and publishes the chord as a degree in its tonality, and
the tonality as tonic and mode; L3 publishes facts read off it (chord symbol, figured bass, harmonic rhythm, cadence with
type at each phrase end, phrase and section grouping, key areas, the pivot re-expression, the modal counts per key area,
and, once the application act amends `FRAMEWORK.md`, the catalog matches); no preset, style label or idiom mixture
reaches L2; no preset chooses the catalog L3 reads, and L3's method uses no idiom tag; a display turns published facts
into text; L3 owns the answers to its own questions. *(Former wording, kept: "no preset, style label or idiom mixture
chooses what L3 reads or computes;" — narrowed at the check to what the L2 tail-style record and the idiom-tags record
state.)*
