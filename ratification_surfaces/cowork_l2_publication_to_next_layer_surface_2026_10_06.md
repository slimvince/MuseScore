## §A. The surface

### 1. What this is about, and the cut

The chord-and-key layer (L2) decides together, over the music loaded for the selection, the key of each
stretch, where one harmony gives way to the next, which notes are chord notes and which are passing notes,
suspensions and so on, and the chord over each stretch. One complete answer of that kind is a **reading**.
Beside its chosen reading it publishes **rival readings**, each with a **mass** — the share of the model's
belief it carries, which your ruling in the last sitting declared a third kind of quantity, "the model's
own distribution" (*checked*, weight-meaning record, Ruling 1). The next layer (L3) reads what L2 publishes and
works out further facts from it: the cadence at each phrase end, the phrase grouping, the chord symbol, the
figured bass, the harmonic rhythm (*checked*, `FRAMEWORK.md` §5, L3). **This family decides what crosses
from L2 to L3.**

**A word kept apart.** In this surface *mass* is a rival's share, as above. The derivation uses *weight* in
another sense: "the number a term is multiplied by", a term being one part of the candidate score
(*checked*, derivation §0). Where the derived sentence below says "weight", it means that multiplier, never a
rival's mass.

The derived statement, whole (*checked*, `cowork_blind_derivation_l2_2026_09_27.md`):

- **L2-S45:** "L2 publishes to L3 exactly what the charter lists, and nothing else. That is the
  segmentation, the tonality per span, the chord per span, the assignments per event, and the rivals with
  their mass (with the rival objects and thresholds of L2-S40 to L2-S42, and the provenance of D-275). No
  term value, weight, partial candidate score or other intermediate quantity crosses. Every published item
  carries its establishment status."

In plain words: L3 receives the decided reading and its rivals with their masses, each item marked with how
far it is established, and none of the arithmetic L2 used to reach them. The derivation records no open
question on this statement (its open questions are at its §4, *checked*; none names L2-S45).
