## 4. The options

### Option A — You paste the workbook's own text for rows R170–R173

Four rows of a spreadsheet you already hold. The pass then either identifies the paper and reads it,
or confirms it is row 6 and dissolves the row into it.

- **Objective:** the only option that can actually **add knowledge**. Either a paper is read, or a
  population row is closed on **evidence** rather than inference. **Bounded value** by fact (f) — at
  most one paper, in an area where four readings already sit.
- **Principles:** #5 (investigate when facts may be scarce — this is the one remaining cheap
  investigation); #19 (settles by evidence rather than by an unfalsified guess); #1 (the row's content,
  if any, enters as read fact). **The workbook's classification is untouched** — you read it, not a
  session.
- **Cost and risk:** about a minute of yours. **Risk: none identified.**

### Option B — Rule row 7 dissolved into row 6

Declare the "Modal Harmony Ontology" to be the NTUA line, already read, and close the row.

- **Objective:** the mode-vocabulary evidence would be treated as complete when it may not be. **If
  the identification is wrong, a paper about returning multiple modal interpretations per progression
  — DP-K-adjacent — is silently dropped.**
- **Principles:** **fails #19 directly** — this converts *merely unfalsified* into *established*,
  which is the exact prohibition. It also runs against the never-work-from-memory rule: the
  identification would rest on a resemblance, and fact (d) records that half the description does not
  match. **This side declines to recommend it on those grounds.**
- **Cost and risk:** free, and wrong-if-wrong with no mechanism to find out.

### Option C — Rule row 7 out of scope; record it as a stated bound

Declare it unidentifiable, carry nothing from it, and record the gap in the findings surface's bound —
where it already is.

- **Objective:** nothing is added and nothing is falsely claimed. **The coverage claim stays honest**,
  which is what the bound section exists for (DT-26).
- **Principles:** #12 is satisfied — a recorded gap is not information loss, it is information about
  the gap. **#5 is arguably not yet exhausted, since option A is still available and cheap.**
- **Cost and risk:** free. **The cost is the foregone knowledge of fact (f), bounded.**

### Option D — Leave it open as a standing STOP

- **Objective:** identical to C in effect — nothing is read either way.
- **Principles:** the record's own experience is against it. An item that is neither resolved nor
  closed sits, and **an apparatus-adjacent open row is exactly the class D-676 lets lapse out of being
  owed.** C at least states the bound deliberately.
- **Cost and risk:** free, and the least honest of the four about its own state.

exist** — they have only failed to find it — and the one source that would settle it in a minute is on

they settle nothing, C follows immediately and the bound is recorded deliberately.

**C is a sound ruling** if you would rather not open the workbook at all, and the cost is bounded and
named at fact (f). **B is declined on #19** and I would ask you not to take it even if it appeals —
it is the one option that could put a false completeness into the record.
