## 4. Documentation and the algorithm, separately

- **Algorithm: no change.** The currently decided method: L3 reads everything L2 publishes (its result and its rivals
  with mass) plus L0's and L1's published facts, and nothing else; never an intermediate quantity of L2's scoring; never
  recomputes a fact a lower layer publishes; never revises L2. L2 reads nothing L3 publishes. A cadence is published only
  where some phrase reading, the principal or a rival, ends a phrase (cadence-placement Ruling 1, quoted above). A
  backward edge is tested by measurement as soon as there is reason to suspect it would help (nothing-from-L3 Ruling 3,
  read at its record).

  leaves the reading file's audit list with it.
