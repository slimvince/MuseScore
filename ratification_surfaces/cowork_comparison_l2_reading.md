## 2. The subject, re-explained from scratch

Once a harmonic analysis knows where a harmony is even allowed to begin — at every moment the set of
sounding notes changes — and what the notation says at each such moment (how strong the beat is,
whether a bar line, fermata or rest falls there, whether the bass has just moved the way a cadence
moves), it still has to decide the music. **L2 is where every such decision is taken, and it takes them
together.**

Four questions are answered at once over the stretch of music being read. **What is the tonality at
each moment** — its tonic, spelled as the notation spells it, and its mode? **Where does each harmony
give way to the next** — which of the possible moments are real harmonic boundaries? **Which of the
sounding notes belong to the harmony and which decorate it** — the passing notes, neighbors,
suspensions and anticipations? **And what chord is read over each stretch** — its scale degree in the
tonality, its quality, which chord member is in the bass together with any added members, and, for a
chord borrowed from another degree's dominant (V/V, for example), the degree it is applied to?

The charter calls this **the one entangled decision**, because each answer changes what the others
should be: the same notes are a chord tone under one chord and a suspension under another, and the same
chord is a tonic in one tonality and a dominant in the next. Beside its chosen reading L2 publishes the
**rivals** — the alternative readings, with how much weight each carries — including rivals that differ
only in where a boundary falls. It may not throw a rival away before the whole stretch has been judged.
What follows from that settled reading — the cadence type at a phrase end, phrase grouping, the chord
symbol, figured bass — is the next layer's.

**The question this file answers is neither of those.** A session that had not read this project's
specifications or code was asked how L2 should work, and answered in forty-nine statements. This file
sets that answer beside the specifications the project already holds, and gives **every statement of
those specifications** in the population exactly one recorded fate. It settles nothing: it tabulates,
so that the user can settle it.
