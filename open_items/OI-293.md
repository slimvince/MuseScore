# OI-293 — The WRITE LIST: six homes the record means to keep whose delegation the bar does not admit, or whose delegation names too few sections

**Section E — user adjudication. Rowed 2026-08-03 by the user's own ruling on [[OI-291]]
(option A3): apply the bar and every unapplied measurement in ONE re-classification pass, "with a
**write list** for the homes the record means to keep".** This row is that list.

## Why a write list rather than a softer test

The alternative on the table was to revise the bar where it excludes something the record plainly
means to keep. The user chose not to: a mechanical test with a case-by-case exception is not a
mechanical test, and the cases below are not evidence that the bar is wrong — they are evidence
that a delegation was never written, or was written for fewer sections than the record relies on.
The repair therefore belongs in the delegating document, not in the criterion.

**Nothing here was written.** Rule (g)'s guard (`CLAUDE.md`, the decisions-register section) is
that only the user writes a delegation into `ARCHITECTURE.md`; the same reasoning applies to
`CLAUDE.md` itself. The pass drafted a wording for each case, in the form the bar admits and the
canonical document already uses, and stopped there.

## The list

The enumeration, with each document's entry count, its current delegating line quoted from its own
surface, why the bar or the section unit excludes it, and the draft wording, is
`tools/audit/decisions/phase1q_reclassification.json` → `write_list`. It is generated from the same
authored block the classification runs on, so it cannot drift from the pass that produced it.

**Shape (a) — no admissible delegation exists.** Four documents:

- `cowork_layer5_function_design.md` — a SIGNED layer specification, delegated only by a bare

- `cowork_prefit_gates.md` — four ratified pre-fit protocols, named inside a list of citations and

- `cowork_joint_key_chord_design.md` — a parenthetical naming inside a bulleted list of arcs,
  naming no section, two bullets away from siblings that DO name their target's sections. **D-433**

## What this row is not

It is not a claim that the six documents are homes. It is a claim that the record reads as though
they were, and that the wording which would make that true was never written. If a draft is
rejected, its entries stay `gap` — and that is then a true statement about the record rather than a
classification artifact, which is the outcome the pass exists to make visible either way.

## Dated note — 2026-08-03 (CC, phase 1r): the list came back WRITTEN, and the pass re-ran against it

The user wrote all six delegations. Four are new delegation clauses (`cowork_layer5_function_design.md`
and `cowork_prefit_gates.md` in `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `cowork_engage_arc_plan.md` in `CLAUDE.md`,
`cowork_joint_key_chord_design.md` in the arc plan's own arc-#10 bullet); two widen the sections an
already-admitted delegation names (`cowork_voiceleading_axis_design.md` §0/§5.3 → §0/§5.1/§5.3/§8/§9,
`cowork_notation_output_contract.md` §3.1–§3.4 → §2–§3.4). Every one carries its own provenance
sentence naming this list.

**All six moved.** The figures are fields, never transcribed (**D-431**):
`tools/audit/decisions/phase1q_reclassification.json` → `the_phase_1r_re_run`
(`entries_moved_since_phase1q`, `moved_to_contract_home`, `moved_to_gap`) and, per document,
`by_document.<doc>.moves_in_since_phase1q`.

**Two moved only in part, and neither residue is a wording gap.**
`cowork_voiceleading_axis_design.md` keeps three `gap` entries because the widening deliberately
excludes §15/§16 and says why in the delegating line itself. `cowork_layer5_function_design.md`
keeps one — D-341, in an open-items section that fails the KIND half — carried on [[OI-295]].

**The dispatch's registered prediction is partly refuted, and it is recorded rather than rounded
off.** It expected the partial cases to be exactly the two documents whose named sections were
widened. One of those two moved in whole, and a newly-delegated document moved in part instead. The
direction of the prediction (gap → contract-home, in whole or in part) held; its attribution did not.

**A premise of Task 4 is also refuted.** The dispatch says to re-run the pass and report the
movement. Re-running alone moves nothing: the delegation FORM grade and the per-section
STATES-RULES judgment are AUTHORED inputs, and the tool refuses to guess either — it stops with
*"Read the section and record the judgment before running"*. Every newly reached section holding an
entry was therefore read in place and graded, and each grade records the range it was read from. A
non-movement would have meant "the authored inputs were not updated", not "a delegation does not do
what it was written to do", so §8's STOP would have fired on the wrong diagnosis.

**One mechanism change rode with it, and it is a #12 repair.** The pass had two frozen class epochs
(`former_class`, `class_before_phase1q`). Running it a second time over entries it had itself moved
would have overwritten the phase-1q RESULT and lost it, so a third epoch, `class_before_phase1r`,
was added and seeded from the committed object. The phase-1p pre-apply record needed the same
treatment for the same reason: its check reads each document's grade, and four of those grades moved
today, so `gen_phase1p_delegation_bar.py` now freezes them in `GRADE_AT_PHASE_1P` while `FORMS` stays
live for the classifier (one live grade, one home — #6). Without it the check would have reported
*"PROCEED — the prediction holds"* and destroyed the finding this row's parent [[OI-291]] cites.
