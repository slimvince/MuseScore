# OI-328 — The reading regime's ORDERING KEY is contaminated by the project's own recording acts: writing a delegation moves a proxy the schedule is fitted on

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Found 2026-08-04 (CC, READ WAVE 5, dispatch `cc_instruction_reads_5.md`), by a freeze check
written for the re-pack firing on its first run.** Measured, not inferred; every figure is
generated at `tools/audit/decisions/reads5_repack.json` →
`why_the_regime_artifact_is_read_and_not_regenerated`, and none is restated here (#17f, D-431).

## What was found

The reading regime orders the owed documents by **whichever of four candidate proxies correlates
best with yield on the 36 already-read documents**. One of the four is
**`named_in_a_user_ratified_surface_count`** — how many lines of `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and
`cowork_engage_arc_plan.md` name the document.

**Writing a delegation into one of those surfaces increments that count.** The user wrote six
delegations on 2026-08-03 (the [[OI-293]] write list) and three more on 2026-08-04 ([[OI-327]]).
Three of the READ documents — the ones whose yield is already known and therefore the ones the
correlation is fitted on — had their naming count moved by that work.

**Recomputed against the files as they stand today, that proxy overtakes document length and the
ordering key FLIPS.** So re-running `gen_phase1n_reading_regime.py` would re-order all 66 owed
documents by a different key, and with them the wave schedule — destroying the record of which
documents each completed wave read (#12) and replacing a registered prediction with one fitted on
data that has moved under it (#20).

## Why this is a defect and not a curiosity

**The register's own homing work edits a feature the proxy is graded on, for documents whose
outcome is already known.** That is the fit/evaluation separation #20 forbids, arriving by a route
nobody designed: the feature is not being tuned, it is being moved by unrelated, correct work.
Nothing in the regime warns that one of its candidate proxies is writable by the project.

**And the margin is inside the uncertainty the regime artifact itself records** for these figures.
Under **#24** a difference within the uncertainty is not a finding — so the flip is not even a
claim that one proxy is better than the other. It is a coin landing the other way. What makes it
consequential is that **the ordering key is read off that coin**, and the reading order, the wave
schedule and the record of what each completed wave read all hang from it. The regime's own
`ordering_decision` block already says the two top proxies are indistinguishable at 95 % and that
length wins by a **stated tie-break, not by its correlation** — this row is what that sentence
costs when the artifact is re-run.

## What was done, and what was deliberately not

**NOT done: the regime artifact was not regenerated.** The re-pack the user ruled (R5) is applied
to the **registered rows, read verbatim**, by a separate generator —
`tools/audit/decisions/gen_reads5_repack.py` → `reads5_repack.json` — which imports the packing
rule from the regime generator rather than copying it (#6). No band, point, tercile cut or reading
order was recomputed.

**Done: the regime generator now STOPS loudly** instead of silently re-deriving. Its freeze check
compares each completed wave's re-derived packing against the documents that wave's own yield
artifact records reading, and refuses to write when they disagree, naming this row and the reason.
`gen_phase1n_reading_regime.py --check` **was already one of the six pre-existing guard failures**
before this wave — the artifact has been stale since the 2026-08-03 delegation writes — so the
verdict is unmoved; what changed is that the failure now says why.

## The relation to OI-316

[[OI-316]] is the parent and this is a second, sharper instance of it. OI-316 found that the
regime's **update protocol** (write each wave's yield into the owed row) cannot be followed without
refitting the bands. This row finds that **no re-run at all** is safe any more, for a different
reason: not the protocol, but the passage of ordinary correct work over the proxy's own inputs.
OI-316's owed remedy — freeze the bands at registration, or make the regime read-only with the
grading beside it, or split partition from prediction — is **unchanged and now covers more**: any
of the three would fix this too, and none is a session's to choose.

## What is NOT claimed

That either proxy is the better one; that the registered ordering was wrong; that any completed
wave read the wrong documents; or that the re-packed schedule is validated by anything. The proxy
remains **unvalidated and registered as such** (#17d), and the re-pack exists precisely so it can
be falsified at all.
