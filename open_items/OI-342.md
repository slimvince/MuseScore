## The finding

`tools/audit/decisions/finish_line_item1_routes.json` carries two authored fields that answer
different questions:

- `owner_is_unambiguous` — is the owning specification determinate?
- `executable_under_this_dispatch` — does that owner lie inside the edit surface the dispatch
  authorizes?

The artifact states the second's purpose in its own words: a small executed set is *"a SCOPE BOUND,
not a verdict that the remaining entries have no owner."* **For part of the population the first
field is nevertheless carrying the second's answer.** Ten open rows have `owner_is_unambiguous:
false` whose recorded reason names **only** the edit surface and no ambiguity at all — for example
D-604, whose reason reads *"That is a GRADING convention and belongs beside the four the gate block
already states"*, and D-602, D-486, D-603, D-564, D-582, D-451, D-473: each names a determinate site
in `CLAUDE.md` and then says it is *"outside this dispatch's edit surface"*.

**Not every member of that ten is the same case, and the row says so rather than proposing a
number.** Three have a genuinely soft owner independent of the edit surface — D-539 (*"the
principles"*), D-538 (*"the project's standing rules"*) and D-281, which names two candidate homes
(`CLAUDE.md` gate block (C) and `BUILD_AND_TEST.md`). So the corrected count is a judgment, and this
row deliberately does not make it: the dispatch that found this instructed *do not guess*, and
re-authoring another wave's judgment table is not a reading act.

## Why it matters

The finish line is **the scope** — additions to it are a user ruling by its own terms — and this
column is what a reader consults to see how much of item 1 is closeable. Read at face value it says
**zero** entries of the re-home class have a determinate owner left, which is true only of the
`ARCHITECTURE.md` surface the preceding dispatch could edit. A dispatch whose edit surface includes
`CLAUDE.md` would find work here that the column currently hides, and the artifact's own separate
field is what was supposed to keep those two facts apart.

## What is NOT in doubt

The population, every entry identifier and every count in that artifact are DERIVED and re-derive at
HEAD — `gen_finish_line_item1_routes.py --check` passes. What this row concerns is one AUTHORED
column, marked authored on every row exactly so that a reader does not take it for a measured
quantity (**D-431**). No route is wrong as a route; no entry's home moved; nothing about the analysis
is touched.
