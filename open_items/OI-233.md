## The decision

`ARCHITECTURE.md:786-787`, verbatim:

> "Region" unqualified is **banned** as
>   ambiguous; every layer names the span it operates on.

`ARCHITECTURE.md:765-768` records the rename as done, verbatim:

> *(★ FAMILY RENAME
>   ✅ CONFIRMED (user, 2026-07-02) — ✅ EXECUTED at the merged Cowork doc pass (2026-07-03),
>   propagated through every layer spec + the dictionary: **harmonic region → chord-span**

The decision is not cosmetic. Its stated reason is that the typology's own atomic member "carried
the banned word", and that a reader cannot tell *which* kind of stretch is meant — the stretch one
chord prevails over, the stretch one key prevails over, or the phrase — when the bare word is used.
That ambiguity is the same failure mode the whole span typology exists to end.

## The state of the canonical document

`ARCHITECTURE.md` — **the** layer specification, the document the rename claims to have been
propagated through — contains **216** occurrences of "region", including four section headings that
a reader navigates by:

- `:1223` `#### Region Analysis — Canonical Modules …`
- `:1722` `#### Region identity modes (decided 2026-04-11)`
- `:5416` `### 11.5 Region Analysis and the Chord Track`
- `:5720` `### 11.6 Region Intonation`

and load-bearing prose throughout the Layer-3 specification (`:1101-1112`, "**Intra-region rule (b)
— duration-majority** over the region's overlapping slice run"), where the reader must work out
unaided that "region" here means the chord-span.

## What this row does and does not claim

It does **not** propose a tree-wide rename. [[OI-229]] records the user's standing ruling on exactly
that: the existing tree is **not** renamed unilaterally, because some names carry correspondence to
the published research the design is grounded in (#1/#2), so a rename is a decision surface and not
a sweep.

What it records is narrower and is a plain contradiction of the record: **the rename was declared
EXECUTED across every layer specification, and in the canonical one it was not.** Either the
execution claim at `ARCHITECTURE.md:765-768` is corrected to name what it actually covered (the
Cowork design documents and the dictionary), or the canonical document is brought into line. Both
are documentation acts; neither is a code change.

The prose fix is bounded and mechanical where the meaning is unambiguous — in the Layer-3
specification every occurrence means the chord-span — and is a judgment call only in the §11.5/§11.6
consumer sections, where "region" sometimes names a user-selected stretch rather than an analytical
one. That distinction is itself worth writing down.

## Disposition

A scoped documentation pass on `ARCHITECTURE.md`, to run with [[OI-232]] / [[OI-236]] / [[OI-238]]
(the other corrections to the same document) and to be sequenced against [[OI-229]] (b)/(c) so that
one ratified terminology decision governs both. **Nothing is edited here.**
