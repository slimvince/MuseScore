## The conflict, stated precisely

Both are user-ratified. Both are recorded in the canonical architecture document. The later one is a
**global joint decode over the coupled variables** — the class of mechanism the earlier one reports
as **measured inert** and rejects by name. The governing banner disposes of the **layer sections**
("NOT the feed-forward, per-layer pipeline the layer sections below still describe") but says nothing
about the §2.14 measurement, which is not a layer section and is not a pipeline description — it is a
recorded empirical finding about the mechanism now shipped.

Three readings are possible and **the record does not say which is correct**:

1. The 2026-06-29 measurement was of a *different* mechanism (a beam search over per-layer candidate
   lists) and does not bear on an exact block-factorized semi-Markov decode over a joint state space.
   If so, the §2.14 block needs one sentence saying so, and its "not by a joint decode" conclusion
   needs withdrawing.
2. The measurement was of the same class of mechanism and has been **overturned by later evidence**
   (the [[OI-178]] adoption moved root agreement 66.0 → 77.0 % on the robust unit). If so, §2.14 is
   **falsified**, and the register should say falsified rather than superseded-in-fact.
3. Something in between — the earlier finding holds for the *cycling* it tested but not for the
   *coupling* the estimator exploits.

Until it is settled, the register records D-025 and D-026 as **superseded in fact** — a later build
replaced what they govern, with no ruling that names them — which is the honest status and is
deliberately not upgraded to "superseded by".

## Why it is worth settling rather than tidying away

The §2.14 measurement is the only recorded evidence in this repository that the mechanism now in
production was once tested and found to add nothing. If reading 1 is right, that evidence does not
apply and saying so costs a sentence. If reading 2 is right, then a load-bearing negative result was
overturned and **how** it was overturned is exactly the kind of thing the decisions register exists to
carry (#12: an exclusion is information). Leaving it unstated leaves a future session free to
rediscover the 2026-06-29 block, believe it, and design against the shipped architecture — which is
the [[OI-207]] failure mode itself.

## Disposition

A **user ruling**, not a Claude Code fix. It is small: one of the three readings above is chosen, the
§2.14 block gains a dated annotation, and `DECISIONS.md` D-025/D-026 move to the chosen status.
