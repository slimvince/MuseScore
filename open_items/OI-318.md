## (1) The Layer-6 section uses the word the rename reserved

`ARCHITECTURE.md:1502-1504` describes the grouping layer as assembling *"**phrases** (from the L1.5
phrase-boundary primitive), **key-areas** … and the **alignment of cadences to phrase ends**"*.

`ARCHITECTURE.md:892-894`, in the same document, records the rename that forbids exactly this: the
grouping span is the **punctuation-span**, *"**renamed from "phrase" 2026-07-01** so that "phrase
[MT]" denotes *only* the accepted **melodic** phrase, which is a voice-leading-axis object, not this
one"*, and points at `cowork_layer6_grouping_design.md` §0 — which spends its whole terminology
section separating the two and states the naming convention in terms: *"the word "phrase" appears in
this document only as "phrase [MT]" … the harmonic-grouping object L6 produces is always the
"punctuation-span.""*

So the canonical layer specification's Layer-6 paragraph uses the bare word in precisely the sense
the ratified rename reserved for something else — a reader of that paragraph alone is told the
grouping layer segments melodic phrases, which is the one thing `cowork_layer6_grouping_design.md`
§0 exists to deny.

**This is [[OI-233]]'s shape one word over.** That row records the *"region"* half of the same
failure: `ARCHITECTURE.md:873-882` already carries a scope correction admitting the ban was *"not
propagated through every layer spec"* in this very document, names four section headings, and defers
the pass under the standing music-theory-terminology convention ([[OI-229]]). The *"phrase"* half is
not in that correction's enumeration.
