## What was ruled, and what it creates

The user's **Rulings 28, 29 and 32** of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_09_fifth_stop.md` settle the
writing-standards conformance question that [[OI-230]] had carried since 2026-07-28, and they settle
it by **splitting** it:

- **Ruling 32 closes the deep limb.** Predicate qualification is ruled KNOWLEDGE. No mechanical test
  can decide whether a two-place word names its argument without understanding the sentence, and a
  noisy checker gets switched off, which is worse than none (**D-473**). Enforcement stays the
  writer's method as stated in `cowork_design_doc_template.md`, the per-session self-check
  (**D-434**), and the reading passes. **Nothing is owed on that half, and this row does not carry
  it.**
- **Ruling 29 opens a mechanical one.** Documents ARE inside the conformance enumeration for a named
  subset: **status-banner presence**, **terms-table presence on a new specification**, and
  **section structure judged per Ruling 28's kind rule**.
- **Ruling 28 supplies what the third of those needs to be checkable at all:** the KIND LIST, now
  enumerated in `cowork_design_doc_template.md`, where a document of an unlisted kind is a **STOP**
  at the conformance check and never a silent pass.

**All three checks read a document's own structure rather than its prose**, which is why they fall
on the mechanisable side of the line the deep half fails.

## What is owed, and what is NOT

**Owed:** the check itself. Nothing at HEAD verifies any of the three. The rule-triage table's entry
for **D-193** is therefore moved into the defect class on this subset, with its former KNOWLEDGE
class and ground preserved beside it (#12), and this row is the register row that class's members
carry.

**NOT owed, and stated so the row is not read as larger than it is:**

- **No conformance VERDICT over the existing tree.** Ruling 28 decides which kinds the structure
  binds; it does not say which documents conform. Producing that answer is what the check would do,
  and the check is not built.
- **No document is judged, re-shaped or re-bannered by this row.**
- **No prose is retired.** Under **D-436** a mechanism is not required to retire the text it
  enforces, and the writing standards stay in force whether or not a check exists.

## What the rulings explicitly do NOT do

**They do not start the mechanism build.** The row joins the **D-436** mechanism backlog like every
other member of the defect class, and what a member of that class is owed is a **measured
establishment before it is trusted** (#19) — a detection rate against documents known to be missing
a banner, a terms table or a listed kind, and a false-positive rate at or near zero on legitimate
documents — followed by a user ruling on whether to build it. Nothing here proposes a design for the
check, and no session may write one under this row.

It changes no published value, no corpus, nothing in `tools/robust_stop/`, no golden and no `src/`
file. It touches nothing about the analysis.

## Why the kind list's STOP matters more than it looks

An exemption that is decided document by document widens by silence: any document a session declines
to judge becomes exempt in practice. The kind list makes the exemption **enumerated** — and makes a
document of an unlisted kind report itself rather than pass. That STOP is the clause of Ruling 28 the
check must implement first, because without it a green conformance run would mean only that nobody
had met a hard case.
