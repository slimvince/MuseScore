The guard is the enforcement behind **D-253**, and the two batches that produced these observations
**each also declared a D-253 departure by the executing side**: the `wc -l` of observation 1, and a
`git rev-parse HEAD` — a branch-tip read outside D-253's by-explicit-hash licence. **Both commands
ran.** Whether the guard's policy reaches a `git rev-parse` at all is not stated here and was not
investigated.
