# OI-340 — Whether criterion C1 reaches SUPERSEDED register entries is unruled: the per-kind scheme it was said to apply does not carry that kind

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section F — doc-sync debt (#10) / documentation gap. Found 2026-08-04 (CC,
`cc_instruction_finish_line_item1b.md`, Task 1.1) by the check the dispatch ordered against its own
assumption A2.**

## What was checked, and why the dispatch ordered the check

The dispatch's ruling **R1** states that a superseded register entry whose live content is carried by
a **homed successor** is correctly homed already — the register plus the successor's home — and that
this **applies OI-272's per-kind scheme to the superseded kind; it is an application, not a new
rule.** The dispatch declared that reading as assumption **A2**, in its own words *"Cowork's reading,
put to the user and agreed"*, and instructed: read OI-272's ruling at its own text, and **if it will
not carry the superseded kind, STOP and report** rather than stretching it.

## The reading, at the ruling's own text

OI-272's ruling is recorded twice, identically — the INDEX row and `open_items/OI-272.md`'s dated
note of 2026-08-02. It reads:

> live constraints/policies homed into the owning specification (an `ARCHITECTURE.md` section or an
> OI-268 contract home); shelvings/falsifications/dead ends NEVER become live specification
> statements (home = the register + the evidence document, with a one-line tried-and-closed pointer
> in the owning spec); process rules → `CLAUDE.md`/the audit protocol; measurement conventions → the
> gate block.

**It does not carry the superseded kind, and four things establish that rather than one.**

1. **The scheme partitions by WHAT A DECISION IS, not by WHAT ITS STATUS IS — and applied at its own
   text it routes these four entries the OPPOSITE way.** *Superseded* is a status; the kinds are
   constraint, shelving, process rule, measurement convention. D-282 (*the oracle/tier metric, never
   a bare proxy*), D-283 (*never learn keys*), D-284 (*selection is saturated, stop adding
   re-ranking gates*) and D-285 (*embellishment is chord-first*) are by kind **standing
   constraints/policies** — OI-270's own row calls them *"meta-findings to institutionalize"*, that
   is, proposed doctrine. Class 1 is the class they fall in, and class 1 says **home them into the
   owning specification** — the act R1 exists to forbid.
2. **The prescribed home is not the same home.** Class 2's is *the register + the evidence
   document*; R1's is *the register + the successor's home*. For D-283 those are different documents
   — its own record sits in `cowork_architecture_reassessment.md`, its successors D-001/D-096 are
   homed in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
3. **Class 2's pointer half would state something false.** It prescribes *a one-line tried-and-closed
   pointer*. None of these four was tried and closed: each was **superseded by a later, more
   explicit, user-ratified decision on the same subject**. Writing *tried and closed* over them puts
   a false statement into a specification, which is exactly what phase 1's TRUE half exists to remove.
4. **R1 carries a CONDITION the scheme has none of.** R1 applies only *where the live content is
   carried by a homed successor*; class 2 applies unconditionally. A rule turning on a fact its
   claimed source never mentions is a new rule, not an application of that one.

**And the record's own vocabulary keeps the two apart.** D-231's clause — the very sentence criterion
C1 is read out of — names them as **two** things the register is the ledger of: *"the decisions
register remains the status ledger (supersession, shelving, the same-commit rule)"*.

## What this row does NOT say

**It does not say R1 is wrong.** R1's *substance* has a textual basis that is stronger than the one
claimed for it, and it is worth putting in front of whoever rules: **D-231's own clause** says the
register remains the status ledger for **supersession**, which is a direct argument that C1 — *every
recorded decision is written into its owning specification* — is about LIVE decisions and that a
superseded entry's supersession is register business. That is a different source from OI-272, and
citing it is a ruling the user makes, not a reading a session may substitute for the one it was
told to check.

**Nothing was moved.** Task 1.2, 1.3 and 1.4 were not performed: no entry left item 1, no route was
re-recorded, no tried-and-closed pointer was noted as owed. The population stands exactly as
`tools/audit/decisions/finish_line_item1_routes.json` records it.

## Why it matters beyond these four entries

The finish line's item 1 carries a **NO HOME EXISTS** class, and the four superseded meta-findings
are its clearest members; others in that class are recorded SUPERSEDED-IN-FACT (**D-571**, **D-572**,
**D-579**) or are verdicts about a stage that has run (**D-453**, **D-535**, **D-610**). Until C1's
reach over non-live entries is ruled, none of them has a closing act, and the finish line cannot
report item 1 as dischargeable. The counts are in the artifact and are not restated here (**D-431**).

It is not a pointer, an anchor, a label or a banner.
