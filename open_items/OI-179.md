## The premise, in the three limbs #17(d) requires

**What the commissioned measurement YIELDS.** Agreement between **two annotation TRADITIONS with one
reading each** — the *When in Rome* / DCML analyses against BCMH's — on the overlap the census

**What principle #21 DEMANDS.** Per-axis **annotator** agreement — how far two people annotating the
same music disagree with each other — as the ceiling every precision target and every "irreducible
residual" verdict is read against.

**The link between them is a PROXY, and it is recorded as an ASSUMPTION.** *That agreement between two
annotation traditions, each carrying one reading, is a usable stand-in for per-axis annotator
agreement.* #17(d) makes a proxy→target link its own ledger premise and forbids a proxy standing in for
the target quantity unvalidated. **Its validation is OWED BEFORE any ceiling derived from it carries
load** — before it bounds a residual, before it interprets an #17(b) target, and before any
"irreducible residual" verdict is read against it. Until then a figure from this measurement is a
measured quantity in its own right and **is not the ceiling #21 names**.

## What the assumption is exposed to, named rather than left to be reconstructed

Two traditions differ in more than two annotators do. They differ in **analytical convention**, in
**segmentation and unit**, and — for this pair specifically — in the two defects the 2026-08-04 note
records: one side sits on a **homorhythmic reduction** while our grading is full-texture, and one side
reached the repository through a **machine translation**. A disagreement measured between them
therefore contains everything an inter-annotator disagreement contains **plus** those.

**So the expected direction is that the proxy is LOOSE — that it overstates disagreement and therefore
understates attainable precision.** That is a **PREDICTION and not a measurement** (#17b): nothing here
measures it, and the proxy's tightness is precisely the quantity that is unmeasured. A ceiling read
off it without validation would licence abandoning error that is ours.

indistinguishable in the residual. **With a ceiling measured through this proxy they are not cleanly
distinguishable either:** what such a ceiling separates is our structural error from
*between-tradition* disagreement, which is a different and wider quantity. Recorded here so the
principle's sentence is met with its limit attached, and recorded as an observation about the
