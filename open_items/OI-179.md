## The two defects that survive establishment

Establishment settles the annotator question. It does not settle these, and any measurement design
must handle both:

1. **Unit mismatch.** The annotations sit on a **homorhythmic reduction**, while our grading is over
   the full texture. An alignment convention is owed before a disagreement number means anything.
2. **Translation noise.** They reached the repository through a **machine translation** into rntxt,
   whose own errors would appear inside any measured disagreement and be indistinguishable from it.
   The local originals bound this one: grading against BCMH's own `**harm` spines removes or
   cross-checks the translated copy, which is the gain the 2026-07-19 zip inspection recorded.
