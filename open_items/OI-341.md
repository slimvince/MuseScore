# OI-341 — A guard dates its line number as an observation and then requires it to equal today's, so it goes stale on any insertion above the section it points at

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — instrument / measurement layer (the audit's own guard apparatus). Rowed 2026-08-04
(CC, `cc_instruction_finish_line_item1b.md`, Task 2.1), where the tool was classified against ruling
R4's test.**

## The finding

`tools/audit/decisions/gen_live_prohibition_pointers.py` appends, to each entry of the
live-prohibition class, a pointer to the live `ARCHITECTURE.md` section that restates that entry as a
standing prohibition. **Its own docstring states the design and the reason for it** — that a line
number written into prose is not a register anchor, that `reaim_home_anchors.py` maintains the `home`
anchors and nothing maintains this one, and that the pointer therefore *"carries the section's own
words, which stay findable after any insertion above them, and dates the line number as an
observation rather than asserting it as current."*

**Its `--check` does not honour that.** It re-derives the whole backbone serialization — including a
freshly located line number for each of the three sections — and compares it to the file as a whole
(`if text != raw`). So a number the docstring calls a dated observation is in fact required to equal
today's, and any insertion above one of the three sections makes the check report every member of the
class stale at once.

## The evidence, at the object

Run at the working tree on 2026-08-04 the tool reports the three sections at
`ARCHITECTURE.md:1495`, `:1389` and `:306`, and then: *"class members (derived from the
verification): 13 · STALE vs the files: 13 entry(ies) do not carry the pointer as derived"*. **All
thirteen** — the whole class, not a subset — which is the signature of a whole-file re-serialization
difference rather than of thirteen independent defects. The insertions that moved those three
sections are the preceding wave's homing acts into the Layer-3, Layer-4 and joint-estimator sections.

## What is NOT in doubt, and what is

**Not in doubt:** the tool's classification. Under ruling R4 it **RE-DERIVES A LIVE INVARIANT** and
stays in the live guard list — its class membership is derived from `phase1w_legacy_verification.json`
rather than listed in it, and it locates each section by that section's own quoted words. It was
re-checked per tool on 2026-08-04 and the earlier verdict was confirmed; the artifact of that check
is `tools/audit/guard_classification.json`.

**In doubt, and not settled here:** whether the remedy is to re-run the tool's apply mode (which
rewrites the dated numbers and is a register write), or to stop comparing the number at all so the
docstring's stated design is what the check actually enforces. **The second looks right and is
deliberately not done** — this wave was under the dispatch's *repair none* instruction, and a change
to what a guard compares is a change to what it guarantees.

## Classification

**Apparatus under D-641, and therefore rowed and left — no wave, no dispatch, no surface.** The
subject is a pointer maintained inside the decisions register's provenance field. It bears on no
analysis, no analysis input, and no instrument any measurement of the analysis depends on. The
prohibitions the pointers point AT are unaffected: each is published once in the specification (#6),
and this tool does not copy it.

**Under D-438 this row gates by the declaration's own default**, its subject column being the
instrument/measurement layer, which the over-inclusive first cut does not propose as an apparatus
candidate. **No gating verdict is hand-added here** — the non-gating set is derived and never
hand-listed, exactly as [[OI-339]] and [[OI-336]] record.
