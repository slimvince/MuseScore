## Why it is a defect and not a nit

**Two of the three sites are published INTO the tool's own artifact**, so a reader of
`tools/audit/session_start_read_size.json` is told a number that the same artifact's own derivation
contradicts. That is the shape **#17f** and **D-431** forbid — *a figure enters a dispatch or a
report by citation to a generated artifact, never by transcription* — and here the transcription sits
inside the generator itself, so the artifact carries it out to every reader.

**The artifact states the opposite of what it does.** Its own opening field reads *"A MEASUREMENT
ONLY: it edits nothing, moves no rule and grades nothing. Every value is computed; none is
transcribed (D-431)."* That sentence is not true of the `216`, which is transcribed and is not
computed — the same shape [[OI-374]] records at a neighbouring artifact, where a field whose whole
job is to say why a run may be trusted is untrue of some of what it covers.

**It is silent, and it goes stale by the mechanism the register itself runs.** The transcribed count
moves whenever a register row opens or closes, so the number is wrong from the next rowing onward and
nothing in the tool notices — the tool's `--check` mode compares the artifact against its own
derivation, and the transcription re-derives identically because it is a literal.

## Why NO REMEDY is ordered, in the ruling's own words

> *"It is rowed and nothing else is done to it. **No remedy is ordered, and the transcription is NOT
> updated to `217`.**"*

Two grounds are recorded with the ruling and both are carried here rather than paraphrased away:

> *"the standing mechanism freeze bars tool work that does not block the work"*

> *"updating the number would re-commit the very defect — a hand-count that goes stale on the next
> row. **The right repair is to remove the transcription and let the figure enter by citation
> (D-431), which is a DESIGN question with real alternatives, not hygiene** — and it gets its own
> surface if and when the user calls for it."*

**The row records the defect; it does not pre-decide its cure.**
