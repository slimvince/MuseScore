# OI-331 — A LIVE, USER-RATIFIED register entry names a convergence proxy the build MEASURED FALSE and dropped: D-261 versus D-622

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Found 2026-08-04 (CC, READ WAVE 6, dispatch `cc_instruction_reads_6.md` Task 6), reading
`cowork_layer3_reachback_design.md` in full against the register.**

## The two statements

**D-261** — *"A layer never guesses how much context it needs — the amount is discovered by
convergence"* — is LIVE, homed at `cowork_bounded_context_design.md:57-69` (a SIGNED contract
document), and **user-ratified as a register entry on 2026-08-02**. Its verbatim includes this
clause:

> In practice a layer uses a **domain proxy that *implies* convergence** rather than re-checking its
> whole output each step (Architectural Layer 3 reach-back: *"a settled, stable prevailing key is in
> view"* — once a confident earlier key is established, the change-cost/decay means reaching further
> back will not move the selection's leading-edge key). The proxy is validated **once, in design**,
> to imply convergence.

**`cowork_layer3_reachback_design.md` §3** — the detail design for the very mechanism D-261 uses as
its worked example — records that this proxy was **measured and disproved**:

> **Proxy REJECTED by measurement (as-built uses the headline criterion directly).** … Phase-3
> measurement **disproved** it: one settled *context* measure does **not** anchor the leading edge;
> the leading-edge key flips only once a confident earlier key is established over a **run** (e.g. a
> V–I two measures back). So the as-built tracks the **leading-edge key across iterations and stops
> when *it* stops changing** — the headline criterion itself, no proxy.

That statement is entered this wave as **D-622**.

## Verified at the code, not taken from either document

`src/composing/analysis/region/regionanalyzer.cpp:653-659` carries the convergence note in the
production source: the design's cheaper proxy *"was found to stop PREMATURELY — a single settled
context measure does not anchor the leading edge"*, and *"the headline criterion is implemented
directly: track the leading-edge SETTLED key across iterations and stop when it repeats."* The loop
itself is at `:660` onward, gated on `opts.reachBack.enabled`, and the leading-edge key comparison
is the `LeadKey::sameKey` test at `:679`. **The code implements the criterion, not the proxy.**

## Two defects, not one

**(a) The worked example is false.** D-261's parenthesis names a proxy the build measured wrong and
removed. A reader of the register meets it as a live illustration of how the rule is applied.

**(b) The GENERAL clause is the thing the measurement bears on.** *"The proxy is validated once, in
design, to imply convergence"* is a method — and the one time it was exercised, a proxy validated
once in design turned out to be false in build. The measurement is therefore evidence about the
clause and not only about its example, which is why this is a ruling rather than a wording repair.

## What is NOT in doubt

The **headline rule** — a layer never guesses how much context it needs; the amount is discovered by
convergence — is untouched by this and is what the code implements. Nothing here weakens D-261's
first two sentences, and nothing here says the bounded-context contract is wrong. The dispute is
confined to the proxy clause and its example.

## Why this GATES

**D-438**'s test: does the row's subject bear on the analysis, its inputs, or an instrument a
measurement depends on? **It does.** The subject is a binding contract rule about *how much music the
analysis reads before it commits to a key* — the analysis's own input scope. A session applying
D-261 as written would build a stop condition already measured not to work.

## What a resolution would have to decide, and why a session may not

Which of the two the record means to keep, and in what form: D-261 annotated with the falsification
(the entry keeps its ratified status and gains the correction); or the proxy clause struck from the
contract document itself, which is an edit to a SIGNED user-ratified surface; or D-622 recorded as
superseding the clause while the rule stands. All three change what a user-ratified entry says, and
**D-261 was ratified two days before this reading**. It is the user's.
