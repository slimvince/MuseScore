## The practice, and what the legacy arm does about it

Baroque scores are frequently notated with one accidental fewer than the modern practice, so the
sounding key sits **one step to the sharp side** of anything a signature-faithful reading could
name. The adopted handling **detects** that situation — the flattened sixth degree pervasive across
the sounding weight and dominating its natural form — and **reinterprets the written signature one
step** toward the missing accidental, for the whole resolution.

The alternative considered and rejected was to let every score choose from two signature families,
which would have added a rival family to correctly-notated music as well. That is D-575, and none of
it is in question here.

## What is established, at the objects

1. **The machinery is in the legacy key path and nowhere else.** It lives in
   `src/composing/analysis/key/keyresolver.{h,cpp}` with the legacy types beside it. The joint
   module carries no construct of that name — its only matches on the search terms are a
   phrase-split remark and a partial-record remark, both read, neither related.
2. **The production arm does not reach the legacy resolver.** The record arm's section derivation
   calls neither `resolveKeyAndModeRanked` nor the legacy key layer, and `--joint-inference` decodes
   through the joint module alone. These are the phase-1w pass's own located production-path
   anchors, not a fresh claim.
3. **The record itself says the question is open**, in D-575's home text: *"Whether the joint
   estimator handles the convention AT ALL is NOT settled by this entry and is not asserted here."*

## ★ What is deliberately NOT claimed — the distinction is the whole row

**Absence of the machinery is not absence of the handling.** The live arm takes the key signature and
declared mode as a **weak fitted soft prior with no conditional gate anywhere** (**D-528**) rather
than as a hard constraint, and conditions the initial key state only (**D-450**). A key one step to
the sharp side is therefore reachable on that arm **without any explicit correction** — the evidence
can simply outweigh the signature.

So this row asserts **no defect and no regression**. It asserts that the question is **unestablished
in both directions**, which is exactly the state #19 refuses to treat as established: a thing is not
established because nothing has contradicted it.

## ★ And one thing substantially reduces its size

**The production arm's key agreement on exactly this repertoire is measured and published**, in
`CLAUDE.md` gate block (A), on both the home and the local key columns. This is therefore **not an
unmeasured failure sitting behind a green gate** — whatever the effect is, it is already inside a
published value.

What is missing is different and smaller: whether a **named, corpus-wide notation practice** is
handled at all on the arm that ships. That bears on how the published key column should be read, and
on what a later precision step would find there.
