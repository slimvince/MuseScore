# OI-252 — Bar-repeat signs contribute zero notes: the repeated bars' music is silently absent from the harmonic evidence

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — Layer 1 / feature handling (#12 information loss).**

`MeasureRepeat` (the % bar-repeat sign) is a Rest subtype (`measurerepeat.h:33`), and the Layer-1
note walk takes chords only (`note_model.cpp:227`), so a bar notated with a repeat sign
contributes NO notes to any analysis surface — the music sounds, the evidence is silent, and
nothing marks the gap (an interior all-rest stretch is at least an EXPLICIT empty slice under the
Layer-2 rule; a bar-repeat bar is indistinguishable from silence). A #12 information loss at the
reading layer. Not fixed here; a Layer-1 completion question (resolve the repeated content, or at
minimum flag the bar as unread-not-silent) at its #8-correct stage.
