**Section D — joint decoder / decision-conformance (declared to Cowork; [[OI-207]] subject matter)**

OI-199 pass-2 (this dispatch, `cc_instruction_oi199_joint_p3_p4.md`, Task 2) — the exhaustive P3
second-side check the pass-1 P3 arm under-delivered ([[OI-224]]) — settles the candidate-admission
question Cowork raised: **the production decoder's candidate-admission rule has no stated basis in the
ratified decode plan.**

**The rule (all in `src/composing/analysis/joint/jointdecoder.cpp`):**
- gate (1) **root-present** (`candidateStates:438`) — a class is offered only if its root pc is among
  the window's ONSET pcs;
- gate (2) **member-overlap** (`:444-445`) — `present < min(2, |members|)` rejects it (the [[OI-215]]
  gate);
- gate (3) **fit / NCT budget** (`:448`) — `(nOnset − present) > max(1, j−i)` rejects it;
- preceding these, the **top-6 key prune** by onset-pc overlap (`candidateKeys` + `kKeyPruneTopK = 6`,
  `:39`).

**What the ratified plan specifies** (`cowork_joint_estimator_factorization.md` §5): the state space is
**"24 keys × the degree vocabulary"** (re-affirmed in `cowork_joint_estimator_architecture.md`); the
only pruning §5 contemplates is the segment-length cap (the ratified semi-Markov default) and, as a
RESERVE, "restricting key-change candidates to a **fitted-mass neighborhood on the circle of fifths** …
an inference technique requiring its own established-loss measurement, never a silent heuristic." The
candidate-admission gates are described NOWHERE in §5, the premise ledger §4, `cowork_prefit_gates.md`,
or `cowork_notation_output_contract.md`; the top-6 prune is a DIFFERENT prune than the reserved one
(onset-overlap ranking, not circle-of-fifths fitted-mass). They entered via the byte-for-byte
`probe_decoder` port carrying only inline comments; the desk simulation (§6a) merely *truncated* the
candidate set as a hand-trace convenience, not a ratification.

**Partial mitigation (recorded, not exculpatory):** [[OI-188]] already MEASURES the cost of "the
decode-time key-fit prune + root-present/member-overlap filters" — GT reachability ~72 %, the GT state
force-added on 14,257 spans, prune cost re-measured at the fitted weights. So the filters' *cost* is
known; what is absent is any *derivation of their form* from theory or any ratification gate.

**Why it matters (bears directly on the [[OI-215]] fix):** the empty-decode cliff is candidate admission
emptying the analysis; the fix cannot be a threshold knob-turn — it must give candidate admission a
ratified basis for the first time, covering the whole family Task 1 enumerates. And that family is NOT
just the member-overlap gate: on the 23 large scores the 312 uncoverable events are **291 member-overlap
(gate 2, [[OI-215]]) + 21 FIT/NCT-budget (gate 3, [[OI-227]])** — TWO gates at opposite density extremes.
So the admission rule that was never ratified is itself the seat of two distinct production failures.
Declared to Cowork; a decision-surface item for the Premise Gate, its own row here so [[OI-207]] (the
repo-wide decision-conformance audit) subsumes a specific, pre-located instance rather than
re-discovering it.
