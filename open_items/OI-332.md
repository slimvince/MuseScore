# OI-332 — Three of read wave 6's documents misdescribe their OWN state: a "no code" banner over a built design, stale code anchors in a built one, and a design whose approach was later falsified with no note saying so

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Found 2026-08-04 (CC, READ WAVE 6, dispatch `cc_instruction_reads_6.md` Task 6), reading each
document in full and checking its own claims about itself at the objects.**

This is the [[OI-276]] / [[OI-232]] class — a document stating as current something false at HEAD —
and the three instances are recorded together because they are one kind and one remedy.

## (1) `cowork_layer1_extend_design.md` — banner says NO CODE; the code exists

Line 2 of the document reads:

> **Status: DRAFT for sign-off. Read-only design — no code.**

**Both operations it designs are built.** Verified at the header, not inferred:
`src/composing/analysis/notemodel/note_model.h:218` declares
`static NoteModel build(const mu::engraving::Score* sc, int loadedStart, int loadedEnd);` with the
overlap-retention contract this design's §4 specifies stated in its own comment at `:213-216`, and
`:228` declares `void extend(Direction dir, int amountTicks);`. The loaded-span accessors the design
introduces are at `:239` and the backing field at `:273`. The reach-back design one layer up says so
in its own words — it rides *"on the Architectural Layer 1 `extend` (now built)"*.

**Why it matters more than a stale word:** every sibling design in this family carries an accurate
as-built banner (the slicer's says BUILT with the built form quoted; the reach-back design's says
BUILT with the gating flag named; the spelling primitive's says BUILT capability-only). A reader
sampling the family learns to trust the banner, and this one is the exception.

## (2) `cowork_layer3_reachback_design.md` — the as-built code anchors have drifted

The status header cites the loop at `regionanalyzer.cpp:585–666`, and §0 states — as a *correction*
of an earlier citation — that the orchestrator is *"now at `regionanalyzer.cpp:506`; the cited `:488`
has drifted"*, with the conditional build at `:536–538`.

At HEAD: `analyzeRegions` is at **`regionanalyzer.cpp:561`**, the conditional build is at **`:591`**,
and the reach-back block begins at **`:660`** with its comment block from `:640`. So the correction
has itself drifted, which is the defect **D-307** exists against — a specification cites code by
function or section anchor, never by line number. The document's substance is accurate; only its
coordinates are wrong.

## (3) `docs/stage4c_cadence_key_design.md` — the approach it designs was later FALSIFIED, unbannered

The document is banner-marked *DRAFT — ratification-gated* and designs a **key-agnostic local cadence
detector** as the note-derived replacement for the declared-mode anchor. **D-290** records that
approach FALSIFIED at its precision ceiling (2026-06-15, one day after this design was written), and
the document carries no note of it. It does carry one inline supersession note, on the gate figures
in §4c-ii, which shows the document has been touched since — so the omission is not "nobody edits
old drafts."

**Stated precisely, because the document is not worthless:** its §3 scope constraint and its §1
ceiling-versus-realized discipline are entered this wave as **D-616** and **D-617** and are not
falsified — they are about WHERE a global prior is applied and HOW a detector is graded, and both
bind whatever the detector turns out to be. What is falsified is the detector the rest of the
document builds toward.

## What is NOT in doubt

No analysis behaviour, no measurement, and no register entry rests on any of the three. Every
substantive claim these documents make was checkable and checked; what is wrong is each document's
account of its own state.

## Why this is an APPARATUS row

**D-438**'s test: does the row's subject bear on the analysis, its inputs, or an instrument a
measurement depends on? **It does not.** Two of the three are pointers and a banner — the line the
non-gating declaration draws explicitly names *a pointer, an anchor, a label, a banner* as
apparatus. The third is a missing cross-reference between two documents, neither of which any
measurement reads. It carries no establishment obligation, so the starred clause that makes those
gate whatever their subject does not reach it. **It gates nothing and is worked in leftover
capacity.**

## Why nothing was corrected

The wave's freeze: no `src/`, no behaviour change, and a defect found is rowed and left. Item (3)
additionally raises the same filing question [[OI-322]] raises and does not answer — whether a
superseded design document is re-bannered as a historical record or left as written — which is a
convention decision, not a session's.
