## The class

The check's **"uncited code claim"** rule fires on the words *reachable*, *dormant* and *unreachable*
in the phase-1w dispatch. In every instance the dispatch is either

- **quoting** the row the wave is executing — *"for each marked entry, is its subject reachable on
  either production surface at HEAD"* is `OPEN_ITEMS.md` OI-289's own sentence, reproduced with its
  citation — or
- **defining the vocabulary of a test** — the four reachability senses a verdict must name are the
  dispatch's own definitions, not assertions about what the code does.

And the facts that *do* make code claims sit two lines above, each with its `file:line`. So the rule
is firing on the one part of the document that is deliberately not a claim.

## Why it is the same shape as the two classes already published

`tools/audit/process_check_establishment.json` → `known_over_breadth_do_not_tune_it_away` records two
classes already: a **structural description that carries a number and a unit noun** read as a bare
quantity, and a **section reference written without the section mark** (`Task 2.3`) read as the same.
Both are the check lacking a model of a construct — a description, a cross-reference. This is the
third: **no model of a quoted or defined term.**

## What must NOT be done

Special-casing the three words. That artifact forbids it in terms and gives the reason: a pattern
adjusted until a particular sentence passes is tuned to a specimen, and the same objection holds in
both directions. The words are also exactly the vocabulary any future reachability or liveness work
will use, so an allowlist would blind the check on the documents it most needs to read.

## What is owed

The treatment the second class got at phase 1s: **the specimen enters the CONTROL corpus**, both rates
are re-measured, and the published false-positive rate **RISES visibly** — so the class becomes a
known property of the check rather than a defect designed around. Extending a mechanism's corpus
re-measures its rates, which is a mechanism act under **D-436** as amended on 2026-08-03: report, do
not execute. That is why this row exists instead of an edit.

## What is NOT claimed

That the check is wrong to have found them. Under D-431's letter a two-place liveness word without a
citation beside it is what the rule is built to catch, and the rule catching it in a definition is
over-breadth, not error — which is the distinction the two published classes already draw.
