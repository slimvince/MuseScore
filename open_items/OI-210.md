# OI-210 — The analysis-extent question is open, and the implementation contradicts the last recorded ruling

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — the analysis-cost / notation-surface cluster (design question)**

**The question, given its own home.** *What temporal extent of the score does the analyzer read when
answering a query, and how often must it re-read it?* These are two independent axes — EXTENT (whole
piece / bounded window / grow-until-stable / enclosing musical unit / viewport) and FREQUENCY (per
query / once per edit / once-then-incrementally-patched). The candidate designs are enumerated in the
analysis-cost dispatch `cc_instruction_analysis_cost_profile.md` §A.

**The recorded history — a ruling contradicted by a later build.** This question has been ratified,
overturned, parked, and then silently re-decided the opposite way:
- originally **ratified as whole-piece** ("decode-once-query-many", question Q1);
- **overturned on measurement at Stage 3.1b (2026-06-12)** — an A/B falsified the whole-piece
  interactive premise for the legacy analyzer (32–40 % tick changes on contrapuntal scores; DCML 59/41
  in the WINDOW path's favor; Mozart 35/65 against whole-score) and whole-score was **SHELVED WITH
  EVIDENCE** (`docs/p3_granularity_ab_3_1b.md`, committed Stage-5 evidence); the bounded-window cache
  shipped instead;
- explicitly **PARKED, not answered** — 3.1b's disposition says whole-score may not be re-attempted
  "without resolving the granularity question as a deliberate product decision … it needs the
  granularity-robust metric the 2.2-i dossier mandated";
- that **precondition is now satisfied** — the granularity-robust unit was ratified 2026-07-06, three
  weeks before the seams dispatches specified whole-score;
- yet the joint estimator's notation record is now **implemented as whole-piece by dispatch
  specification, with NO ruling** (`produceNotationRecord` takes no tick range — [[OI-212]]).

So the last deliberate ruling on the extent question shelved whole-score with evidence, and the current
implementation is whole-score without a ruling. This is exactly the class the decision-conformance audit
[[OI-207]] was opened to sweep for, and the motivating case behind the decisions-register proposal
[[OI-208]] (a decision's evidence lived in an archive outside the session-start read, so a later
dispatch contradicted it unknowingly). The specified-but-never-coded temporal-extension design [[OI-18]]
is the related build-side gap.

**Disposition.** The analysis-cost measurement dispatch supplies the facts (the phase profile, the
scaling law, the extent-candidate discrimination); Cowork then presents the analysis-extent decision
surface — the §A candidates rated on those numbers against the guiding principles and the ultimate
objective, with the 3.1b evidence and the Tristan review ([[OI-209]]) as required inputs, and the
effort control's temporal dial as the framing. A ruling, not a CC fix.

*Provenance: the §A history above, recorded as its own row so the extent question has a home it never
had; rowed by CC at the analysis-cost dispatch's Task 0, 2026-07-28, from the Cowork re-framing of the
OI-206 fix surface.*

**Dated note — 2026-08-01 (CC, the [[OI-207]] adjudication): the recorded history in this row is
missing its most recent entry, and it is a ratified one.** The row's history runs
whole-piece (Q1) → shelved with evidence at Stage 3.1b (2026-06-12) → parked → implemented as
whole-piece by dispatch specification with no ruling. Reading the layer specifications in full adds a
fourth entry between the third and the fourth: on **2026-07-02** the user ratified the
**Bounded-context contract** into the canonical architecture document (`ARCHITECTURE.md:798-806`,
register entries **D-030** and **D-031**), whose requirement R1 is "cost scales with the working
span, not the whole score" and which states plainly that "Whole-score analysis is the degenerate case
(selection = score)". The same directive made it "the hard gate before L6".

This sharpens the row rather than answering it. The **extent** axis is not undecided in the sense of
never-ruled: the last ruling on it says bounded, and it postdates the 3.1b shelving rather than being
superseded by it. What remains genuinely open is the axis this row's title names second — **how
often** the analysis re-reads its span — and *which* bounded form (grow-until-stable, enclosing unit,
viewport) satisfies R1/R2/R3 at acceptable accuracy. The measurement that discriminates them exists
(`tools/joint_estimator/window_study.json`: every query stabilizes at a bounded window, most by ≈4
measures, at essentially zero ground-truth accuracy cost on the chorale envelope).
