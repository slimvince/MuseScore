# OI-362 — The one index parser silently drops a malformed row, and a dropped row is absent from every derivation

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — instrument / measurement layer. Found 2026-08-09 (CC,
`cc_instruction_return_continuation_4.md`, Task 2).**

## How it was found

By the both-ways decision table the user's **Ruling 25** requires, which counted the rows matching
the index's own row pattern against the rows the parser ADMITS. The two disagree — and nothing in
the record had ever compared them.

## The mechanism, read at the tool

`tools/audit/gen_nongating_apparatus_rows.parse_rows` splits a row on the cell separator and, where
the result is not the expected number of cells, **skips it and continues with no report of any
kind**. `gen_phase1_completion_inventory.py` and the finish-line machinery import that function
rather than re-implementing it (#6), so the omission propagates to every derivation over the index.

**One row is dropped at this tree.** Its prose contains an unescaped cell separator, so the split
yields one cell too many. Its identity, its line and its opening are at
`tools/audit/oi356_parser_correction.json` and are not restated here (**D-431**).

## ★ Why this is worse than either of its siblings

A MIS-READ row is at least COUNTED, in one population or the other, and a count that moves can be
noticed by someone watching it. **A DROPPED row is in neither.** The open-row count, the true-half
cuts, the finish line's populations and the apparatus declaration's candidate cut all behave exactly
as though it did not exist, and **nothing anywhere reports a difference**. The bijection check uses a
looser row pattern and passes, so the row is present and accounted for on one surface while being
invisible on another — which is the condition that lets it persist indefinitely.

## The fix has two halves and NEITHER is taken here

1. **Making a malformed row a STOP rather than a silent skip** is a mechanism change, which
   **D-436** reserves to the user.
2. **Escaping the separator in the offending row** is one character — but its CONSEQUENCE is that a
   row enters the open population, which moves the open-row count, the true-half cuts, the finish
   line's populations and the apparatus declaration's candidate cut. A population movement belongs
   to an act that ACCOUNTS for it, not to a parser task that would slip it in unremarked.

**The order matters and is stated so it is not reversed:** the STOP goes in FIRST. With the silence
closed, the second half's movement is visible instead of merely happening — and any future instance
announces itself rather than waiting to be counted.

## What is NOT in doubt

- **The row itself**, which is well-formed prose, correctly filed, and paired with its detail file.
- **The bijection check**, which holds and is not questioned.
- Nothing about the analysis, any measurement of it, any golden, or anything in
  `tools/robust_stop/`.

## Why no gating verdict is written here

A non-gating verdict is DERIVED from the apparatus cut and never hand-written. The subject is the
open-items index's own parser; it bears on no analysis, no analysis input and no measurement tool
any measurement of the analysis depends on.
