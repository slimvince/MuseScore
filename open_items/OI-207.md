> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section E — user adjudication / process (repo-wide)**

User-directed 2026-07-27, immediately after the 3.1b contradiction surfaced (OI-206's dated
correction): the seams dispatches specified a whole-score interactive producer while the Stage
3.1b record (2026-06-12) had A/B-measured and SHELVED whole-score interactive analysis with
committed evidence — a recorded decision contradicted by a later build because its record
lived in an archive outside the session-start read. The user's question: what ELSE is
implemented in direct opposition to something decided?

**Scope (read-only):** enumerate every recorded ruling with binding force — ratifications,
shelvings-with-evidence, falsifications, dead ends, excluded alternatives — across:
`cowork_handoff_archive.md`, `STATUS_ARCHIVE.md`, `docs/scoring_model.md` §8 (constraints/dead
ends), `docs/p3_granularity_ab_3_1b.md` and the other committed evidence docs,
`DEFECT_TYPES.md`, the `cowork_*` design docs' excluded-alternatives/constrained-optimum
records, and the ratified governing docs' amendment trails. For each: what was decided, on
what evidence, its binding scope — then check the CURRENT implementation for direct
opposition. Every hit gets its own register row with the evidence cited both ways.

**Method note:** the enumeration half doubles as OI-208's population input (the decisions
register). The audit is the OI-84 sweep pattern applied to DECISIONS instead of code — blind
enumeration first, conformance check second, no fixing inside the audit.

**Timing:** priority — slots immediately after the OI-206 diagnosis dispatch (the live
regression outranks it; nothing else does). It does NOT wait for the post-map reviews: the
worry is CURRENT builds contradicting recorded evidence, and the map itself (deletions) should
not proceed over unknown contradictions.
