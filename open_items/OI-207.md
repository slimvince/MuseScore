enumeration first, conformance check second, no fixing inside the audit.

## ★ 1. The ruling says "order by the stronger proxy". THERE IS NO MEASURABLY STRONGER PROXY.

interval (#24): `proxy.correlations_with_yield_on_the_read_set`. The result is

established between the top two**, and is not made.

## 3. The bands, registered in advance

`bands.rule` states the band rule — terciles of the READ set's own length distribution, the
prediction being that tercile's observed minimum, median and maximum yield, with its mean as the
point prediction. The bands are wide on purpose: a narrower band would assert precision a
36-document sample cannot carry, and `bands.by_tercile` publishes each tercile's
`zero_yield_documents` so a reader sees how much of the distribution sits at zero.

`bands.registration` carries the protocol the next wave inherits: **every later read wave writes
the actual yield into `actual_yield_when_read` on the row it read and reports it against
`predicted_yield_band`.** That is what makes the proxy tested as a by-product of the reading rather
than by a separate study, and it is the only route by which a tail could ever become boundable.

`proxy_counter_examples` names, from the measured set rather than hypothetically, the documents the
length proxy gets most wrong — long documents that yielded nothing or almost nothing. They are in
the data that fitted it.
