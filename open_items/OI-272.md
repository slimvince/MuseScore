## Why the archive is worse than the live handoff

[[OI-240]] ruled that a handoff block tracks a session and is not a home for a standing decision.
The archive is that, plus a second remove: the live handoff is at least opened by the planning side
each session, while the archive is declared reference-only by two governing documents. A decision
recorded there is not merely mis-homed; it is placed where the reading protocol says not to look.
**This is the exact mechanism [[OI-207]] was opened to investigate**, and D-286 — the Stage-3.1b
shelving of whole-score interactive analysis — is that audit's founding case, now entered.

## What is NOT in doubt, and what is

Not in doubt: the fourteen were invisible to the register until this wave, and several bind
something live (D-292 is [[OI-271]]; D-296 sits beside the ruled D-229; D-297 scopes principle #12;
D-294's two further clauses — never call a measurement ground-truth agreement, and no
self-annotation in any measurement — are recorded nowhere else).

In doubt, and the user's: whether homing them is the right act for each. Three plainly want a
specification home (D-294 the measurement conventions, beside `CLAUDE.md` gate block (A) where
D-210/D-211/D-212 now live; D-296 beside D-229 in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.3; D-297 beside D-099 in
§2.15). Three are shelvings and falsifications whose value is precisely that they are *history with
evidence* — a homing act must not turn a shelving into a live specification statement. The rest are
process or product decisions whose homes are the same candidates [[OI-240]] used.

## Disposition

For the user: rule per kind, not per entry. Then the homing acts follow [[OI-237]]'s and
[[OI-240]]'s pattern — the rule in the specification's own voice, with its defense, citing the
ratifying event, former home preserved in provenance (#12). **No fix, no design, no specification
edit is made here.** This row is the sibling of [[OI-268]] and should be ruled with it.

**★ Dated note — 2026-08-02 (CC, the phase-1g triage wave): A CORRECTION OF THIS ROW'S OWN
PREMISE — at least THREE of the fourteen are NOT archive-only. Their fuller record lives in
design documents, and for one of them the archive entry SAYS SO.**

This row states that of D-286…D-299 "every one carries `nonspec_kind: "unhomed"` — their only
recorded home is the archive." Reading five design documents in full shows that is not true of
three of them:

- **D-286** — whole-score interactive analysis SHELVED WITH EVIDENCE, **the founding case of
  [[OI-207]] itself**. Its full record, with the measurement and a standing prohibition, is
  `docs/decoder_design.md`'s dated **§8 amendment** ("Q1 RE-DECIDED → bounded-window cache";
  whole-score "changed the displayed P3 chord on 32–40 % of ticks … combined root verdict 59/41
  in the WINDOW path's favour, Mozart 35/65 against whole-score"; and, closing the section, "Do
  not re-attempt whole-score P3 before that decision"). The evidence itself is a second document,
  `docs/p3_granularity_ab_3_1b.md`, committed as Stage-5 input.
- **D-287** — key-as-distribution SHELVED. Its full record is `docs/redesign_plan.md` "Step 3 —
  Key-as-distribution ⛔ SHELVED (premise obsolete)", with the investigation, the two additional
  findings and the named cleanup. **The archive entry itself points there** — its own verbatim
  ends "See `docs/redesign_plan.md` §Step 3."
- **D-288** — beam widening SHELVED. `docs/beam_widening_design.md` was banner-shelved and
  retained for its derivation, and `docs/decoder_design.md` §11 carries the dated erratum that
  applies it.

**What the correction changes, and what it does not.** It does **not** weaken the row: the three
were still invisible to the register until the archives were read, and the other eleven are
unaffected by this note. What it changes is the **diagnosis**. The failure was not "the decision
was buried in an archive"; for the audit's own founding case the decision was recorded three
times, twice in design documents, one of them the RATIFIED decoder design. What none of the three
homes is, is **a session-start read** — and that is the property the remedy has to attack. A
homing act that moves a shelving out of an archive and into a design document would, for these
three, move it to where it already is.

**Consequence for the ruling this row wants.** The per-kind ruling should distinguish "recorded
nowhere a reader is directed" from "recorded in several places, none of them read at session
start", because the second is the case for the founding instance and the remedy differs. It also
bears on [[OI-268]]: the two rows' populations overlap more than either row assumed, which is a
further reason to rule them together.
