**Section F — docs / process. Found by the [[OI-207]] register completion pass, 2026-08-01. This is
[[OI-237]]'s sibling one level out: OI-237 found decisions with the wrong specification home; these
have no specification home at all.**

## The rule this measures against

`open_items/OI-208.md:55-57`, user-ratified 2026-07-28, verbatim:

> **A decision belongs, wherever possible, in the OWNING LAYER'S SPECIFICATION** — that layer's
>    section of `ARCHITECTURE.md` — and the register is the **index and pointer**, never a
>    substitute home.

An **open-item row** tracks an unresolved issue and closes when it resolves. A **session handoff
block** hands one working session to the next and ages into an archive that is explicitly outside
the session-start read (`STATUS.md:5-6`). Neither is a place a later reader is directed to for a
standing decision — which is the exact mechanism [[OI-207]] was opened to investigate.

Two of them sharpen the problem. **D-209** is recorded on a row whose own status is
`✅ COMPLETE 2026-07-12` — a rule with standing force sitting on a closed row. **D-210**, **D-211**
and **D-212** are measurement conventions every reported number depends on; the `CLAUDE.md` gate
block narrates two of them in passing, but the conventions themselves are stated only in the rows
that once tracked building them.

## Why the register does not fix it

The register is the index and pointer, never a substitute home ([[OI-208]] ruling 2). Pointing at a
tracking surface is what these twelve entries now do, honestly; giving each a home is a separate
act, and for the three measurement conventions and the two effort/large-score requirements it is a
**governing-document edit**, which is the user's.

## Disposition

Twelve homes owed, and the natural homes differ by kind:

- **D-200, D-203, D-204** — how work is sequenced and how a fix is scoped. `CLAUDE.md` (the
  guiding principles or the Conventions section) is the candidate home.
- **D-201, D-202** — standing design requirements every later design is judged against.
  `ARCHITECTURE.md` §2 (architectural principles) is the candidate home; D-202 partly overlaps
  D-035, which is already there.
- **D-205, D-206** — scope rulings about ground truth and about the intonation feature.
  `ARCHITECTURE.md` §16 (scope reference) and §11 respectively are the candidate homes.
- **D-208, D-209** — audit-method rules. `cowork_audit_protocol.md` / `DEFECT_TYPES.md` are the
  candidate homes.
- **D-210, D-211, D-212** — measurement conventions. `CLAUDE.md` gate block (A) or
  `BUILD_AND_TEST.md` is the candidate home; the gate block already carries the narrative of two.

None was written here: writing a home for a ratified decision is a separate, ratified act
([[OI-208]] ruling 2), and several of the candidate homes are governing documents.
