The user ratified D-248 (applied-chord labels such as V/V are not produced; the feature is
deferred) with the direction that its revisit be PLANNED rather than left open-ended, because for
the ultimate objective — maximum-precision inference — the feature may be needed. The
precision connection is concrete: the ground truth annotates applied chords, so an analysis that
never produces them disagrees with the annotator wherever an applied label is the right reading —
a bounded, measurable share of the Roman-numeral column.

   vii°/x labels (and a richer dormant Layer-5 labeler), while D-248's home states the feature is

   which surface (legacy arm vs record arm vs batch), and what the joint estimator's degree

2. **The measured stake.** Before scheduling, measure how much Roman-numeral disagreement is
   attributable to absent applied-chord labels on the grading corpus (a read-only decomposition
   of the existing residual — the OI-192 fifth-substitution family is the suspected neighbour).
   The measured share decides the revisit's priority in the phase-3 plan.

**★ Dated note — 2026-08-02 (CC, the phase-1i delivery acts; Cowork's measured probe, user-reviewed
before rowing): BOTH of this row's owed items are ANSWERED — and item 1's answer is that D-248's
recorded scope was wrong.**

**Item 1 — the OI-53 tension is substantially ANSWERED, and the answer is a premise correction.**
D-248's plain restatement said applied-chord labels "are not produced." That is false of the
production surface at HEAD. The joint estimator has been the inference layer on the batch/corpus
surface since the OI-178 adoption and on the notation surface since the switch, and its renderer
emits the applied suffix: `jointRenderRn` "adds … the applied \"/target\" suffix"
(`src/composing/analysis/joint/jointrender.h:62-63`). Measured on the committed corpus, **8.62 % of
scored duration (1,756 cells, 715,920 ticks) carries an applied label in our Roman numeral** — top
emissions `V/IV` 101,160, `vi/IV` 94,080, `V6/IV` 54,120, `V/V` 24,240, `V6/5/V` 17,280 ticks —
including **50,280 ticks of EXACT string matches against applied ground truth** (`V6/5/V`→`V6/5/V`,
`viio7/V`→`viio7/V`, `V/V`→`V/V`). D-248's verbatim is about the LEGACY function-layer structure —
"no `relativeRoot`/secondary-dominant field in `ChordFunction`" (`chordanalyzer.h:287`) — a
different surface. **The register entry's scope is corrected in the same commit that records this
note**: the plain restatement now names the legacy structure, and the status source carries the
correction with its citations. So: **the joint surface emits applied labels; the legacy
`ChordFunction` structure does not carry them** — which is the split the tension was asking for. What
is NOT settled by this, and is not claimed to be: how ACCURATE the emitted labels are beyond the
exact-match duration above.

**Item 2 — the measured stake is SMALL, and it is a scheduling input, not a mandate.** Measured on
the committed `tools/corpus/baroque` analyses (326 WiR-covered stems; inference is
preset-independent, so one preset carries the result) against the When-in-Rome ground truth, on the
robust unit's union-of-boundaries cells, duration-weighted:

- Applied ground-truth cells carry **4.11 %** of scored duration (340,920 / 8,300,640 ticks) and
  **4.52 %** of total Roman-numeral-disagreement duration (134,520 / 2,978,400).
- **The hard ceiling is +1.62 points**: if EVERY applied-ground-truth disagreement vanished, root
  errors included, RN-agree would move 64.12 % → at most **65.74 %**.
- **The pure right-chord-wrong-name slice is +0.38 points** (31,200 ticks of root-agree-but-
  RN-disagree, 2.91 % of that bucket).
- Applied-ground-truth cells are **already RN-AGREE on 60.6 % of their duration** — consistent with
  the labels actually being emitted.
- **The suspected [[OI-192]] kinship is REFUTED in both directions.** The whole fifth-offset family
  among all root disagreements is 737,520 ticks / 1,839 cells; applied-ground-truth cells contribute
  **30,360 ticks = 4.12 %** of it, and the fifth shape explains only **29.38 %** of applied-ground-truth
  root errors (the offset histogram is broad — 5: 23,040; 3: 14,160; 6: 10,320; 8: 9,360; 4: 9,240
  ticks). The fifth-substitution family is overwhelmingly NOT an applied-label phenomenon.

**All four registered prediction bands came in BELOW their predicted ranges** (5–12 % → 4.11 %;
15–35 % → 5.42 %; 10–25 % → 2.91 %; 40–70 % → 29.38 %) — the measured reality is smaller than
predicted, and the diagnosed cause is the premise failure in item 1.

**Establishment (#19).** The run drives the committed `a8_rebaseline_measure.build_piece_grid`
verbatim (self-validating its variant-(b) decomposition byte-identical to `compare_rn.
grid_score_regions` per piece) and reproduces the ratified baselines to the digit — root-agree
77.03 %, RN-agree 64.12 %, scored duration 8,300,640 ticks; the applied-label test is the ground-truth
parser's own `dcml_parser._split_rntxt_applied`. No metric primitive was reimplemented. Two caveats
carried honestly: the single-corpus caveat (#24 — no resampling; differences of a few tenths of a
point on this one corpus are not findings), and a stated defect in the raw artifact
(`raw_results.json.n_our_applied_label_violations = 0` tested the chord-LETTER field, not the Roman
numeral; it is superseded by `overlap_results.json.ours_applied_dur`). As with [[OI-277]], the
measurement script named in the report is not among the committed artifacts, so a repo-side re-run
would be a re-implementation against the committed substrate.

**Disposition — the revisit's priority.** With the premise corrected, the question is no longer
"build the feature" but "reconcile the recorded scope, then decide whether the emitted labels'
ACCURACY warrants targeted work." The scope reconciliation is done here. The remaining revisit
enters the phase-3 plan at **LOW priority** with these numbers: a bounded +1.62-point ceiling on a
column whose total disagreement is 35.88 % of scored duration, and no kinship with the larger
fifth-substitution family to ride on.
