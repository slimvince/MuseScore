## What is owed

The ratified amendment **D-497** has two halves. The RULE half — that a style constant or an idiom
no ground truth has calibrated carries the *empirically-unvalidated* mark, that the corpus which
would validate it is named beside it, and that the mark is maintained until an established corpus
measures the value — is now written into `ARCHITECTURE.md` §6, beside the preset constants, on the
user's ruling of 2026-08-07.

The APPLICATION half is **not** discharged and is what this row tracks: putting the mark on the
**Jazz preset constants** and on the **idioms of the §6.7 taxonomy for which no gate-grade ground
truth exists**, constant by constant and idiom by idiom, each with its validating corpus named
beside it.

## Why a new row rather than an existing one — the check that was run

The homing dispatch named **[[OI-7]]** as the candidate. It is **not** the same obligation, and the
difference is not a nuance:

| | [[OI-7]] | this row |
|---|---|---|
| what it asks for | establish a jazz ground-truth corpus, **or** de-scope the Jazz correctness claims | put the existing mark on the affected constants and idioms, and name the corpus that would validate each |
| what discharges it | a corpus, or a scope decision | a documentation act over a constant table |
| who it blocks | the Stage-3 entry gate (EG-6) | nothing — it is apparatus |

[[OI-7]] is about **acquiring or renouncing** the evidence; this row is about **declaring the
absence of it** where a reader meets the values. Either could be discharged without the other, so
folding this into that row would make a closed row assert something it never checked. No other open
row was found that names the mark's application: the search was over the index's own rows for the
mark, for the preset constants and for the idiom taxonomy.

## What was marked, and the establishment performed inside the act

**Two families, both of them the Jazz preset's own constants, each carrying the SAME validation path
beside it** — jazz ground truth carrying written-out bass and piano voicings, converted and
score-aligned (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.1c; `cowork_score_census.md` Tier J):

1. **The six Jazz mode-prior overrides** at §4.6's preset table — LydianDominant, Altered,
   MelodicMinor, DorianB2, PhrygianDominant, HarmonicMinor.
2. **The Jazz chord-scoring constants** beside the `ChordAnalyzerPreferences` structure — the
   extension threshold, and the reduced individual inversion bonuses that are where the preset's
   inversion behaviour actually comes from.

**The establishment is the record's own, read at its objects and not assumed (#19).** §4.1c records
the standing consequence that **jazz accuracy is not measurable on the corpora held at all** — the
held jazz material is melody-and-chord-symbol transcription with the bass and the piano voicings
absent — measured by the bass-injection experiment; and the census records that the jazz fit is
**deferred to the jazz-ground-truth conversion**, because a fit of that idiom has nothing to be
evaluated against (#20).

**★ ONE THING A READER COULD MISTAKE FOR VALIDATION IS NAMED AT THE MARK ITSELF.** The Jazz BIR
regression check runs the Jazz preset over the **Bach chorale** corpus. It is a regression surface
that holds behaviour against change, and our own gate corpus is not jazz ground truth — so passing it
establishes nothing whatever about these values on jazz repertoire. Without that sentence beside the
mark, a reader meeting a marked constant and a passing Jazz gate in the same document would
reasonably conclude the mark was stale.

## Why the idiom half is HELD, which is a result rather than a shortfall

The rule reaches *the idioms of the §6.7 taxonomy for which no gate-grade ground truth exists*, so
applying it needs a **per-idiom verdict**. The record does not supply one, and the gap was found by
looking for it rather than assumed:

- **What the record DOES establish:** that only the **classical common-practice** idiom is covered by
  held annotated music, and that the jazz fit is deferred until the conversion yields ground truth
  (`cowork_score_census.md` §5). Both are stated about idioms in the singular and in prose.
- **What no surface supplies:** a mapping from those statements onto the five §6.7 idiom NAMES.
- **And the one mapping §6.7 does give is complicated by the study it rests on.** §6.7 records that
  Baroque, galant and Classical share **one** idiom, Chromatic-functional. But the discovery study's
  own v1.1 finding is that the **chordified Bach chorales form no distinct chord-idiom** — they
  scatter across clusters rather than grouping — and the chorales are the gate corpus.

**So a per-idiom verdict here would be a session's reading, and #19 forbids it in BOTH directions:** a
mark placed on an idiom that IS validated states something false about it, and an exemption granted to
one that is not states something worse. The safer-looking direction is not safe either, which is why
neither was taken.

**What would close it:** a statement, on a surface that owns the question, of which of the five idioms
the held gate-grade annotated music covers. That is a corpus-census question rather than a
documentation act, which is why it is not performed here.

## What this act did not do

No analysis behaviour, no measurement, no baseline, no golden, no corpus of scores, nothing in
`tools/robust_stop/`, and no `src/` change — the marks are documentation acts in the specification,
and the constants they describe are untouched. **§6.6's own clause saying the mark is not applied at
HEAD was corrected in the same act**, with its former wording preserved in place (#12), because
leaving it would have made the specification contradict the marks a reader meets two sections away.
