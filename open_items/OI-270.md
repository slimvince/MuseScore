# OI-270 — Four cross-cutting "meta-findings to institutionalize" whose ratification the record does not state

**Section F — documentation gap. Found by the phase-1d enumeration wave, 2026-08-02. This is the
wave's one recorded REMAINDER: an entry the register would have gained if the pass were permitted
to resolve what the record leaves open, which its dispatch makes a STOP.**

## What was found

`cowork_architecture_reassessment.md:95-103`, a section headed "Meta-findings to institutionalize
(cross-cutting)", lists four:

> - **Oracle/tier metric, never a bare proxy** (BIR rewards wrong-root=bass). Make the dual metric standing.
> - **Never learn keys; the lever is keychain structure (cadence precision).** Settled from both sides.
> - **Selection/competition is saturated** — stop adding re-ranking heuristics/gates; the residual is
>   candidate-generation, key-quality, or floor.
> - **Embellishment = chord-first** (segmentation + NCT post-process), never union re-derive / richer vocabulary.

Each is stated as a conclusion carrying evidence from **two independent directions** — this
project's own measurements and an external engine that beats the published state of the art — and
the document tabulates both columns per question (`:25-32`).

## Why the entry was not written

The same document's closing section (`:105-112`) is headed **"Recommendation"** and opens
"Ratify: …". So the four are put to the user, and **this document does not record an answer.**
Writing a register entry would have required deciding one of:

1. that they were ratified elsewhere (the register's status vocabulary needs a ratifying event, and
   this pass found none in what it read — but it read 21 of 145 documents and neither archive);
2. that they are live by conduct (the record shows the project did not learn keys and did not
   invest further in selection heuristics — but inferring ratification from conduct is exactly the
   inference the never-work-from-memory rule and this dispatch both forbid); or
3. that they are dead — which nothing supports.

Under the dispatch's stop condition, the entry is not written and this row is the record instead.

## Why it matters rather than being bookkeeping

Two of the four are load-bearing on the current architecture and would, if live, be among the most
consequential entries in the register:

- **"Never learn keys"** bears directly on the joint estimator, whose key axis is fitted from
  counts. Whether that is the same thing the meta-finding forbids is not obvious and is not stated
  anywhere: the finding is about a *learned key detector* replacing rule-based key detection, and
  the estimator fits *factors* inside a declared generative form. A reader cannot tell from the
  record whether the rule binds the current design or was superseded by it.
- **"Selection/competition is saturated"** is the recorded reason the gate-dissolution work was
  deprioritized as code-health rather than accuracy (`:56`), which is still the standing
  disposition of that refactor.

## Disposition

For the user: rule whether the four were ratified, and if so when. If they were, they are four
register entries with `cowork_architecture_reassessment.md` as their home (or, better, homing acts
into the specifications they govern). If they were not, the document's Recommendation section is an
open decision surface that has been sitting since 2026-06-20, and that is itself the finding. The
archives are the most likely place an answer is recorded, and they are **not** among the surfaces
this wave read — see the [[OI-207]] note of this date.
