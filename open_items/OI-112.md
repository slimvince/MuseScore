**Dated note — 2026-08-02 (CC, the phase-1 truth-sync, commit `9df7f3ee9d`): RESOLVED, all three.**

**(a)** §4.3's single "File:" line is replaced by the two-file truth — declared in
`analysis/chord/chordanalyzer.h`, implemented in `analysis/chord/chordsymbolformatter.cpp`, with no
`chordsymbolformatter.h` — and the §4.1i backlog item that proposes creating the pair is marked
PARTLY DONE, naming the header half as the residue ([[OI-108]](b)) and noting that the boundary it was
written to protect is now enforced mechanically both ways by `inference_presentation_boundary_tests`.

**(b)** The "extensions beyond the 7th are not yet emitted" claim is corrected against
`csfDiatonicRoman`, which appends the `b9`/`#9`/`#11`/`b13` alterations above a seventh and the
`(add9)`/`(add11)`/`(add13)` forms without one (`chordsymbolformatter.cpp:590-616`). The corpus counts
stay in this row rather than in the specification prose — a hand-transcribed figure in a document is
what #17(f) exists to prevent.

**(c)** §5.11's asserted preset gating is corrected: `formatRomanNumeral()` has no preset context, so
the gate is explicitly deferred at the site (`chordsymbolformatter.cpp:882-883`) and the augmented-
sixth labels are emitted under every preset. The Jazz corpus's absence of them is recorded as the
upstream-analysis coincidence this row identified (`SharpThirteenth` unset), not as the documented
gate. **Whether the gate should exist at all is left OPEN.**
