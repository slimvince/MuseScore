# OI-347 — The two Layer-1.5 primitives are sited in different sections of the canonical architecture document, and only one of the two sitings states its reason

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — documentation / apparatus. Found 2026-08-07 (CC,
`cc_instruction_owner_rulings_homing.md`) while discharging the STOP the homing of **D-625** carries
— *STOP if the primitive is specified elsewhere*.**

## The finding

`ARCHITECTURE.md` names **two** Layer-1.5 primitives, in the terminology note of §3.3 that defines
the term: *"the shared `lineOfFifths` spelling interpreter, the phrase-boundary primitive."* The
two are sited in different sections, and the record gives a reason for only one of them.

- The **phrase-boundary** primitive's contract pointer sits in the **Layer 1** section, and that
  siting states its own reason in full: the document defines L1.5 as the thin derived-view layer
  over the L1 note model and carries no section of its own for it, so the layer the primitive is a
  view OVER is the nearest owning section; the only other section naming it consumes it and does
  not own it.
- The **shared line-of-fifths spelling** primitive is named in three places and **specified in
  none**: §3.3's terminology note defines the term L1.5 and lists it; the **Layer 4** section names
  it as the interpreter the symmetric-root spelling-pin reads through and states the one-interpreter
  rule about it; Layer 1's own *derived views over the model* list does not mention it at all.

## Why this is recorded rather than acted on

The STOP the dispatch carries asks whether the primitive is **specified elsewhere**, and the answer
is **no** — no section specifies it, so the STOP does not fire and **D-625** was homed at Layer 4
as ruled. What the check surfaced is the adjacent fact: the sibling primitive's siting reason, if
applied to this one, points at Layer 1 instead. That is a question about where a primitive's own
specification should live, and it is not the question the ruling answered.

**Nothing is changed on it.** The freeze is not relaxed, and re-siting a specification is not a
homing act. It is rowed and left.

**NOT in doubt:** the primitive itself, its behaviour, and the rule **D-625** records — that
spelling presence is tested with the validity predicate and never with a non-negative test — which
is published once, at Layer 4, with §5.14 pointing at it. No analysis behaviour, no measurement and
no register status is affected.

**In doubt:** whether the two L1.5 primitives should be sited alike, and if so where; and whether
Layer 1's *derived views over the model* list is incomplete for omitting the spelling interpreter
that §3.3 names as one of exactly two L1.5 views.
