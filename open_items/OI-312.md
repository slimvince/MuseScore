## The two halves, and why only one of them is a defect

**The flag omission is RIGHT.** The `52/24/52` case-identity sets those commands reproduce are
LEGACY-pipeline figures. Adding `--joint-inference` would measure a different system than the one
the sets describe, so the omission is what makes the diagnostic mean what it says. This is where
the phase-1x dispatch's assumption A3 came back **partly** refuted rather than refuted.

**The destination is what is owed.** `run_bach_preset.py:374-378` clean-slates its `--output-dir`
before regenerating (skipped only by `--resume` or `--skip-cpp`), and `tools/corpus/<preset>` is
exactly the directory `tools/a8_rebaseline_measure.py:290-294` reads for the block-(A) hard stop.
Following a documented, historically-correct procedure therefore destroys the production corpus.

## What phase 1y changed, and what it did not

Changed: the swap is now **visible and refused**. The regen writes `inference_arm: "legacy"` into
`corpus_manifest.json`, and `a8_rebaseline_measure.py` declares `expect_arm=joint`, so a
block-(A) measurement taken after these commands is refused instead of reported as a regression.
Measured both directions at `tools/audit/corpus_arm_establishment.json`.

Not changed: **the corpus is still destroyed.** Detection is not prevention. Re-creating it is a
full three-preset regen with the flag, and the arm stamp only ensures nobody measures the wrong
thing in the meantime.

## The remedy, and why it is not a session's to take

Send the retired diagnostic to its own directory — `tools/corpus/batch_diag/<preset>` or
similar — so the two never share a destination. One word per command, and the case-identity sets
are unaffected because the diagnostic reads whatever dir it is pointed at.

It is not taken here because **gate block (C) is a user-ratified surface**, and changing a
procedure such a surface states is a ruling rather than an edit (D-249). `BUILD_AND_TEST.md` §2
carries the same two command pairs; it now WARNS at both sites, and deliberately does not
redirect either, for the same reason.

## What is NOT in doubt

That the omission is defensible; that `tools/corpus/<preset>` is what the hard stop reads; and
that the batch diagnostic is deliberately KEPT rather than deleted, so "just remove the commands"
is not the answer.

## 2026-08-04 (CC, phase 1z, Task 5.1) — the corpus is unversioned, so recovery is not a re-run

**The fact this row was missing.** `.gitignore:26` ignores `/tools/corpus/`. The corpus the block-(A)
hard stop reads is **not under version control at all** — there is no commit that produced these
directories, no git object to compare them against, and no `git checkout` that brings them back.
Established at the object by phase 1y while back-stamping the arm ([[OI-307]]'s dated note), where it
also refuted the phase-1y dispatch's own premise that the corpus directories were committed.

**What follows for this row, and it is the part that changes the remedy's weight.** "Restore the
corpus" is not a re-run of a documented command. Re-creating it is a **re-baseline-grade act**: a
fresh three-preset regeneration produces a corpus with a new timestamp and a new `git_hash`, and gate
block (A)'s own re-baseline discipline then applies to it in full — the outgoing reference snapshotted
first (O-12), the run-level set-diff explained per case, the class-(b) duration non-increase proven per
preset, the manifest re-stamped, and the whole thing ratified. That is the cost of following block (C)'s
commands once, and nothing in either block says so.

**Detection is not prevention, restated with its consequence.** Phase 1y made the swap visible and
made the block-(A) instrument refuse a swapped-arm corpus (register entry D-468). That stops a wrong
measurement being reported as a regression. It does nothing about the corpus being gone, and with no
version control behind it, "gone" means re-derived rather than restored.

**Unchanged: redirecting the procedure stays the user's ruling.** Gate block (C) is a user-ratified
surface and changing a procedure it states is a ruling, not a session's edit (D-249). This note adds a
fact to the row; it does not narrow the question or pre-empt the answer.
