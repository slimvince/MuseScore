# OI-295 — A user-ratified rule left with no home because its section is an open-items list

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section: doc-sync / decisions register. Rowed 2026-08-03 (CC, phase 1r, Task 4), by the
re-classification pass itself.**

## The finding

**D-341** — *the licensed root-motion set is completed by theory; the ascending fifth, the descending
second and the diatonic diminished fifth are added* — is a RULE, and its own verbatim records
*"★ RATIFIED by the user 2026-07-03; the §5.0 enumeration is amended"*. It is homed at
`cowork_layer5_function_design.md` **§15, "Open items & deferred refinements"**.

The delegation the user wrote for that document names the whole document, so under the granularity
clause of `CLAUDE.md` rule (h) it reaches every section, §15 included. The pass then applied the
rule-stating half and judged §15 **RECORDS FINDINGS**. D-341 therefore stays `gap` while the
document's other seven entries move to `contract-home`.

## Why §15 was judged that way, and why the alternative was not taken

The ground is the **user's own adjacent judgment of the same day**, not this session's taste. The
voice-leading-span criterion, widened in the same write-list act, names §0/§5.1/§5.3/§8/§9 and says
in terms:

> §15/§16 are deliberately NOT named — they are ratification asks, not rule-stating sections.

That is about `cowork_voiceleading_axis_design.md`, a sibling design document whose §15 carries the
identical heading, *"Open items & deferred refinements"*. Judging the two differently would make a
mechanical criterion turn on which document a reader happened to open, which is the case-by-case
exception **D-435** was ruled to avoid.

The section's own content agrees. Its body is delivery status — *"✅ RESOLVED 2026-06-26"*,
*"✅ BUILT (dormant) + Cowork-verified 2026-06-26"*, a demotion, deferrals — which is what a tracking
surface does. Item 12 is the exception, and it is the whole of this row.

**The alternative is recorded and not taken.** Judging §15 rule-stating would move D-341 and close
this row on the spot. It is refused twice over: it contradicts the user's judgment of the same day
on the identically-titled sibling section, and it would admit a delivery-status list as a contract
home, which is the thing the kind half exists to prevent.

## This is [[OI-290]]'s shape, at a second document

OI-290 records `cowork_score_census.md` §8c as a rule-stating section that also holds a findings
table, with most admitted entries being rows of that table. This is the mirror image: a
findings-recording section that also holds one ratified rule. Both are the same underlying fact —
**the section is the unit of the criterion, and these documents were not written in sections that
separate rules from findings.** Two instances at two documents make it a class rather than an
accident, and that is the reason to row it rather than note it.

## The remedy, and why it was not executed here

At the **DOCUMENT**, not at the classification and not at the delegation: move the ratified amendment
out of the open-items list into the rule-stating section it amends (§5.0), in the
[[OI-269]]/[[OI-282]] shape — preserve and re-section, never rewrite. No delegation can repair it,
because the delegation already reaches the section.

Not executed in this wave: the dispatch licenses the register, the rows, `CLAUDE.md` and the guards,
and re-sectioning a SIGNED layer specification is none of those.

## What is not in doubt

That D-341 is a rule and was ratified; that the delegation reaches §15; that §15 also records
findings. The disagreement available to the user is only about which of those the kind half should
weigh, and both readings are written out above.
