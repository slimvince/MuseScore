# OI-358 — Five register entries quote a different rule than they are about, and both guards pass over it

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section E — verification owed / decisions register. Rowed 2026-08-09 (CC,
`cc_instruction_return_continuation_3.md`, Task 2).**

## How it was found

By the defense-gap task's own required act. The recorded method for that task is: read the entry's
record, **then open the home document and read the text AROUND the quoted span**, because a defense
is very often in the sentence after the decision rather than inside it. Reading around the span is
what makes a mismatch between an entry and its quote visible; reading the entry alone never can.

## What was found, read at both ends

In `docs/scoring_model.md` §8 the bullets stand in the same order as the identifiers **D-214 …
D-224**. For the first six the recorded home lands inside the correct bullet. From **D-220** on it
does not, and the offset grows:

| Entry | What its title, restatement and defense describe | What its recorded verbatim actually quotes |
|---|---|---|
| **D-220** | The augmented-seventh guard requires BOTH the major third and the augmented fifth — its defense correctly naming the Schumann D-major and Corelli G-major snapshot flips | the **`hasStructuralBass`** bullet |
| **D-221** | A sparse upper-register lowest note earns no inversion bonuses — defense naming the Corelli trio-sonata measure | a fragment of the **pre-sort capture** bullet |
| **D-222** | The diminished-bonus rotation fallback | the **joint-scoring** bullet |
| **D-223** | A gate judging the pre-correction winner reads a snapshot | **a horizontal rule and the heading `## 9. How to add a new template safely (checklist)`** |
| **D-224** | Joint scoring requires regional accumulation | **§9's checklist opening line and its first numbered step** |

## What is established — the STATE, not the cause

**In all five, the title, the plain restatement and the `Why` field are correct, mutually consistent,
and describe the bullet the identifier's position predicts.** The entries' MEANING survives intact.
What is wrong is the QUOTE and the LINE.

That asymmetry is itself the evidence: five consecutive entries entered with correct titles and
correct measured defenses but wrong quotes is not a plausible entry error. The offsets grow down the
section, which is what an insertion above the later bullets produces — and §8 did gain a
re-homed block on 2026-08-07.

## ★ Why no guard sees it — the reason this is a row and not a quiet repair

- `gen_decisions_register.py --check` verifies that **every verbatim resolves at its cited home**.
- `gen_cluster_dispositions.py --verify` reports **zero line drift**.

**Both are satisfied by a corrupted pair.** Once the verbatim IS the text at the drifted line, the
two agree with each other perfectly and forever. The condition is self-sealing: the moment it
happens, the machinery that exists to detect drift confirms health.

The only surviving witness is the entry's own title and defense, and **nothing compares those
against the quote.**

## The candidate cause, named and NOT asserted

A verbatim **RE-TAKE performed against an already-drifted anchor** would produce exactly this state
and would be invisible from that moment on; several entries' provenance records re-takes in those
words. **This row does not assert that is what happened.**

**One tool IS refuted as the cause and is not under suspicion.** `reaim_home_anchors.py`, read at
its own source, moves **only the line part of `home`**, never the verbatim, and it locates an entry
by **finding that entry's own quote** — so it follows a quote rather than overwriting it. It is the
correct behaviour and it is not what did this.

## ★ The detection method, which is the finding's most useful half

**Compare each entry's verbatim against its own title and defense.** That is a check nobody runs,
and it is the only one that could have caught this. It is stated here so it can be built without
rediscovering it.

## What is NOT in doubt

- **The five decisions themselves.** Their content is correctly recorded in each entry's title,
  restatement and defense, and correctly stated at `docs/scoring_model.md` §8.
- **The §8 bullets.** None is wrong; the section is fine.
- **The six entries above them**, whose homes land inside the correct bullet.
- **Nothing about the analysis's behaviour**, no measurement, no golden, nothing in
  `tools/robust_stop/`.

## Why nothing is corrected here

Correcting five verbatims and five home anchors is register DATA maintenance under **D-230**, not
the `rationale` fill the task that found it is authorized to perform. And the act needs a decision
the record does not carry: **is a corrupted verbatim re-taken from the correct bullet, restoring the
quote — or is the former, wrong quote preserved beside it (#12)?** That is a filing judgment about
the register's own unit, of the same kind the record reserves elsewhere.

## Why it GATES

Two independent grounds, either sufficient.

1. **D-438's line inside the documentation rows** puts a correction to a statement about the
   analysis on the gating side. Five entries currently state one rule's identity over another
   rule's words.
2. **An establishment obligation (#19)**, which always gates whatever its subject: two guards'
   green verdicts do not bound what they appear to bound. That is precisely the shape #19 exists to
   refuse — a thing trusted because nothing contradicted it, where what would have contradicted it
   cannot.

## Dated note — 2026-08-09 (CC, `cc_instruction_return_continuation_4.md` Task 1): the user's Ruling 24, both halves

**(a) THE FIVE ARE RE-TAKEN.** Each quote was taken from the bullet the entry's own title and
defense describe, **read in place** at `docs/scoring_model.md` §8 — not inferred from the offset,
and not from the table above. The five home anchors follow the quotes. In every one, **the former,
incorrect quote and the former anchor are preserved whole in the entry's provenance** (#12) while

the defense-gap task against the decision their title identifies keep those fields exactly as
written**, each still carrying its marker.

**(b) THE DETECTION METHOD IS BUILT, AND ITS ADOPTION VERDICT IS MEASURED.** The method this
document states in its own §"the detection method" is now
`tools/audit/decisions/gen_verbatim_subject_consistency.py`, built corpus first, with the five
known-bad quotes recorded as its labelled corpus BEFORE the mechanism was measured.

**★ THE MEASUREMENT SAYS IT MAY NOT BECOME A GUARD.** No threshold separates the five known-bad
quotes from the remainder, and after the repair four of the five entries rise while **one stays at

motivating cases. Every value is at the artifact and none is restated here (**D-431**).

**★ WHAT THIS RESOLUTION DOES NOT DISCHARGE.** This row gated on TWO independent grounds. The first
