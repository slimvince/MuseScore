## Why "commit only the three named waves" is not an available act

The six waves edit the **same files**, each layering on the last: `STATUS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`,
`OPEN_ITEMS.md`, `tools/audit/decisions/backbone_decisions.json`, the twenty rendered group files
under `decisions/`, and the guard artifacts. There is no per-wave partition of the working tree to
stage. Committing the three R4 names necessarily commits read waves 4–6 with them; the only choices
actually on the table are **one commit covering all six**, or **none**.

This is stated as a fact about the tree, not as a recommendation. Which of the two is taken is the
user's, because a commit is the act **#14** makes user-ratified and because R4's premise — that two
waves were outstanding — is not the state on disk.

## What is NOT in doubt

The work itself. Every one of the six waves is on the record in `STATUS.md` with its dispatch named,
its artifacts on disk and its guards run; the register, the open-items register and the generated
artifacts all re-derive at HEAD. This row is about the work existing **only in the working tree**,
which is the exposure [[OI-285]] recorded for a different population and closed for that one: an
uncommitted file is one `git clean`, one machine change or one working-tree reset away from gone.

The exposure is larger here than it was there, because it now includes **instruments** — the guard
tools, the yield generators and the derivation this wave's own conclusions rest on — and an
instrument that exists only in a working tree cannot support a reproducibility claim (**#16**).

## Disposition

The user's, and it is one question with two answers:

- **(a) One commit covering all six waves**, provenance-stamped, naming each wave and its dispatch.
  This is what R4's intent — get the outstanding waves committed — actually requires on this tree,
  and it is what the inseparability above forces.
- **(b) None**, leaving the backlog as it stands until a dispatch scopes the commit against the real
  population rather than against three waves.

The non-wave paths above are a **separate** decision and are not swept into either: none of them is
this arc's work, and committing them would be exactly the unilateral filing act [[OI-285]] declined
for the seven ratification surfaces.
