## The two statements

`ARCHITECTURE.md:1643-1646`, §4.1b, verbatim:

> **Safety constraints (lesson from three-attempt history):** Bonuses never fire for
> Diminished, HalfDiminished, Augmented, or Suspended candidates — only Major and Minor.
> The existing `inversionSuspicionMargin` / `inversionBonusReduction` mechanism is left
> unchanged.

`ARCHITECTURE.md:1931-1939`, §4.1g, verbatim in part:

> `supportsContextualInversionBonuses()` and `qualifiesForCompleteTriadInversionBonus()`
> in `chordanalyzer.cpp` were extended to include `Augmented` and `HalfDiminished` in
> addition to the original `Major`/`Minor` … Extending these gates put Augmented and
> HalfDiminished inversion candidates on equal footing with Major/Minor.

They name the same two helper predicates and the same four bonuses
(`stepwiseBassInversionBonus`, `stepwiseBassLookaheadBonus`, `sameRootInversionBonus`,
`completeTriadInversionBonus`). The second reverses the first for two of the four excluded
qualities, and the first carries no supersession note.

## Why it is not merely untidy

The §4.1b statement is written as a **safety constraint with a stated cause** — "lesson from
three-attempt history", the three earlier attempts recorded as having consistently regressed
(`ARCHITECTURE.md:1628-1629`). A reader encountering it reads a hard-won prohibition. The Iter 46
entry, 290 lines later in a different subsection, records that the prohibition was relaxed *because
keeping it made correct readings unreachable* — "correct inverted readings (e.g. `C+/E`, `Yø7/X`)
fell below the `results[]` cutoff entirely and were unreachable by Gate H/Q" (`:1936-1938`) — and
that relaxing it produced "the largest single improvement of iteration path 1" (`:1941-1942`).

So the two statements do not merely differ; the second is the *correction* of the first, with a
measured reason, and the first is the one a reader meets first.

The residual constraint after Iter 46 — the bonuses still do not fire for **Suspended** or
**Diminished** candidates in the `supportsContextualInversionBonuses` case — is nowhere stated as
such. It has to be reconstructed by subtracting the second passage from the first.

## Disposition

One dated annotation on §4.1b pointing at Iter 46, plus one sentence stating the constraint that
actually survives. **No code change** — the code is the later behaviour and is correct. To run with
the other `ARCHITECTURE.md` corrections ([[OI-232]] / [[OI-233]] / [[OI-238]] / [[OI-107]] /
[[OI-112]]). **Nothing is edited here.**
