# OI-302 — The LEGACY mark has a third meaning the register cannot express: the subject is dormant and the prohibition is LIVE

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section E — user adjudication / decisions register. Rowed 2026-08-03 (CC, phase 1w) as the residue
of [[OI-289]]'s verification.** Every figure and per-entry verdict lives in
`tools/audit/decisions/phase1w_legacy_verification.json`; nothing is restated here (D-431).

## What the verification met

The LEGACY marker, since the user's weakening of 2026-08-03, says one thing: what the decision is
ABOUT. That was the right correction — the clause it replaced asserted more than the marking pass had
checked, and it failed twice.

Applying the [[OI-289]] test to the whole marked set turns up a case the weakened wording still does
not cover. A group of entries is **about the dormant pipeline** — the mark is correct — **and is at
the same time restated by a LIVE specification section as a standing do-not-retry prohibition**:

> **Tried and closed on the chord layer — do not retry; the register carries each with its
> measurement: …** — `ARCHITECTURE.md:1420`, with the same construction on the key layer at `:1344`
> and on the search at `:306`.

`docs/redesign_plan.md:4`, the home of four of them, states the distinction in its own words: the
dead ends *"are LIVE prohibitions about the legacy chord path"*. So a reader who meets only the mark
is told the subject is dormant, and is not told the rule still binds what they may attempt. **This is
the largest transfer class in the marked set** — larger than the ruling-transfer class the [[OI-289]]
row was opened on, and it was not in this wave's registered prediction at all.

It is not a wrong mark and it is not a missing transfer variant either: the transfer variant says a
ruling carried the *principle* to the live design, and that is a different act from a specification
telling a future designer not to re-open a closed line of work.

## The three things this row carries, none of them a session's to settle

**(a) Does the register need a field for it, or is the existing pointer machinery enough?** The
entries are already reachable from `ARCHITECTURE.md`; what is missing is the reverse direction — the
entry does not say that a live specification enforces it.

**(b) D-423's programme-wide claim.** The entry says its three prohibitions hold *"for the whole
programme"* while carrying the LEGACY mark, and **one of the three is the rule the user's 2026-08-03
ruling already carried to the live design as D-429's transfer**. Either the programme-wide claim is a
transfer the record has not ruled, or it is scoped to the legacy path and the wording overstates it.
Both readings are consistent with the text, which is why the verification recorded UNDETERMINED
rather than choosing.

**(c) The remaining undetermined transfers**, each with its stated reason in the artifact. The ones
worth the eye: **D-061** (the never-widen-a-Baroque-threshold rule is also a standing `CLAUDE.md`
instruction, so the prohibition may already be live independently of the register); **D-068** (a
minimum-content admission rule, which is the same KIND of rule the live family design is about —
[[OI-226]]); **D-321**/**D-322** (an exact-comparison ranking convention and the corpus-A/B
obligation that follows from it — decoder-wide properties whose live counterpart was not read);
**D-325**/**D-326**; **D-344**; **D-358**; **D-380**.

## The one candidate wrong mark — reported, NOT corrected

**D-055** — *the 21 mode priors are independent and user-configurable*. The twenty-one mode-prior
preferences are registered unconditionally in the module configuration
(`composingconfiguration.cpp:63` onward), which is production code that runs at startup, while the
**consumer** that reads them is the legacy key scorer. Whether the settings are themselves
legacy-scoped — and so retire with the scorer — is a scoping judgment, not a code fact, and the
phase-1w dispatch licenses correcting a mark only where the wave ESTABLISHES it wrong. The mark
stands and the question is here.

## What is NOT in doubt

The marking convention (`CLAUDE.md` decisions-register rule (f), user-ratified 2026-08-02) and the
2026-08-03 weakening. This row proposes nothing that weakens either; it records that the weakened
mark, applied honestly across the set, leaves one thing unsaid.
