# OI-217 — buildAdapterFacts is near-quadratic in events (repeated full note scans)

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — the analysis-cost / notation-surface cluster (fact-extraction cost; sealed §S finding S3)**

**The observation (Cowork, at the analysis-cost measurement).** `buildAdapterFacts` — the score→facts
step that precedes the decode on the production notation path — fits **`events^1.80`** (95 % CI
1.65–2.09, R²=0.965, n=27), i.e. near-quadratic, and takes **16.8 s on the largest committed score**
before the decode even begins. It was reported as a scaling FACT in CC's analysis-cost report; §S notes
it should have been flagged as a surprise (a near-quadratic fact-extraction floor is a robustness/cost
concern for the "very large scores must be handled" requirement, [[OI-209]]).

**The mechanism (CC-confirmed at the code, OI-199 pass 1).** Two repeated-scan structures in
`jointfactadapter.cpp::buildAdapterFacts`:
1. **The event-lattice build** (`:465-491`) nests `for (const NoteRec& n : notes)` (`:471`, the
   `anySounding` / fermata scan) INSIDE `for (bi …)` over the boundary list (`:466`). Since the boundary
   list length is O(events) and the inner scan is O(notes), and notes ≈ events, this is **O(events ×
   notes) ≈ O(events²)**.
2. **`melodic`** (`:279`) scans the whole part-note list to find the adjacent onset (`:288`) and again to
   pick the min-|Δmidi| note (`:303`), and it is called twice per note (`:439-442`) → **O(notes ×
   part_notes)** per part.

Both are the "repeated derivation over the full collection" shape (DT-6-adjacent). A sweep-line over
sorted boundaries (the notes are already sorted) and an interval index would make the event-lattice
build linear; `melodic` needs only the immediately-adjacent onset, obtainable in O(1) amortized from the
sorted per-part list. The near-quadratic law is therefore a fixable structure, not an inherent cost of
the analysis.

**Not a correctness matter.** The extracted facts are correct (byte-parity with the Python
`gen_note_events` on the pinned corpus). This is a cost/algorithm concern. **Declared to Cowork; NOT
acted on** (OI-199 read-only; any refactor is a performance-design decision at its #8-correct stage, and
is OI-199's own subject matter). Related: [[OI-216]] (the decode's cost structure — the other half),
[[OI-215]] (the decoder robustness cliff), [[OI-209]] (the large-score requirement this bears on).

*Provenance: the scaling law is CC's analysis-cost measurement (2026-07-28, `events^1.80`); the
repeated-scan mechanism was confirmed independently at OI-199 pass 1 (`cc_oi199_pass1_report.md` §5).
Sealed §S finding S3, rowed at the OI-199 pass-1 unblind commit.*
