# OI-253 — Fermata detection is track-exact: a fermata on one voice does not flag a simultaneous chord in another voice, and the flag feeds two fitted factors

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — Layer 1 / fermata fact (small, fitted-covariate-affecting).**

`note_model.cpp:81` matches a fermata to a chord only when `a->track() == chord->track()` — a
fermata engraved on voice 1 does not flag a simultaneous voice-2 chord on the same staff, and
cross-staff placement is missed. The flag is not cosmetic: it enters the boundary factor as a
phrase-end prior and the cadence factor as a location prior (factorization §7 factor 8, both
FITTED). On SATB chorales engraved two-voices-per-staff the fermata typically sits on one track
per staff, so up to three of four voices' simultaneous chords carry no flag — the fit corpus
itself is affected, making this one of the census findings that is NOT confined to
out-of-envelope repertoire. Whether the fitted values absorbed the track-exactness (they were
fitted on facts extracted the same way) is exactly the [[OI-228]]-class refit caveat. Not fixed
here.
