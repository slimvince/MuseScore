## What was established, and at which object

**The item, read at the artifact.** `tools/audit/phase1_finish_line.json` carries an item named
*"Register entries with no home at all — recorded only on a session-handoff archive or a superseded
status archive"*. Its population is one entry, **D-289**, whose current home it gives as
`cowork_handoff_archive.md`. Its closing act reads: *"Per entry: write the decision into the
specification that owns its subject, and leave the archive text in place."*

**The entry, read at the register data.** `tools/audit/decisions/backbone_decisions.json` records
D-289 with `status: "superseded-by"` and `superseded_by: "D-284 (and through it D-036 with
D-001/D-010), with D-288 and D-287"`. Its own plain restatement says so in terms — *"The rule is
SUPERSEDED as an entry: every part of it is stated by a later decision that is written into a
specification, so it is recorded here and homed nowhere, which is what keeps one rule in one
place."*

**The ruling, read at its carrier.** Ruling 6 of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_09_return.md` ruled D-289
SUPERSEDED by the D-642 route, conditional on the successors carrying all of its live content — a
condition the second return continuation checked clause by clause at the successors' own homes and
recorded as HOLDING. That ruling's own words: *nothing is written into any specification for it — a
supersession is register business.* Its excluded alternative is recorded there too: homing it as
doctrine, which would duplicate its successors' rules (#6).

**The asymmetry, read at the generator.** `tools/audit/gen_phase1_finish_line.py` builds the
neighbouring homing item with a subtraction: it imports
`tools/audit/decisions/r1_superseded_reach.json` and publishes, beside it, **D-642**'s own words —
*"criterion C1 reaches every decision whose content is LIVE; a superseded entry's obligation moves
to its successor and is discharged where that successor is homed."* **This item performs no such
subtraction.** Its population is `class_2_recorded_only_on_a_tracking_surface` from the completion
inventory, which is the register's raw `unhomed` class with no status test of any kind.

**And the gap is STRUCTURAL rather than one run's oversight, which is the sharper half.** Four of the
five homing items are built through the same `recut` helper, and that helper reads its discharged set
from `r1_superseded_reach.json` — whose own population is **item 1's NO-HOME class and nothing else**.
D-289 is not in that class; it is in the register's `unhomed` class, which is a different cut. **So
the subtraction could not reach this entry even if the item were built through the helper**, because
the artifact the helper consults never considers it. That is why neither closing act below is a
one-line repair, and why choosing between them is a mechanism question rather than a typo.

## Why it is worth a row rather than a remark

The finish line is the artifact a commissioning reader opens to learn what phase 1 still requires,
and its own closing statement is the ninth item on it. **An item asking for an act the user has
ruled must not be performed is not a small inaccuracy on that surface** — a session reading it in
good faith would write D-289's meta-principle into a specification, which is precisely the
duplication Ruling 6 excluded by name.

It is also the [[OI-283]] shape at one remove: an obligation that has been dispositioned but still
reads as owed sends the next session to do work the record has already declined.

## What is NOT claimed

**That the item is wrong.** Whether a derived cut subtracts a class is a derivation question, and
changing a derivation is a mechanism change **D-436** reserves to the user. Both readings are
defensible: the item may be right to count what the register's `unhomed` class holds and wrong only
in the ACT it names, or right in neither.

**That D-289's whole successor chain is homed.** D-287 and D-288 are — both `home_is_layer_spec:
true` at `ARCHITECTURE.md`, read at the register data. **D-284 is not**: it is itself
`superseded-by` and `nonspec_kind: "gap"`, and its own successors (D-036 with D-001/D-010) were NOT
re-checked by this pass. The second return continuation checked the chain when the ruling was
applied; this row does not re-establish it and does not rest on it.

**Anything about the analysis** — no measured value, no golden, no corpus of scores, nothing in
`tools/robust_stop/`. Every subject here is the record's own completion map.

## The two closing acts, neither of them a session's

**(a) Subtract, by the sibling's machinery.** The item applies the same D-642 subtraction the
neighbouring homing item applies, using the same artifact rather than a second implementation (#6).
This empties the item and moves the finish line's counts.

**(b) Keep the entry and correct the ACT.** The item goes on counting the entry — which is a true
statement about the register's `unhomed` class — and its closing act is corrected to say what
Ruling 6 ruled: no home is owed, the entry is dispositioned, and what closes it is the record
saying so rather than a write.

Either moves a population or an authored closing act on a derived surface, which is why neither was
taken here.
