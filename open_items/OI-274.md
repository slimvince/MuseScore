## The finding

`docs/scoring_model.md` §1 opens, verbatim (`:32-34`):

> `RuleBasedChordAnalyzer::analyzeChord` is a **bottom-up** vertical-sonority
> scorer: pitch-class evidence → chord identity. It performs no harmonic-function
> reasoning (that lives in Phase E — secondary dominants, tonicization, etc.).

The whole document is written in that present tense — the pipeline "is", the gates "run", the
oracle "computes". Nowhere does it say that the scorer it describes is **dormant on both
production surfaces**: the joint estimator is the production inference layer on the batch and
corpus surface since 2026-07-26 (`CLAUDE.md` gate block (A), register entry **D-005**) and on the
notation surface since 2026-07-27 (**D-010**), with the legacy path compiled and selected only by
an explicit `useJointNotationRecord = false`.

That is exactly the defect [[OI-232]] found eight times in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and [[OI-265]] found
a ninth time in its Layer-4 body. Both were resolved by adding a **scoping sentence** — the
Layer-3 form, "the description below remains accurate for the dormant decoder … it is not a
description of what runs". `docs/scoring_model.md` has no such sentence.

## Why it matters more here than in an ordinary stale document

`CLAUDE.md` makes this document a **mandatory session-start read** for a whole class of session:

> **Read `docs/scoring_model.md` at the start of any session that touches scoring
> logic in `chordanalyzer.cpp`** — this includes adding or modifying templates,
> bonuses, guards, gates, score matrices, or post-scoring passes.

So a session directed here is directed to a document that presents a dormant scorer as the
system's scoring pipeline, and no other mandatory read corrects it: `CLAUDE.md`'s own scoring
sections describe the same scorer in the same tense. The one place the truth is stated —
`ARCHITECTURE.md`'s corrected Layer-3 and Layer-4 blocks — is not part of that session's
mandated reading.

## What is NOT in doubt

The scoring content itself. Nothing in this row says a term, a gate or a constant is described
wrongly; §8's twelve constraints are all registered (**D-214**…**D-224**, **D-061**) and the

not say which of the two chord paths it is describing.
