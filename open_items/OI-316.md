# OI-316 — The reading regime's registered predictions cannot survive their own update protocol: the generator refits the bands on the documents being graded

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section D — instrument / measurement layer. Found 2026-08-04 (CC, READ WAVE 1,
`cc_instruction_reads_1.md`) on the first attempt to follow the regime's own recording protocol.**

## The protocol, and what following it would do

`tools/audit/decisions/phase1n_reading_regime.json` → `bands.registration` instructs each read wave:

> *"Every later read wave writes the actual yield into `actual_yield_when_read` on the owed row it
> read, and reports it against `predicted_yield_band`. That makes the proxy tested as a by-product
> of the reading rather than by a separate study, and it is the only route by which a tail could
> ever become boundable."*

The field lives in a **generated** artifact, so the only lawful way to write it is through
`tools/audit/decisions/gen_phase1n_reading_regime.py` (register rule (d), #17f). Read at the
generator this session:

- the read/owed partition comes from `gen_phase1m_measurements.READ_WAVES`, an authored per-wave
  table — so recording a wave's reads means adding the wave there;
- the bands are then computed as **terciles of the READ set's own line-count distribution**, and each
  tercile's `observed_yield_min/median/max/mean` from the yields of the documents **in that tercile**;
- `actual_yield_when_read` is emitted as `None` unconditionally, and a document that has been read
  leaves `owed_rows` for `read_rows` altogether.

So updating the read set does three things at once: it moves the document out of the row that holds
its prediction, it **recomputes the prediction from a set that now contains that document**, and it
leaves no place for the recorded actual. A prediction refitted on the data it is being graded against
is the fit/evaluation collapse #20 forbids, and losing the registered value is #12.

## Why this matters beyond bookkeeping

The registration is the *whole* basis on which a tail could ever be bounded — `no_tail_is_bounded`
says so in terms, and the proxy is declared unvalidated (#17d) precisely because it is fitted and
self-measured on the read set. A registration that silently refits itself each wave would leave the
record with a proxy that always looks calibrated and never was tested.

## What READ WAVE 1 did instead, and what is owed

**Did:** left `gen_phase1m_measurements.READ_WAVES` and the regime artifact untouched, and recorded
the wave's six reads and their measured yields in a generated artifact of their own —
`tools/audit/decisions/reads1_yield.json`, from `gen_reads1_yield.py`, which reads each document's
registered band and point off the regime artifact rather than recomputing them, and re-derives.

**Owed, and none of it is a session's to choose:** whether the registered bands are FROZEN at
registration (a fourth epoch, the pattern `gen_home_classification.py` already uses for its class
fields and `gen_phase1p_delegation_bar.py` for its grades), or whether the regime artifact becomes
read-only at registration with the grading living permanently beside it, or whether the read/owed
partition and the prediction are separated into two artifacts. Until one is chosen, **the read count
moves in `reads1_yield.json` and in the [[OI-207]] note and NOT in the regime artifact**, which is a
divergence a later reader must not mistake for a stalled wave.

## One measured fact the wave produced that bears directly on the fix

Every registered band has a **minimum of 0**, because every length tercile of the read set holds at
least one zero-yield document. A band from 0 to the tercile maximum can only be missed by a document
yielding more than any document of its tercile ever has — so `inside_the_registered_band` is close to
unfalsifiable and passing it establishes almost nothing. Wave 1 passed it 6/6 while landing 4 of 6
**below** the point prediction. Whatever epoch treatment is chosen, the band as registered is not the
informative half; the point prediction is.
