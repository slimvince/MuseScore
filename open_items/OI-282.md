## The finding

The clustering plan is titled "Empirical style clustering — planned future work (**the style taxonomy
+ the style weights**, from corpora)" and states the pair as one undelivered object:

- `:10-14` — "Cluster a large body of tonal scores by their **harmonic-feature distributions**, and
  read the result two ways: the **emergent clusters** are data-derived **styles** — replacing/refining
  the hand-made style taxonomy … each cluster's **feature distribution** is the **per-style weight
  set**";
- `:16` — "The **taxonomy and the weights are one data-derived object**";
- `:29-30` — "it lets us **factually decide the taxonomy** rather than assert it from theory".
- Banner `:3` — "committed future direction (user-ratified 2026-06-29) — 'we will do this sooner or
  later.' **Not now**".

The taxonomy half was delivered the following day. `cowork_style_taxonomy_proposal.md:3-9` banners
"**RATIFIED (2026-06-30) · EXECUTED (StyleTag swap … 2026-07-02)**", grounded in the completed
discovery study `cowork_idiom_discovery_findings.md` v1.5 (5,243 pieces, cap-robust, `:122`); the
five idioms are encoded as `enum class Idiom` + `IdiomSet`; and
`cowork_progression_schema_dictionary.md:317-330` records the supersession. The clustering plan
carries **no annotation of any of this** — a full-text search of the document for *idiom*, *five*, or
*2026-06-30* returns only unrelated hits (its corpus-survey dates and one use of "idiomatic").

So a reader who reaches the clustering plan — which is exactly where `ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.7 and the
dictionary §12.1 both send a reader for the remaining empirical grounding — is told that the
categories are still to be discovered, when they are discovered, ratified and compiled in.

## Why it matters now, specifically

The user's 2026-08-03 ruling narrowed register entry **D-132** to the **per-preset weights alone**,
marking the clusters half superseded by the 2026-06-30 ratification, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.7 was
restated to say so. The clustering plan is the document §6.7 names as the home of the surviving half.
The register and the canonical architecture document now say one thing and the delegated plan says
another — the same shape as [[OI-279]] itself, one document further out.

## What is NOT in doubt

The plan's substance. Its §2 defense of the weights (load-bearing for a future chord-suggestion tool;
a sub-point inference gain worth banking in the best-possible-inferrer regime, `:20-30`) is the
recorded defense the narrowed D-132 now cites, and this row does not disturb it. Nor is the plan
superseded as a whole: the weights half is genuinely owed and genuinely lives here.
