# OI-298 — `CLAUDE.md`'s phase-2 clause leans on an unratified draft for the enumeration it requires

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section F — doc-sync debt (#10) / governance. Rowed 2026-08-03 at phase 1u on the user's ruling
of the same date (option 2B: the inventory becomes the one home and is ratified; the clause points
at it). This row carries the gap until that ratification happens.**

## The finding

`CLAUDE.md`'s three-phase rule — user-directed, register entry **D-231** — makes phase 2 turn on
*"the enumerated discovery channels"* and then names six subjects in a parenthesis: populations,
oracles, invariants, residual decomposition, concept gaps, requirement side.

**The only place in the record where those channels are actually enumerated is
`cowork_oi200_perspective_inventory.md`.** Read at the document this session:

- its banner reads *"STATUS: DRAFT for discussion (Cowork, 2026-08-01)"* (`:3`);
- its §9 records that its one requested decision — adopt, amend or reject its proposed program —
  has not been taken (`:335-336`);
- its §4 enumerates **ten** channels, headed *"Channel 1"* through *"Channel 10"* (`:113`–`:234`).

So a binding rule leans on an unratified draft, and the six subjects the rule names under-name what
that draft holds. The four the rule does not name are channels 4, 8, 9 and 10.

## Why it is more than a filing problem

Phase 2's completion condition is *"every channel enumerated"*. A completion condition whose
enumeration lives in an unratified draft cannot be met by reading the rule: either the six subjects
are the whole of it — in which case four of the draft's channels are outside phase 2 and nobody has
said so — or the draft is the enumeration, in which case a user-directed gate rests on a document
the user has not ruled on.

The phase-3 gate partition already had to work around it. Its
`the_channel_enumeration_source.status_of_this_source` block states the problem and then states
each verdict against the SUBJECT the rule names, using the draft's channel numbers as locators
only. That workaround is why no verdict there depends on the draft's authority — and it is the
workaround this row exists to retire.

## What this session found in the document while checking it

Reported here because it bears on what a ratification would ratify:

- **Channel 4 is not a distinct search.** The inventory says of it *"Proposed probe: none new — the
  channel is already mandated"* — it is an obligation carried by the other probes.
- **Channel 8 is the audit passes the rule already names**, under other words: *"already scheduled
  — OI-199 pass 2 and partitions 2–3 run exactly this"*.
- **Channels 9 and 10 are distinct searches the rule names nowhere**, and both are GATING on the
  phase-3 partition's own criterion.
- **One statement in the draft is not true at HEAD.** Channel 9 says *"none new — the adjudication
  is this channel run to completion"*. The OI-207 adjudication is not run to completion: its
  residual second pass ran on 2026-08-02 and both faces are live — the unresolved cluster residual
  (`tools/audit/decisions/disposition_manifest.json` → `disposition_counts.unresolved`) and the owed
  full document reads tracked on the [[OI-207]] row.

The per-item reading is recorded, generated, at `tools/audit/phase3_gate_partition.json` →
`assumption_A1_of_the_phase1u_dispatch.findings`.

## What was done here, and what was deliberately not

**Done:** a dated note at the phase-2 clause recording that the enumeration it relies on is
unratified and that its ratification is owed; and the reading surface built, at
`ratification_surfaces/cowork_perspective_inventory_ratification.md`.

**Deliberately not done: the clause was NOT changed.** Pointing a binding rule at content the user
has not ratified is the defect this row records; changing the clause to point at the inventory
before the ratification would re-commit that defect in a new form. The pointing half is the act
that follows a ruling, not the act that anticipates one.

**Also not done: the draft's channel-9 statement was not corrected.** Correcting a document while
presenting it for ratification changes what is being ratified (**D-249** — a ruling is only
readable against the surface it answered). It is flagged on the reading surface instead.

## What is NOT in doubt

That the six subjects the clause names bind on the clause's own authority — they are the user's
words in a user-directed rule, and nothing here weakens them. This row is about what lies *beyond*
those six, and about the document they were drawn from.

## Disposition

For the user: rule on `ratification_surfaces/cowork_perspective_inventory_ratification.md` §5. The
act that follows a ratification is stated there in full — the banner flip, the clause change, the
register entry, the partition's regeneration, and this row's flip.

**Under the non-gating declaration (`CLAUDE.md`, the open-items register section, register entry
D-438) this is an apparatus row: its subject is a pointer and a ratification status, not the
analysis or an instrument a measurement depends on. It blocks nothing and stays owed.**
