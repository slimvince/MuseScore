# OI-334 — The decisions register's own published account of itself is FALSE at HEAD in three places, and one of the three is a figure its own computed table contradicts

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Found 2026-08-04 (CC, the phase-1 completion inventory) by deriving what D-231's phase-1
completion statement would rest on, and checking each load-bearing statement at its object.** This
is the [[OI-332]] / [[OI-276]] class one document over, and the document is the register index

## What is false, each checked at the object

### (1) The scope block says the per-layer design documents were NOT read in full. They were.

`DECISIONS.md` → *"What was read, and what was not"* → **"Not read in full."** names *"The per-layer
and per-component design documents (`cowork_layer*_design.md`, `cowork_progression_schema_dictionary.md`,
`cowork_voiceleading_axis_design.md`, `cowork_bounded_context_design.md` and their siblings)"* and
closes *"Each was opened where a specific citation required it and is not claimed to be swept."*

At HEAD **every design document that sentence names — literally and by glob — has been read in
full**, and each one's yield is on the record. The enumeration and the per-document evidence are
generated at `tools/audit/phase1_completion_inventory.json` →
`what_the_register_says_about_itself_that_is_no_longer_true.statement_1_not_read_in_full`; no list or
count is restated here (**D-431**).

**And it was not the read waves that made it false.** The three documents the sentence names by
filename were read in full by the register's **own earlier phase-1 waves** — they sit in
`phase1n_reading_regime.json` → `read_rows`, each with the wave that read it and its yield — so the
sentence was already false when the reading regime was registered on 2026-08-03, and the six read
waves only widened it to the glob.

### (2) The scope block publishes an unresolved-residual figure the same file's computed table contradicts

The same block states a figure for how many clusters carry the `unresolved` disposition. The
register's own **computed** coverage table, four sections above it in the same generated file, gives
a different one — materially smaller. Both quantities are in
`phase1_completion_inventory.json` → `...statement_2_measured_remainder`; neither is restated here
(**D-431**).

Two things are wrong, not one. **The published figure is superseded:** `open_items/OI-207.md` records
the residual moving when the disposition layer was regenerated at the ratified backbone, and the
scope block still carries the pre-move figure. **And the figure is HAND-CARRIED into a generated
file:** `header.scope.measured_remainder` in `tools/audit/decisions/backbone_decisions.json` is an
authored string that `gen_decisions_register.py` renders verbatim, so a number inside it is a
transcription — exactly what **D-431** / #17f forbids — and nothing regenerates it when the layer it
describes moves.

### (3) The home-granularity section claims every `gap` and `contract-home` entry carries a section block. Most `gap` entries do not.

`DECISIONS.md` → *"The home field's granularity, and what sets a home class"* states that *"Every
entry whose class is `contract-home` or `gap` therefore carries a `home_section` block naming its
section, the criterion that decided it, and the class it carried before this pass."*

At HEAD that holds for every `contract-home` entry and for a minority of `gap` entries: the entries
the six read waves entered carry **no `home_section` block at all**, because the only writer of that
field is `gen_home_classification.py`'s apply mode, which is UNRUN. The counts are at
`phase1_completion_inventory.json` → `the_complete_half.class_1b_gap_NOT_YET_section_classified`.

**This instance has a known cause and an owner:** [[OI-319]] rows the growing `--check` report and
[[OI-305]] rows why the apply may not simply be run. What THIS row adds is that the consequence
reached the register's own published prose, where a reader meets it as a completed fact.

## What is NOT affected

**No decision, no home, no status and no measurement of the analysis is wrong on account of any of
the three.** Every entry's `home` field is what it always was; `gen_decisions_register.py --check`
passes, because the false statements are authored INPUTS that regenerate faithfully; the coverage
table is computed and correct. What is wrong is the register's account of its own scope and coverage.

## Dated note — 2026-08-04 (CC, dispatch `cc_instruction_phase1_delegations_and_corrections.md`, ruling R3): all three are true at HEAD, closed by three DIFFERENT acts

Recorded here rather than only in the INDEX because the three closures are not one act, and a later
reader tracing any one of them should not have to infer which.

**(1) Corrected in prose.** The `not_read_in_full` sentence now names only the two archives and the
`cc_*` session reports. Its design-document half is struck, and the sentence carries the reason in
itself — the OI-207 reading programme read every one of them in full, finishing with read wave 6 on
2026-08-04 — with the per-document enumeration cited to the generated artifacts rather than restated
(**D-431**).

**(2) Corrected AND made computed, which is the half that mattered.** `header.scope.measured_remainder`
is no longer a sentence someone wrote: it is a **template**, and `gen_decisions_register.py` fills
every figure in it from the same computed data it renders the coverage table from. A placeholder the
generator cannot fill, or one left unfilled after formatting, **stops the generator** — so a figure
can no longer be hand-carried into this generated file at all, which is what #17f asks for and what
the old field defeated. Two qualifications the old wording did not carry are now stated with the
figure: it is the COMMITTED layer's, derived at a smaller backbone than the register now holds and so
overstating the residual in a known direction; and the layer cannot be re-derived at HEAD
([[OI-333]]), so no refreshed figure may be published and this one may not be called current.

**(3) NOT corrected in prose — it became TRUE.** This row said (3) *"self-heals when [[OI-305]] is
ruled and the apply runs"*, and that is what happened rather than a re-wording. The user ruled (R2)
that the phase-1q record be snapshotted and the snapshot **established as faithful before** the apply
ran; both were done (`tools/audit/decisions/snapshot_2026-08-04_pre_home_classification_apply/`,
with its `establishment.json` recording three independent checks), and the apply then ran. Every
`contract-home` and every `gap` entry now carries a `home_section` block, so the register's claim is
true as written.

**Both former wordings are preserved verbatim (#12)** at `tools/audit/decisions/backbone_decisions.json`
→ `header.scope.corrections_2026_08_04.former_wording_preserved_verbatim_12`, and the rendered
register publishes a correction block naming what changed and why it was not erased.

**One thing this wave changed that the row did not ask for, and it is the durable half.** The check
that grades these three statements carried an **authored verdict string** per statement — *"FALSE AT
HEAD…"* — which would have gone stale the moment the statements were corrected, in exactly the way
the statements themselves did. Every verdict is now **derived from the checks beside it**, so the
block re-reports the state at HEAD on each run instead of asserting a past one.
