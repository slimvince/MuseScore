## What is stale, and against what

(`CLAUDE.md` gate block (A); `composingconfiguration.cpp:178` sets `Val(true)`). The prose beside the
flag did not move with it. Three shapes:

- **The four branch-point comments** — one at each of the four places the flag is read — still open

  legacy arm is what runs, which is the opposite of the truth, and the four comments are the natural
  place to look for exactly that answer.
- **The record section adapter's own header** still says its function is *"DORMANT — no `src/` caller
  yet"*. Three `src/` call sites exist — the span emitter, the implode bridge and the tuning bridge —
  and they are the production ones.

  *"stays on the legacy analysis (its increment is the named successor)"*. That was the staged scope
  before the switch; the switch is the successor, and it landed.

## Why this is more than tidiness

It is the [[OI-289]] failure shape reproduced in the code's own prose: a statement about liveness
that nothing checked, sitting where a reader goes to check liveness. The register's LEGACY marker had
its liveness clause withdrawn on 2026-08-03 precisely because a swept claim about the live system is
not established (#19); these six make the same claim, in the same direction, in the file the claim is
about. `ARCHITECTURE.md:1330-1340` already carries the corrected statement — so the specification and
the code comments disagree, and the specification is the one that is right.
