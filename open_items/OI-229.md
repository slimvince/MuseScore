## The convention

Generalizing the earlier "instrument" case (that word means a violin, not a measurement script):
**any term that coincides even slightly with music theory is reserved for its music-theory
meaning.** The rationale, in the user's framing: this is a music-analysis system, and an
ambiguous domain vocabulary makes every document harder to read and every specification easier to
misapply. The rule is now in `CLAUDE.md` Conventions (a riding Cowork edit committed with this
row) and **binds new writing immediately**. The disambiguation convention is one rule covering
every case: **the bare word always carries the musical meaning; every non-musical use is
explicitly qualified** (e.g. *candidate score* / *content score* for a number, bare *score* for
the music; *map key* / *cache key* for a lookup handle, bare *key* for tonality; *measurement* /
*to gauge* for the gauging sense, bare *measure* for the bar).

## What is NOT decided here

The existing tree is **NOT renamed unilaterally.** That pass is its own scoped, ratified work
item, because some names carry correspondence to the published research the design is grounded in
(#1/#2), which makes the rename a **decision surface** rather than a mechanical sweep. What binds
now is: no NEW collision is introduced, and anything written for the user avoids the collided
sense entirely.

## The starting inventory of known collisions (from the CLAUDE.md entry)

*instrument*, *score* (numerical vs musical), *key* (map key vs tonality), *measure* (to gauge vs
the bar), *stem* (file-name stem vs note stem), *note* (annotation vs pitch event), *mode*
(operating mode vs musical mode), *tie* (tie-break vs notated tie), *dynamic* (dynamic
programming vs dynamics), *register* (the open-items register vs pitch register), *beat* (to
defeat vs the pulse), *scale* (to grow with vs the collection), *figure* (a reported value vs
figuration), *interval* (uncertainty range vs pitch interval), *resolution* (level of detail vs
harmonic resolution), *sharpen* (to refine vs to raise a pitch), *flat* (featureless vs the
accidental), *root* (underlying cause vs chord root), *part* (portion vs musical part), *rest*
(remainder vs silence).

## The two structural cases (not careless — real renaming cost)

**score** (a candidate's numerical score versus the music) and **measure** (to gauge versus the
bar) are pervasive, are used in this project in **both** senses already, and both carry a real
cost to renaming. They are flagged so the eventual scoped cleanup treats them deliberately rather
than mechanically.
