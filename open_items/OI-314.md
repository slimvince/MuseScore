## The four that do not

**Reading `.ours.json` CONTENT out of the production corpus:**

- `tools/audit/l4/pass1_satellites_firerate.py:36-38` — globs every `*.ours.json` in
  `tools/corpus/<preset>` and reads every region. It opens the manifest at `:29-34`, but for its
  `git_hash` alone.
- `tools/classify_key_disagreement.py:509-514` — opens `tools/corpus/<preset>/<stem>.ours.json`
  directly and loads it through `compare_analyses.load_analysis`. No import of
  `characterise_bir_false` anywhere in the file.

**Copying the manifest's `git_hash` into a report as provenance, without validating what it
describes:**

- `tools/measure_fanout.py:162-164`
- `tools/measure_joint_probe.py:507-509`

## Why the second pair is not merely tidiness

Stamping a corpus hash into a report is a **provenance claim about a directory the tool never
checked**. If the corpus is incomplete, contaminated, or — now — the wrong inference arm, the
report says nothing about it while carrying a hash that reads as if it did. That is the
recorded-but-unestablished shape #19 exists for, and it is worse than no stamp, because a stamp
invites trust.

All four also miss the arm check added on 2026-08-03, so a swapped-arm corpus is invisible to
them for the same reason it was invisible to everything before this wave.

## What is NOT in doubt

The guard itself: its own tests pass (`tools/tests/test_metric_scripts.py`, 67 tests), and its
establishment history is on [[OI-129]] and [[OI-124]].

## Why it is rowed rather than fixed

The phase-1y dispatch's accepted-outcomes section names this as a second hole to be **rowed, not
fixed here**. Part of the fix is a decision this row does not settle: the two content readers are
audit-pass artifacts of named earlier arcs (EG-7 Layer-4 pass 1; the key-disagreement
classification), so whether they are ever re-run — and therefore whether they need the guard or
need retiring — is part of the same judgment.
