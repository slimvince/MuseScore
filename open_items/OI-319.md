## What moved, and why it is this wave's doing

`gen_home_classification.py --check` reported **4** stale classification fields at the committed tree
(D-321…D-324, the [[OI-305]] finding). After this wave entered its register entries it reports **29**:
the same four, plus **25 new gap-homed entries that carry no `home_section` at all**.

Those 25 are not drift. The four are a *recorded* value that no longer re-derives; the 25 are a field
that has never been written, because the only writer is `gen_home_classification.py` in apply mode.

## Why the apply was deliberately not run

[[OI-305]] states the reason in its own words, and it is a warning against exactly this act:

> *"If the artifact is historical, what is owed is the epoch treatment the classifier already uses
> for its class fields, not a regeneration that would overwrite the record of what phase 1q found —
> the [[OI-301]] hazard."*

Running the apply would have written `phase1q_reclassification.json` from today's inputs, discharging
[[OI-305]]'s first half as a side effect and destroying the evidence its second half asks a diagnosis
about. A read wave has no licence to resolve a rowed finding by regenerating over it, and the row
asks for a ruling rather than a fix.

## What the delegation grades this wave DID author, and why that is different

Five documents became somebody's home for the first time, and
`gen_phase1p_delegation_bar.FORMS` **stops** on a home document with no authored grade — by design,
so the grade cannot be skipped. The five grades were made by reading the located line in place
(`docs/llm_integration.md`, `cowork_layer6_grouping_design.md` and `cowork_target_architecture.md` all
graded **bare-appended-citation**; `cowork_factorization_desk_simulation.md` and
`docs/layer_architecture_audit.md` are **named in none of the three ratified surfaces**), all five
therefore EXCLUDE, and no section-kind judgment is reached. Supplying an input a tool refuses to run
without is not the same act as regenerating an artifact another row is protecting.

## What is owed

The 25 fields are populated by whatever ruling [[OI-305]] receives — one apply run, once the phase-1q
artifact's status (stale or historical) is settled. Until then **each read wave adds to the count**,
and a later reader must not read a growing stale number as a growing problem: the number is the wave
count times the entries it homed, and it clears in one run.
