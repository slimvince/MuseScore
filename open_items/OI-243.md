## The invariant, and why it was probed

Music theory: a piece transposed by k semitones has the transposed analysis — every key tonic and
chord root shifts by k (mod 12), the degree structure and segment boundaries are identical. This
is the first invariant check of the perspective-inventory program
(`cowork_oi200_perspective_inventory.md` channel 3), run 2026-08-01/02 as an EXPLORATORY,
read-only probe (surprises are the stage's product, per the scope-of-surprise rule) while the
register-completion dispatch held the register surface.

## The protocol and the measured result

Apparatus: the pure-Python reference decoder (`tools/joint_estimator/probe_decoder.py`, decode
bit-parity-established against production). Established first: 12 deterministically sampled
pieces reproduce the committed `decode_parity_ref.json` exactly, 12/12. Predictions registered
before measuring: ≥99 % segment equivalence under +2, −3, +6 semitone transposition (each applied
as a uniform respelling — pitch +k, line-of-fifths shifted by the matching fifths delta,
signature shifted the same way).

**Measured: 811 of 1224 segments equivalent (66.26 %). Only 6 of 36 piece×shift conditions fully
equivariant; segment boundaries moved in 27 of 36; per shift +2 = 83.3 %, −3 = 65.2 %,
+6 = 50.2 %, with three near-total collapses at +6 (worst 0/32 segments).** The six bit-exact
conditions (total-score delta 0.0) are the apparatus's own soundness proof.

## The mechanism (Cowork-verified at the source)

- `probe_decoder.py:743` — the spelling factor anchors each key at the CANONICAL tonic spelling,
  `key_lof = _PC_TO_FIFTHS[tonic % 12]`; the fitted note table bins each note's line-of-fifths
  position relative to that anchor (`gen_note_tables.py:218-233` per the probe's diagnosis).
  Under a uniform respell, the canonical anchor moves by the fifths delta ±12 for wrap-set
  tonics, re-binning a whole tonality's diatonic tones as pooled chromatic (or a competitor's
  chromatic tones as diatonic). Content scores drive the semi-Markov segmentation, so boundaries
  move — 396 of the 413 violations are this class, in both directions (committed-tonic wraps
  collapse; competitor wraps produce strictly better-scoring transposed paths).
- Exonerated: the signature prior — its fold of `collection_fifths − sig_fifths` into (−6,6]
  (`probe_decoder.py:406-412`) is invariant.
- The remaining label-only violations are the prune tie-break ([[OI-244]]) and propagation from
  adjacent flipped segments (byte-identical factor vectors; the competitor moved).
