# OI-354 — The legacy-mark verification's population has grown since it was declared verified, and a drifted citation hid that

## How it was found, and why the finding is not the thing that was licensed

Ruling 14 licensed ONE act on `tools/audit/decisions/gen_phase1w_legacy_verification.py`: re-aim
the recorded citation at the line the tool's own output names. It also stated a predicted
consequence — *"the standing guard failing set empties"* — to be stated by derivation when it
happened.

**The act was performed and the prediction was refuted.** The citation now resolves; the guard set
does not empty. What happens instead is that a SECOND stop fires, which the first had been hiding.

## The mechanism, read at the tool rather than recalled

The tool checks its declared assumption **A1** — that the legacy marker is generated text emitted
from one data field — at the generator that emits the mark, and it does so BEFORE the derivation
below it rests on that assumption. It raises on the first failure it meets.

Its A1 citation into `gen_decisions_register.py` had drifted, because that generator's preamble grew
above the emission site. So **every run since that drift stopped at A1** and never reached the check
that comes after it: that the tool's AUTHORED per-entry verdict table covers the DERIVED marked set
**exactly** — no derived entry without a verdict, no verdict for an entry the derivation does not
carry.

With A1 resolving again, that second check runs and reports register entries carrying the legacy
mark for which the tool holds no verdict. **Their identities and the tool's exact wording are in the
captured output** at `tools/audit/guard_state.json` → the run for this tool; none is restated here
(**D-431**).

**Checked, not assumed:** the committed verification artifact
`tools/audit/decisions/phase1w_legacy_verification.json` does not mention them.

## Why this is [[OI-289]]'s subject

OI-289's status of record is **✅ VERIFIED**, and what it verified is a **POPULATION** — the
legacy-marked set as it stood at the phase-1w pass of 2026-08-03, with a per-entry verdict on
reachability and on transfer. Entries have been marked since. So that status now describes a set
smaller than the one carrying the mark at HEAD, and **the tool that exists to notice exactly this
was mute for an unrelated reason**.

That is the shape this project's own establishment rule is written against: a claim trusted because
nothing contradicted it, where the thing that would have contradicted it was not running (#19).

## What is owed

**A verdict per newly marked entry, on the two axes the phase-1w pass used:**

1. **Reachability** of the decision's subject at HEAD — with the false-negative path enumerated,
   since OI-289's own finding is that *dormant* is a statement about a DEFAULT and not about
   compilation.
2. **Transfer** — whether a later ruling, or the decision's own text, carries its principle to the
   live design.

## Why a session did not simply author them

Each verdict is a **code trace over the entry's subject plus a citation scan across the
repository** — an establishment act about the live system. That is neither the authored-input
maintenance Ruling 4(b) legitimised (moving an existing judgment WHOLE into a retired block, with
its reason) nor what Ruling 14 licensed, whose whole scope is one citation. Authoring eleven
reachability verdicts under a licence to re-aim an anchor would be a session widening its own
authority, and the verdicts would be the weakest kind — written to clear a guard.

## What is NOT in doubt

- **The marking convention itself** (`CLAUDE.md` decisions-register rule (f), user-ratified
  2026-08-02). This row does not question it, and neither did OI-289.
- **The phase-1w verdicts already recorded.** None is reopened.
- **The re-aim performed under Ruling 14.** It is complete and correct: the citation resolves, and
  the tool's comment records both re-aims and the cause they share.
- **Nothing about the analysis, its inputs, or any measurement of the analysis.** The subject is the
  register's own verification apparatus.

## Why it GATES

Not by the documentation test — by the clause that overrides it. `CLAUDE.md`'s non-gating
declaration states that **an establishment obligation (#19) always gates, whatever its subject,
including one whose subject is the tracking apparatus itself**. A verification recorded as complete
whose population has since grown is precisely that obligation.

## Dated note — 2026-08-09 (CC, `cc_instruction_return_continuation_3.md` Task 0): the user's Ruling 18

**The question this row's own "why a session did not simply author them" section put — whether a
later session is licensed to perform the establishment, or whether it waits — is SETTLED.** Ruling 18
of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_09_third_stop.md` licenses it, and fixes three things about how.

**The METHOD is fixed and is not the session's to choose (#6, #16).** The two axes above are the
phase-1w pass's own, and the ruling says *"by that method and no invented one"*. A session that finds
the method under-specified for a case states the gap; it does not substitute a method of its own.

**The REVIEW CONDITION is what answers this row's objection rather than overriding it.** The
objection was not that the work is hard — it was that verdicts authored to clear a guard are the
weakest kind of establishment there is. That stands, and the remedy is that **the verdicts clear
nothing when they are written**: they are delivered as a ratification-surface reading file for the
user's review, **nothing self-ratifies (#14)**, and the guard clears only when the reviewed set is
applied, in a commit that cites the user's ruling on the registration queue.

**What does NOT move.** [[OI-289]]'s ✅ VERIFIED stays as it stands — it is true of the population it
covered, and this row has never claimed otherwise. The marking convention is untouched. No phase-1w
verdict already recorded is reopened. Ruling 14's re-aim remains complete and correct.

**This row stays OPEN.** The establishment was performed at Task 1 of the same dispatch and is at
`ratification_surfaces/cowork_oi354_legacy_mark_establishment_2026_08_09.md`. The standing guard
failure is CARRIED rather than cleared — deliberately, and by the ruling's own design.
