# OI-338 — The phase-1 completion inventory publishes as an open question one the user has since ruled

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Section F — doc-sync debt (#10). CC-found 2026-08-04 at the commit-and-finish-line wave, Task 2.1,
by re-reading D-231's clause at the file instead of reusing the earlier reading of it.**

## The finding

`tools/audit/phase1_completion_inventory.json` publishes a block named
`the_question_the_two_halves_do_not_settle`, whose fields read:

- `the_question` — *"Does phase 1's TRUE half reach a document's account of ITSELF — a status
  banner, an as-built anchor, a missing supersession note — or only its account of the analysis?"*
- `who_settles_it` — *"the user. It is reported, not decided."*

and an authored criterion **C5** closing *"The question is REPORTED, not settled — see
`the_question_the_two_halves_do_not_settle`."*

**At HEAD the question is settled.** The user ruled it on 2026-08-04 as **D-639**: the doc-sync half
reaches a document's account of itself **only where that account changes how the document's analysis
content is read**, with three worked examples that *are* the test (an as-built banner over a dormant
mechanism — IN; a missing supersession note on a superseded plan — IN; a stale anchor or a
formatting artifact — OUT) and an explicit fallback, option (1A), for a case the test does not decide
mechanically.

## Why the file did not move when the ruling landed — which is the part worth keeping

The inventory's two halves are maintained by different mechanisms, and only one of them tracks HEAD:

- Its **clause quote is DERIVED** — `locate_clause()` finds D-231's clause by anchor string and
  quotes the span. D-639's text sits inside that span, so `the_requirement.phase_1_verbatim` contains
  the ruling at HEAD.
- Its **criteria are AUTHORED** — a Python constant in the generator. It did not move, and nothing
  makes it move.

So the artifact quotes the ruling and, a few fields later, reports the question the ruling settles as
open. And `gen_phase1_completion_inventory.py --check` **PASSES**, correctly: the committed artifact
does match a fresh derivation. The check compares the artifact against the generator, and the
generator carries the stale half, so the two agree about being wrong.

This is the [[OI-332]] / [[OI-334]] shape a third time — a computed half that is current beside an
authored half that is not — and it is the sharpest instance so far, because here both halves live in
**one file** and a guard passes over the contradiction between them.

## What is NOT in doubt

Everything the inventory derives. Every population, every entry and row identifier, every home class,
every defense gap, every gate verdict and the read-programme completion are functions over the
register and the open-items index at HEAD; they moved when the applier ran and when the census
delegation landed, and they re-derive byte-identically. The dispatch that found this had declared the
opposite assumption — that the COMPLETE-half figures were **stale** — and that assumption was
**refuted** at the guard: `--check` passes and the figures reflect both later acts.

The corrected reading is also on the record rather than lost: it is carried at
`tools/audit/phase1_finish_line.json` → `the_re_reading_of_the_clause`, which is where the dispatch
ordered it written.

## Why it was not fixed

Two reasons, and the dispatch's freeze is only the first.

1. The dispatch forbids fixing anything the session finds, in its own header: *"Do not fix anything
   you find. If a task tempts you to repair something, that is the behaviour this dispatch exists to
   stop."*
2. Independently of that: the inventory is also **the record of the wave that produced it**. Editing
   its authored reading in place is a **#12** question — whether a wave artifact is a snapshot of what
   that wave concluded or a living surface — not a typo correction. And a repair that only rewrote the
   constant would leave the same hazard for the next ruling, since nothing would still make the
   authored half move.

Both a re-wording and a structural remedy (deriving the criteria, or making the generator STOP when
the clause span contains a ruling its criteria do not carry) are available. Neither is proposed here.

## Disposition under D-641

**Rowed and left.** D-641, homed the same day at `cowork_audit_protocol.md`, sorts every finding by
D-438's test: does the subject bear on the analysis, on the analysis's inputs, or on an instrument a
measurement depends on? Applied here:

- The subject is an **authored prose field inside an audit artifact**.
- It bears on **no** analysis behaviour, **no** analysis input, and **no** instrument that any
  measurement of the analysis depends on — the corpus measurement tools, the robust-stop reference
  and the pinned measurement tool are all untouched by it.
- It carries **no establishment obligation (#19)**, which is the one exception that would make it
  gate and be surfaced whatever its subject.

So the row is the whole of what is owed: no wave, no dispatch, no surface. That verdict is recorded
here as this row's own reading of its subject; the non-gating set itself is **derived and never
hand-listed** (`tools/audit/nongating_apparatus_rows.json` from `gen_nongating_apparatus_rows.py`),
per `CLAUDE.md` and the reason [[OI-319]]'s provenance records.
