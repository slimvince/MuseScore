## What was ordered, and what was done

The user's **Ruling 24(b)** of `cowork_rulings_2026_08_09_fourth_stop.md` ordered the detection
method [[OI-358]] had stated — *compare each entry's verbatim against its own title and defense* —
to be built **CORPUS FIRST**, as an advisory report over the whole decisions register, with its
separation measured on the five known-bad entries against the remainder, and **adopted as a guard
ONLY on measured clean separation**.

All of that was performed. `tools/audit/decisions/gen_verbatim_subject_consistency.py` carries the
five known-bad quotes as its labelled corpus, recorded before the mechanism was measured and kept
as DATA rather than read from the live entries — because the repair re-takes those five quotes, so
a corpus read from live data would empty itself at the moment of repair and the measurement could
never be reproduced.

## The result, which is negative and is reported rather than tuned

**The separation is not clean.** All five known-bad quotes sit at the floor of the value, and a
substantial population of legitimate entries sits at or below that same floor. No threshold divides
the two populations, so a guard built on this value would deny legitimate entries — and **D-473's
ground is exactly that a guard which fires on legitimate work gets switched off, which is worse than
none.** The three measured conditions a mechanism is judged on (**D-436**) are the same test.

**★ The sharper result is about the signal's CEILING rather than its threshold.** After the repair,
four of the five entries rise. **One stays at the floor** — its correct bullet states the rule in
code names while its own title paraphrases them in ordinary words, which is precisely the blindness
the tool declares about itself in advance. So this method is not merely un-thresholded: it is
measurably unable to see one of the five cases it was derived from.

Every value lives at `tools/audit/decisions/verbatim_subject_consistency.json` and none is restated
here (**D-431**).

## What remains open — [[OI-358]]'s second gating ground, untouched by the repair

[[OI-358]] gated on two independent grounds. The first — five entries stating one rule's identity
over another rule's words — is discharged by the repair. The second is not:

- `gen_decisions_register.py --check` verifies the rendered files match the source data;
- `gen_cluster_dispositions.py --verify` verifies every quote is found at its cited home and starts
  at the cited line.

**Both are satisfied by a corrupted pair.** Once the quote IS the text at the drifted line, the two
agree with each other permanently. Their green verdicts therefore do not bound what they appear to
bound, which is the shape **#19** refuses — a thing trusted because nothing contradicted it, where
what would contradict it cannot.

## What is NOT proposed here

reserves to the user, and the order this record fixed for a guard family puts the corpus before the
clause — the corpus for this class now exists and is committed, so a later act starts from it rather
than from a blank page.

**The advisory artifact is explicitly NOT to be tuned until it separates.** Adjusting the token
count, the stopword list or the comparison until the five known-bad cases fall below the remainder
would be fitting the signal to the cases that motivated it, which `DEFECT_TYPES.md` **DT-2** forbids
and which is the reason the adoption condition was ruled *in advance* rather than after the
measurement.
