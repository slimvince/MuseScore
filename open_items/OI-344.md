# OI-344 — A LIVE guard carries a DATED recomputation of a proxy figure taken off the cluster-disposition layer, so refreshing that layer makes it fail

> STATUS IS AUTHORITATIVE IN THE INDEX (OPEN_ITEMS.md) — this file carries narrative and provenance only and is NEVER the status of record

**Found 2026-08-07 (CC) at this wave's own guard run, and caused by its own licensed act** — the
OI-333 repair the user ruled in the same dispatch (`cc_instruction_five_rulings.md` §0a, R4), which
regenerated the cluster-disposition layer at the register's current backbone.

## What fails

`python tools/audit/decisions/gen_reads5_repack.py --check` reports *"STALE:
reads5_repack.json does not re-derive"* and exits non-zero. It passed at the committed tree.

## The cause, measured at the objects rather than inferred

The artifact carries a block — `why_the_regime_artifact_is_read_and_not_regenerated` — whose
`per_proxy` table records, per candidate ordering proxy, a rank correlation and how far it moved.
The entry for the `unresolved_cluster_count` proxy holds that correlation under the key
`recomputed_2026_08_04`, beside a `moved_by` field.

**That value is recomputed on every run, against the disposition layer as it stands at the time of
the run.** Refreshing the layer therefore moves it, and the check — whole-artifact equality — fails.

A field-by-field comparison of the committed artifact against a fresh build shows the difference
**confined to that one proxy entry** — the recomputed correlation and its companion `moved_by` — with
no other field of the artifact moving. Nothing about the re-pack itself, the registered bands or any
wave's yield is in question.

## Why it was NOT repaired

Two candidate repairs, and neither is a session's:

**Regenerate the artifact.** That writes a figure taken on 2026-08-07 into a field whose own key
says it was recomputed on 2026-08-04, so the artifact would then assert something false about when
its own measurement was taken. This is the [[OI-330]] / [[OI-301]] refusal exactly — a historical
measurement destroyed by the regeneration meant to maintain it.

**Re-key or freeze the field.** Freezing the dated figure and reading it back is the shape that
works, and `gen_phase1q_snapshot_establishment.py` already uses it for its own two moment-in-time
checks. But it changes a registration artifact's structure, and the dispatch that found this
forbids fixing anything beyond its own tasks.

## What is NOT in doubt

The re-pack itself, the registered reading bands, and every completed wave's measured yield are
untouched. The tool's classification as re-deriving a live invariant is untouched — it does assert
one, and it asserts it correctly. The figure that moved is a **proxy used to order reading waves**,
not an input to any analysis and not a quantity any measurement of the analysis rests on.

## The general lesson, stated so it outlives the row

**A live guard whose artifact contains a DATED recomputation cannot stay green across a legitimate
refresh of what it recomputes against.** Either the figure is frozen and read back — which is what
makes a dated measurement survive its own guard — or the guard eventually fails because the project
did correct work, which is the definition of measuring the clock rather than the repository (the
user's ruling R4 of 2026-08-04, `OPEN_ITEMS.md` OI-330). This one is a single field inside an
otherwise live tool, which is why it surfaced as a whole-artifact drift rather than as a claim
anyone would read.

## Why the gate verdict is the default

**D-438**'s test: does the row's subject bear on the analysis, its inputs, or an instrument a
measurement depends on? **The row's own text does not settle it.** The proxy orders reading waves,
which is apparatus; the layer it is computed off is an instrument the OI-207 coverage measurement
reports against, which is not. The declaration's own default therefore applies and the row **GATES**
— and no gating verdict is hand-added, for the reason [[OI-319]]'s provenance records: a hand-added
verdict for a row the derived cut never reached is exactly the hand-listing `CLAUDE.md` forbids.

## Dated note, 2026-08-07 (CC) — the repair, and the structure it replaces (#12)

The user ruled **R3** (dispatch `cc_instruction_licensed_homing_and_oi344.md` §0a): freeze the dated
proxy figure and read it back. Done in `tools/audit/decisions/gen_reads5_repack.py`. The status of
record is in the INDEX row; what this note carries is the structure that was replaced and the two
readings the ruling's wording admitted.

**THE FORMER STRUCTURE, PRESERVED VERBATIM (#12).** Inside `measure_the_proxy_contamination()` the
per-proxy table was built by recomputing every figure on every run:

```python
    registered = regime["proxy"]["correlations_with_yield_on_the_read_set"]
    now = {}
    for name, xs in proxies.items():
        rho = round(regime_gen.spearman(xs, ys, "average"), 4)
        was = registered[name]["spearman_rho_average_ties"]
        now[name] = {"registered": was, "recomputed_2026_08_04": rho,
                     "moved_by": round(rho - was, 4),
                     "ci95_registered": registered[name]["ci95_on_the_average_ties_figure"]}
```

The tool's docstring also listed *"the correlation measurement above"* among the things the file
DERIVES. That line was corrected rather than left standing: after the freeze the derived half of
that measurement is the proxy vectors and the moved-naming list, and the two dated fields are
frozen — a `derived` list naming a frozen field is the same defect this row records one level down.

**WHY THE FREEZE COVERS ALL FOUR PROXIES.** The ruling names the fields `recomputed_2026_08_04` and
`moved_by`; every one of the four candidate proxies carries both. The narrow reading — freeze only
the `unresolved_cluster_count` entry, the one the disposition refresh moved — was available and was
NOT taken. All four correlations are computed against the same `yield` vector, which is the count of
register entries homed at each of the 36 READ documents, so a HOMING ACT that touches one of those
documents moves all four together; phase 1's criterion C1 obliges homing acts. The narrow reading
would therefore leave three fields labelled with a 2026-08-04 date carrying figures taken today —
the same false statement this row objects to.

**★ AND THE COUNTERFACTUAL WAS MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED, WHICH CORRECTED THIS NOTE'S FIRST
VERSION.** It read that the narrow freeze *"would have left the guard red again at the end of this
same wave"*, preserved here (#12) because it is wrong and the correction is the point. Recomputing
all four proxies at the tree of 2026-08-07 reproduces the three non-disposition proxies EXACTLY and
moves only `unresolved_cluster_count` — so **a narrow freeze would have held at this wave.** The
reason is checkable per entry: **none of the eight entries homed here had a former home among the 36
read documents**, so this wave moved no yield count at all. What makes the width right is the NEXT
act rather than this one — entries whose home IS one of the 36 remain in the re-home population, and
**D-281**, one of the three this wave's assumption A1 refused, is homed in one of them. Stated this
way so a reviewer can disagree with the width in one place, and so that no claim here rests on a
counterfactual nobody ran.

**WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT FROZEN, and the residual it leaves.**
`read_documents_whose_naming_count_moved_since_registration` carries no date and is a statement
about the three user-ratified surfaces as they stand today, so it keeps the semantics it had.
It can still move: a delegation, or a homing act that writes a read document's FILE NAME into a
governing document, would move it — which is the [[OI-330]]/[[OI-328]] lesson and the reason a
homing act records its provenance in the register's own fields and never in the specification text.

**ASSUMPTION A3, DISCHARGED AT THE OBJECTS.** A recursive field-by-field comparison of
`reads5_repack.json` before and after the change reports exactly one difference in the whole file:
the added `★_the_dated_figures_are_FROZEN_and_read_back` block. No value moved, none was removed
and none was re-typed — the figures the tool now reads back are the ones the artifact already
carried, so the repair restores the guard without touching a recorded measurement.
