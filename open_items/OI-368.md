## What was established, and how

**The five discharged sites, read at the files rather than at the record of them.** The four
record-arm branch comments — two in `notationcomposingbridge.cpp`, one in
`notationimplodebridge.cpp`, one in `notationtuningbridge.cpp` — now read *default ON* and name the
notation switch. The record section adapter's *"DORMANT — no `src/` caller yet"* comment is gone from
`sectionrecordadapter.cpp`. All five are covered by the one licensed comment-only act, the user's
**Ruling 16**, and its own enumeration `tools/audit/arm_comment_sweep.json` records what it did to
each.

**The surviving site, quoted from the file.** `tools/batch_analyze.cpp:4657-4659` opens a paragraph
headed *"STAGED SCOPE (the re-scoped dispatch)"* and reads *"the in-app NOTATION layer stays on the
legacy analysis (its tables-packaging + richer output-surface contract are the named successor
increment); this flag touches only the batch/corpus output."* That was true when the OI-178 adoption
staged its scope to the batch/corpus surface; the user-ratified notation switch of 2026-07-27 ended
it.

**The same file's corrected block, also quoted.** `:4915-4919` reads *"The OI-178 adoption's staged
scope was the batch/corpus surface only; that staging is CLOSED — the 2026-07-27 notation switch put
the joint estimator on the in-app notation surface too (useJointNotationRecord defaults ON), so both
production surfaces are the joint arm and the legacy notation path is dormant, awaiting deletion at

**So the defect is worse than the original single false statement**, and that is the reason it is
worth a row rather than a remark: a reader who meets `:4657` is told one thing, a reader who meets
`:4916` is told the opposite, and neither block points at the other.

## Why it is a row about a DERIVATION rather than only about a comment

The comment is one edit. What is not one edit is the reason nobody saw it.

`gen_phase1w_legacy_verification.py`'s side-finding block **re-locates its class on every run** — that
is the point of it, and it is why the block can honestly report `count: 0`. But a locating pattern
reports what it matches, and **its reach against the text it scans has never been measured**. That is
the condition **D-436** requires of every mechanism (a measured detection rate) and the answer
**D-661** gives to what *complete* means (complete relative to a NAMED derivation, with its measured
miss rate as a term of the claim). Neither is satisfied here, so the block's EMPTY verdict bounds
nothing.

**It is [[OI-367]]'s shape one instrument over.** There a family of index cells was enumerated by a
pattern that had never been measured against the openings the index actually uses, and a fourth
member turned up while a later task derived its own population. Here a family of code comments was
enumerated by a pattern that has never been measured against the comment text the tree actually
carries, and a surviving member turned up while a later task verified a row before flipping it. **In
both cases the derivation was right about what it matched and silent about what it did not**, which
is the failure #19 refuses to treat as establishment.

**The sibling sweep does not cover it either, and that is checked rather than assumed.**
`tools/audit/gen_arm_comment_sweep.py` derives the same class from a scan of `src/`, by the scope its
own ruling gives it; this site is under `tools/`, so it is outside that population by construction and
not by an oversight in the sweep.

That Ruling 48 was performed wrongly. It licensed ONE comment and that comment was corrected; the
enumeration that would have told the applying session there was a second site is the thing that was
silent.

That [[OI-303]] is discharged. Five of its six named claims are; the sixth stands, which is why that
row is recorded rather than flipped.

Anything about the analysis's behaviour, any measurement of it, any golden, any corpus of scores or
anything in `tools/robust_stop/`. Every subject here is a statement ABOUT which arm ships and the

## 2026-08-11 — RESOLVED, and the two acts closed by two different routes (CC, `cc_instruction_return_continuation_13.md` Task 0, on the user's Ruling 59)

**(a) The comment is corrected.** `tools/batch_analyze.cpp`'s staged-scope block now says what the
same file's flag-parsing block already said: the OI-178 adoption's staging is CLOSED, both production
surfaces are the joint arm, and the legacy notation path is dormant awaiting deletion at the OI-180
retirement map. The former wording is preserved in place inside the comment (#12), and the thing that
is **not** closed — this flag's own default, still OFF, so a flag-less run still measures the legacy
pipeline — is kept apart in the same words the sibling block uses. **The diff was verified
comment-only MECHANICALLY**, against the committed blob read as a git OBJECT by explicit hash
(**D-253**'s sanctioned form): every changed line on both sides begins with a comment marker.
**And the three suites were run rather than the reasoning trusted**, on the eighth continuation's own
lesson that *a comment cannot change behaviour* is an argument and not a measurement.

**(b) The pattern's reach is BOUNDED rather than measured, and that is the ruling's own choice.**
`gen_phase1w_legacy_verification.py`'s side-finding block now carries an **ADVISORY WITH UNMEASURED
REACH** statement: the enumeration is an authored table whose members a session reading found, it
re-locates what it holds and looks for nothing new, so an empty live table bounds nothing about the
class. **No detection measurement is seeded**, on the ruling's stated ground — establishment is spent
where an analysis decision consumes it, and no analysis decision consumes a comment sweep. The
seventh site is recorded in that file's source as a **MISS** rather than as a retirement, because it
never entered the live table and so never left it.

**What this resolution does NOT claim**, recorded here as well as on the INDEX row because the
distinction is the whole content of half (b): that the pattern has been measured; that no further
instance exists; or that any other enumeration of the same shape inherits this ground. **The test the
ruling gives is whether an ANALYSIS DECISION consumes the enumeration**, and it is applied per
enumeration rather than once for the class — [[OI-367]]'s pattern is the neighbouring case and this
row settles nothing about it.

**Nothing about the analysis moved**, no measured value, no golden, no corpus of scores, nothing in
`tools/robust_stop/`.
