**Section D — review (user-directed) / correction of record on [[OI-199]]**

In receiving OI-199 pass 1, Cowork initially faulted the 1,069 joint dispositions as "generated, not
made" — produced by `gen_joint_dispositions.py` with a closed verdict set, therefore (the claim) not a
genuine reading and provisionally void — and proposed a "verdict-entropy guard" to reject low-diversity
disposition sets. **Measured against the certified L4 precedent, that claim was WRONG and is withdrawn**
(user's ruling, 2026-07-28):

- The certified L4 decoder pass (`tools/audit/l4/pass1_dispositions_decoder.csv`, user-certified) carries
  **nine distinct (kind, verdict, reason) combinations over 311 rows, all 127 of its branch rows on ONE
  verdict** (SURVIVES).
- The OI-199 joint pass carries **ten combinations over 1,069 rows** — MORE verdict diversity than the
  precedent the user certified.
- **Generated dispositions with hand-curated exception sets ARE the ratified audit method** (the same
  `gen_*_dispositions.py` pattern the L1/L2/L3/L4/L5 passes all used). A low verdict entropy is the
  EXPECTED shape of a clean production-surviving module, not evidence of a non-reading.

**The verdict-entropy guard is withdrawn** — it was calibrated against nothing (no threshold, no
false-positive analysis) and would have failed the very precedent the user certified. CC's OI-199 pass-1
inventory, partition proposal, feasibility stop, field curation, and rows all STAND. The genuine
shortfall was narrower and is tracked at [[OI-224]] (two arms under-delivered), completed by
`cc_instruction_oi199_joint_p3_p4.md`.

Recorded so no future reader treats the committed OI-199 pass-1 dispositions as discredited.
