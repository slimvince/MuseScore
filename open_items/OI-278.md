## Why this is a row and not a correction made on the spot

Three reasons, each of which alone is enough.

- **The refutation is a measurement about the code, and what follows from it is a design call.**
  Whether the right response is to drop the unification (accepting four sites that genuinely ask
  different questions), to make the four ask the same question first and then unify, or to let the
  whole item lapse with the legacy path, is a decision — and the standing rule forbids designing
  a fix while the scaffolding is still being built.
- **The target the deferral pointed at no longer exists as planned.** FQ-1's sequencing put it
  before the Layer-5 engagement; that engagement was itself overtaken by the joint estimator with
  no ruling naming it (`OPEN_ITEMS.md` [[OI-259]] re-dispositions the whole engage-era agenda). So
  "do FQ-1 before Layer 5" cannot be executed as written.
- **Three of the four sites are on the dormant path.** They retire at the [[OI-180]] map, which
  changes the value of unifying them — but the fourth site is in the dormant DECODER, and the
  question of what the pedal reader consumes ([[OI-194]], D-385/D-386) is live work.

## What is NOT in doubt

The measurement itself. The four sites are named, the divergence is named predicate by predicate,
and the stop was reported rather than worked around — which is the behaviour the standing
surprise-is-a-STOP rule asks for. The finding here is about the RECORD, not about that judgment.
