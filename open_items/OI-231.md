## Why this is a conformance item and not a defect report

The **one in-tree consumer that would have been broken is guarded**: `detectCadences` /
`detectPivotChords` read the STORED per-region `hasAssertiveExposure` flag and never re-threshold
the confidence field (`sectioncadencedetection.cpp:76-89`, the P2a unification — register entry
D-018). So no wrong reading is produced today, and this row is not a live inference defect.

What it *is*: an **unbounded quantity published on a layer-boundary field whose declared contract
says [0,1]**, in direct opposition to a decision ratified by the user on 2026-07-02, with **no
recorded amendment to that contract**. Three specific exposures follow, each cheap to check and
none currently checked:

1. **A future consumer has no way to know.** The field name, its type-header comment
   (`keymodeanalyzer.h:111`) and the canonical architecture document (`ARCHITECTURE.md:2224`) all
   still promise [0,1]. A consumer written against the declared contract — the obvious form being
   any `>= 0.5` / `>= 0.8` comparison — is wrong on the production arm and right on the legacy arm,
   with nothing to make that visible. This is exactly the "silent re-interpretation downstream" U4
   forbids.
2. **The value crosses the module boundary.** `NoteHarmonicContext::keyConfidence`
   (`notationcomposingbridge.h:63`) is a `mu::notation` public type; the quantity therefore leaves
   the composing module carrying no declaration of its scale or class.
3. **`groupinglayer.cpp:101` clamps a key confidence into [0,1]** (`std::clamp(units[i].keyConfidence, 0.0, 1.0)`)
   — a consumer that already assumes the contract. It is fed from a different producer today; it is
   named here so the assumption is on the record.

## The disposition owed

This is a **decision surface, not a fix**: either (a) the record arm publishes a squashed value at
the boundary and keeps the raw gap internally, as U2 prescribes; or (b) the confidence contract is
amended, with the amendment ratified and recorded, to admit a declared unbounded model-internal
class at the boundary — in which case the field must be renamed and its declared contract corrected
at `keymodeanalyzer.h:111` and `ARCHITECTURE.md:2224`. Choosing between them is a Premise-Gate
decision at its #8-correct stage. **Nothing is fixed here** (a read-only adjudication).
